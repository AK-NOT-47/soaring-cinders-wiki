Narrative Themes & Storytelling Philosophy

CANONCaelian OdysseyNotes / Design

Narrative Themes & Storytelling Philosophy CANON

The guiding themes and taste rules: death and the regrets of the dead; a specific flavor of underdog story (small fish in a big pond, not “everybody is mean to you”); and easter eggs everywhere, Undertale-style.

Summary — This page collects the thematic and philosophical throughlines the team set for the game’s writing: the central Death and Regrets theme (carrying the influence of the dead into the story), the largely-undeveloped Love and Heroism themes, the strongly-held underdog storytelling philosophy (a taste debate about meanness, with Ranking of Kings / Game of Thrones as models and Witcher 3 as the anti-model), and the intent to pack in easter eggs everywhere as exploration incentives.

Theme: Death and Regrets CANON

The team’s signature theme. “I really want to deeply explore the regrets of the dead as one of our themes is death” (M04644, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-24). What makes death sad is unattained dreams (004 §Core Concept).

Implementation avenues (004 §Implementation Avenues):

  • Flynt/Locke Evertide and Thane Farrow are the primary avenues; Thane carries a lot of his comrades’ wants and dreams — “a bit of them lives with him.”
  • Ruby once she kills Citrine; Astrid possibly regretful about Raphael’s fate; Steele picks up Warpath after Flynt dies.
  • “Carrying the influence of the dead visibly into the latter parts of the story is generally just good storytelling.”

Done well (004 §Examples of Done Well): Spider-Verse; Spider-Man’s “great power, great responsibility” from Uncle Ben (“I like it when there’s some physical object involved though” — M04680, FreshToast, 2023-10-24); Game of Thrones (big ramifications, Ned Stark is peak — M04682, 2023-10-24).

Additional concepts (004 §Additional Concepts): a minor villain who kills for vengeance of the dead; the dead who died terribly and wish to curse their killers — “Dying curses are a fun trope” (M04669, FreshToast, 2023-10-24). (Cross-ref: Clara’s vengeance branch.)

Theme: Love & Theme: Heroism CANON

  • Love: “I don’t know what to do with the theme of love so I will leave that to you” — deliberately handed off, undeveloped (004 §Theme: Love).
  • Heroism: “But I think I can have some ideas for death and heroism” — flagged as a theme, developed mainly through Lysander’s hollow heroism vs. Locke Evertide’s earned kind (004 §Theme: Heroism).

Underdog Storytelling Philosophy CANON

A taste debate (2023-11-16) about how to establish an underdog. FreshToast: “don’t really enjoy the ‘everybody is mean to you’ way of establishing an underdog” — prefers making the character feel like a small fish in a big pond, with “stuff beyond their scope and strength, people to aspire to” (M05060, FreshToast, 2023-11-16). Rudeness’s purpose is to make you dislike a character, and audiences wish unlikeable characters would die (004 §Debate on Meanness).

Models (004 §Examples):

  • Ranking of Kings — CrunchyConniptions’s preferred underdog story; “does both” (mean and not) (M05065, M05072, 2023-11-16).
  • Bocchi the Rock — “I would not want people to be mean to Bocchi” (the gentle-underdog model).
  • Game of Thrones (good example) — measured; “cruelty is reserved for appropriate characters,” reacted to properly, used to characterize.
  • Witcher 3 (bad example) — “everybody is awful for no clear reason”; “the Witcher is a bit much… Then why does everyone like it?” (M05075, M05078, 2023-11-16).

Underdog formula (004 §Underdog Formula): small fish in big pond; stuff beyond scope and strength; people to aspire to; not everybody being mean; personal and reasonable conflict when it exists.

Design & voice principles CANON

  • “Audience doesn’t want to be duped. They want to feel smart” (M04693, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-25). Seeds of doubt (about Lysander) will be immediate; he should unravel to the audience faster than Dutch from RDR2, likely devolving entirely by the time the dragon is dealt with at end of Act 2 (M04692, M04701, M04702, FreshToast, 2023-10-25). See Lysander’s Villain Turn & Two-Phase Final Boss.
  • Character-voice constraints for Lysander and Locke Evertide (Disco Elysium / Game of Thrones frame, not One Piece; Lysander is not a buffoon; Locke isn’t “sir”/“hero”-speak) — 004 §Character Voice Constraints.

Easter Eggs & Meta Content CANON

  • “I mentioned I want easter eggs everywhere, like Undertale. How do we feel about that?” — “Easter eggs are fun. Exploration incentives” (M04848–M04850, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-10-30).
  • Shared-abilities easter egg: “Imagine Cyrus gets some powerful named attack in [the] first game” and Locke Evertide earns the same/similar ability as a skill in the second game — “since both wield flame” (004 §Shared Abilities). (Scope note: the wider Cyrus/Luminarium story belongs to the separate Caelian Odyssey iteration; only this cross-game link is in scope here.)
  • Mystery creates village interaction: creating mystery intrigues the player to interact with NPCs to find out what’s happening (004 §Mystery Creates Village Interaction) — the design engine behind Moonstone Mania (Meta Collectathon Venture).

Design history

  • 2023-10-24 — the Death & Regrets theme is deepened (regrets of the dead; dying curses; Spider-Verse/GoT models; carrying the dead’s influence forward) (M04644, M04669, M04680, M04682, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-10-24).
  • 2023-10-30 — “easter eggs everywhere, like Undertale” agreed as fun exploration incentives (M04848–M04850, 2023-10-30).
  • 2023-11-16 — the underdog/meanness taste debate settles the formula: small-fish-in-big-pond over “everybody is mean,” with RoK/Bocchi/GoT as models and Witcher 3 as anti-model (M05060–M05078, 2023-11-16).
