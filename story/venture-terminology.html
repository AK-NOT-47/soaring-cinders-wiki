"Ventures" — Quest Terminology Decision

CANONCaelian OdysseyNotes / Design
Also known as: Ventures, Quest naming, What we call quests
First conceived 2024-02-13 (M05273, FreshToast) · last in chat 2024-02-13 (M05316, spirit_noodle)

“Ventures” — Quest Terminology Decision CANON

What the game calls its quests: after a terminology debate, spirit_noodle decreed “Ventures.”

Summary — The team debated what to call quests in the game — “Quest,” “Venture,” “Errand,” “Trial,” and others were on the table. “Trial” was ruled out because Ajaxian trials already exist as a distinct thing. After narrowing to Quest/Venture/Errand, the decision was handed to spirit_noodle, who decreed “Ventures.” So quests in Soaring Cinders are Ventures (004 §Spirit_noodle’s Decision, CANON).

The debate CANON

Options considered (004 §Options Considered): Quest (classic, familiar, homey), Venture (exciting, adventurous), Trial (already used for Ajaxian trials), Expedition, Challenge, Errand (mundane but sounds nice), Favor, Legend, Task (“most boring but applicable” — rejected: “no”), Mission (“anything but this”).

Reasoning (M05273–M05286, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13): “Quest is classic but it might be overused or even inappropriate, considering there’s only one literal ‘quest.’” “Maybe something more loose and adventure-y like ‘Venture.’” “We’ve already got Ajaxian trials in game… so probably something more generic, even though I like ‘trial’ too.”

Narrowed (M05295, M05298, FreshToast, 2024-02-13): “Yeah I prefer Venture, Errand & Quest so far.” “I think Errand is worse than the other two.” “I’m content with Quest or Venture then.”

The decision CANON

The choice was handed to spirit_noodle (Noods): “We will let noods pick… And if we don’t like his choice, then we kick him.” “NOODS GIVE YOUR OPINION — DECIDE THE FATE OF THE GAME — ARE THEY VENTURES OR QUESTS? — DECIDE OR DIE” (004 §Final Decision Process).

spirit_noodle’s ruling (M05283/M05316–M05321, spirit_noodle, 2024-02-13):

“Ventures.” — “I have decided.” — “I AM THE DECIDER.” — “It is written.”

Outcome: VENTURES selected. “VERY WELL. IF WE ARE CONTENT WITH THIS DECISION, SO IT SHALL BE.” “I guess we are hipsters then.” “INTO THE WAVE OF CREATIVE REIMAGINATION WE ARE SWEPT” (004 §Spirit_noodle’s Decision).
