Moonstone Mania (Meta Collectathon Venture)

Caelian OdysseyNotes / Design
Also known as: Meta Collectathon Quest, Cult of Mundane Moments, Moonstone Messiah
First conceived 2024-02-13

The self-aware Skyrim-gems parody: hoard an absurd pile of moonstones just to see an NPC’s baffled reaction — and accidentally trigger a world-altering religion of everyday gratitude.

Summary — A meta side-venture riffing on Skyrim’s rare-gems quest. The player collects a ludicrous number of moonstones; the only real motive is a single line of dialogue. When Archmage Xarthus demands to know why, the player admits it was “just to see your surprised face” — and Xarthus, misreading it as profound, founds the Cult of Mundane Moments, spreads it across the nation, and the game visibly turns saccharine. The point is that it isn’t worth it, played self-aware and funny (004 §Design Intent).

Concept

  • A deliberate reference to Skyrim’s rare-gems quest — collect an absurd amount of crystals, with the game making clear there’s a reward (004 §Meta Collectathon Quest).
  • The player then meets an NPC confounded as to why they did this; the player says it was for this dialogue. The NPC is puzzled that all the player wanted was simple dialogue, finds it hard to accept, and misconstrues the simple desire as something deeper — suggesting we should cherish everyday interactions (004 §Meta Collectathon Quest).
  • The NPC has a eureka moment, becomes a monk, starts a religion, spreads it across the nation, and the game’s appearance changes — people are happier (004 §Meta Collectathon Quest).

Design intent (004 §Design Intent): “The point is it isn’t worth it” — “and be self aware and funny about it,” “rather than bad quest by design.” “This is deranged but part of me does want to throw in an absurd collectathon for the sake of tradition.”

Full quest example (written)

Set in a bustling marketplace in Caelia (004 §Full Quest Example Written):

  • The player approaches a weary merchant named Elara: “Remember [that] crazy gem-hoarding quest? Could pull off again with Moonstones!” Elara: “Moonstones?! Are you mad? Rarer than a dragon with manners!” The player insists the reward’s worth it; Elara relents, warning of “angry werewolves.”
  • [Quest Begins: Moonstone Mania] — months pass; the player becomes a moonstone magnet, amassing a ludicrous pile. News reaches the reclusive Archmage Xarthus.
  • Xarthus encounter: “Why? Why all the moonstones? What grand magic?” Player: “No magic, just wanted to see your surprised face.” Xarthus is dumbfounded, then revelation dawns: “Of course! True magic lies in the mundane! We take for granted simple interactions!”
  • Cult of Mundane Moments: Xarthus founds it; his charisma wins over the masses; Caelia changes — people greet each other with genuine smiles, birds chirp louder, even bandits offer discounts on stolen goods. Player: “I just wanted the Archmage’s reaction, man! This cult thing is getting weird…” Xarthus: “Nay! You are the Moonstone Messiah!” Player: “Oh gods, what have I done?”
  • Ending: the screen zooms out, Caelia bathed in an overly-saccharine glow, birds chirping excessively, a bandit offering a flower. Player (V.O.): “I just wanted some dialogue. But now the fate of Caelia rests on my moonstones and questionable motives. Can’t a hero get some peace and quiet around here?” The End (or is it?)

Related

  • Uses the “venture” framing agreed the same day (2024-02-13).
  • Elara (weary Caelia merchant) and Archmage Xarthus (the Moonstone Messiah) are the two named NPCs — see characters batch for any dedicated pages.
  • Echoes the “Mystery Creates Village Interaction” idea: a mystery draws the player to interact with NPCs (004 §Mystery Creates Village Interaction).

Design history

  • 2024-02-13 — the whole quest is conceived and written out in one session: the Skyrim reference, the “player just wanted the dialogue” twist, the Cult of Mundane Moments, and the full Elara → Xarthus → Moonstone Messiah script (2024-02-13). No later revision recorded.
