The Combat-Strategy Arc (Goblins → Dragon → Lysander → the Wish)

CANONProject MagitechMixed
Also known as: Combat Strategy Arc, Become as the Dragon, Story Through Gameplay
First conceived 2024-02-16 (M05345, CrunchyConniptions) · last in chat 2024-02-16 (M05345, CrunchyConniptions)

The Combat-Strategy Arc (Goblins → Dragon → Lysander → the Wish) CANON

The player’s hands learn the theme before the cutscenes spell it out: beat goblins with raw power, beat the dragon by becoming a goblin, beat Lysander by becoming the dragon — and beat the wish itself by refusing to swing at all.

Summary — The combat difficulty arc is deliberately the same arc as the narrative. Three escalating lessons: beat goblins with raw individual overwhelming power; beat the dragon by becoming as a goblin (many, united, humble); beat Lysander by becoming as the dragon. Mapped to the three acts, and capped by the Daemon of Want, which cannot be cut at all — only refused (004 §Combat Strategy Arc, CANON).

The three lessons CANON

The core quotes, part of a “Story through gameplay” note (M05345, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-16):

  • “Best strat to beat the goblins is raw individual overwhelming power.”
  • “Best strat to beat the dragon is to become as goblin.”
  • “Best strat to beat Lysander is to become as dragon.”

The intent: “the player’s hands learn the theme before the cutscenes spell it out” (004 §Story Through Gameplay Philosophy).

Mapped to the three acts CANON

The mapping the team settled on (proto GameBible, “How the Story Is Told in Play”; grounded in the quotes above):

  • Act I — tutorial dungeons (goblins & beetles): small, swarming foes. Lesson: be overwhelming — hold formation, exploit joints and reach, end fights fast before escalation makes the swarm bold. The “joints, not the wall” golem lesson is implemented in the prologue boss fight (proto: src/core/campaign/content.ts, src/core/combat/content.ts).
  • Act II — the dragon, Vaenngrym: a force-of-nature set-piece that fills every quarter of the field. You cannot out-stat or flank it. Lesson: become as a goblin — many, united, humble; Locke Evertide rallies a crowd and grounds the god (see The Dragon Confrontation (Act 2 Climax) & Dragon’s Blood Choice).
  • Act III — Lysander, then the Daemon: a two-phase final boss (radiant himbo who telegraphs his glory → the curse made flesh, the dragon his bloodline hid). Lesson: become as the dragon.
  • The full arc: goblins → dragon → Lysander → the wish itself. The true superboss, the Daemon of Want, cannot be cut at all — it is beaten by refusal.

(Combat positioning mechanics themselves are documented in 008 / the systems batch; this page covers the narrative-through-gameplay mapping only.)

Design history

  • 2026-06-16 — proto reconciliation maps the three lessons to the three acts and appends the Daemon as the un-cuttable fourth rung (“beaten by refusal”) in the GameBible (proto: gamebible/data/story.js).
