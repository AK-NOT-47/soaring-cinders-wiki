The Round Boi Decision (The Unrefusable Choice)

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
Also known as: Round Boi, Big Round Boi, Round Big, The Meaningless Choice
First conceived 2023-02-10 · last updated 2023-08-26

The Round Boi Decision (The Unrefusable Choice) CANON

The one decision the player can’t refuse to make: befriend the giant beetle. A deliberately “meaningless” choice — the beetle has to be part of the story — played for humor and to contrast with choices that actually matter.

Summary — In a game full of meaningful decisions, Round Boi — the giant beetle — is the one the player cannot refuse. Meeting the beetle is framed as a narrative decision (“Would you like to be friends with this cool giant beetle that likes you or do you suck?”), but the beetle must be part of the story, so the “choice” is meaningless by design. The team agreed there’s humor in that, and that it helpfully contrasts with the choices that do matter (004 §Round Boi Decision). The bestiary owns Round Boi as a creature; this page covers the story beat of the decision.

The decision CANON

  • The player meets the beetle, called “round boi” or “big round boi” or “round big” (2023-08-26).
  • The prompt: “Would you like to be friends with this cool giant beetle that likes you or do you suck?” (2023-08-26).
  • CrunchyConniptions: “All I am saying is if you want to be friends with me apparently it’s easy, just give me a round boi and I am sold” (2023-08-26).
  • “In our game full of meaningful decisions, there’s one decision they can’t refuse to make” — the choice is meaningless because the beetle has to be part of the story.
  • “I think there’s humour in that we could probably pull off”; “It also helps to contrast with choices that do matter somewhat.” Good idea agreed upon (004 §Round Boi Decision, tagged CANON).

Vertical slice context

The Round Boi befriending sits inside the First City “beetle infestation” vertical slice (design intent, not yet coded): the core party arrives at the first city, deals with a beetle infestation, meets the big beetle, Locke befriends it, and it leads them to a dungeon they clear so the beetles can return home — “Good times. End of final beetle” (2023-02-19; 004 §Vertical Slice Content). The proto’s implemented vertical slice is instead the prologue “Descent into Ajax’s Tomb” — no contradiction, just a different slice; the GameBible places Round Boi at the First City as a Side Venture (proto: gamebible/data/quests.js).
