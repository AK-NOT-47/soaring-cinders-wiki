Brickwings (The Brick Arc)

CANONProject MagitechNotes / Design

Brickwings (The Brick Arc) CANON

A throwaway joke that becomes a party member: Gaius animates a brick, it sprouts wings and escapes, later rejoins the party, is beloved, and dies comically — with Ruby quietly relating to it.

Summary — Brickwings is a running gag arc. The party needs to throw a brick but can’t reach far enough, so Gaius animates it — the brick grows wings, flies through a window, and escapes to freedom. It’s found again later (Ruby berates Gaius for not taking care of it), joins the party, becomes wholesome and beloved across a couple of scenes, then dies comically — a literal throwaway joke. Ruby relates to the brick (004 §BRICKWINGS Story Arc).

The arc CANON

Beat sequence, all from one 2023-02-08 brainstorm titled “BRICKWINGS” (M01176, spirit_noodle):

  • Need to throw a brick; can’t throw it far enough (M01177, M01178, FreshToast, 2023-02-08).
  • Gaius animates the brick, it gets wings (M01179, 2023-02-08).
  • The brick flies through a window but escapes; the brick is now free (M01180, M01181).
  • Later they find the brick again; Ruby berates Gaius for not taking care of it (M01182).
  • The brick joins the party; it’s wholesome and beloved, with a couple of scenes (M01183, M01184).
  • The brick dies comically — a literal throwaway joke (M01186).
  • Ruby relates to the brick (M01188, FreshToast, 2023-02-08).

The arc demonstrates Gaius’s object-animation ability in a comedic register, and gives Ruby a small, oblique emotional beat (relating to something discarded/freed). It fits the “death and regrets” theme’s lighter end — carrying the influence of the dead (even a brick) into later story.

Design history

  • 2023-02-08 — the entire arc is spitballed in one short exchange, opened by spirit_noodle’s single-word “BRICKWINGS” and fleshed out by FreshToast: throw → animate → wings → escape → reunion → party member → beloved → comic death → Ruby relates (M01176–M01188, 2023-02-08). No later revision recorded.
