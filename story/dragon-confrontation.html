The Dragon Confrontation (Act 2 Climax) & Dragon's Blood Choice

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
Also known as: Act 2 Climax, Grounding the Dragon, Dragon's Blood Choice

The Dragon Confrontation (Act 2 Climax) & Dragon’s Blood Choice CANON

The Act II set-piece: Lysander refuses to face the dragon and lets towns burn while Locke Evertide rallies a crowd, grounds Vaenngrym, and earns the public’s faith as Ajax’s true heir — then chooses whether to drink the dragon’s blood.

Summary — With Lysander entranced by Ajax’s legacy and unwilling to fight, Locke upstages him: she rounds up help, works with people to ground the dragon Vaenngrym, and enters its den to face it. The moment defines Locke as the proper heir to Ajax’s legacy, shifts public opinion toward her and away from Lysander, and is the climax of her personal arc. Afterward, in a moment of levity, she may drink the dragon’s blood — a purely characterizing choice that changes the Daemon of Want’s dialogue.

Setup CANON

  • Lysander refuses to face the dragon, gets more entranced by the power of Ajax’s legacy, and continues to let towns burn (004 §Dragon Confrontation).
  • Locke upstages him by rounding up help, working with various people to ground the dragon, and finding a way into its den (004 §Dragon Confrontation). This is the Act II combat lesson made narrative: “become as a goblin” — many, united, humble.

Motivations revealed CANON

The dragon highlights each figure’s motivation (004 §Character Motivations Revealed):

  • Ajax wants glory.
  • Lysander wants strength.
  • Locke wants to protect and make change.
  • Vaenngrym is a force of nature — a narrative tool, a big scary boss monster.
  • Lysander is the failure — the only one who failed to step up when needed.

Outcomes CANON

  • The moment defines Locke as the proper heir to Ajax’s legacy.
  • Shifts public opinion in favor of Locke, out of favor of Lysander.
  • Climax of Locke’s personal character arc.
  • Locke may get infected with Dracosis (undecided or player choice) — Ajax himself was infected upon stabbing his dragon (004 §Outcomes).

Lysander’s response (004 §Lysander’s Response): outraged at Locke succeeding; more likely to warn than forbid her, and she ignores him. Can be shown via vignettes rather than a direct conversation. Open questions floated: is he jealous, envious, angry, a crybaby, loud and obnoxious? “Going with the flow on this part.”

Dragon’s Blood Choice CANON

Upon killing Vaenngrym, Locke is given the opportunity to drink the dragon’s blood — a moment of levity after the fight (M02661, FreshToast, 2023-02-21). Either:

  • she drinks it, everybody is grossed out, and nothing happens; or
  • she finds solace in her own strength and refuses to partake.

It has no mechanical bearing, but informs Locke’s opinion on Lysander’s pursuit of power and her understanding of Ajax. The Daemon of Want’s dialogue changes based on this choice (M02663, FreshToast, 2023-02-21). DEBATED “Perhaps mechanical change in fight as well” — left unresolved, and objected to as unsatisfying if there’s no mechanical change (M02678, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22).

Lore tie-in: Ajax’s cover story was that he gained strength from drinking the dragon’s blood after wounding one — see Ajax’s Story below.

Ajax’s Story (background) CANON

Ajax became desperate for strength to defend his people, the only warrior truly brave enough to fight the dragon. He made a deal with the Daemon of Want, trading half his lifespan for the ability to steal the strength of others. His cover story was that he got strength from drinking the dragon’s blood after wounding it — but it was actually the first instance of using his stolen-strength power to drain life from the dragon during battle; he later killed it with that stolen strength (M02653, FreshToast, 2023-02-21; 004 §Ajax’s Story).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Whether Locke gets infected with Dracosis is left undecided or made a player choice (004 §Outcomes).
  • DEBATED Whether the dragon’s-blood choice carries a mechanical fight change, not just dialogue (M02664/M02678).

Design history

  • 2023-02-21 — Ajax’s deal, cover story, and the drain-the-dragon truth are established (M02653); the Dragon’s Blood Choice and its reactive Daemon dialogue are drafted the same day (M02661–M02664, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • 2023-02-22 — objection that “no mechanical change” is unsatisfying (M02678, CrunchyConniptions), leaving the mechanical-vs-cosmetic question open.
  • 2026-06-16 — proto reconciliation maps this to the Act II “become as a goblin” combat lesson (proto GameBible; see The Combat-Strategy Arc (Goblins → Dragon → Lysander → the Wish)).
