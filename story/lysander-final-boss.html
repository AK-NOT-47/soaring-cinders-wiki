Lysander's Villain Turn & Two-Phase Final Boss

CANONProject MagitechMixed
Also known as: The Radiant Himbo, Lysander Phase 2, Final Boss Phase One/Two
First conceived 2023-02-16 (M02211, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-02-16 (M02303, FreshToast)

Lysander’s Villain Turn & Two-Phase Final Boss CANON

The Act III collapse of the golden hero: Lysander stops caring where his power comes from, devours souls to feed his fading light, sheds his humanity into the dragon his bloodline always hid, and is finally put down by Locke Evertide with Ajax’s leash in hand.

Summary — Lysander grows too strong for Eris to manage and has a third-act breakdown. His power comes from a pact with the same spirit Ajax dealt with — the Daemon of Want — letting him devour souls for strength. The final fight is two phases: Phase 1, the radiant himbo who telegraphs his glory and refills his fading light by leeching the crowd (counter: cut off the leech); Phase 2, the curse made flesh, where the seven relics act as Ajax’s pre-prepared leash and Locke ends him — “Your light dims, Lysander. Let it fade away.” Defeating Lysander is not the true final beat; see The True Ending.

The breakdown CANON

Lysander gets too strong for Eris to handle and has a 3rd-act breakdown. He doesn’t care where his power comes from — “Oh I have demon blood powers? Sweet!” — and doubles down, acquiring more by any means (M02211, FreshToast, 2023-02-16). He only now learned how to grow his power and must be finished while weak, “otherwise no hope for us all.” He wants to kill and sacrifice the party, then do the same to the rest of the people. This mirrors Ruby’s story — whether the ends justify the means (004 §Lysander’s 3rd-Act Villain Turn).

The seeds of doubt in his heroism are immediate; his reputation lasts a little longer but likely devolves entirely by the time the dragon is dealt with at the end of Act 2 — the audience should be allowed to feel smart, not duped (M04701, M04702, FreshToast; “audience wants to feel smart, not duped” — M04693, CrunchyConniptions; the “unravel faster than Dutch” answer — M04692, FreshToast; 2023-10-25). See The Dragon Confrontation (Act 2 Climax) & Dragon’s Blood Choice for the Act 2 turn.

The Eris kill — lead-in to Phase 1 CANON

Eris tells Lysander about the soul/sacrifice power. In the canonical staging (the one that reached the docs), as she begins to explain “something about soul and sacrifice,” Lysander interrupts her by grabbing her by the head; she is immediately dust (M02282, M02283, FreshToast, 2023-02-16). He is left with one hand still gripping her dusted robe hanging off skeletal remains (M02285). This kicks off his breakdown. (See Eris’s Arc for the twist framing — the obvious twist-villain twisted back onto Lysander.)

Boss mechanics CANON

Soul-devour gimmick: when Lysander kills a party member he regains HP, gains a buff, and gains AP — the core gimmick of the fight. He can grab someone by the head and turn them to dust; sickly bluish/indigo light pours from his mouth and eyes (M02282–M02292; 004 §Lysander’s 3rd-Act Villain Turn).

Phase 2 crawler mechanic: one weakened compatriot is left crawling on the ground. If the player does not attack them, Lysander takes his first turn to devour them, gaining AP and a buff for 2 turns; if the player kills them first, Lysander gets no buff/AP but the player has spent a couple of turns (M02292, FreshToast, 2023-02-16). A grim reframing of the Locke Evertide/party HP-sacrifice theme.

Phase 2 turn (the leash): Locke is doomed, humanity is doomed — but Ajax prepared for this, leaving behind “a relic that had helped him contain his own power in the past.” This relic is with Locke (or a guardian character; or it was on Lysander the whole time, suppressing him unknowingly until it fell/broke). A silhouette of Ajax appears and inspires the party: “Weary warriors, a curse of sorrowed days past stands in front of you but with the relic of old at hand, the curse’s power can be defeated. Now, stand tall and fight.” Locke: “Your light dims, Lysander… {looks up fiercely} Let it fade away.” Lysander is infuriated and growls in a manner reminiscent of the dragon; the fight begins (004 §Lysander 2nd Phase Fight Scene). See the relics-as-leash.

Final line: “Why settle for only twice as bright?” — a reference to the family/Ajax motto “Burn twice as bright, half as long” (M02231, M02303, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).

Two-phase structure (summary) CANON

  • Phase 1 — the radiant himbo hero, fighting spectacularly and telegraphing his glory, refilling his fading light by leeching the crowd (counter: cut off the leech).
  • Phase 2 — the curse made flesh: scales surface, the pact starved of strangers eats its host, and he becomes the dragon his bloodline always hid; the seven relics are Ajax’s leash; Locke ends him.
  • True Ending — the Daemon of Want.

This maps to the combat-strategy arc: Act III’s lesson is “become as the dragon” (proto GameBible, “The Final Boss — Phase One/Two”).

The late-game spear conflict (lead-up) PROPOSED

A proposed 2nd/3rd-act conflict: Lysander finds out the spear is Ajax’s weapon and gets “BIG” jealous, demanding Locke hand it over; she disobeys (SYMBOL). He sends someone to retrieve it, setting off the explicit antagonism between the two bands. DEBATED this could also be the fight where Flynt dies — “Idk yet but I had something like that in mind.” The Sunderer is the explicit symbol of Locke’s status as Ajax’s heir, so Lysander wants it (004 §Lysander Discovers Spear is Ajax’s Weapon).

Design history

  • 2023-02-16 — the whole villain-turn is brainstormed in one evening: the “dud revelation” that emboldens him (“Oh I have demon blood powers? Sweet!”) (M02211); the soul-devour gimmick and Ajax “devouring his enemies” (M02257); the Eris by-the-head → dust kill (M02282–M02285); the phase-2 crawler mechanic (M02292); the “Burn twice as bright, half as long” motto (M02231, M02303) — all FreshToast, 2023-02-16.
  • 2023-02-16 — alternate Eris-kill staging (considered, superseded). Instead of grabbing Eris by the head, a variant has Lysander kill her with his sword: “as we are about to defeat him, he looks at his sword and says ‘It sings to me’ and proceeds to plunge into Eris” (M02270, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16). This is a distinct staging of the same beat. The by-the-head / turn-to-dust version is the one that reached the docs and is treated as canonical; the “It sings to me” sword staging did not make it into the 000 notes (ledger R12, status partial). Both were floated the same day, minutes apart — genuinely contemporaneous — but the head-grab version won by being carried forward into the compiled notes.
  • 2026-06-16 — proto reconciliation formalizes the two-phase structure (radiant himbo → curse made flesh) and the relics-as-leash climax in the GameBible (proto: gamebible/data/story.js).
