Pirate Monkeys CANON

Comic-relief enemies who sing their own sea shanty — one of them has an eye patch, a parrot, and the parrot has an eye patch too.

Summary — Pirate Monkeys (a.k.a. Monkey Pirates) are a deliberately silly, comic-relief enemy type. Their defining bit is that they “sing their own sea shanty” (immediately amended to a “Land shanty”) (M01019–M01020, 2023-02-05), and one of them has an eye patch, a parrot, and the parrot also has an eye patch (M01157–M01159, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-08).

Characteristics

  • Sing their own sea shanty (M01019, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-05) — corrected to a “Land shanty” (M01020, FreshToast, 2023-02-05). The source likens the vibe to “that TikTok cat but monkey” (005 §Characteristics; TikTok cat cited as inspiration for the shanties, 005 §Other Media).
  • One of the monkey pirates has an eye patch, a parrot — and the parrot also has an eye patch (M01157–M01159, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-08).

Role

  • Enemy type; explicitly a comic-relief enemy (005 §Role).

Design history

  • 2023-02-05 — introduced as monkeys that sing their own sea shanty (M01019, CrunchyConniptions), instantly re-dubbed a “Land shanty” (M01020, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-08 — the eye-patch/parrot gag added: an eye-patched monkey with a parrot that also has an eye patch (M01157–M01159, spirit_noodle). Current presentation.
