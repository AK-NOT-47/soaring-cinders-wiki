Skeletons CANON

The mandatory dungeon-crawler skeletons — because “Dungeons and no skeletons? A travesty.”

Summary — Skeletons are a deliberately classic dungeon-crawler enemy, treated as mandatory for dungeons (005 §Status). The team’s affection for the trope is on the record: “I’d be surprised if there were no skeletons. Like come on” and “Dungeons and no skeletons? A travesty” (M05373–M05376, FreshToast, 2024-03-12). They anchor a desired skeleton tomb area, and appear in the proto as ranged Skeleton Archers.

Description & role

  • Classic dungeon-crawler trope, embraced wholesale: “Maybe we need [a] funny skeleton” (M05365, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-03-12); “Do I like skeletons. Yes i do” (M05371, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-03-12).
  • Mandatory for dungeons — recorded as a finalized decision (005 §Status; §Design Decisions Finalized).

Location

  • Tied to a desired skeleton tomb area: “we need skeleton tomb… I dunno” (M05368, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-03-12). Whether to include a dedicated skeleton-tomb area is listed as unresolved, “though skeletons are now mandatory” (005 §Unresolved Questions). See Encounters, Ambushes & Enemy Areas for enemy-type-by-area.

Proto realization: Skeleton Archer [IMPLEMENTED]

The vertical slice ships skeletons as Skeleton Archers (skeleton_archer, 💀) — a ranged marksman with Arrow and Bone Shot (any-rank shots); full stats in Proto Enemy Roster (Implemented) (proto: src/core/combat/content.ts — ENEMY_DEFS). This is consistent with the “mandatory for dungeons” decision; they fight in the Bone Gallery and the ambush narrows (proto: src/core/campaign/content.ts nodes n2b, n4) (005 §Common / elite enemies (proto), Notes).

Design history

  • 2024-03-12 — the whole decision in one exchange: CrunchyConniptions floats a “funny skeleton” and a “skeleton tomb” (M05365–M05368, M05371); FreshToast declares dungeons without skeletons “A travesty” (M05373–M05376). Recorded thereafter as mandatory-for-dungeons. Current lore.
  • (proto, undated) — realized as ranged Skeleton Archers in the Bone Gallery and ambush encounters. [IMPLEMENTED]
