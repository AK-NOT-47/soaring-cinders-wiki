Fear Effect

PROPOSEDCaelian OdysseyNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-02-11 (M01770, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-02-11 (M01786, FreshToast)

Fear Effect PROPOSED

A monster-inflicted debuff — first seen on the dragon, which “just existing” terrifies the party for the opening turns, and floated as a shared tool other scary monsters could use.

Summary — The Fear Effect is a debuff that scary enemies inflict to weaken the party. It originated as a Vaenngrym (best-a) mechanic: the dragon “literally inflicts a fear effect on the party to weaken them at the start of battle” (the first two turns), with the party’s initiative maybe decreased “because they fear facing him” (M01770–M01786, 2023-02-11). It was explicitly floated as reusable “elsewhere by other monsters to weaken you” (M01786, FreshToast, 2023-02-11). As of the proto it is not implemented; only its inverse (Fear Immunity) exists.

Effect (as designed)

  • Inflicted at battle start: “Maybe it literally inflicts a fear effect on the party to weaken them at the start of battle” (M01770, FreshToast, 2023-02-11); for the dragon, “everybody gets it for the first two turns” simply because the dragon exists (M01786, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • Decreases initiative: “Maybe they are terrified and their initiative is decreased because they fear facing him” (M01783, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-11). Recorded as “maybe fear decreases initiative” (005 §Fear Effect).
  • Reusable across monsters: “Maybe it’s used elsewhere by other monsters to weaken you” (M01786, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • Connects to the broader “making enemies scary” toolkit and the lantern tension (some monsters fear the lantern; some are feared themselves).

Proto status PROPOSED → not yet implemented

  • A standalone “Fear” negative status does not exist in the proto status bank (proto: src/core/combat/types.ts StatusId; status.ts STATUS_DEFS — no fear entry). The dragon’s fear effect and the “fear decreases initiative” idea remain design intent (005 §Fear Effect, proto note).
  • What is implemented is the inverse: a Fear Immunity positive status (fear_immune) that blocks the disrupt-class effects — Stun, Blind, and Silence (proto: src/core/combat/status.ts — DISRUPT = ['stun','blind','silence']).
  • If Fear were added, it would most naturally land as a Speed Down-style debuff, since Speed Down (spd_down, −3 speed/stack) is the implemented initiative-reduction effect (005 §Fear Effect, proto note). See the systems namespace for the status bank and escalation.

Open questions

  • Exact implementation of fear-effect mechanics — no “Fear” status exists in the proto yet (005 §Unresolved Questions).

Design history

  • 2023-02-11 — conceived as a dragon mechanic: a battle-start fear effect weakening the party (first two turns), possibly by decreasing initiative, and floated as reusable by other monsters (M01770–M01786, FreshToast & CrunchyConniptions). Current design intent; unimplemented.
  • (proto, undated) — only the inverse Fear Immunity positive status is implemented; no negative “Fear” exists. [IMPLEMENTED — inverse only]
