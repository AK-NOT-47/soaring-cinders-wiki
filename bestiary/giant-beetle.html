Giant Beetle (“Round Boi”) CANON

A house-friendly giant beetle — grown large by Dracosis (The Dragon Virus), hard-shelled, and befriendable by Locke Evertide. The one “meaningful decision” the game won’t actually let you refuse.

Summary — The Giant Beetle is a large, semi-metallic-shelled beetle that grew big because of Dracosis (The Dragon Virus) (M02709, FreshToast, 2023-02-24). In the vertical slice a beetle infestation is dealt with at the first city; Locke befriends a big beetle (“round boi”), which then leads the party to a dungeon, after which the beetles return home. Its befriending is used for humor as a deliberately meaningless “decision” — you can technically choose, but the beetle has to be part of the story. The exact name (“round boi” / “big round boi” / “round big”) is still open.

Characteristics

  • Large because of dracosis, with “a hard, semi-metallic shell” (M02709, FreshToast, 2023-02-24). See Dracosis (The Dragon Virus) for the mutation lore and other dracosis-mutated creatures.

Narrative role CANON

  • A beetle infestation is dealt with at the first city; a big beetle is befriended by Locke (M02459, FreshToast, 2023-02-19; the vertical-slice scope).
  • The large beetle “pulls [a] wagon” (M02460, FreshToast, 2023-02-19) and leads the party to a dungeon; the beetles “can return home” after the dungeon is cleared (M02459, FreshToast, 2023-02-19).
  • The “meaningless decision” gag: meeting the beetle is framed as “Would you like to be friends with this cool giant beetle that likes you or do you suck?” (M03623, FreshToast, 2023-08-26) — “In our game full of meaningful decisions, there’s one decision they can’t refuse to make,” a deliberately meaningless choice (the beetle must be in the story) used for humor and to contrast with choices that do matter (005 §Narrative Role).

Naming DEBATED

  • Called “round boi” (M03615, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-08-26), then “big round boi” (M03617) or “round big” (M03618). CrunchyConniptions: “if you want to be friends with me… just give me a round boi and I am sold” (M03624, 2023-08-26). Exact name still open (005 §Status).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Final name: “round boi” vs “big round boi” vs “round big” (M03615–M03618, 2023-08-26).

Design history

  • 2023-02-10 — first appearance: “pet the giant beetle” / “Definitely pet the beetle” (M01374, spirit_noodle; M01375, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-19 — folded into the vertical-slice scope: beetle infestation at the first city → Locke befriends the big beetle → it leads them to a dungeon → beetles return home; beetle also pulls a wagon (M02459–M02460, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-24 — lore basis added: the beetle is large due to dracosis and has a hard, semi-metallic shell (M02709, FreshToast).
  • 2023-08-26 — the “round boi” naming and the “meaningless decision” humor: the befriend prompt and the deliberately-unrefusable-choice framing (M03614–M03624, CrunchyConniptions & FreshToast). Agreed to be a good idea; name left open. Current presentation.
