DD, the Death Daemon PROPOSED

A non-binary personification of Death, workshopped as a boss/character but never given a finalized name beyond the placeholder “DD”.

Summary — DD, the Death Daemon, is a Daemon-class entity representing Death itself. They are explicitly enbie (non-binary) and were counted separately from the game’s other major protagonists and antagonists in the gender tally (M02741, FreshToast, 2023-02-25). FreshToast wanted “a cute nickname for them” but never settled on one — the working shorthand stayed DD, and the fallback was simply “they’re just death” (M02745–M02755, 2023-02-25). Beyond the placeholder name and their non-binary identity, the concept is undeveloped in the notes.

Description

  • A Daemon representing Death — in the setting, Daemons manifest when significant, shared emotion is experienced in an area and attach themselves to the concept at its centre (see Daemon of Want for how Daemons work) (M02653–M02654, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Non-binary (enbie) — counted apart from the “8 boys and 9 girls” of the major cast, alongside Vaenngrym, because neither has a conventional gender (M02741, FreshToast, 2023-02-25).

Naming

FreshToast tried to “workshop a cute nickname for them” (M02746). spirit_noodle was fine with DD as-is (M02750, M02752), but FreshToast preferred something better and concluded: “It’s not something I’m going to use so either I think of something better or they’re just death” — to which spirit_noodle replied “then they’re just death” (M02753–M02755). No name was finalized, so placeholder_name: true.

Open questions

  • DEBATED The name — “DD” / “Death Daemon” / “just death” are all live; no final nickname was chosen (M02745–M02756, 2023-02-25).
  • PROPOSED Everything about DD’s role, appearance, and combat function is unstated in the notes; only their existence, Daemon nature, and non-binary identity are recorded.

Design history

  • 2023-02-25 — Introduced while tallying the cast’s gender ratio: “DD, the Death Daemon is enbie”; FreshToast workshops a nickname, spirit_noodle likes “DD” as-is, and the pair settle on “then they’re just death” for now (M02741, M02745–M02756, FreshToast & spirit_noodle) — matches the current presentation.
