Telekinetic Sword Boss

A slumbering giant at the end of a cave who commands a swarm of magical swords by telekinesis — every blade a trophy from an adventurer who failed to kill him.

Summary — The Telekinetic Sword Boss is a formidable enemy “ensconced at the terminus” of a cave, flanked by a multitude of magical swords. With no hands touching them, he uses telekinesis to lift and command the blades, “reminiscent of Gilgamesh from Fate” (2023-10-08). The swords are “silent testimonies to the many adventurers who previously sought, unsuccessfully, to vanquish him.” FreshToast liked “the idea of a monster that collects and fights with a swarm of swords” and floated a variant: it could even be a Daemon made of swords in a trench coat pretending to be a person (2023-10-08).

Description

  • A formidable boss at the end of a cave, flanked by magical swords (2023-10-08; 005 §Description).
  • No hands make contact — he uses telekinesis to lift and command a multitude of swords, reminiscent of Gilgamesh from Fate (Part 14).
  • The swords are testimonies to the many adventurers who previously sought and failed to vanquish him.

Abilities

  • Each sword possesses unique attributes, inflicting various damage types depending on which weapon is chosen in battle (005 §Abilities).
  • The sheer volume of weapons attests to the countless battles he has won.
  • Key distinction: the boss is telekinetic; the weapons are magical. The original draft conflated the two; this was clarified in a writing critique (005 §Abilities).

Lore

For eons the boss has been disturbed in slumber by persistent adventurers. The player is the disturbance that is different — “the final ripple in a long, turbulent dream” — who defeats the slumbering giant once and for all (005 §Lore).

Open questions

  • Daemon-of-swords variant — “Could even be a Daemon that is made of swords, possessing a cloak it resembles a person until you realise it’s just a dozen swords in a trench coat and all of them want to stab you” (2023-10-08). The Daemon framing is not finalized.

Design history

  • 2023-10-08 — CrunchyConniptions drafts the cave boss: a Gilgamesh-like adversary who telekinetically commands a swarm of magical swords, each blade a trophy of a failed adventurer.
  • 2023-10-08 — FreshToast endorses “the idea of a monster that collects and fights with a swarm of swords” and proposes the Daemon-made-of-swords / trench-coat variant — current presentation, with the Daemon variant left open.
