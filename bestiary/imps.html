Imps DEBATED

The game’s small feral-mob enemy: green-less little guys with an obsession/mania streak and horridly slurred trash-talk. The name itself (Imp vs Goblin vs a made-up word) is unsettled.

Summary — Imps are the low-tier feral swarm enemy, deliberately keeping the classic goblin fantasy intact (“There aren’t enough goblins out there”) while dodging the misused “goblin” name. As most recently discussed, they are not green and carry an “obsession/mania quality,” and the team kept debating the right name — Imp, Goblin, or an invented “slezzy” word like skugg (M04618–M04639, 2023-10-24). The playable proto ships them as Goblin Imp (melee) and Hex Imp (caster), but neither is the lore-final name.

Description & characteristics

  • Not green; the “obsession/mania quality” is layered on top of the classic goblin template (M04622, FreshToast, 2023-10-24).
  • All classic traits of creatures like goblins are meant to “stay intact” (M04622, FreshToast, 2023-10-24).
  • Have barks like “Gee guu, skug” when they crit — described as “horridly slurred english trash talk” (M04225, FreshToast, 2023-10-16).

Combat role

  • The low end of the game’s strategic progression arc: goblins → dragon → Lysander; the intended lesson is that “the best strat to beat goblins is raw individual overwhelming power” (005 §Combat Strategy). See Enemy Design Philosophy for the progression-through-tactics rationale and Vaenngrym / Lysander as the later rungs.
  • Assigned their own region — a “Goblin area” is one of the game’s main enemy-type-by-area zones (005 §Enemy Type Areas). See Encounters, Ambushes & Enemy Areas.

Naming debate DEBATED

The right name was argued at length and never fully settled (M04618–M04639, 2023-10-24):

  • “Call em Goblins. Superior name” (M04624, CrunchyConniptions).
  • Counter: “goblin” is a misuse — goblins are actually Celtic house spirits that cause mischief, and the team isn’t “big on appropriating names or ideas without purpose” (M04627, FreshToast). Related creatures get called “a million different things: kobolds, hobs, bugbears,” and kobold is technically most accurate; but Imp “is just a word so it’s free reign” (005 §Name Change Reasoning).
  • Concern against Imp: “Maybe I’m biased against imp… always been forgettable monsters”; biblical demon imps are “largely forgettable” (005 §Alternative Names Considered).
  • Alternatives floated: Hellion (“feral little guys”), a made-up Skugg, and Dregs / dreglings / boyes (dregs = residue or scum) (M04635–M04639, FreshToast, 2023-10-24). CrunchyConniptions asked for “a more slezzy name, like skugg or something” (M04633).

Proto realization [IMPLEMENTED]

The vertical slice (“The Descent into Ajax’s Tomb”) ships two imp variants (proto: src/core/combat/content.ts — ENEMY_DEFS); full stats live in Proto Enemy Roster (Implemented):

  • Goblin Imp (goblin_imp, 👺) — a 16-HP skirmisher; Stab and Snatch (which applies Speed Down).
  • Hex Imp (imp_caster, 🔮) — a magic-using caster variant; Hex (magic + Blind) and Ward (Resistance Up on self). Neither name is the lore-final decision — the Imps/Goblins question above is still open.

Open questions

Design history

  • 2023-10-16 — first concrete characterization: the “imps” have crit barks like “Gee guu, skug”, slurred-English trash-talk (M04225, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-24 — the naming debate: CrunchyConniptions wants plain “Goblins” (“Superior name,” M04624); FreshToast objects on the grounds goblins are misused Celtic house-spirits and prefers the free-reign word “Imp,” noting the mob isn’t green and has an obsession/mania quality (M04618–M04627). Alternatives (Hellion, Skugg, Dregs/dreglings/boyes) proposed; left unresolved (M04633–M04639). This is the current, still-open state.
  • (proto, undated) — implemented as Goblin Imp + Hex Imp in the playable slice, hedging the name by keeping both “Goblin” and “Imp.” [IMPLEMENTED]
