Vampires

DEBATEDCaelian OdysseyNotes / Design
First conceived 2024-02-10 (M05185, FreshToast) · last in chat 2024-07-08 (M05434, FreshToast)

Vampires DEBATED

Few, hunted, and stubbornly old-fashioned — “every old vampire should just be a weird boomer who cringes at current day cultural norms.”

Summary — Vampires are a rare enemy type: few in number because they are actively hunted and killed, with some loose organization “but nothing so crazy as Vampire the Masquerade” (M05430–M05434, 2024-07-08). Their most vivid characterization is thematic: vampires are “naturally conservative,” and “every old vampire should just be a weird boomer who cringes at current day cultural norms” (M05185, FreshToast, 2024-02-10) — tying into the game’s progressivism-vs-conservatism opposition, where vampirism is the thematic opposite of Dracosis (The Dragon Virus) (stagnation vs evolution). Their exact organization and headcount remain open.

Description & characteristics

  • Few, actively hunted: not many exist because they are “actively hunted and killed” (005 §Characteristics).
  • Some organization, kept simple: “Should the vampires act more like individuals or more like a collective?” (M05430, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-07-08); resolved toward “some organization to them maybe” but “Nothing so crazy as Vampire the Masquerade though” (M05434, FreshToast, 2024-07-08).
  • Naturally conservative: “I think vampires would be naturally conservative. Every old vampire should just be a weird boomer who cringes at current day cultural norms” (M05185, FreshToast, 2024-02-10).

Thematic role

Vampirism is framed as the deliberate opposite of Dracosis (The Dragon Virus): dracosis is about “evolving into a superior being… changing for the better… changing to be strong” (the future), whereas vampirism is stagnation — preserving the present/past (M05173–M05187, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10). Both are “external factors messing with the natural order” (M05199, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10). See Raphael (best-a), the tragic super-dragon-vampire in whom both afflictions blend.

Location

  • A dedicated Vampires area is one of the game’s main enemy-type-by-area zones (005 §Enemy Type Areas; see Encounters, Ambushes & Enemy Areas).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Exact vampire organization structure (individuals vs collective; strong sense of self?) (M05430, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-07-08; 005 §Unresolved).
  • DEBATED How many vampires exist in the world (005 §Unresolved Questions).

Design history

  • 2024-02-10 — first characterization: vampires are naturally conservative, old ones are “weird boomer[s]”; established as the thematic opposite of dracosis (stagnation vs evolution) (M05173–M05187, FreshToast & CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2024-07-08 — organization questions raised (individuals vs collective, sense of self, scattered or grouped) and partly answered: some loose organization, but “nothing so crazy as Vampire the Masquerade” (M05430–M05434). Exact structure and headcount left open. Current, still-debated state.
