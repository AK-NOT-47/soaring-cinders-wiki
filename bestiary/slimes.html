Slimes

CANONCaelian OdysseyMixed
Also known as: Cinder Slime
First conceived 2024-09-01 (M05679, FreshToast) · last in chat 2024-09-01 (M05683, FreshToast)

Slimes CANON

Jelly-like enemies that can develop legs and partially control their own shape, mostly settling into natural, four-legged forms.

Summary — Slimes are a classic common enemy given a twist: they are amorphous but tend to “follow natural forms,” with some control over their shape (M05681–M05682, FreshToast, 2024-09-01). Most are four-legged with tails; occasional “weirdos” are six-legged or serpentine (M05683, FreshToast, 2024-09-01). How many distinct slime types to include is still open. The playable proto ships one, the Cinder Slime, as a stone-cored bruiser.

Description & characteristics

  • “Jelly-like slimes can develop legs ‘n’ shit” (M05679, FreshToast, 2024-09-01).
  • Have “some control over their shape” (M05682, FreshToast, 2024-09-01) — one floated idea was that they act “like chameleons, but instead of colour its shape they change to hide” (M05680, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-09-01).
  • Tend to follow natural forms: “Lots of four legged lil guys. Some have tails,” with occasional “weirdos who have 6 legs or are serpentine” (M05681, M05683, FreshToast, 2024-09-01). CANON — recorded as a finalized decision (005 §Design Decisions Finalized).

Combat role

  • Listed among the baseline enemy types in the brainstorm test setup (Goblins, Slimes, Golems, Brigands) (M02168, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).
  • In the proto, the slime archetype is realized as a mid-durability bruiser (see below).

Proto realization: Cinder Slime [IMPLEMENTED]

The vertical slice ships one slime — the Cinder Slime (slime, 🟢), a bruiser with 26 HP that uses Splash and Dissolve (which applies Defense Down); full stats in Proto Enemy Roster (Implemented) (proto: src/core/combat/content.ts — ENEMY_DEFS). It is described as a “stone-cored bruiser whose body shatters the borrowed sword in the prologue” (proto: src/core/campaign/content.ts node n2a). “Cinder” ties the name to the game’s title rather than to any decided lore name (005 §Common / elite enemies (proto), Notes).

Open questions

  • DEBATED How many slime types to include, and the exact variety of forms (005 §Unresolved, §Unresolved Questions).

Design history

  • 2024-09-01 — the entire slime concept in one session: FreshToast proposes jelly-slimes that grow legs (M05679); CrunchyConniptions asks whether there’d be many types or a shared shape, floating a shape-shifting “chameleon” idea (M05680); FreshToast lands on slimes following natural forms (mostly four-legged, some tails), with some shape control and occasional six-legged/serpentine oddities (M05681–M05683). This is the current presentation.
  • (proto, undated) — implemented as the Cinder Slime bruiser in the playable slice. [IMPLEMENTED]
