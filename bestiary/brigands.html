Brigands & Bandits CANON

The game’s basic human enemy — outlaws and cutthroats, used as the early-game yardstick the rest of the bestiary scales up from.

Summary — Brigands (a.k.a. bandits) are a basic human enemy type and one of the four baseline test enemies (M02168, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16). They are the low anchor of the game’s anti-HP-bloat design: you “go from fighting bandits to dragon,” not “bandits to bandits with quadruple HP” (Enemy Design Philosophy). Narratively they connect to characters who fell into banditry, and to rumors of areas harboring near-supernaturally stealthy bandits. The proto realizes them as the River Brigand.

Description & role

  • Basic human enemy type; used as the early-game comparison point for progression (005 §Characteristics).
  • The canonical example of “tactical complexity, not HP bloat”: bandits → dragon, not bandits with quadruple HP (M02100, FreshToast, 2023-02-16 on linear scaling; see Enemy Design Philosophy).
  • Listed in the test character setup alongside Goblins, Slimes, Golems (M02168, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).

Narrative context

  • The character Cervantes “had dreams of rebellion and social reform but ended up in a life [of] banditry as an outlaw backed by cutthroat killers” (M01361, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Rumors surround at least one mountain for “harbouring bandits with near-supernatural stealth” (M01927, FreshToast, 2023-02-14) — a hook tying bandits to the game’s stealth/ambush texture (Encounters, Ambushes & Enemy Areas).

Proto realization: River Brigand [IMPLEMENTED]

The vertical slice ships the River Brigand (brigand, 🗡️), a 24-HP cutthroat using Slash and Cutpurse (which applies Bleed); full stats in Proto Enemy Roster (Implemented) (proto: src/core/combat/content.ts — ENEMY_DEFS). “River” ties it to the Mediterranean/Caribbean river throughline (005 §Common / elite enemies (proto), Notes). It fights in the “Ambush in the Narrows” elite encounter (proto: src/core/campaign/content.ts node n2b).

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — Cervantes’ fall into “a life of banditry… backed by cutthroat killers” establishes bandits in the fiction (M01361, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-14 — rumor of bandits with “near-supernatural stealth” (M01927, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-16 — brigands added to the baseline test-enemy roster (M02168, CrunchyConniptions); the “bandits → dragon, not quadruple-HP bandits” scaling framing (M02100, FreshToast). This remains the current lore.
