Vampires

Few, hunted, and stubbornly old-fashioned — “every old vampire should just be a weird boomer who cringes at current day cultural norms.”

Vampirism

One of the two supernatural conditions that “mess with the natural order” — the conceptual opposite of dracosis , centred on immortality.
