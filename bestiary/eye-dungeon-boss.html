Eye Dungeon Boss PROPOSED

The boss of a dungeon full of watching eyes: if the eyes see you, it studies your party and builds counters tailored to exactly how you fight.

Summary — The Eye Dungeon Boss governs “a dungeon where there are eyes constantly following you.” The player must destroy the eyes and avoid being seen, because any eye that sees you feeds information to the end boss, which then “gather[s] information on you and create[s] countermeasures to your specific party by analysing your information” (M04224, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-15). It is an info-gathering, adaptive boss whose difficulty scales with how much the player let the eyes observe.

Description

  • A dungeon where eyes constantly follow you (Part 15) (M04224, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-15; 005 §Description).
  • The player has to destroy the eyes and avoid them seeing you.

Mechanic

  • If the eyes see you, the end boss gathers information on you (005 §Mechanic).
  • The boss analyzes your information to create countermeasures tailored to your specific party — an info-gathering adaptive boss. Letting fewer eyes see you presumably leaves the boss less able to counter your composition (M04224, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-15).

Open questions

  • PROPOSED The concept is a single-message pitch; the boss’s appearance, the exact form of its “countermeasures”, and how eye-avoidance translates to fight difficulty are all unspecified in the notes.

Design history

  • 2023-10-15 — Full concept introduced in one message: an eye-filled stealth dungeon whose end boss adapts to your party based on what its eyes observed (M04224, CrunchyConniptions) — matches the current presentation.
