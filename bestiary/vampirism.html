Vampirism

DEBATEDCross-IterationNotes / Design
Also known as: Vampire curse
First conceived 2023-02-10 (M01398, FreshToast) · last in chat 2024-02-10 (M05199, CrunchyConniptions)

Vampirism DEBATED

One of the two supernatural conditions that “mess with the natural order” — the conceptual opposite of Dracosis (The Dragon Virus), centred on immortality.

Summary — Vampirism is a supernatural affliction developed alongside Dracosis as one of “two [external factors] messing with the natural order of things” (M05199, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10). Where Dracosis is about draconic recklessness, Vampirism is “the opposite of dracosis” and “about immortality” (M05173, M05177, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10). It is most fully expressed through Raphael, who embodies both conditions.

Nature

  • An external factor that interferes with the natural order — paired with Dracosis as a conceptual dyad (M05187 “Vampirism vs Dracosis”; M05199, 2024-02-10).
  • Thematically tied to immortality and its impulses (M05177).
  • Design flavour: vampires imagined as “naturally conservative… every old vampire should just be a weird boomer who cringes at current-day cultural norms” (M05185, FreshToast, 2024-02-10).

Carriers

  • Raphael embodies both Vampirism and Dracosis — “he would literally embody them both and it would drive him mad,” but he rises above them (M05192–M05193, 2024-02-10). His Vampirism’s impulses and Dracosis’s recklessness are implied not to impede his already-broken psyche (M05201).
  • Early, undeveloped floating of the idea: “Lyra or Eris is a vampire?” (M01398, FreshToast, 2023-02-10) — never resolved onto those characters.

Open questions

  • DEBATED The precise mechanics of Vampirism vs Dracosis and how far each condition drives its host were still being workshopped in the 2024-02-10 session (M05187–M05229).

Design history

  • 2023-02-10 — first floated loosely: is Lyra or Eris a vampire? (M01398, FreshToast) — not taken up.
  • 2024-02-10 — developed as a named supernatural condition, defined as the opposite of Dracosis and centred on immortality; attached to Raphael as one of two curses he embodies (M05173–M05229, Crunchy/Fresh). Current framing.
