Proto Enemy Roster (Implemented)

The runnable truth: five fully-statted rank-and-file enemies shipped in the browser vertical slice, “The Descent into Ajax’s Tomb.”

Summary — The playable proto ships a small, fully-statted enemy roster used by the combat prototype. These are five common/elite archetypes — Goblin Imp, Cinder Slime, Skeleton Archer, River Brigand, Hex Imp — plus one boss (the Ossuary Sentinel, owned on its own page). The numbers are tuned for the slice, not final balance, but they are how these archetypes currently behave. All values: proto: src/core/combat/content.ts (ENEMY_DEFS, ENEMY_SKILLS). For enemies, a skill’s targetSide: 'enemy' means it targets a hero; position 1 = front rank.

Notes (005 §Common / elite enemies (proto)):

  • The Cinder Slime is the proto’s realization of the slime archetype — a stone-cored bruiser whose body shatters the borrowed sword in the prologue (proto: src/core/campaign/content.ts node n2a). “Cinder” ties it to the game’s title rather than any decided lore name.
  • The Hex Imp is a magic-using imp variant (caster), distinct from the melee Goblin Imp. Neither is the lore-finalized name (Imps/Goblins naming still open).
  • The River Brigand realizes the Brigands/Bandits archetype; “River” ties to the Mediterranean/Caribbean river throughline.
  • Skeletons appear as Skeleton Archers (ranged), consistent with “mandatory for dungeons”; they fight in the Bone Gallery and the ambush narrows (proto: content.ts nodes n2b, n4).

Boss

The slice’s single boss — the tomb-golem Ossuary Sentinel (tomb_golem, 🗿) — has its own page (best-a). It realizes the adaptive-armor design once pitched as the Blacksmith; see also Golems.

Encounter deployment [IMPLEMENTED]

Enemies are placed across the dungeon graph (proto: src/core/campaign/content.ts):

  • The Crumbling Stair (n1, fight): 2× Goblin Imp + Cinder Slime.
  • Ambush in the Narrows (n2b, elite, ambush: true): River Brigand + Skeleton Archer + Hex Imp. The ambush flag scatters the party’s formation at battle start (proto: src/core/campaign/run.ts — scatterFormation) — see Encounters, Ambushes & Enemy Areas.
  • The Bone Gallery (n4, fight): Cinder Slime + 2× Skeleton Archer.
  • The Ossuary Sentinel (n5, boss): the tomb golem alone.
  • Repeated archetypes get unique instance ids and are placed front-to-back (positions capped at rank 3) (proto: src/core/campaign/run.ts — buildEncounter).

Related systems

The behavior of this roster is shaped by systems owned by other batches: enemies telegraph via the proto’s 6 readable intent categories; they draw statuses from the shared status bank; and they get deadlier under Escalation (attackers gain an accuracy/crit “edge” the longer a fight runs), with the Wounded amplifier (×1.25 taken below 50% HP) and a one-per-fight shield blunting big hits. See the systems category for those.

Design history

  • 2023-02-16 — the brainstorm “versus” test roster listed Goblins, Slimes, Golems, Brigands as enemies. The implemented roster differs: it uses Goblin Imp + Hex Imp for the imp/goblin archetype, Cinder Slime, Skeleton Archer, River Brigand, and puts the golem only as the boss.
  • (proto, undated) — the five-enemy roster + golem boss shipped in the vertical slice. [IMPLEMENTED] (005 §Design Decisions Finalized: “a 6-enemy roster… + a golem boss”).
