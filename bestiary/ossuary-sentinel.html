Ossuary Sentinel CANON

The single boss of the playable vertical slice — a tomb-golem guardian (🗿) that stands watch at the threshold of Ajax’s Tomb, beaten by reading its telegraphs and coordinating rather than out-damaging its 132 HP.

Summary — The Ossuary Sentinel (“Boss · Tomb Guardian”, glyph 🗿, id tomb_golem) is the lone boss shipped in the browser vertical slice, “The Descent into Ajax’s Tomb.” It is an adaptive heavy that self-hardens mid-fight — the “changes its armor / hardens itself” behavior once pitched for the Blacksmith Boss is prototyped here instead, via its Adamant Shell skill (005 §Boss: Ossuary Sentinel (proto)). As an implemented golem it is currently a pure tomb guardian, not yet tied to Gideon’s soul-harvest golem origin — that origin remains design lore (005 §Implemented golem boss).

Description & role

  • The slice’s single boss — a tomb-golem guardian of Ajax’s Tomb, deployed at graph node n5 (“The Ossuary Sentinel”, boss), where it fights alone (proto: src/core/campaign/content.ts — node n5; 005 §Encounter context (proto)).
  • It realizes the adaptive / self-hardening design the group wanted from the Blacksmith; that concept was prototyped on the golem rather than on a distinct blacksmith enemy (005 §Boss: Ossuary Sentinel (proto)).
  • As a golem, it appears in the proto only as the boss, not as rank-and-file (005 §Implemented golem boss; §Proto reconciliation).

Design read

The Sentinel telegraphs hard and is meant to be beaten by reading its intent and coordinating — “become as the goblin — many, coordinated, humble” — not by out-damaging its 132 HP, directly enacting the “tactical complexity over HP bloat” philosophy (proto: src/core/campaign/content.ts node n5 story; 005 §Boss: Ossuary Sentinel (proto)). Fights also grow deadlier over time via Escalation, so a stalling war of attrition is discouraged.

Design history

  • 2023-02-08 — the adaptive-armor idea is first pitched as the Blacksmith Boss: “We fight blacksmith who changes their armor based on your attack” (M01162, CrunchyConniptions), one of the gaming-deconstruction pillars.
  • 2023-02-16 — Golems established as a baseline enemy type (M02168, CrunchyConniptions) with a soul-harvest origin under Gideon (M02173–M02174, FreshToast); Locke is noted uneasy around them (M02180, FreshToast).
  • (proto, undated) — the vertical slice ships the golem only as this boss, the Ossuary Sentinel, a self-hardening tomb guardian decoupled from the soul-harvest origin; it inherits the Blacksmith’s adaptive-armor behavior via Adamant Shell. [IMPLEMENTED] — current presentation.
