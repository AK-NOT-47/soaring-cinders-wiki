Vaenngrym

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
Also known as: The Dragon
First conceived 2023-02-11 (M01771, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-10-25 (M04708, FreshToast)

Vaenngrym CANON

The Act 2 climax dragon — a house-sized force of nature, son of Ajax’s dragon, that fills all four battle positions and inflicts fear on the party.

Summary — Vaenngrym is the game’s central dragon boss: “much less of a character than a force of nature, a narrative tool and a big scary boss monster” (M04708, FreshToast, 2023-10-25). He is a house-sized, fire-breathing, scale-and-fang monster, the son of Ajax’s dragon, and a three-phase boss whose fight is the Act 2 turning point for Locke. Mechanically he is the only enemy that fills all four positions, opens with his biggest attack, and inflicts a fear effect to weaken the party (M01770, FreshToast, 2023-02-11). Whether he should be feathered — and whether that tips him toward a phoenix — was debated but resolved toward “DRAGON but DRAGON” (M03023, FreshToast, 2023-03-11).

Name Origin

Vaenngrym is a bastardization of Old Norse / Icelandic words for wings and despair, coined to sound “mythical, fantasy, grand” (M01771–M01772, FreshToast, 2023-02-11; 005 §Name Origin).

Lineage

Vaenngrym is imagined as the son of Ajax’s dragon, tying him into the Ajax dragon-blood throughline — Ajax ate a dragon, and draconic features run in his descendants (M02686, FreshToast, 2023-02-22; 005 §Lineage). Draconic creatures’ irises are coloured like a sunset; Lysander and Raphael are described as having “purple-orange-yellow gradient eyes coloured irises like Vaenngrym” (M02904, FreshToast, 2023-03-05).

True Nature (dragons as living disease) CANON

A “dragon” is not merely a flying lizard but the visible late stage of Dracosis — the process by which a bloodline becomes steadily more draconic. The condition was first framed as a bloodborne parasite named Drykksar (005 §True Nature; 000 Part 10), then reframed in the Part 18 “Final Version” as a virus-like living disease, not a parasite (005 §Dracosis Lore) EVOLVED -&gt; Dracosis-as-virus. Over generations a host’s descendants develop tough/metallic hide, fiery breath (or laser / volcanic spit / flammable gas), increased size, and functional wings; current dragons are held to be descendants of dinosaurs taken over by the disease (005 §True Nature).

Physical Description

  • Monster the size of a house or bigger; has scales, fangs and flames (005 §Physical Description).
  • Breathes fire; roars “in a fashion that would make lions whimper”; genuinely frightful.
  • Can smash through rock; towers over the party, bears its teeth, sudden and brutal.

Combat Role

  • The only enemy in the game that fills all 4 positions (005 §Combat Role).
  • Has 3 phases; make it huge (M01769, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • Escalation-scaling trait: like the most powerful enemies, the dragon has skills that directly increase or scale based on Escalation (005 §Combat Role, Part 8). See Escalation Mechanic.
  • Spoken threat: “Filthy follower of a false blessing. Give penance for the sins of your forefathers and receive terror for your children.” — hints at the hero’s power (M02276, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).
  • Opens with its big attack (a “Sans approach”, after Undertale), putting players on the back foot the entire battle (005 §Mechanics).
  • Pursuit phase: the party is trapped fleeing while the dragon smashes through rock in positions 1 and 2 to reach them; the party takes pot shots and heals before the onslaught continues (M01812, FreshToast, 2023-02-11). It resembles a horror scene of a monster smashing a door down, and mechanically gives breathing room so the fight can be harder before and after.
  • Fear effect: literally inflicts a fear effect on the party to weaken them for the first two turns of battle; “maybe fear decreases initiative” (M01770, FreshToast, 2023-02-11; 005 §Fear Effect). See Open questions — a standalone “Fear” status is not yet in the combat proto (005 §Fear Effect proto note).
  • Chases the party down a volcano.

Narrative Role

  • Act 2 climax and a turning point, “especially for Locke” (M01767–M01768, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • A metaphorical fear to overcome (failure or similar); the party begin to recognize their strengths and Locke is “grounded” with help.
  • Highlights the motivations of Ajax, Lysander and Locke; the den is accessed to face him; characters are terrified and fighting for their lives (005 §Narrative Role).
  • Upon killing Vaenngrym, Locke is given the option to drink the dragon’s blood — no mechanical bearing, but it informs her opinion of Lysander’s pursuit of power and her understanding of Ajax (M02661, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).

Design References

  • Grigori in Dragon’s Dogma as “the best dragon sequence” (005 §Design References).
  • Sans approach (Undertale) for opening with a big attack.
  • A playlist was made for the fight: “You’re probably not surprised how well the lyrics kinda fit a dragon fight.”

Open questions

  • DEBATED Feathered or not? Whether Vaenngrym should be feathered — referencing the feathered-dinosaur theory to add majesty/flair and hint at his dinosaur origin, versus keeping him unfeathered to look more menacing. This spun into a phoenix-vs-dragon argument and was resolved toward staying a dragon, but the Discord notes leave the exact visual unresolved (M02986–M03026, 2023-03-11). See Design history.
  • DEBATED Fear mechanic specifics — “Fear feels a little generic” (M01774, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-11); whether fear decreases initiative and how it is implemented is open. No “Fear” negative status exists in the combat proto yet (005 §Fear Effect proto note).

Design history

  • 2023-02-11 — Core concept laid down in one session: lean into the dragon’s frightful nature as a metaphorical fear to overcome; a good Act 2 climax and turning point for Locke; give it 3 phases, make it huge; literally inflicts a fear effect at the start of battle (M01767–M01770, FreshToast). Crunchy: “Fear feels a little generic” (M01774).
  • 2023-02-11 — Named Vaenngrym, from bastardized Old Norse/Icelandic for wings + despair (M01771–M01772, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-11 — Pursuit phase added: trapped fleeing while the dragon smashes through rock in positions 1–2 (M01812, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-16 — Spoken threat drafted: “Filthy follower of a false blessing…” (M02276, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-21 — Post-kill dragon’s-blood choice for Locke added (M02661, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-22 — Lineage established: son of Ajax’s dragon (M02686, FreshToast).
  • 2023-03-05 — Iris colour tie-in: draconic creatures (and Ajax’s descendants) have sunset-coloured irises “like Vaenngrym” (M02904, FreshToast).
  • 2023-03-11 — Feather/phoenix debate. FreshToast proposed feathers because “dinosaurs might have had feathers” and it “hints, subtly, at Vaenngrym being a dinosaur” (M02986, M03025). spirit_noodle argued he’d “look more menacing if he was not feathered” (M02987), then pivoted to wanting “the majesty of a phoenix… different shades of red feathers” (M03001–M03003). FreshToast pushed back: “the look of a phoenix is more than just stapling red feathers onto a lizard” (M03010), and when asked “would it not be cooler to have DRAGON but PHOENIX?” answered “No because DRAGON but DRAGON is cool” (M03022–M03023) — “but that doesn’t mean he should also just straight up be a bird” (M03026). Left unresolved in the notes but leaning dragon.
  • 2023-10-25 — Framing crystallized: “much less of a character than a force of nature, a narrative tool and a big scary boss monster” (M04708, FreshToast) — matches the current presentation.
