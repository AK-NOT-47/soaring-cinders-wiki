Dracosis (The Dragon Virus)

CANONCaelian OdysseyNotes / Design
Also known as: The Dragon Virus, Dragon Parasite
First conceived 2023-02-22 (M02691, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-11-07 (M04893, CrunchyConniptions)

Dracosis (The Dragon Virus) CANON

A “dragon” isn’t a lizard — it’s a disease. Dracosis is a virus-like, alien “living disease” that turns a bloodline progressively draconic over generations.

Summary — Dracosis is the biological engine behind dragons and many mutated creatures in the setting. In its most recent (Part 18 / 2023-11-07) form it is a virus, not a parasite: a virus-like entity in a state of “borrowed life” — more animate than a typical virus yet distinct from a conventional parasite; an alien “living disease” (M04853–M04893, 2023-11-07). It carries a subtle intention, guiding host behavior (aggression, especially toward dangerous foes), and over a few generations produces hard scales, flame resistance and breath, large size, and enhanced lifespan. The condition that causes Dracosis is named Drykksar. Its earlier framing as a “bloodborne parasite” (Part 10) was superseded by the virus reframe.

What Dracosis is (current: living disease / virus) CANON

  • A virus, not a parasite EVOLVED — “remove bacteria… I’d rather you refer to parasites instead” → “Ok lol, it can be a virus instead of a parasite then… Final: virus” (M04864, CrunchyConniptions; M04870, FreshToast, 2023-11-07). Supersedes the Part 10 parasite framing (see Design history).
  • Borrowed life: a virus-like entity in “a unique state of ‘borrowed life,’ more animate than a typical virus yet distinct from a conventional parasite” — alien, perplexing, “otherworldly”; the “living disease” is “the closest we’re gonna get” (M04853, M04876, M04883, 2023-11-07).
  • Intention / will: “it possesses intention and, hopefully, purpose, though it may not be technically alive.” Symptoms guide host behavior more than other diseases — violence/aggression, especially toward seemingly dangerous foes; its intentions are “conveyed through [the] lives of others,” “like possession but more sensual” (M04890, M04893, 2023-11-07; 005 §Dracosis Lore).
  • Physical changes (latter stages, a few generations deep): hard scales, flame resistance and breath, large size, enhanced lifespan (M04890, FreshToast, 2023-11-07). Earlier phrasing listed “metallic skin, fiery breath, increased size and functional wings,” with fiery breath alternatively a laser, volcanic spit, or flammable gas (M02699–M02700, FreshToast, 2023-02-22).

Dragons as descendants, not beasts CANON

  • “A ‘dragon’ is actually a bloodborne parasite and not just a flying lizard. Rather, it infects a host and causes their descendants to become more and more ‘draconic’… Current dragons are the descendants of dinosaurs, who never actually died off but were instead taken over by dragons” (M02691, FreshToast, 2023-02-22). (The “parasite” wording here is the superseded framing; the descent-from-dinosaurs and generational-mutation ideas persist.)
  • Ties into the Ajax dragon-blood throughline: Ajax got infected upon stabbing/eating his dragon, so draconic features run in his descendants (M02684, FreshToast, 2023-02-22; M04722, FreshToast, 2023-10-25). Vaenngrym (best-a) is described as the son of Ajax’s dragon.
  • Draconic creatures have irises “coloured like a sunset” (M02905, FreshToast, 2023-03-05).

Philosophy & in-setting virology

  • A play on Dark Souls “hollowing”: a purposeless (hollow) individual gets infected and is given purpose — “arguably it is more alive than real living beings without purpose”; described as the opposite of hollowing (Dark Souls) or Drogo (Game of Thrones) (M04893, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-11-07).
  • Kept nebulous: nobody has invented the microscope yet, so virus and bacteria are functionally identical in-world; characters cannot reasonably deduce what it is or whether it’s alive (005 §Virology in setting).
  • Thematic pairing: Dracosis is the thematic opposite of vampirism — Dracosis = evolving into a superior being (the future, changing to be strong); vampirism = stagnation (M05173–M05187, 2024-02-10). See Raphael (best-a), in whom both blend.

Creatures produced by Dracosis

  • The dragon Vaenngrym (best-a), the Giant Beetle (large + semi-metallic shell), and Striga (mosquito-dragons) are all dracosis-mutated. See also the Grafting Cult and general monster evolution (monsters “growing just a little too fast”).

Open questions

  • The name of the causative condition/parasite was chosen as Drykksar (over the candidate “Sardrykkr”) (M02695–M02697, FreshToast, 2023-02-22). Drykksar has its own page in the characters/supernatural batch.
  • Whether Locke gets infected with Dracosis (or it becomes a player choice) was long undecided; “go with the flow” for now (M04717–M04727, 2023-10-25).

Design history

  • 2023-02-22 (Part 10) — original framing: a dragon is a “bloodborne parasite,” infecting a host so descendants become progressively draconic (tough hide, fiery breath); current dragons descend from dinosaurs “taken over by dragons.” The causative condition is named Drykksar (over “Sardrykkr”); Dracosis is the process of a bloodline becoming draconic (M02691–M02700, FreshToast).
  • 2023-11-07 (Part 18, “Final Version”) — reframed from parasite → virus: after “remove bacteria… refer to parasites instead,” the team settles on “virus instead of a parasite” — an alien “living disease” in “borrowed life,” with intention/will and generational physical changes; explicitly a play on Dark Souls hollowing (M04853–M04893, CrunchyConniptions & FreshToast). This supersedes the parasite framing and is the current presentation. EVOLVED
  • 2024-02-10 — positioned as the thematic opposite of vampirism (evolution vs stagnation) (M05173–M05187).
