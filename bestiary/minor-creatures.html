Minor & Undeveloped Creatures DEBATED

A holding page for creatures that exist in the notes only as a line or two — dracosis-mutant bugs, classic legendaries, and the ambush critters. Each is a seed, most still undeveloped.

Summary — Several bestiary entries in 005 are only a line or two long. They are grouped here so each keeps its citation without inflating into a stub page: the dracosis-mutated Striga and Wargs; the classic Hydrae and Griffons (whose “classic traits must stay intact”); and the ambush-associated Ninja Frogs and Charging Bulls. Where a creature is more fully realized elsewhere it links out.

Striga (Mosquito Dragons) DEBATED

  • “Striga: ancient mosquitos having undergone Dracosis (The Dragon Virus)” (M02706, FreshToast, 2023-02-24), introduced under the heading “Mosquito dragons” (M02705, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).
  • Single-line stub; otherwise undeveloped in the notes (005 §Mosquito Dragons (Striga)). A visible product of accelerating monster evolution.

Wargs DEBATED

  • Source is “Mentioned” only — “Wargs” (M02707, FreshToast, 2023-02-24); no further details provided (005 §Wargs).

Hydrae

  • Classic trait that “must stay intact”: hydrae have loads of heads (M04623, FreshToast, 2023-10-24; 005 §Legendary Creatures). This is part of the game’s core creature-design principle — keep classic traits intact (see Enemy Design Philosophy §Creature design). No further details provided.

Griffons

  • Classic trait that “must stay intact”: griffons are bird-lions (M04623, FreshToast, 2023-10-24; 005 §Legendary Creatures).
  • Earliest mention is a throwaway wish: “I also lowkey want tiny griffons instead of pigeons but that can be sacrificed for our sanity if need be” (M00746, FreshToast, 2021-08-17). No further development.

Ninja Frogs

  • Found in foggy bogs (M02929, FreshToast, 2023-03-09; 005 §Ninja Frogs). spirit_noodle: “foggy bogs with ninja frogs sounds AWESOME” (M02932, 2023-03-09).
  • Associated with ambush mechanics — see Encounters, Ambushes & Enemy Areas.

Charging Bulls

  • Found in open fields (M02929, FreshToast, 2023-03-09; 005 §Charging Bulls).
  • Associated with ambush mechanics — see Encounters, Ambushes & Enemy Areas.

Open questions

  • DEBATED Striga and Wargs are undeveloped stubs (005 §Mosquito Dragons (Striga), §Wargs).
  • Hydrae and Griffons have only their “classic trait” defined; no stats, role, or region.

Design history

  • 2021-08-17 — Griffons first wished for (“tiny griffons instead of pigeons”) (M00746, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-24 — Striga (ancient mosquitos that underwent dracosis) and Wargs (mentioned only) added (M02705–M02707, FreshToast).
  • 2023-03-09 — Ninja Frogs (foggy bogs) and Charging Bulls (open fields) introduced as ambush-locale creatures (M02929–M02932, FreshToast & spirit_noodle).
  • 2023-10-24 — Hydrae (loads of heads) and Griffons (bird-lions) fixed as classic-traits-must-stay-intact creatures (M04623, FreshToast).
