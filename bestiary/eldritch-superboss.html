Eldritch Super Boss DEBATED

A hidden endgame superboss you cannot kill — you “beat” it, and it rewards you with a super cool item.

Summary — Listed under Hidden Super Bosses, the Eldritch is an unkillable capstone encounter: “You cannot kill it. You beat it. Gives you super cool item” (M00867–M00870, spirit_noodle & CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-02). It is a special game-mechanic feature — a true-ending / superboss test rather than a standard enemy. In the cross-doc reconciliation, GAME_DESIGN.md and the proto gamebible name a Daemon superboss as the endgame test and treat it as the same encounter as this unkillable Eldritch (005 §Hidden Super Bosses).

Description

  • A hidden super boss — an optional endgame encounter (M00866, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-02; 005 §Hidden Super Bosses).
  • You cannot kill it; you beat it — the win condition is not a kill (M00867–M00869, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-02).
  • Beating it gives you a super cool item (M00870, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-02).
  • Recorded as a special game-mechanic feature.

Open questions

  • DEBATED Eldritch vs Daemon — is the hidden unkillable superboss (Eldritch) and the GAME_DESIGN “Daemon superboss” one encounter or two? The proto gamebible treats them as the same capstone; this page records the alternate name (005 §Daemon vs Eldritch; §Hidden Super Bosses). See Daemon.
  • PROPOSED What “beating without killing” mechanically entails, and what the “super cool item” is, are unspecified.

Design history

  • 2023-02-02 — Coined during an early feature brainstorm under “Hidden Super Bosses”: spirit_noodle names it “Eldtrich [sic]”, “You cannot kill it / You beat it”; CrunchyConniptions adds “gives you super cool item” (M00866–M00870) — matches the current presentation.
  • (cross-doc, undated) — Reconciled with the “Daemon superboss” of GAME_DESIGN.md / the proto gamebible, treated there as the same endgame test (005 §Hidden Super Bosses reconciliation).
