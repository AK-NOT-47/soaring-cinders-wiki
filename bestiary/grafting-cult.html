Grafting Cult & Monster Evolution

Also known as: The Cult, Monster Grafters

Grafting Cult & Monster Evolution CANON

People who graft monster parts onto their own bodies — escaping physical weakness at the cost of their humanity, and foreshadowing Dracosis (The Dragon Virus).

Summary — Two linked ideas from the same session: monsters in the setting are evolving too fast (people have noticed monsters “growing just a little too fast”), and a cult has arisen of people who graft monster parts onto themselves (M02715, FreshToast, 2023-02-24). The cult is a short-term, antagonistic faction used to foreshadow Dracosis (The Dragon Virus) and the game’s theme of discarding one’s humanity — humans escaping physical weakness at the cost of what makes them human (M02717, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).

Monster evolution CANON

  • Monsters are evolving; “people have identified that monsters are growing just a little too fast” (M02715, FreshToast, 2023-02-24). This is the same accelerating-mutation phenomenon that Dracosis (The Dragon Virus) describes at the bloodline level (see the Giant Beetle and Striga as visible results).

The Grafting Cult CANON

  • “Cult of people who graft monster parts onto themselves” (M02715, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).
  • Function: a “short term, antagonistic faction used to introduce fore[s]hadowing of dracosis and discarding one’s humanity” (M02717, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).
  • Theme: humans escaping physical weakness at the cost of their humanity (005 §Monster Evolution & the Grafting Cult). The source notes the full lore lives in the World Bible.

Design history

  • 2023-02-24 — introduced in one session: monsters growing too fast, and a cult grafting monster parts onto themselves as a short-term antagonistic faction foreshadowing dracosis and the loss-of-humanity theme (M02715–M02717, FreshToast). Current presentation; undeveloped beyond this seed in the notes.
