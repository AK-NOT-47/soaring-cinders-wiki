Drykksar

EVOLVEDCross-IterationNotes / Design
Also known as: Sardrykkr
First conceived 2023-02-22 (M02695, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-11-07 (M04903)

Drykksar EVOLVED

The living disease that causes Dracosis: a virus-like entity that, despite not being strictly alive, exhibits a subtle but noticeable intention — and turns a bloodline steadily more draconic over generations.

Summary — Drykksar is the name of the condition which causes Dracosis, the process by which a bloodline becomes steadily more draconic (M02697, FreshToast, 2023-02-22). In its current (November 2023) conception it is a virus-like “living disease” rather than a conventional parasite: something that, despite not being strictly alive, has a subtle but noticeable intention — “a will of sorts but not in a familiar sense” — conveyed through its symptoms and the way it guides host behaviour more strongly than other diseases do, toward violence and aggression especially against seemingly dangerous foes (M04860–M04865, FreshToast, 2023-11-07). Latter stages of infection, occurring a few generations deep, bring physical changes: hard scales, flame resistance and breath, large size and enhanced lifespan (M04890–M04891, FreshToast, 2023-11-07).

Nature & Rules

  • Causes Dracosis: a bloodline steadily becoming more draconic (M02697, FreshToast, 2023-02-22).
  • Difficult to contract; requires a living host; 100% hereditary success; lasting but slow spread (M02710–M02711, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).
  • Only has subtle effects on a primary host — the first instance in a bloodline (M02713, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).
  • Current framing (2023-11-07): a virus/disease rather than a parasite — “Bacteria/Virus is functionally identical to us because nobody’s invented a microscope yet”; virology in the setting isn’t developed enough for characters to define what a virus even is (M04858, M04867, FreshToast, 2023-11-07). CrunchyConniptions specifically vetoed “bacteria” (“because fuck bacteria… I honestly have no good reason”, M04869) so it is a virus (M04870, FreshToast, 2023-11-07).
  • The key hook is intention without conventional life: “the disease is alive and suggests intention with its symptoms” (M04890, FreshToast, 2023-11-07); “arguably it is more alive than real living beings without purpose” (M04895, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-11-07). CrunchyConniptions likens it to an inverse of Dark Souls hollowing / Drogo from Game of Thrones — it provides rather than strips purpose (M04893, 2023-11-07). Hard limit agreed by all three: “no avatars” — no individual embodiment of the drykksar (M04900–M04903, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions/spirit_noodle, 2023-11-07).

Thematic role

  • “It’s the physical manifestation of evolution with replacing ‘trial and error’ with ‘Victory by battle’ (bloodshed).” (M02698, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22)
  • Eventually the hero’s lineage would have become like-dragons themselves (002 §Drykksar).
  • The bastard Damaris is a herald or champion of sorts for the draconic parasite (M02694, FreshToast, 2023-02-22).
  • Dracosis also drives the bestiary: monsters are evolving — e.g. Striga, “ancient mosquitos having undergone dracosis”, and the large semi-metallic beetle (M02705–M02709, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).

Sequel hook

  • PROPOSED In a sequel there could be some kind of inquisition or order dedicated to eliminating drykksar — “There’s no real point talking about this now but it’d be cool to future proof” (M03048–M03049, FreshToast, 2023-03-15).

Design history

  • 2023-02-22 — naming: two candidate spellings floated in one message, “Sardrykkr / Drykksar” (M02695, FreshToast); Drykksar is the spelling used in every subsequent message, so Sardrykkr is the superseded alternative (preserved in 009 §Appendix — Considered & Dropped). Same day: defined as “the name of the condition or parasite which causes Dracosis” (M02697, FreshToast); framed as evolution-by-bloodshed (M02698, CrunchyConniptions); the bastard named its herald (M02694, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-24 — parasite traits fixed: mutagenic parasite; difficult to contract, requires a living host, 100% hereditary success, lasting but slow spread, subtle effects on the primary host (M02710–M02713, FreshToast). Dracosis end-state: metallic skin, fiery breath (or laser / volcanic spit / flammable gas), increased size, functional wings (M02699–M02700, FreshToast).
  • 2023-03-15 — PROPOSED sequel anti-drykksar inquisition/order (M03048, FreshToast).
  • 2023-11-07 — evolved: parasite → virus-like living disease with intention — supersedes “mutagenic parasite” above. CrunchyConniptions pushed for a “Virus+” with “borrowed life”: “alien, perplexing, otherworldly” rather than a mundane parasite (M04853). FreshToast countered that a virus being “more alive than others” is the wrong hook and proposed instead a disease that despite not being strictly alive has a subtle but noticeable intention (M04857–M04861); “parasite’s a little different, more complex and doesn’t pair as well with our current iteration of the idea” (M04868). Agreed outcome: it’s a virus, “the disease is alive and suggests intention with its symptoms”, latter-stage generational physical changes (hard scales, flame resistance and breath, large size, enhanced lifespan), and no avatars (M04870, M04890–M04903, all three authors).
