The peachwood staff wielded by Cyrus, made from a branch he played with as a child.

Summary — Transcend is the signature weapon of Cyrus Sunworth: a peachwood staff he carries and wields, made from a branch he played with as a child (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). The sentimental origin — a childhood branch — ties the weapon to his character.

Description

  • A staff of peachwood (M04227).
  • Fashioned from a branch Cyrus played with as a child, giving it personal, sentimental significance (M04227).
