Protection

Caelian OdysseyNotes / Design
Also known as: Armour, Armor, Wards, Shields
First conceived undated

Caelian armour, shields, and wards for travellers facing beasts, brigands, sorcery, and monsters.

Summary - The submitted protection note frames armour as prudent travel gear: dangers beyond civilisation stalk roads with opportunistic malice, so travellers wear protection against beasts and brigands alike (Submitted warfare notes section Protection, undated). Caelian armour is imagined like 18th-century Europe except that body armour never fully declined: full medieval plate fell out of fashion as wasteful against magic and overpowering monsters, but half-plate, brigandine, shields, and wards remain useful.

Armour style

Caelian armour keeps body protection in common use longer than real-world European history. The submitted note lists typical user groups: soldiers and knights, rogues and Yorusha, mercenaries and bounty hunters, and monster hunters.

Motley armour

Motley armour is preferred by mercenaries and bounty hunters.

Materials

  • Padding: padded cloth and hide.
  • Leather: soft leather and hardened leather.
  • Layered metal: chain, scale, and brigandine. The note calls this the most popular armour form for mercenaries and explorers, advanced beyond historical brigandine.
  • Plate metal: a breastplate or full cuirass is common, but expensive, as an alternative to brigandine.
  • Unorthodox materials: hide and chitin.

Plate in Caelia

Full medieval-style plate quickly fell out of fashion. It is seen as a waste of metal because Caelia is full of overbearingly strong monsters and magic. As with the real-world impact of firearms, full plate is largely obsolete, but strategic half-plate and brigandine remain popular because beasts are still common threats.

Shields

Common shield shapes include round, heater, kite, and tower shields. Greatshields are used by people with exceptional strength and are particularly useful with impulse.

Wards

Traditional armour and shields can comfortably turn a blade or sorcerous wind, but they are ineffective against conjured flame or lightning. Wards are magical shields specialized for defence against such magical attacks.

A ward is an enchanted metal brace worn on the forearm. Its calyx produces a shimmer barrier when activated. The barrier can deflect light physical attacks like a normal shield, but it excels at repelling targeted magic.

Wards are outclassed by metal and wooden shields against ordinary metal weaponry, so guards and soldiers rarely use them. They are much lighter than conventional shields and can be concealed under clothing, making them popular with travellers who want fashionable, lightweight protection.
