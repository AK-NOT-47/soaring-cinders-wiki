Skyward CindersProto-Implemented
First conceived 2023-09-10

The Sunderer

A rusty spear with a GLIMMER — the first of Ajax’s seven relics, and the explicit symbol of Locke’s claim to Ajax’s legacy.

Summary — The Sunderer is a rusty spear, marked by a distinctive glimmer, that Locke stumbles into and claims during the prologue/tutorial dungeon. It is the first of Ajax’s seven relics and, thematically, the load-bearing prop of Locke’s arc: where the sword Lysander gave her shatters against a golem, the spear she finds and takes for herself endures — making the Sunderer a symbol of her independence and her status as heir to Ajax’s role. It is realized in the proto vertical slice as a permanent relic-kind treasure reward.

Description

  • A rusty spear with a GLIMMER — the glimmer marks it as more than scrap (006 §Specific Weapons).
  • The first of “Ajax’s seven thingies” (relics), acquired in the trial Locke passes in the tutorial (2023-09-10). See Ajax’s Relics / Ajax’s Tomb (locations batch) — the trial’s end is the secret entrance to the tomb, which Lysander and Eris mistake for a dead end.
  • Locke stumbles into and acquires the Sunderer during her separation from Lysander and Eris in the tutorial, then arms herself with it and reunites with them for the next battle (006 §Specific Weapons).

Story role & symbolism

  • Explicit symbol of Locke’s status as heir to the role of Ajax (2024-08-24).
  • Independence from Lysander — the sword Lysander gave Locke SHATTERS against the golem’s stone body (“the power he gave her breaks”); the Sunderer is power she sought for herself and developed, which is why the spear represents her independence (2024-08-24; 006 §Specific Weapons). Locke chooses to do her own thing, though the notes concede the spear doesn’t strictly enable that — “a little murky, if convenient”.
  • Lysander notices it — a “somewhat important” beat where Lysander comments on the spear or tries and fails to pick it up (mjolnir/excalibur style), dismissing it as worthless/a waste of time while it turns out to be Locke’s best find (2024-08-24). Meant to read as a footnote that becomes the real prize.

Implementation (proto) [IMPLEMENTED]

The Sunderer ships in the proto prologue dungeon as a relic-kind treasure reward at the “Glimmer in the Rubble” node — “Ajax’s spear — the first relic. You are armed in earnest now.” It is found after the borrowed sword shatters on the slime’s stone-cored body, so the two-weapon thesis is coded into the dungeon graph itself. Locke’s in-combat spear kit (Pierce / Sunder / Phalanx / Emberlance) is the Pierce family; Sunder applies Defense Down on hit, matching the spear’s guard-breaking flavour (proto: src/core/campaign/content.ts node n2a; run.ts applyReward case 'relic'; src/core/combat/content.ts HERO_SKILLS — per 006 §Specific Weapons).

Companion prop — Lysander’s backup sword. The simple sword Lysander gives Locke in the tutorial is symbolic of “power he gives her”; it shatters against the golem’s stone body (“the power he gave her breaks”), setting up the Sunderer’s meaning (006 §Lysander’s Backup Sword). Not a standalone item — it exists to be broken.
