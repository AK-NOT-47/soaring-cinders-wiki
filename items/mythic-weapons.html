Mythic Weapons

Mythic Weapons DEBATED

A 4th quality tier: unique, fixed-stat weapons that sit outside the Tier/Quality systems — “overclocked” for a while, then intentionally outclassed.

Summary — Mythic is a fourth Quality tier reserved for unique weapons with fixed statistics that exist outside the normal Tier and Quality systems. This lets the designers overclock a weapon a little and place it anywhere in the game (not just the endgame): a Mythic can appear as an Act 1 reward and then be outclassed by ordinary Act 2 finds, so the player gets a cool signature weapon for a stretch without breaking the power curve. The name “Mythic” was chosen over “legendary” — “a bit of a mouthful” (M01443, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).

Rules / Effects

  • Fixed statistics, unique — Mythic weapons have set stats and exist outside the Tier and Quality ladders, letting developers overclock them but scatter them throughout the game rather than only at the end (M01442, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • A 4th rarity — an “overclocked rarity but similar tier”: decked out with rune slots and properties or a special effect, and maybe higher-than-normal stats (M01485–M01486, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • Time-limited relevance — strong only for when you acquire it, so it can be outclassed later. Example: a unique Mythic appears as an Act 1 reward but is later outclassed by Act 2 finds (M01456, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • Locke is noted as suited to the idea / it being a Mythic geared toward her (006 §Characteristics).

Named Mythic — Ajax’s Broken Sword Hilt

A Mythic weapon that appears to be just a broken sword hilt but is actually a magical catalyst that functions like a sword through wind or magical force (M02720, FreshToast, 2023-02-24). It fits the game’s alchemy/wind-magic flavour and ties into the Ajax mythology; not to be confused with the separate spear relic Sunderer.

Design history

  • 2023-02-11 — proposed: unique fixed-stat weapons outside Tier/Quality, dubbed Mythic (over “legendary”); an “overclocked rarity but similar tier,” strong on acquisition but outclassable later; single-vs-category left open (M01442–M01443, M01453, M01456, M01485–M01486, FreshToast). The single-vs-category question was never resolved in chat — hence status DEBATED.
  • 2023-02-24 — first concrete Mythic named: Ajax’s Broken Sword Hilt, a disguised magical catalyst (M02720, FreshToast).
