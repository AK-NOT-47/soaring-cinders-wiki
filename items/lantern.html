Lantern

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
Also known as: torch
First conceived 2023-02-17 (M02377, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-02-19 (M02442, CrunchyConniptions)

Lantern CANON

The light source the player carries into the game’s pitch-dark dungeons — a double-edged tool that reveals the way but also reveals you.

Summary — The lantern is given to the player to see in dark places like dungeons, and is carried by Locke Evertide in the prologue dungeon (006 §Lantern). Its defining feature is a trade-off: it lets you see, but also lets monsters see you, making it harder to sneak. Some monsters are attracted to the lantern; some may fear it (M02379–M02380, FreshToast, 2023-02-17).

Description

Dungeons are conceived as completely dark, with vision restricted to how far your light reaches; different dungeons can have different levels of darkness (M01422, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-11). The lantern is the player’s answer to that darkness. In the earliest phrasing it was a “torch” (M01422); by the dedicated design pass it is a lantern given to the player for dark places (M02377, FreshToast, 2023-02-17).

It appears in the prologue script: “You and Lysander are in a dark cave and a lantern is carried by Locke” (M02442, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-19).

Rules / Effects

  • Vision vs. visibility trade-off — the lantern lets you see but also lets monsters see you, making it harder to sneak (M02378, FreshToast, 2023-02-17).
  • Monster reactions — some monsters are attracted to the lantern (M02379); some monsters may FEAR the lantern (M02380, FreshToast, 2023-02-17).

Related systems

  • Rest points / camps reset the dungeon Dark-Souls-style; while in a dungeon the main camp occupies the start (ledger row R13, based on M02377–M02381).
  • Sneaking and roaming-creature interaction were pitched as things “different tools you can use” or level design should facilitate, rather than a first-strike bonus (M01429, FreshToast, 2023-02-11) — the lantern is one such tool.
  • Machina — a hovering automaton later proposed as Locke’s portable light, a more characterful alternative to a plain lantern (M03517–M03518, FreshToast, 2023-07-17).

Design history

  • 2023-02-11 — dungeons are dark with vision restricted to torch range; different dungeons can have different darkness levels (M01422, spirit_noodle). Early framing calls it a “torch.”
  • 2023-02-17 — the dedicated pass names it a lantern: given to the player for dark places; the see/be-seen trade-off; monsters attracted to it or fearing it (M02377–M02380, FreshToast). supersedes the informal “torch.”
  • 2023-02-19 — appears in the prologue script, carried by Locke in a dark cave with Lysander (M02442, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-07-17 — Machina proposed as Locke’s portable light — a dragonfly-like automaton — an in-world flavor alternative to carrying a lantern (M03517–M03518, FreshToast). The lantern remains the baseline system; the machina is the characterful light source. Both are recorded as CANON in 006.
