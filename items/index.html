The three-axis model for all weapons and armour: Tier (material = base power), Quality (rarity = bonus bandwidth), and Runes (materia-like slots with trade-offs

Lantern

The light source the player carries into the game’s pitch-dark dungeons — a double-edged tool that reveals the way but also reveals you .

Machina

A tiny hovering automaton — Locke’s dragonfly-like portable light, its wings woven from freeform shimmer.

Mythic Weapons

A 4th quality tier: unique, fixed-stat weapons that sit outside the Tier/Quality systems — “overclocked” for a while, then intentionally outclassed.

Rainfall

Pandora’s cannon — a magical outlet that lets her use her immense-but-uncontrollable capacity safely, “kinda like a prosthetic for a disability.”

River Flask

The strongest, most precious heal — a flask that drinks the river’s power and carries it into the deep dungeons, kept separate from your everyday healing.

Rune System

Materia-like slots on weapons and armour; every rune carries an upside and a downside, so building is optimization, not best-in-slot.

Shimmer Sails

Energy-fin sails made of freeform shimmer — glowing orange like a bubble.

The Jet & Shard System

A single reusable item — the Jet — whose effect is reconfigured by slotting Shards. Ships as the party’s primary, customizable heal.

The Sunderer

A rusty spear with a GLIMMER — the first of Ajax’s seven relics, and the explicit symbol of Locke’s claim to Ajax’s legacy.

Transcend (Cyrus's Staff)

The peachwood staff wielded by Cyrus, made from a branch he played with as a child.

Warpath

Flynt’s massive greatsword — a Dragonslayer-sized blade that Steele inherits, broken, and carries as a relic that can briefly reforge itself in energy.
