Machina CANON

A tiny hovering automaton — Locke’s dragonfly-like portable light, its wings woven from freeform shimmer.

Summary — A machina is a type of automaton. This particular little machina has a tiny core that programs it to hover close to its owner, and its wings are freeform shimmer it uses to flit around like a dragonfly (M03518, FreshToast, 2023-07-17). It serves as Locke Evertide’s portable light — a characterful alternative to a plain Lantern (M03517, FreshToast, 2023-07-17).

Description

  • Automaton type — “machina” is the name for a class of automaton in the setting (M03518, FreshToast, 2023-07-17).
  • The core — this particular machina is little and carries a tiny core that programs it to hover close to its owner (M03518).
  • Shimmer wings — its wings are freeform shimmer, which it uses to flit around like a dragonfly (M03518). This links it visually to the Shimmer Sails concept, which uses the same “freeform shimmer” material.

Rules / Effects

  • Serves as Locke’s portable light (M03517, FreshToast, 2023-07-17) — filling the same role as the Lantern but as a living-seeming companion object rather than a held tool.

Related systems

  • Lantern — the baseline dark-dungeon light system; the machina is the in-world, character-flavored version of it for Locke (cross-linked in 006 §Machina).
  • Shimmer Sails — shares the “freeform shimmer” wing material (M03516–M03518, FreshToast, 2023-07-17).
  • Later, the automaton/machina motif ties into character design: Pandora is written as being especially into ergonomic, supportive craft “like prosthetics and machina” (M04346, FreshToast, 2023-10-17; ledger row R23).

Design history

  • 2023-07-17 — FreshToast pitches the visual reference for Locke’s portable light: “this is the closest to what I’d like Locke’s portable light to be” (M03517), then defines it — a little machina with a tiny core that hovers close to its owner, freeform-shimmer dragonfly wings (M03518). No later revision found; the concept is recorded as CANON in 006.
