Equipment System (Tier · Quality · Rune)

CANONSkyward CindersMixed
Also known as: No Rarity System, Tier + Quality + Rune
First conceived 2023-02-11 (M01433, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-02-16 (M02100, FreshToast)

Equipment System (Tier · Quality · Rune) CANON

The three-axis model for all weapons and armour: Tier (material = base power), Quality (rarity = bonus bandwidth), and Runes (materia-like slots with trade-offs).

Summary — Equipment is defined by three orthogonal axes rather than a single “power level.” Tier is the material a piece is made of and sets its base statistics (Iron < Steel < … < Welkinite). Quality — renamed Bronze / Silver / Gold instead of common/rare — does not change base stats but is the item’s bandwidth for extra properties, special effects, and rune slots. Runes are optional materia-like inserts that each carry an upside and a downside, turning gear into an optimization puzzle. The guiding intent is that later gear should expand the player’s repertoire and function differently, not just show bigger numbers (M01433–M01439, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).

Description

The system began as a hard “no numbers go up” stance and evolved, within the same discussion, into the Tier + Quality + Rune model (see Design history). The design philosophy the group settled on:

  • Weapons and armour found in the world are unique and function differently rather than having “higher bigger numbers” (006 §Weapon and Armour Philosophy).
  • Later weapons should expand the player’s repertoire of skills, giving more choice and more interesting decisions (M01448, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-11).
  • The precious-metals / alchemy theming (“making lead into gold, but metaphorically”) is deliberate, tying gear to the game’s growth-through-work theme (M01440, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).

The three axes

Tier = weapon level — the material ladder; sets base power (M02096, FreshToast, 2023-02-16). See Weapons (Tiers, Types & Skills) for the full five-tier ladder, act gating, and drop rules.

Quality = weapon rarity — Bronze, Silver, Gold (M01441, FreshToast, 2023-02-11; M02094, 2023-02-16):

  • Quality won’t affect basic statistics but tends to come with additional effects. Example: a plain steel sword is Bronze; a steel sword with the “light” property (usable by high-finesse characters, with a special weapon skill attached) is a higher quality (M01434, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • Quality is the weapon’s bandwidth for bonuses — extra properties, special effects, and (higher quality) more rune slots (M01439, M01437, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • Rarity lets developers make loot with neat features drop less frequently and be more desirable — “fun to get new cool rare items” — so long as it isn’t just another name for the illusion of progression a levelling system provides (M01448, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-11).
  • Mythic is a 4th quality tier for unique fixed-stat weapons — see Mythic Weapons.

Runes — materia-like slots on weapons and armour, each with an upside and a downside. See Rune System.

Weapon archetypes this enables

Because power is expressed as difference rather than magnitude, weapons can occupy niches (M01448, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-11):

  • lower overall damage but higher crit; lower accuracy but higher overall damage;
  • terrible single-target damage but great for AoE;
  • cursed weapons — high damage/accuracy/crit but drain the user’s life or another negative effect;
  • blessed weapons — lower overall stats but heal for X;
  • poison weapons.

Anti-number-bloat guardrails

  • Enemies do not scale; later enemies are not given bigger numbers unnecessarily — fights get more complex/tactical instead (M02099, FreshToast, 2023-02-16; M01448, CrunchyConniptions).
  • If a levelling system is ever layered on, it must use linear over exponential scaling to limit number bloat and keep the tactical weight on equipment choice (M02100, FreshToast, 2023-02-16). Note the group’s stated order of operations: “We should make our game without leveling, then add leveling later on top of it” (006 §Equipment Progression).
  • Character stats are otherwise static — modification comes from equipment only (M00846, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-02).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Whether a levelling layer is added at all; if so, effects scale off level while health/base damage rise only a small amount (M02098, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • DEBATED Whether Mythic weapons are a single item or a whole category (006 §Unresolved Questions) — see Mythic Weapons.
  • Various Tier 2/3 material names still unnamed (006 §Unresolved Questions) — see Weapons (Tiers, Types & Skills).

Design history

  • 2023-02-11 (Part 6 opening) — original stance: “NO RARITY, TIER OR LEVEL UP SYSTEM WHATSOEVER with respect to characters, weapons, armour or anything else”; weapons/armour would be unique and function differently rather than have bigger numbers (000 Part 6; the “no rarity” bullet). EVOLVED
  • 2023-02-11 (same session) — supersedes the above. Developed by FreshToast into the Tier (material) + Quality (rarity) + Rune model: Tier = base strength, Quality = bonus bandwidth, Runes = tweaks (M01433–M01439, FreshToast). Quality renamed to Bronze/Silver/Gold (M01441). The “no illusion-of-progress numbers” intent survives — Quality/Runes add properties, not stat bloat — but the literal “no tier/rarity” stance no longer holds (006 §NO Rarity note).
  • 2023-02-16 (Part 8) — finalized: five-tier material ladder, act-based tier gating, drop rules, and the crisp mapping “Tier = weapon level; Quality = weapon rarity” (M02093–M02097, FreshToast). Codified as CANON in 008 §Equipment Progression.
