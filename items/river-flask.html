River Flask

CANONSkyward CindersProto-Implemented
Also known as: river heal, the river flask, estus flask (early framing)
First conceived 2023-10-13 (M03948, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-10-30 (M04803, CrunchyConniptions)

River Flask CANON · IMPLEMENTED

The strongest, most precious heal — a flask that drinks the river’s power and carries it into the deep dungeons, kept separate from your everyday healing.

Summary — The river flask is a flask that can channel the river’s power, giving a precious effect that lets the party go longer without resting (M04743, FreshToast, 2023-10-30). It is deliberately kept as a separate flask from the ordinary healing jet (M04803, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-30), works like the Estus/Embers relationship, and can be hoarded and used freely for important battles rather than being a per-rest limited resource (006 §River Healing Mechanic). In the notes it is ranked the strongest single heal — and one of the very limited ways to heal past the degrading-HP limit between rests (006 §Healing System).

Description

Any fresh water source will refill the flask, but the charge drawn from the river is especially good: the flask “gets the properties from the river” and retains them for a while (M03959–M03963, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-13). The river charge decays over time — “it needs recharging like every few months or something” — so over the course of a run the player will want to head back to the river, at a cadence that depends on their skill (M03949, FreshToast; M03964–M03966, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-10-13). No hard timer is imposed; a timer “would be annoying, like if you’re trying to explore” (M03968–M03970, 2023-10-13).

The mountain-provenance mystery

The river’s healing power is written as a lil mystery: it “seems to stem from some mountain feature and is similarly reflected in Fiore’s river” (M03950–M03951, FreshToast, 2023-10-13). The flask is tied to that mystery, and may gain a buff when the mystery is solved (M04793–M04795, FreshToast, 2023-10-30; 006 §River Healing Mechanic). DEBATED exactly when in the story the mystery is solved and the buff received (006 §Unresolved Questions).

Rules / Effects

  • Precious, party-affecting heal — the design goal is a heal that lets you go farther without resting; floated as a possible full-party heal that ignores the normal heal limit (M04743–M04745, FreshToast, 2023-10-30).
  • Refill — any fresh water source refills it; the river charge is superior and retained for a while (M03959–M03963, 2023-10-13).
  • Hoardable, not per-rest limited — “not a limited resource, can use freely whenever they intend”; players are meant to hoard it for important battles (006 §River Healing Mechanic).
  • Separate from the jet — kept as its own flask so the two heals “work slightly different — more immersive” (M04803, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-30; 006 §River Healing Mechanic).

Implemented values (proto) [IMPLEMENTED]

The vertical slice ships the flask with concrete numbers (006 §Implemented Jet/Flask Numbers; proto src/core/campaign/run.ts):

  • Starts at 1 charge (flask: 1).
  • Heals 35% of base max HP to the entire party (FLASK_HEAL_RATIO = 0.35, useFlask).
  • Its strength in code is being party-wide and hoardable, not per-target size (a single jet heals more per target at 60%).

Open questions

  • DEBATED “Heals past the limit.” The notes’ intent is that the river heal is one of very limited ways to heal past the degrading-HP limit between rests (006 §Healing System). The implemented flask does not yet do this — useFlask clamps every character to their current ceiling exactly like the jet (proto run.ts). As coded, only resting lifts the ceiling. Flagged for reconciliation (006 §River Healing Mechanic).
  • DEBATED When the river mystery is solved and the buff is received (006 §Unresolved Questions).
  • DEBATED “Strongest heal” framing — notes rank the river heal strongest, but per-target the proto jet heals more; unreconciled (006 §Implemented Jet/Flask Numbers).

Design history

The mechanic was hammered out live across two sessions; latest version is the “separate flask, hoardable, tied to the river mystery” model above.

  • 2023-10-13 — FreshToast’s compromise: in the first game people get a healing item from the river; it needs recharging “like every few months or something”; its power stems from a mountain feature reflected in Fiore’s river — a lil mystery (M03945–M03951, FreshToast). Any fresh water refills it, but the river charge is best and is retained a while (M03959–M03963).
  • 2023-10-30 — FreshToast’s proposal: “you get some kinda flask that can channel the river’s power” providing a precious effect that lets you go longer without resting; weaker players make more regular river visits; could be a full-party heal that ignores the heal limit (M04743–M04745, FreshToast). This is the current core concept.
  • 2023-10-30 — Crunchy’s competing proposal (superseded): the river would just give a flat 2 extra flasks (M04761, CrunchyConniptions). FreshToast pushed back: “I don’t really like the idea of bloating the flask number to achieve that purpose but the idea is sound enough” (M04765). superseded by the combined solution.
  • 2023-10-30 — Combined solution: have the normal healing item improve over time, and have a separate river item heal a fixed amount from the start — keep it separate, “all upside,” tied to the mystery of the river (M04767, FreshToast; M04803, CrunchyConniptions). Confirmed as: river healing is a separate flask from main healing, “works like Estus & Embers” (006 §Critical Design Decisions). This is the current model.
  • 2026-06-16 — Proto reconciliation: implemented at 1 starting charge, 35% party-wide heal, currently ceiling-clamped (does not yet pierce the limit) (006 §Implemented Jet/Flask Numbers).
