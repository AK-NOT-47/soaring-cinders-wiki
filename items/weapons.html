Weapons (Tiers, Types & Skills) CANON

The material tier ladder, the categorized weapon roster, and the built-in skills each weapon type carries.

Summary — Weapons sit on a five-tier material ladder (Iron/Ash up to Titanstone/Welkinite) where the tier sets base statistics, gated by act so power creep is paced. The roster is grouped into Melee, Ranged, and Magic families, and each weapon type carries built-in skills (Slash, Pierce, Snipe, Wild Swing, etc.), several of which use the game’s positional and overkill mechanics. Tier is only one of three axes — see Equipment System (Tier · Quality · Rune) for how Quality and Rune System layer on top.

Tier ladder (material) CANON

Five tiers, tiered by material like Skyrim — “an orichalcum sword will outperform a steel sword”; the material only sets base statistics (M01433, FreshToast, 2023-02-11). Some tiers hold two similar-but-different variants for variety. The finalized ladder (M02093, FreshToast, 2023-02-16):

  • Tier 1 — Iron / Ash — weakest in all regards.
  • Tier 2 — Steel / Yew — well-rounded, direct upgrade to Iron/Ash.
    • [H material] variant — lower base damage than Steel/Yew but larger range.
    • [H material] variant — less damage than Steel/Yew but improved critical-hit chance.
  • Tier 3 — Ebonsteel / [J material] (second name TBD).
  • Tier 4 — Orichalcum / Bruniglas — Bruniglas is dragon glass: an obsidian-like material formed from stone melted by a dragon’s breath and re-cooled (alt name floated: Hyrrglas) (M02090–M02091, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Tier 5 — Titanstone / Welkinite.

Earlier informal name: Windsteel — “slightly lower damage than Orichalcum but higher crit chance” (M01433, FreshToast, 2023-02-11). In the finalized Part 8 ladder the crit-variant role is filled by the unnamed Tier 2 [H material] variant; “Windsteel” is the earlier, informal label. See Design history.

Tier distribution by act CANON

Tiers are gated by story progression (M02093, FreshToast, 2023-02-16):

Act First half Second half
Act 1 Tiers 1 & 2 Tier 2 only
Act 2 Tiers 2 & 3 Tiers 3 & 4
Act 3 Tiers 4 & 5 Tier 5 only

Drop rules CANON

When the player has access to two tiers, the lower tier takes priority and drops more often; the higher available tier appears infrequently. Gear is not available for purchase, and gear will never drop at Gold quality — Gold must be earned/found, not bought (M02093, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).

Weapon roster CANON

Full categorized roster (M02161, FreshToast, 2023-02-16):

Melee — Axe · Hammer · Spear · Sword Ranged — Shortbow · Longbow · Crossbow Magic — Staff · Orb

Notes on association and behavior:

  • Spear — Thane’s weapon; also Locke’s, once she takes up the Sunderer (006 §Weapon Types).
  • Bow — associated with Sable (006 §Weapon Types).
  • Staff — associated with Isaac / Percival; sits in a more back position (006 §Weapon Types).
  • Orb — sits in a more front position; “THE ORB IS USED TO BONK AND TELEPORT WITH MAGIC WIND” (M02162, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).

Weapon skills CANON

Weapons carry built-in skills tied to their type, several using positional / overkill mechanics (M02157–M02160, FreshToast, 2023-02-16):

Sword

  • Slash — single-target front attack; deals half of any overkill damage to the next enemy behind.
  • Pierce — single-target attack, front rank; low damage but halves the enemy’s damage resistance.

Axe

  • Slash — single-target melee; deals half overkill damage to the next enemy behind.
  • Wild Swing — Cleave attack that hits the two front enemies.

Staff

  • Magic attack against any target.
  • Modular AoE skill that damages both front or both back.

Longbow

  • Ranged attack against any target.
  • Snipe — ranged attack, back rank only; high damage.

Keyword — Cleave: causes a skill to hit all targets rather than one (M02154, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).

Positioning and rank-targeting these skills rely on (the 2×3 grid, front/back ranks) are owned by the systems batch; the skills above are the equipment-facing surface of that system.

Design history

  • 2023-02-11 — first tier concept: material-based tiers à la Skyrim (Steel, Orichalcum, informal Windsteel = lower damage/higher crit) (M01433, FreshToast). EVOLVED
  • 2023-02-16 (Part 8) — supersedes the above. Finalized as the five-tier ladder (Iron/Ash → Welkinite), with the crit variant demoted to an unnamed Tier 2 [H] slot and Windsteel dropped; act distribution and drop rules added (M02093, FreshToast). Weapon roster and per-type skills defined the same day (M02154–M02162).
