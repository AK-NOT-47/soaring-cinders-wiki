Conflux CANON

Percival’s made arm — a crafted/prosthetic arm built for the main-game mage, with Pandora as the inventor who helps him make it.

Summary — Conflux is “his arm” — a made arm belonging to Percival (Percy), the renamed Isaac and main-game party mage. The one canonical mechanical fact about it is its origin: Pandora “is also gonna be the person who helps Percival make Conflux (his arm)” (M04343, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). Beyond being a made/crafted arm and a piece of Pandora’s inventor résumé, the notes give Conflux little further detail.

Description

  • Conflux is Percival’s arm — described consistently in the notes as something made, i.e. a crafted or prosthetic arm rather than his natural limb (M04343, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • It sits within Pandora’s wheelhouse of “ergonomic, supportive stuff like prosthetics and machina” (M04346, FreshToast, 2023-10-17) — the same inventor sensibility that produced her cannon Rainfall and automaton Dianthus.

Origin / Related characters

  • Maker: Pandora helps Percival make Conflux (M04343, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). This is her explicit “role in Skyward Cinders.”
  • Earlier earmark: the “person who helped Percy make Conflux” existed as a role slot before Pandora filled it. On 2023-08-12, FreshToast earmarked the celebrated “Sha’worr-ma is a contradiction personified…” description for reuse on that person — “Crunchy I like this part and am gonna steal it for the person that helped Percy make Conflux” (M03535, FreshToast, 2023-08-12). See Sha’worr-ma. The two threads were never explicitly reconciled — Pandora was later named the maker, but whether the Sha’worr-ma description folds into her or a separate helper is left open.

Open questions

  • DEBATED Is the Conflux-maker (and its “contradiction personified” description) Pandora, Sha’worr-ma, or a distinct helper? Pandora was named the maker (M04343) after the Sha’worr-ma earmark (M03535); the reuse target and Pandora were never explicitly reconciled (002 §Sha’worr-ma the Second PROPOSED).
  • PROPOSED What Conflux actually does — its mechanics, magical function, and appearance — is not stated in the notes.

Design history

  • 2023-08-12 — first mention of “Conflux (Percy’s arm)”: FreshToast earmarks the Sha’worr-ma “contradiction personified” paragraph for “the person that helped Percy make Conflux” — “Steal away. I am the flinger, you are the catcher” (M03535, M03538–M03540, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions). The arm exists as a fixture; its maker is an open slot.
  • 2023-10-17 — the Conflux-maker is identified: Pandora “is also gonna be the person who helps Percival make Conflux (his arm)” (M04343, FreshToast). Fills the slot left open in August.
