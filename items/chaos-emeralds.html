Chaos Emeralds (Ajax’s Seven Relics) CANON

Seven super-rare relics scattered across the world — shards of something Ajax once owned. Collecting them all doesn’t grant power; it makes you powerful, and lets you inherit Ajax’s legacy.

Summary — There are seven unique relics scattered in the game, super special and super rare, referred to as the “chaos emeralds” (M01280, FreshToast, 2023-02-09; M01422, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-11; ledger row R08). They are pieces of an object owned by Ajax that was shattered, related to the spirit he made a pact with (M02420, FreshToast, 2023-02-19). Brought together they do not grant superpowers — instead they give “some kind of revelation” and are the way the player learns about Ajax and his bloodline (M02431–M02432, FreshToast, 2023-02-19). Once all seven are acquired the bearer will “inherit Ajax’s legacy” (M02907, FreshToast, 2023-03-06). Each unique relic has a different interaction with each unique character (M01422, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-11). The name is a Sonic reference and is treated as a working label — hence placeholder_name: true.

Description

  • Seven, super-rare — a handful of unique relics scattered in the game, super special/super rare — “7 relics? Like the Chaos emeralds we discussed!” (M01422, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-11).
  • Shards of Ajax’s pact-object — pieces of an object owned by Ajax that was shattered, related to the spirit he made a pact with (M02420, FreshToast, 2023-02-19).
  • Per-character interaction — each unique relic interacts differently with each unique character (M01422, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-11).
  • The first relic is the Sunderer — “First of Ajax’s seven thingies,” the rusty glimmering spear Locke acquires in the tutorial trial (006 §Specific Weapons; proto-implemented as the prologue relic reward).

Rules / Effects — what collecting them does

  • Brought together, the relics don’t grant superpowers — they give “some kind of revelation” (M02431, FreshToast, 2023-02-19).
  • They are the way the player learns about Ajax and his bloodline (M02432, FreshToast, 2023-02-19).
  • “Inherit Ajax’s legacy” — the seven relics are informal trials; once all are acquired the bearer inherits Ajax’s legacy (M02907, FreshToast, 2023-03-06). People misinterpret this as acquiring Ajax’s power; in truth the act of acquiring makes one powerful, and the legacy is a final task to challenge the descendant (006 §Ajax’s Relics).
  • Not garbage — must have purpose — the group explicitly resolved the relics should be actually valuable and sentimental to Ajax, tied to “his whole deal,” not treated as loot filler (M02426–M02429, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-02-19).
  • May lead to his tomb — floated that the relics lead the player to Ajax’s Tomb (M02718, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).

The “deliberate dud” DEBATED

FreshToast recalled the seven chaos emeralds “were meant to be somewhat of a dud in the end if I recall” (M01425, FreshToast, 2023-02-11). This is the deliberate-dud intent: the emeralds’ payoff is intentionally anticlimactic as a reward — the point is the journey and the revelation about Ajax, not a power spike. This aligns with the story-side motivation chain where Locke gathers them to prove herself / keep them from Lysander (see the story doc’s Chaos Emerald Motivations); the emeralds are a plot engine, not an endgame superweapon. Recorded here as a cross-reference to the story-side treatment; the exact nature of the “dud” payoff remains unsettled.

Story hooks

  • Motivation chain (story-owned; summarized): Locke meets Lysander → wants to get stronger to support him → overhears Lysander and Eris planning to gather the emeralds to defeat the dragon → decides to gather them herself to prove worthy → finally keeps them away from Lysander. Her motive shifts from hero-pleasing to people-saving — she does not turn selfish/evil (M02419–M02425, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-19; ledger row R13).
  • Prologue tie-in — Lysander enters the prologue dungeon looking for a chaos emerald but doesn’t find it (he “misread the clue”); the initial dungeon gains a secret endgame section where the rumored emerald is later found (M02445–M02448, FreshToast/spirit_noodle, 2023-02-19).
  • Family history — a relic can inform Locke of family history and key info “like where a chaos emerald is” (M02809, FreshToast, 2023-02-26).

Open questions

  • DEBATED The exact nature of the “deliberate dud” payoff when all seven are gathered (M01425, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • placeholder_name — “chaos emeralds” is a Sonic-derived working label; a final in-world name for the relic set was not settled in these notes.

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — first appearance: Lyra the Necromancer “has one of the seven chaos emeralds” (M01280, FreshToast) — the seven-relic set exists as a hook.
  • 2023-02-11 — framed as a handful of super-rare unique relics, “7 relics? Like the Chaos emeralds,” each with a different per-character interaction (M01422, spirit_noodle). Same day, FreshToast notes they “were meant to be somewhat of a dud in the end” (M01425). EVOLVED toward a plot device rather than a reward.
  • 2023-02-19 — the design crystallizes: the group insists the relics be valuable and sentimental to Ajax, not garbage (M02426–M02429); defined as shards of Ajax’s pact-object (M02420) that grant a revelation, not superpowers (M02431) and teach the player about Ajax’s bloodline (M02432). Motivation chain and prologue secret-section established the same day (M02419–M02448). supersedes the loose “collect the gems” framing.
  • 2023-02-24 — floated that the relics lead to Ajax’s tomb (M02718, FreshToast).
  • 2023-03-06 — the “inherit Ajax’s legacy” resolution: seven relics are informal trials; the acquiring makes one powerful; the legacy is a final task to challenge the descendant (M02907, FreshToast). current framing.
