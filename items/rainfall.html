Rainfall CANON

Pandora’s cannon — a magical outlet that lets her use her immense-but-uncontrollable capacity safely, “kinda like a prosthetic for a disability.”

Summary — Rainfall is a cannon built by Pandora, the inventor of the 4 Boyes. It is one of the “things” she makes — “magic bombs, her cannon ‘Rainfall’, or the automaton ‘Dianthus’” (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). Its narrative purpose is tied to Pandora’s core quirk: she has immense Flow capacity but channels it inefficiently, tiring fast and casting slow. Rainfall gives her “a highly controlled magical outlet… a safe way to use her capacity” (M04350–M04351, FreshToast, 2023-10-17) — FreshToast’s own gloss: “kinda like a prosthetic for a disability” (M04352).

Description

  • A cannon — one of Pandora’s signature creations, alongside magic bombs and her automaton Dianthus (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Named, like all Pandora’s creations, after a smell she likes — she “names the stuff she makes after smells she likes” (M04227).

Rules / Effects

  • Controlled magical outlet — Rainfall gives Pandora “a highly controlled magical outlet… a safe way to use her capacity” (M04350–M04351, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). It is her answer to a genuine weakness rather than raw firepower.
  • Function framing — FreshToast recasts it as “kinda like a prosthetic for a disability” (M04352, FreshToast, 2023-10-17): it compensates for her lack of control, letting her deploy big magic without the fatigue and slow-cast penalties of channeling the Flow directly.

Related

  • Answers Pandora’s quirk: immense Flow capacity but inefficient channeling — she “gets tired fast and casts slow,” a danger to herself and everyone around her; Rainfall is “sorta that” solution (M04227, M04347–M04350, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Sits beside her other inventions — the automaton Dianthus and, in Skyward Cinders, Percival’s made arm Conflux — as part of her “ergonomic, supportive” inventor sensibility (“prosthetics and machina”, M04346).

Design history

  • 2023-10-17 — introduced in the 4 Boyes roster write-up as one of Pandora’s creations: “magic bombs, her cannon ‘Rainfall’, or the automaton ‘Dianthus’,” named after smells she likes (M04227, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-17 — purpose clarified while settling Pandora’s joining motive: with her lack of control and ease of fatigue, “Rainfall is sorta that. It gives her a highly controlled magical outlet… a safe way to use her capacity… (kinda like a prosthetic for a disability, I’m now realising)” (M04350–M04352, FreshToast). Refines the outlet into a named coping mechanic for her quirk.
