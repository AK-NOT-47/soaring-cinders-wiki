Dianthus

CANONCaelian OdysseyNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-10-17 (M04227, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-10-17 (M04319, FreshToast)

Dianthus CANON

Pandora’s automaton — one of her signature creations, which “eventually attains sentience” and grows close enough to her that “they maybe kiss a lil UwU.”

Summary — Dianthus is an automaton built by Pandora, the inventor of the 4 Boyes. It appears in the roster write-up as one of the “things” she makes — “magic bombs, her cannon ‘Rainfall’, or the automaton ‘Dianthus’” (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). Its distinguishing beat: Dianthus “eventually attains sentience” and its bond with Pandora turns tender — “and they maybe kiss a lil UwU” (M04227). Because of that arc it is a character-adjacent creation, but in the notes it originates and is catalogued as one of Pandora’s inventions.

Description

  • An automaton made by Pandora, named — like all her creations — after a smell she likes (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Part of Pandora’s “ergonomic, supportive stuff like prosthetics and machina” inventor sensibility (M04346, FreshToast, 2023-10-17); a companion-scale creation rather than a weapon like her cannon Rainfall.

Sentience & relationship

  • Dianthus eventually attains sentience, and its relationship with Pandora becomes romantic-adjacent — “and they maybe kiss a lil UwU” (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). This is the note’s phrasing; the “maybe” marks it as a floated idea rather than a locked beat.
  • Because Pandora already has Dianthus as a companion-creation, a separate “magical pet” pitch was routed to another party member instead: FreshToast noted “We have Dianthus already but I think it maybe could be cool if the demi-human got something that’s supposed to be dangerous but is surprisingly loyal and docile” (M04319, FreshToast, 2023-10-17) — see Titan and Demi-Humans.

Open questions

  • PROPOSED The sentience/romance beat is hedged (“maybe kiss a lil UwU”); whether Dianthus’s arc resolves into a genuine relationship is unsettled (M04227).
  • PROPOSED Dianthus’s form, abilities and combat/utility role are not specified in the notes beyond “automaton.”

Design history

  • 2023-10-17 — introduced in the 4 Boyes roster write-up as one of Pandora’s creations — “the automaton ‘Dianthus’” — with the note that it “eventually attains sentience and they maybe kiss a lil UwU” (M04227, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-17 — treated as an established fixture when a new “magical pet” idea comes up: “We have Dianthus already…” so the dangerous-but-docile creature is offered to the demi-human instead (M04319, FreshToast). Confirms Dianthus is already Pandora’s companion-creation.
