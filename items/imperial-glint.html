Imperial Glint

Caelian OdysseyNotes / Design
Also known as: Glint, Crescent, Sovereign, Half-Glint, Sharp, Bit
First conceived undated

The primary legal currency of Khorath, minted in Lycosia.

Summary - Imperial glint is the primary legal currency used in Khorath and is minted in Lycosia, its place of origin (Submitted item notes section Imperial Glint, undated). The currency uses glint and crescent units: crescents are valued at a 100:1 ratio to glint. Its coinage is backed by the gold and silver content of Sovereign, Glint, and Half-Glint coins, alloyed with tougher metals like copper for durability and metal-value control.

Glint and crescents

Crescents are valued at a 100:1 ratio to glint. The value of the currency is backed by precious-metal coinage: Sovereigns, Glints, and Half-Glints. All gold and silver used in imperial coinage is alloyed with tougher metals such as copper to increase durability and manipulate raw metal value.

Coin features

  • Legend: each coin’s obverse edge states its denomination and minting year; the reverse legend carries the motto “One with all”.
  • Edge: recent glint coins have reeded edges for ease of handling. Some traders can identify forgeries by the sound of a nail running along the edge.
  • Relief: all imperial coinage bears a variant of the four-pointed imperial star on the obverse. Some generations add further embellishments for major events such as a new emperor or empress.

Coin notes

  • Sovereign: the largest and highest-value coin in circulation on Khorath. It is bimetallic, with a gold core and silver edge. Its reverse depicts Lycosia’s current ruler.
  • Glint: the iconic 1-glint gold coin. A trade ship appears on the reverse. The currency and coin are named for the imperial star relief on all imperial coins.
  • Half-Glint: a silver 50-crescent coin, identical to a glint coin except for its material. Argentist communities tend to circulate many of them for superstitious reasons.
  • Banesilver: Reckoners forge a variant of the Half-Glint from fine, unalloyed silver to trick Accursed into revealing themselves.
  • Sharp: the larger copper coin, named for the three crossed swords on its reverse. The swords represent the founder of the empire and his two most trusted allies.
  • Bit: the smallest and least valuable coin, a copper piece with iris flowers on the reverse.

Glint coin sound

Glint coins are renowned for the loud singing sound they make when pinged or flipped into the air. This makes them popular in gambling houses, and different generations may sound slightly different.

First-generation glints

The first generation of glint coins were made from unalloyed fine gold. They are now status symbols: originally awarded to prominent figures and nobility who proved loyalty to the empire, most have since been recycled for their gold, leaving only a few intact.
