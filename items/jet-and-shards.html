The Jet & Shard System CANON

A single reusable item — the Jet — whose effect is reconfigured by slotting Shards. Ships as the party’s primary, customizable heal.

Summary — The Jet is a reusable item (the game’s Estus-flask analogue) that starts slotted with the Renewal healing shard but can be re-tuned by swapping Shards into it. Shards come in six families — Anodyne (healing), Shape (physical defense), Dream (magical defense), Balance (trade-offs), Acuity (speed/movement), and Fracture (modifiers) — and two shards of the same [type] are mutually incompatible. This makes the single primary heal a customizable build slot rather than a fixed potion. FreshToast’s own verdict: “This is my first go at it. There’s room for more if you think of anything”; CrunchyConniptions: “This is gold, I like 👍” (000 §System Notes).

How it works

  • Shards are slotted into the reusable Jet to determine its effects (M03685, FreshToast, 2023-09-26).
  • Shards that share a [type] are incompatible with one another — you pick one per family (M03686, FreshToast, 2023-09-26).
  • The Jet comes with the Renewal shard pre-slotted (M03684, FreshToast).
  • The Jet is the primary heal in the game’s healing hierarchy — “precious, versatile and customizable” — sitting above consumables and Locke’s heal skills but below the river flask (006 §Healing System). See the Healing System (systems batch) and River Flask (items-b) for the full hierarchy.

Shard roster CANON

Full roster, all from a single message (M03684, FreshToast, 2023-09-26):

Anodyne — Healing

  • Renewal — instant heal. (Jet ships with this slotted.)
  • Tenacity — heal over time; total health recovered exceeds Renewal.
  • Sanctuary — temporary barrier; functions like temporary bonus health.

Shape — Physical Defense

  • Rend — boosts physical defense; brief slash resistance.
  • Puncture — boosts physical defense; brief pierce resistance.
  • Crush — boosts physical defense; brief impact resistance.

Dream — Magical Defense

  • Ember — boosts magical defense; brief burn resistance.
  • Frost — boosts magical defense; brief freeze resistance.
  • Spark — boosts magical defense; brief shock resistance.

Balance — Trade-offs

  • Ferocity — boosts attack power but reduces defenses until next turn.
  • Serenity — boosts defenses but reduces attack power until next turn.
  • Confidence — guarantee critical hit but reduce accuracy until next turn.
  • Providence — guarantee hit but nullify critical chance until next turn.

Acuity — Speed / Movement

  • Zeal — boosts initiative.
  • Rush — move freely until next turn.

Fracture — Modifiers

  • Emphasis — reduces charges by 1; other effects are strengthened.
  • Divergence — reduces charges by 1; other effects are weakened but may now affect the entire party.

Open questions

  • DEBATED Name collision on “Zeal.” The Acuity shard Zeal shares its name with the favoured (but un-finalized) proposed rename of the AP / Advantage Point resource (Part 11). The AP→“Zeal” rename was never locked and clashes with this shard (Part 14); until resolved the shard keeps “Zeal” and the resource stays “AP” (006 §Acuity Shards note). AP itself is owned by the systems batch.

Implementation note (proto)

The Jet is realized in the proto as the healing economy’s primary item, though the notes’ shard customization layer is design-intent-facing while the proto pins concrete heal numbers (jets start at 2, cap at 5; each heals 60% of base max HP). Those implemented healing values and the degrading-HP/rest economy are documented under the Healing System (systems batch) and 006 §Implemented Jet/Flask Numbers CANON · IMPLEMENTED; the River Flask (items-b) is the separate, stronger, hoardable heal.
