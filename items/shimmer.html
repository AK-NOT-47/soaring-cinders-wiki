Shimmer

Caelian OdysseyNotes / Design
Also known as: Shimmer barrier, Freeform shimmer
First conceived undated

A light, fluid, fragile magical substance used for barriers, architecture, transport, and anti-magic protection.

Summary - Shimmer is described in the submitted notes as non-Newtonian and as a foundational element of shimmerglyph sorcery (Submitted item notes section Shimmer, undated). It can form protective barriers, temporary bridges, selective architectural gates, shimmer sails, and the anti-magic shields used by wards. It is poor as a blade, bludgeon, or armour because it is light, fluid, and prone to shattering.

Sorcery

Temporarily converting pneuma into shimmer is the foundational element of shimmerglyph sorcery. The note cuts off before defining what a skilled sorcerer can fully create, so the exact upper limit remains open.

Architecture

The most common use for shimmer is a simple protective barrier. Barriers range from window-sized panes to translucent domes that encase entire structures. Larger barriers require more fuel to maintain, so very large barriers are usually emergency measures for large-scale attack.

Shimmer can also be used to create temporary bridges with only a few skilled workers. These bridges are accident-prone because they can be magically disabled and lack the sturdiness of ordinary wooden or stone crossings. As a result, shimmer bridges are rare, small, and suited only to light loads.

More advanced architecture can use shimmer in place of windows, doors, or walkways. Selective shimmer barriers can allow a key-like object or designated people to pass while blocking everything else. The submitted note lists the Galdrim and Astrum’s imperial palace as examples of locations that integrate shimmer into architecture.

Transport

In addition to wind, shimmer sails can catch ethereal currents for added lift and speed.

Protection

Many attempts have been made to integrate shimmer into arms and armour, with little success. Shimmer can retain a shape through enchantment, but its light weight makes it poor for blades and bludgeons. It is also too light, fluid, and fragile for reliable armour.

Its successful combat niche is as a specialized shield against magical attack: the shimmer barrier used by wards.
