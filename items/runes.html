Rune System

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-02-11 (M01435, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-02-11 (M01439, FreshToast)

Rune System CANON

Materia-like slots on weapons and armour; every rune carries an upside and a downside, so building is optimization, not best-in-slot.

Summary — Runes are optional inserts that slot into weapon and armour rune slots (like materia). The defining rule is that all runes have both an upside and a downside, so choosing runes is a matter of what to sacrifice rather than simply slotting the strongest option. Because a base weapon with no rune is still fine (if sub-optimal), runes stay optional and add build depth that grows as the player finds more of them (M01435–M01437, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).

Rules / Effects

  • Slots — weapons and armour can carry rune slots (M01435, FreshToast, 2023-02-11). This should all apply to armour as well (M01438, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • Salvage — if a weapon drops with a rune already slotted but the weapon itself is bad, the player can dismantle that weapon for its rune (M01435, FreshToast).
  • Two-sided by design — every rune has an upside and a downside, making it “a matter of optimisation and not just slotting the best runes” — a choice of what to sacrifice (M01435–M01436, FreshToast).
  • Optional — since a downside-free base weapon still works, runes are optional; they let the player tweak a weapon to their preferences (M01436, M01439, FreshToast). In FreshToast’s words: “It also makes runes kinda optional since a base weapon with no downside will still be fine, if sub-optimal” (M01436, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • Slot count scales — higher Quality and Tier weapons might have more rune slots (M01437, FreshToast).
  • Described as a material-like system with upsides and downsides that adds a fun layer of complexity as you find more and build your own kit (006 §Rune System).

Rune examples

Illustrative runes, each showing the upside/downside pattern (M01435, FreshToast, 2023-02-11):

  • Chance to poison on hit, but reduces base damage.
  • Increases base damage, but reduces crit chance.
  • Increases base damage, but makes the wielder take more damage in return.
