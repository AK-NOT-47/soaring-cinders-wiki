Considered & Dropped (Name Brainstorms and Alternates) SCRAPPED

A traceability record of name brainstorms and alternate ideas that were floated in the Discord chat but were not kept or never finalized.

Summary — This page transcribes the 009 “Considered & Dropped” provenance appendix. It preserves material that survives only in the raw Discord archive: discarded name brainstorms, alternate spellings, and abandoned framings. None of the following is canon — every entry is a discarded or alternate idea unless it was adopted elsewhere (in which case it lives on that entity’s own page). Pure joke entries from the same brainstorms are omitted. Each entry cites its raw message id(s) (009 §Appendix — Considered & Dropped).

Alt spelling for Drykksar — “Sardrykkr”

Listed alongside “Drykksar” as candidate spellings; Drykksar was kept (the condition/parasite causing Dracosis). (raw M02695, FreshToast, 2023-02-22)

Thorne “represent two groups” — mixed-lineage rationale

On whether Thorne should specifically be a cat-man, the answer was “just mixed” / “He should represent two groups”; non-cat options floated were gerbil and (theoretically) squid. (raw M03405–M03409, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-06-04)

Death-pictured-as-a-child alternative

An early alternate framing — Death was at first perceived “as a child, partly because of an inherent thought… that thinks they must have poor judgement skills” — but the other approach was preferred (“but i dig this approach too”). (raw M00932–M00933, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-05)

Upload-to-superintelligence ending (Project Magitech)

A proposed ending for the magic-humans-vs-AI conflict — “maybe you upload your intelligence and become a superintelligence.” Floated for the separate Project Magitech concept. (raw M03244–M03246, spirit_noodle, 2023-05-20)
