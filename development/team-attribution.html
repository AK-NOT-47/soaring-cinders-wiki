Team Attribution

Caelian OdysseyNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-10-13

The record of who created what, as stated by FreshToast during the 2023-10-13 team argument: Caelian Odyssey is a confluence of the team’s ideas collated by FreshToast, with CrunchyConniptions having concepted 6 of the 7 Odyssey main characters and chosen the core classic-fantasy aesthetic.

Summary — On 2023-10-13, during a heated exchange over creative ownership and design cohesion, FreshToast laid out an explicit attribution of who contributed what to Caelian Odyssey (2023-10-13). The key facts: of the 7 Odyssey main characters, CrunchyConniptions concepted 6; the core classic-fantasy aesthetic was CrunchyConniptions’s choice; and the overall project is “a confluence of our ideas, collated by [FreshToast],” who states his sole creation “would not even resemble what we have discussed so far”. This attribution is drawn directly from the raw chat record and is absent from the compiled notes and the 00X docs by design — it comes from an interpersonal dispute rather than a design section, so it is preserved here as the canonical authorship record.

Note on tone. These lines are excerpted from an argument. They are quoted faithfully as the record; the frustration in them is part of the source (see [Style Guide §Honesty rules]).

Authorship (2023-10-13)

  • 6 of 7 Odyssey mains are Crunchy’s concepts. “You concept 6 of our 7 main characters in Odyssey” (2023-10-13).
  • The core classic-fantasy aesthetic is Crunchy’s choice. “The core classic fantasy aesthetic was your choice” (2023-10-13).
  • FreshToast is the collator/producer of the setting and narrative. He describes spending “an evening redefining half of my vault and notes because you wanted to change the magic system”, and asks “why on earth am I going to produce a setting and narrative for us?” — noting “TWO narratives now, in fact” (2023-10-13).
  • Major decisions were jointly confirmed. “I have confirmed every major decision along the way with you MANY [times]…” — noting Crunchy’s “casual disinterest,” “which is fine but you CHOSE not to contribute to it” (2023-10-13).
  • Some elements were added on Crunchy’s request. “You asked me JUST NOW to name this river you’re so in love with for you”; “Ruby arbitrarily has a hammer now because you asked” (2023-10-13).
  • The project is a collaboration collated by FreshToast. “It is a confluence of our ideas, collated by me. My sole creation would not even resemble what we have discussed so far” (2023-10-13).

Context

These statements come from a 2023-10-13 argument that began over whether the river (Caelia’s Breath) needed a dedicated mechanic. The dispute broadened into questions of design cohesion and whose ideas the game embodies (2023-10-13). The exchange later cools with “I think there is a lot of miscommunication happening here” (2023-10-13) and FreshToast affirming “I will try to make what you want work and I like the idea too but it must be cohesive” (2023-10-13).

The three-person team throughout is CrunchyConniptions, FreshToast, and spirit_noodle.

Open questions

  • The identity of the 1 of 7 Odyssey mains not concepted by CrunchyConniptions is not stated in these messages. — undetermined from the recorded exchange alone.

Design history

  • 2023-10-13 — during the river-mechanic argument, FreshToast states the authorship split: Crunchy concepted 6 of 7 Odyssey mains, chose the core classic-fantasy aesthetic; the project is a confluence of the team’s ideas collated by FreshToast. This is the sole and current attribution statement.
