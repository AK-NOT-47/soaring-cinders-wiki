Art & Animation Direction CANON

The team’s agreed art-and-animation pipeline for the JRPG: simple 3D/sprite models paired with gorgeous 2D portraits, with work split across specialised artists to minimise animation load.

Summary — The overarching art goal is to minimise the need for animation and split work across specialised artists (M01351, M01355, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). The pipeline is structured by where each asset is used: simple animated chibi sprites for the overworld and roaming monsters, portrait art drawn from a portion of each character’s key art (the “PMD portrait” idea), simplified stretchy key-art with Darkest-Dungeon-style splash frames in combat, and overworld-sprite cutscenes with occasional full-art visuals. The guiding line is Crunchy’s: “simple game models with gorgeous portraits work well” (M01359, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09). This section also folds in the earlier (2021) souls-like-era visual identity work — the Lambent Corporation logo, the Leora emblem, and the first colour palette — which predate the JRPG but established the team’s design instincts.

The art-and-animation pipeline (Caelian Odyssey / Skyward Cinders)

Agreed 2023-02-09; the overarching goal is to minimise animation and split work among specialised artists (M01351, M01355, FreshToast) (000 Part 5 §Art & Animation Direction).

Overworld. Out-of-combat sprites should be simple, easily animated chibis; the same chibis are reused for roaming monsters (M01344, M01353, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Portraits / conversation. For conversation, use a portion of the character’s key art as the portrait, with expression variants. FreshToast: “The portrait idea I took from PMD” (Pokemon Mystery Dungeon) (M01345, M01358, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Combat. A simplified version of the key art with stretchy animation; skills are splash frames, “like Darkest Dungeon” (M01346, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). PROPOSED Maybe really flashy skill frames, “Like kill bill/borderlands character intros” (M01349, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Cutscenes. Use the overworld sprites with the occasional full-art visual, “like PMD:EoS” (Explorers of Sky). Pokemon as a franchise “basically does semi-narrated overworld cutscenes” (M01348, M01357, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Art-team division. Split work among specialised artists — a background/full-visual artist, a character artist for the key character art, and a chibi character artist (M01355, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

References. Ni no Kuni does the model/portrait split (M01347, CrunchyConniptions), Fire Emblem Awakening does this (M01354, CrunchyConniptions), and “New pokemon does roaming lil dudes instead of grass these days” (M01356, FreshToast) (all 2023-02-09).

Guiding line. “I think simple game models with gorgeous portraits work well” (M01359, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).

Related aesthetic notes from later parts: film-style black bars for cutscenes, icons instead of full art for weapons/armor, 4-directional sprites, and the paladin’s winged bucket helm (000 Part 7 §Aesthetic Details).

Lambent Corporation logo (souls-like era)

FreshToast concepted a logo for the Lambent Corporation on 2021-08-02, from the early souls-like project (M00559, FreshToast, 2021-08-02). Design intent:

  • Simple enough to be understood “at a distance or a glance,” and not colour dependent so it can be reused in many places — e.g. as a glowing icon in the light of a boss’s assigned colour (M00559, FreshToast, 2021-08-02).
  • Somewhat villainous: angular shapes meant to emphasise “the angular, faceted nature of Lucite” (a crystal-like being/thing) (M00559, FreshToast, 2021-08-02).
  • “A little uneven and jagged, to try and lowkey get across that it can’t be controlled,” because “sharp means danger in most people’s minds” (M00560, M00561, FreshToast, 2021-08-02).
  • Versions produced: orange-glow and white (M00562, FreshToast, 2021-08-02). The proposed “official” primary advertising logo is “White with prismatic edge and glow” (M00563, FreshToast, 2021-08-02).

Leora emblem / game silhouette (souls-like era)

FreshToast produced a silhouette-emblem for the game / for Leora (the souls-like-era protagonist) on 2021-08-26, iterating through many attempts (M00809, FreshToast, 2021-08-26):

  • Multiple attempts shown — labelled Attempt 1, 2, 2.5, 3, and 4; “Maybe simple is best” and eventually “This is the one I think I like the most? I’m gonna leave it for now” (M00816–M00824, FreshToast, 2021-08-26).
  • The design is built around a “rainbow/6 colour concept,” with colouring “on the flame-y tips” to be added later (M00812, M00826, spirit_noodle/FreshToast, 2021-08-26/27).
  • A gold version was attempted the next day; FreshToast felt “a bit lost” trying to keep it within the 6-colour concept, and spirit_noodle suggested the gold version “might require more shading” (M00825, M00826, M00827, 2021-08-27/28).
  • FreshToast was simultaneously “trying to get a janky ass gradient working” for the emblem’s colour (M00811, M00813, FreshToast, 2021-08-26).

Color palette (souls-like era)

FreshToast produced a “First attempt at the overall colour palette” on 2021-08-06 (M00587, FreshToast, 2021-08-06).

Open questions

  • PROPOSED Whether combat gets “really flashy skill frames” like Kill Bill / Borderlands character intros, or the plainer Darkest-Dungeon splash frames only (M01349, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Design history

  • 2021-08-02 — souls-like-era visual identity begins: Lambent logo concepted — simple, colour-independent, angular/villainous to echo Lucite; orange-glow and white versions, “official” white-with-prismatic-edge advertising version (M00559–M00563, FreshToast).
  • 2021-08-06 — first attempt at the overall colour palette (M00587, FreshToast).
  • 2021-08-26 → 2021-08-27 — Leora/game emblem silhouette iterated (Attempts 1–4 plus a gold version), built on a rainbow/6-colour concept with “flame-y tips” (M00809–M00827, FreshToast/spirit_noodle).
  • 2023-02-09 — for the JRPG restart, the full art-and-animation pipeline is agreed: chibi overworld sprites (reused for roaming monsters), PMD-style key-art portraits, simplified stretchy key-art with Darkest-Dungeon splash frames in combat, PMD:EoS-style cutscenes, and a three-way split among specialised artists — all to minimise animation. Guiding line: “simple game models with gorgeous portraits work well” (M01344–M01360, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions). This is the current pipeline.
