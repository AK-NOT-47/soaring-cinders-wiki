🛠️ Development

Development Timeline

Cross-IterationNotes / Design
First conceived 2021-02-13

The chronological arc of the project across the design-chat archive, 2021 → 2024, tracing the major design eras from a co-op souls-like to the JRPG later called Caelian Odyssey — the game now finalized as Soaring Cinders.

Summary — The team (CrunchyConniptions, FreshToast, spirit_noodle) began chatting in early 2021 about a co-op souls-like, spent 2021 building combat/movement systems and a souls-like visual identity, went largely quiet through late 2021 and 2022, then relaunched in February 2023 as a turn-based JRPG. Through 2023 they built out characters, mechanics, the magic system, and the world; branched off a separate second-game concept (Project Magitech); and in October 2023 renamed the JRPG around the Luminarium as Caelian Odyssey (the game’s current, post-archive name is Soaring Cinders). Work continued into 2024 with prologue screenplay drafts and worldbuilding. The team’s own timeline estimate was roughly “2 years to put things together. Another year for polish and last touches before launch”.

Era 1 — Co-op souls-like (2021-02 → 2021-09)

The archive opens 2021-02-13 with casual chat (2021-02-13). The early direction was explicitly a “Coop dark souls adventure with engaging combat,” drawing on Dark Souls, Bloodborne, and Sekiro. The aspirational pitch was “Skyrim times 10 with good gameplay and online co-op,” cross-platform, with an epic narrative (2021-05-25).

Project names in this era were SHATTER GLASS (the current starting project) and PL (a wall-running project). This era’s protagonist was Leora, and antagonists were framed around the Lambent Corporation and Lucite.

Milestones/work in this era:

  • 2021-08-02 — Lambent logo concepted (2021-08-02).
  • 2021-08-06 — first overall colour palette (2021-08-06).
  • 2021-08 — FreshToast begins a “more legible document containing all of what we’ve talked about,” reorganising and stuffing it with worldbuilding — “It’s like 6% done though lol” (2021-08-06/14).
  • 2021-08-26 → 27 — Leora/game emblem silhouette iterations (2021-08-26/27).
  • Technical decisions: debate over UE4 vs UE5 (UE5 “not production ready”), purchase of cel-shading marketplace assets to reverse-engineer.

Era 2 — Quiet / hiatus (2021-10 → 2023-01)

Activity drops sharply. The 2021-10-25 “Game Idea: Planet of the apes… but cats” exchange (2021-10-25) is followed by sparse link-sharing through late 2021 and only scattered messages in 2022 (2022-05-04). The next substantive burst is February 2023.

Era 3 — JRPG restart / Skyward Cinders (2023-02 → 2023-09)

The project relaunches as a turn-based JRPG on 2023-02-02 with a wave of JRPG and dungeon reference material (2023-02-02). The core “gaming deconstruction” pitch is laid out days later: “We fight the hero,” “We fight blacksmith who changes their armor based on your attack,” “We don’t level up” (2023-02-08).

Key work in this era:

  • 2023-02-09 — the art-and-animation pipeline is agreed (chibi sprites, PMD-style portraits, splash-frame combat) (2023-02-09).
  • 2023-02 — character roster, party composition, and the Ruby/homunculus decision solidify.
  • 2023-02-22 — Drykksar / Dracosis condition named (2023-02-22).
  • This era’s working title is Skyward Cinders, later referenced explicitly as the prior iteration whose “personal conflicts” were more central than in the successor (2023-10-17).

Era 4 — Project Magitech branch (2023-05)

A separate, second-game concept — Project Magitech — is brainstormed at length on 2023-05-20 and 2023-05-31: an all-powerful AI antagonist, a simulation twist, and a “sell it as a normal fantasy game” marketing plan (2023-05-20/31). It is explicitly deprioritized in favor of the main JRPG: “this effort should be put into Caelian Odyssey instead” / “Caelian Odyssey needs priority” (2023-05-20). Notably this is the first appearance of the name Caelian Odyssey.

Era 5 — Caelian Odyssey / the Luminarium (2023-10 → 2024)

In October 2023 the main JRPG is reframed around the Luminarium. On 2023-10-17 the title is written as “Caelian Odyssy - The Luminarium” (2023-10-17), and the design pivots to be about the Luminarium with no rival antagonist faction (2023-10-17). The 4 non-mage party members are recast as nonmagical historical/fictional archetypes, with Cyrus as the mage (2023-10-17).

  • 2023-10-13 — a significant team argument over creative ownership and cohesion of the design.
  • 2023-10-30 — healing/skills system worked out for Skyward Cinders naming still in use (2023-10-30).

Era 6 — Prologue drafting & worldbuilding (2024-08 → 2024-09)

Work continues into 2024 with the tutorial/prologue screenplay (“FADE TO TITLE: ‘CAELIAN ODYSSEY’”) and structural questions like “Is our game linear?” — answered “basically linear but there’s some branching,” compared to Dark Souls 3 (2024-08-22). Setting worldbuilding continues, e.g. golden-age-pirate grog/citrus lore for the Caribbean-like climate (2024-08-28). The archive’s last message is 2024-09-06 (2024-09-06).

The team’s own launch estimate

“When do you think we will have game to sell? 3 years?” — “Idk, a year? 2 years?… I think 2 to 3 years is likely.” “I think 2 years to put things together. Another year for polish and last touches before launch.” “It really depends on how intense production stage will be.”

Open questions

  • The exact boundary between the “Skyward Cinders” name and “Caelian Odyssey” is fuzzy: both names appear in October 2023 (2023-10-17, 2023-10-30). whether these were parallel names for the same iteration or a rename-in-progress.

Design history

  • 2021-02 → 2021-09 — co-op souls-like era: SHATTER GLASS / PL, Leora protagonist, Lambent/Lucite antagonists; combat, movement, and visual identity work.
  • 2021-10 → 2023-01 — hiatus; sparse activity.
  • 2023-02 → 2023-09 — JRPG restart as Skyward Cinders: “gaming deconstruction,” no leveling, art pipeline agreed, roster built.
  • 2023-05 — Project Magitech second-game concept branched and deprioritized; name “Caelian Odyssey” first used.
  • 2023-10 — main JRPG reframed as Caelian Odyssey around the Luminarium; 4 archetypal party members + mage.
  • 2024-08 → 2024-09 — prologue screenplay and setting worldbuilding; archive ends 2024-09-06. This is the latest state.
