Cosmology & Metaphysical Rules CANON

The metaphysical operating rules of the Skyward Cinders world: a familiar fantasy surface that gets stranger the deeper you dive, where death is final, evolution is an active force, and the world’s aesthetic runs on the Flow.

Summary — The world presents as a simple fantasy setting upfront with “weird stuff out back” (001 §Setting Goals): a second layer of oddity sits under an unassuming surface, and strangeness accelerates the deeper you dive (M02702, FreshToast, 2023-02-24). Its hard metaphysical rules are few but firm — true resurrection is impossible by any means, evolution manifests physically through bloodshed, and the cultural/aesthetic throughline of rivers and breath ties into the Flow (M03779, FreshToast, 2023-10-13).

Narrative structure of reality

  • “Second layer of oddity under an unassuming surface, strangeness accelerates the deeper you dive” (M02702, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).
  • Explicit model: “Like Bloodborne’s transition from Gothic to Eldritch” (M02703, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).
  • “Begin with the familiar and dip into the strange” (M02704, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).
  • Despite the overall light, humorous tone, the design goal is to “inject a sense of hopelessness somewhere” (M02714, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).

Death & resurrection

  • ‘True resurrection’ is assumed impossible by any means, magical or otherwise (M02659, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Eris is mistaken in her belief that stolen life force could possibly be used to resurrect the dead (M02659, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • The strength-stealing power (Ajax’s pact power, granted by the Daemon of Want) “can only take and never give back” (M02660, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Death spirits do not bring death; they appear where death occurs and ease folks’ passing — which is why many believe them a frightful bad omen (001 §Death Spirits; see Death for the named example).

Evolution as a world force

  • “It’s the physical manifestation of evolution with replacing ‘trial and error’ with ‘Victory by battle’ (bloodshed).” (M02698, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22) — the core rule behind Drykksar/Dracosis.
  • Monsters are evolving: “People have identified that monsters are growing just a little too fast” (M02715, FreshToast, 2023-02-24). A cult of people who graft monster parts onto themselves foreshadows dracosis and the discarding of one’s humanity (M02717, FreshToast, 2023-02-24; see Cultboy).
  • Eventually the hero’s lineage would have become like-dragons themselves (M02681, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22) — see Lysander and the bastard Damaris.
  • Vampirism and Dracosis “are both external factors messing with the natural order of thing” (M05199, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10) — the thematic treatment lives on Themes.

The Flow linkage

  • Rivers — especially the big one — are culturally important across Khorath, with river-related holidays and sayings; “This works pretty well with Flow and the general aesthetic of the setting” (M03777–M03779, FreshToast, 2023-10-13).
  • The Flow system itself (capacity, channeling efficiency) is a magic-system page; this page records only that the world’s river reverence and aesthetic were deliberately aligned with it. See the river system for the geography.
  • Related motif: magic connects to stamina and the theme of breath, “giving ‘out of breath’ another meaning” (001 §Theme of Breath).

Visual language

  • “Warm/orange/good vs cool/blue/bad for a subtle visual language” (M02311, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).

Open questions

  • None recorded as DEBATED in 001’s Cosmic and Metaphysical Rules section; the rules above are untagged descriptive canon or dated agreements.

Design history

  • 2023-02-16 — visual-language rule set: warm/orange/good vs cool/blue/bad (M02311, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-21 — death rules fixed: true resurrection impossible; Eris mistaken; the pact power “can only take and never give back” (M02659–M02660, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-22 — evolution framed as a physical world force acting through bloodshed (M02698, CrunchyConniptions); humanity/lineage consequences noted (M02681, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-24 — narrative-strangeness structure agreed: second layer of oddity, Bloodborne Gothic-to-Eldritch model, begin familiar and dip into strange, inject hopelessness somewhere (M02702–M02704, M02714, FreshToast); monsters observed evolving too fast (M02715, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-13 — river reverence tied to the Flow and the general aesthetic of the setting (M03779, FreshToast).
