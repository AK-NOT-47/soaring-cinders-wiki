The Undying

SCRAPPEDEarly ConceptNotes / Design

The Undying SCRAPPED

An ancient eternal species from the earliest “Age of Light” concept (2021): a downtrodden people set in opposition to the Heroes, who come back to life on defeat and survive by invading and borrowing heroes’ powers.

Summary — The Undying are a species from the earliest recorded concept phase, the “Age of Light” narrative (M00242, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-06-26). They are “an ancient eternal species that was kept down by the heroes’ species” who “became shriveled jerky due to the eons of suffering” (M00243, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-06-26). Mechanically they were the souls-like/F2P side of an asymmetric hero-vs-undying design: they always come back to life after defeat and survive by invading heroes to syphon and borrow their power. The whole Age-of-Light cosmology — and the Undying with it — belongs to the 2021 early concept and was superseded by the Ajax-centred history of the JRPG (see History & Ages).

Description

  • “The Undying are an ancient eternal species that was kept down by the heroes’ species. They became shriveled jerky due to the eons of suffering.” (M00243, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-06-26)
  • In the Age of Light narrative they are told to end the age of light to save their own kind, opposite the Heroes who are told to prolong it (M00242, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-06-26).
  • Framing options floated: Undying as enemies to Heroes, or Undying and Heroes uniting for a common purpose / being “sun-bros”; the invasion-and-enemies option seemed “more fun” (M00242, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-06-26).

Rules / mechanics (early souls-like design)

  • Undying: “You always come back to life after defeat (souls like)” — the F2P side; to experience a true hero’s power they must invade and borrow it (000 §Age of Light / M00243).
  • Invasion syphons strength from heroes: an undying facing a boss weak to fire could invade a fire-affinity hero and borrow that power “till they die and lose it (similar to embers in Dark Souls 3)” (000 §Age of Light).
  • The original Undying is very good at borrowing other heroes’ powers, whereas the undying counterparts of specific heroes are not (M00244, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-06-26).
  • Contrast with Heroes/True Heroes, who do not come back to life but have far better stats — the two sides “are always fighting each other” (000 §Age of Light).

Narrative role

  • The Age of Light’s four outcomes reduce to two — “let there be light or no light” — with Undying and Heroes each able to fulfil or betray their own kind (M00242, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-06-26; see History & Ages Design history).

Open questions

  • The Undying “version” of a completed hero was left “complicated and unclear” — “heroes that have been abandoned,” while normal undying “were not originally heroes but just normal people” (M00243, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-06-26).

Design history

  • 2021-06-26 — the Undying introduced as the ancient eternal species opposed to the Heroes in the Age of Light narrative, with come-back-to-life / invade-and-borrow mechanics (M00242–M00244, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023 onward — SCRAPPED the Age of Light cosmology and the Undying were superseded by the Ajax-centred history when the project became a JRPG; the strength-stealing motif survives instead in Ajax’s pact power and the Daemon of Want (see History & Ages).
