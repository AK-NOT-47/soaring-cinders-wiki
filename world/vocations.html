Vocations

Caelian OdysseyNotes / DesignCulture
Also known as: Arcanist, Empath, Maleficar, Explorer, Mage, Mercenary, Bounty Hunter, Blackguard, Physis, Ranger
First conceived undated

Occupations and specialist roles, from sorcerous researchers to bounty hunters and wildland rangers.

Summary - This page groups submitted vocation notes. The most developed entries are Empaths and Mercenaries, while Arcanists, Explorers, Physis practitioners, Rangers, and Mage specialisations are shorter but useful enough to preserve.

Arcanist

An arcanist is an anti-magic specialist and researcher who deals with forbidden magic and forbidden knowledge.

Empath

An empath is essentially a spirit specialist, similar to an exorcist. Empaths can get information about nature and remove harmful spirits. Attunement to spirits is necessary to become one, but this also makes the empath vulnerable. Some people are born attuned.

Maleficar

A Maleficar is an empath who specialises in daemons. Their reputation is ambiguous, which creates stigma around maleficarum and often leads them to work in secret.

Maleficarum have typically made one or more malefic pacts, whether for power or protection. The more pacts one makes, the more intensely daemons are drawn to that maleficar. Some use this to lure daemons out deliberately; others consider it a curse. Experienced maleficarum may act as intermediaries for people seeking pacts with daemons.

Explorer

Explorers go out seeking money rather than taking on ordinary jobs. They are wandering adventurers who can sell information such as metal vein locations, new landmarks, and maps. They also find treasure.

Mage

A mage is a sorcerer with an emphasis on combat, and is frequently mercenary.

Magi tend to master one or more schools of magic, much like martial-arts specialists. They may fall under specific delineations such as Crescent Moon, Half Moon, and Full Moon.

Cultural specialisations

  • Aqua Magia: Fioran water magi. They can draw water from the air around them, are important to much of Fiore’s society, and are full-moon, monk-like specialists.
  • Pyroclast: practitioners of explosive fiery magic, originating from Volanta. All practitioners must have genesis affinity. Some become pirates because fire is useful against enemy ships. Their moon category varies.
  • Yorusha: infamous Cahresian magic spies, similar to ninja, known for working under cover of night. Because of their exclusive state loyalty and frightening reputation, they are feared even by their own people. An honourable faction of Cahresian warriors looks down on them. They are half-moon specialists.

Mercenary

Mercenaries get paid to fight and kill.

Bounty hunter

Bounty hunters are licensed mercenaries who work with the imperial state instead of the public. They may legally assist state agents or local law enforcement with investigations, arrests, and similar work.

Bounty hunters are eligible to claim bounties on outlaws, missing persons, and other troublesome issues outsourced by law enforcement. Since killing or capturing outlaws is also available to other mercenaries, bounty hunters often skirmish with them during pursuit.

A bounty hunter must earn a license from the highest local law-enforcement authority or an agent of the state. The license can be revoked for unlawful activity or acting in the name of the law without warrant or sanction. Temporary licenses may be granted to the public as a last resort to lawfully employ unlicensed mercenaries.

Blackguard

A blackguard is a dishonoured knight: typically well-equipped and trained, but carrying a poor reputation. Blackguards may hide their former lives or use them to prove strength and find work.

Physis

Physis is a general magic practice performed for money. Specialists include alchemists, potionists, artificers, and enchanters.

Ranger

Rangers get in touch with magic in the wilds and connect people to it.

Open questions

  • Assassin is listed as a mercenary type but not defined in the submitted note.
  • Crescent Moon, Half Moon, and Full Moon are named but not explained as a mage taxonomy.
  • Ranger and Physis are compact notes and need more social context.
