Mercenaries (Social Class)

Caelian OdysseyNotes / DesignCulture
First conceived 2023-10-25

A low social class in the setting: rough-reputationed hired fighters, many of whom turn to illegal or criminal work — though the very best can end up well respected.

Summary — Mercenaries occupy a low social class in the world, but the best of them can end up well respected (2023-10-25). The rough reputation follows from circumstance: “many mercenaries turn to illegal work or outright criminal activity for money,” so “most people are wary of them if unknown” (2023-10-25). This class framing ties into the guild system and the underdog, isekai-flavoured treatment the protagonist receives.

Description

  • Newest submitted vocation note gives additional mercenary categories: bounty hunters are licensed to work with the imperial state and local law enforcement, while blackguards are dishonoured knights with training and equipment but poor reputations (Submitted culture notes section Mercenary, undated; imported 2026-07-08). See Vocations.
  • Mercenaries are “a low social class but the best can end up well respected” (2023-10-25).
  • “Many mercenaries turn to illegal work or outright criminal activity for money and have a rough reputation, so most people are wary of them if unknown” (2023-10-25).

Social connections

  • Interacts with the guild system: guilds are the institutions mercenaries operate through, and the debate over whether new mercenaries are bullied or “treated nicely” plays out there (2023-11-16).
  • The rough-reputation, wary-of-strangers framing supports the underdog protagonist arc (cf. Guild System “gang of bullies”).

Open questions

  • None recorded — the class description is a small, consistent block of canon.

Design history

  • 2026-07-08 - submitted mercenary vocation note adds bounty hunter licensing, state work, temporary licenses, and blackguards.
  • 2023-10-25 — mercenary social class defined: low class but the best become respected; many turn to illegal/criminal work, giving a rough reputation and making people wary of unknown mercenaries.
  • 2023-11-16 — revisited in the guild discussion: whether new mercenaries are bullied or welcomed. Latest reference.
