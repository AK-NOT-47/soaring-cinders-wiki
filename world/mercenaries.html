Mercenaries (Social Class) CANON

A low social class in the setting: rough-reputationed hired fighters, many of whom turn to illegal or criminal work — though the very best can end up well respected.

Summary — Mercenaries occupy a low social class in the world, but the best of them can end up well respected (M04689, FreshToast, 2023-10-25). The rough reputation follows from circumstance: “many mercenaries turn to illegal work or outright criminal activity for money,” so “most people are wary of them if unknown” (M04691, FreshToast, 2023-10-25). This class framing ties into the guild system and the underdog, isekai-flavoured treatment the protagonist receives.

Description

  • Mercenaries are “a low social class but the best can end up well respected” (M04689, FreshToast, 2023-10-25).
  • “Many mercenaries turn to illegal work or outright criminal activity for money and have a rough reputation, so most people are wary of them if unknown” (M04691, FreshToast, 2023-10-25).

Social connections

  • Interacts with the guild system: guilds are the institutions mercenaries operate through, and the debate over whether new mercenaries are bullied or “treated nicely” plays out there (“Plenty isekai have people treat new mercenaries nicely” — M05049, FreshToast, 2023-11-16).
  • The rough-reputation, wary-of-strangers framing supports the underdog protagonist arc (cf. Guild System “gang of bullies”).

Open questions

  • None recorded — the class description is a small, consistent block of canon.

Design history

  • 2023-10-25 — mercenary social class defined: low class but the best become respected; many turn to illegal/criminal work, giving a rough reputation and making people wary of unknown mercenaries (M04689, M04691, FreshToast).
  • 2023-11-16 — revisited in the guild discussion: whether new mercenaries are bullied or welcomed (M05049, FreshToast). Latest reference.
