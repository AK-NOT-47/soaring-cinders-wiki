Lucite SCRAPPED

A “crystal-like being/thing” from the early (2021) co-op souls-like iteration, the entity around which the antagonist Lambent Corporation and its emblem were themed — dropped when the project became a JRPG.

Summary — Lucite is recorded in the archive only as “a crystal-like being/thing” whose “angular, faceted nature” the Lambent Corporation logo was designed to emphasise (M00559, FreshToast, 2021-08-02). It belongs to the team’s first project era, the co-op souls-like starring Leora (see Development Timeline). Whether Lucite was a boss, a substance, an entity, or the corporation’s guiding intelligence is not spelled out in the notes; the one certain trait is its crystalline, faceted, “can’t be controlled” character. The concept was abandoned in the 2023 JRPG relaunch and never appears in Skyward Cinders / Caelian Odyssey.

Description

  • Explicitly “a crystal-like being/thing” (M00559, FreshToast, 2021-08-02) — the notes hedge between “being” and “thing,” so its nature is genuinely undetermined.
  • Angular and faceted, the shape that drives the Lambent Corporation’s villainous emblem (M00559).
  • Its uneven, jagged geometry was used to convey that “it can’t be controlled,” on the principle that “sharp means danger in most people’s minds” (M00560–M00561, FreshToast, 2021-08-02).
  • Associated with a prismatic/glowing visual treatment (the “official” logo is “white with prismatic edge and glow”), and with per-boss “assigned colours” in the souls-like design (M00562–M00563, FreshToast, 2021-08-02).

Open questions

  • DEBATED What Lucite actually is — being vs thing — is left open in the single message that names it (M00559, FreshToast, 2021-08-02).
  • No lore, powers, role, or relationship to Leora beyond “the thing the Lambent Corporation is themed on” is recorded.

Design history

  • 2021-08-02 — Lucite named as the crystalline being/thing behind the Lambent Corporation’s identity, as part of FreshToast’s logo brief (M00559–M00561, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02 onward — SCRAPPED dropped in the JRPG relaunch alongside the rest of the souls-like framing (Leora, the Lambent Corporation); not carried into Skyward Cinders or Caelian Odyssey (see Development Timeline).
