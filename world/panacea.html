Panacea

Caelian OdysseyNotes / DesignCulture
Also known as: Ambrosia, Panacea Fruit
First conceived undated

A miraculous Santier fruit whose curative power is matched by dangerous revelations and eventual madness.

Summary - Panacea is a peculiar magical fruit native to Santier. Arthur Marais discovered and named it during his final travail on the continent. Fresh panacea can restore grievous wounds and even regenerate lost flesh, but those who eat it begin suffering strange revelations, hallucinations, and the sound of nearby panacea trees singing. Ambrosia is the preserved syrup Arthur made from panacea; it is less potent, but far safer.

Discovery

Arthur Marais discovered panacea on Santier during the expedition that became his final travail. After eating the fruit, the wounds he had earned while exploring faded rapidly and his lost arm was restored within minutes.

Arthur’s translated journal describes the tree as slender, with spiralling branches, amber leaves, rich dark bark hard enough to dull a keen knife, and a white fruit suspended within reach. The fruit itself was scarcely larger than an apple, with alabaster skin and tender golden flesh. Arthur named the miracle panacea after witnessing its healing effect.

Healing

Fresh panacea has immense healing power. It can restore vitality, heal wounds, and regenerate lost flesh. The note frames its detailed healing basics, body-modification limits, and exceptions as areas still needing design detail.

Growth and rarity

Panacea grows on rare trees native to Santier. The trees are sparsely scattered, and Arthur only handled seven mature fruit during his time on the continent.

The fruit decays within a couple of days of being picked. Its means of propagation is unknown because the fruit bears no seed.

Panacea trees carry ominous ecological signs. Santier wildlife avoids eating the fruit, and the trees are found in clearings barren of all but the most resilient flora.

Ambrosia

With three fruit remaining, Arthur sought a form of panacea that could be transported over long distances or stored until needed. With the final fruit, he produced Ambrosia: a thick golden syrup that retains much of panacea’s healing property.

When consumed, Ambrosia instantly purges the body of toxins and disease. It soothes pain and causes rapid, though not instantaneous, wound healing.

Ambrosia lacks the full potency of fresh panacea. It cannot undo permanent injuries or regenerate lost flesh. It also does not seem to inflict madness or visions, even through excessive consumption, which is assumed to be due to its diluted effects.

The recipe for Ambrosia returned to Khorath without Arthur after his final journey to Santier, where he vanished. Since then, alchemists have refined it into an extremely valuable curative. Around seven servings of Ambrosia can be made from a single panacea.

Revelations

Consuming panacea causes chronic revelations. A single fruit may create strange sightings at the edge of one’s vision. As a person eats more panacea over time, the clarity and frequency of these revelations intensify until they become delirium and the afflicted can no longer distinguish hallucination from reality.

The contents of the revelations remain unclear. Recorded reactions range from profound wonder to unease and extreme terror. Arthur’s assistants reported episodes where he became distressed that they did not share his urgency about an unknown crisis, and where he rebuked temptations from unheard voices.

The singing

Those who have eaten panacea claim they can hear nearby panacea trees sing. The singing intensifies as one consumes more panacea, eventually becoming an intolerable furore.

Arthur used this sound to locate his third and largest panacea tree, which stood isolated within a shallow pool.

Open questions

  • The exact limits of panacea healing are still undecided.
  • Its relationship to body modification is still a placeholder topic.
  • The exceptions to panacea’s healing effects are not yet defined.
  • Its seedless propagation remains unexplained.
  • The nature and source of the revelations are unknown.
