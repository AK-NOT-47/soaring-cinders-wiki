Peoples & Social Class

Caelian OdysseyNotes / DesignCulture
Also known as: Humanity, Thera, Demi-Humans, Social Class
First conceived undated

Human populations, Thera, and regional class structures from the submitted culture notes.

Summary - This page combines notes on humanity, Thera, and social class because several entries are taxonomic WIP material. The submitted notes emphasize that race is less politically prominent in this world because monsters are a more immediate threat. Thera are also known as demi-humans and are culturally similar but divided into broad Westron and Eastron groups.

Humanity

The submitted Humanity note says race is less politically prominent because “everybody gets eaten by monsters.” Khorathian races contain numerous ethnic groups.

Khorathian race notes

  • Western Khorathian / Imperial: highest population; pale; western and coastal.
  • Eastern: associated with Fiore and Lunoir minorities; highest population; brown; eastern.
  • Alabast / Leukos / Cereus: northern, Cahras and Straid; high population; most pale; pallid and hairy.
  • Lunoir dominant: moderate population; dark; far eastern.
  • Uldrav: moderate population; darkest; desert, fallen.
  • Volant / Volanta: moderate population; brown; seafaring.

Thera

Thera, also known as demi-humans, are descended from various clans of humans. The note references big summer-solstice importance and positive flame symbolism, with Theros named but not developed.

Thera are culturally similar, but typically divided into two races based loosely on skin colour and lifestyle. The split occurred before the fall of the southern kingdoms, as Theran clans came under pressure from Lycosia and Fiore on either side.

Westron Thera

Westron Thera are paler Thera indigenous to southern Lycosia. They are sedentary.

Eastron Thera

Eastron Thera are nomadic, darker-skinned, and integrated across eastern Khorath.

Social class

  • Fiore: discriminates less, lacks knights, and has significantly more momentum between social classes.
  • Lunoir: has a distinct, discriminatory divide between upper and lower classes. Upper classes are especially predatory toward one another.
  • Cahras: grants titles but does not recognise nobility or give much credence to social class. Wealth and strength rule its social hierarchy.
  • Uldrav: has a similar caste system, but it functions less as hierarchy and more as a social role one is usually born into.

Open questions

  • Thera origin and the meaning of Theros are not developed in the submitted note.
  • Several race entries are shorthand only.
