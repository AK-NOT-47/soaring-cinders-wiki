Themes EVOLVED

The narrative themes of the project across its iterations — currently headlined by the Stagnation-vs-Rampant-Evolution opposition (Vampirism vs Dracosis) and the selfishness of humanity, with the earlier god/desire power-fantasy themes preserved as history.

Summary — As most recently discussed (February 2024), the project’s central thematic axis is the opposition of Vampirism and Dracosis: “Stagnation vs Rampant Evolution”, read as “a cool meta narrative of progressivism vs conservatism” (M05186, M05181, FreshToast, 2024-02-10). Alongside it stand the humanity theme — “Humanity is too selfish to allow for another being to exist” (M02681, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22) — and sun symbolism woven through the Damaris family. A chaos/order/random/luck theme remains PROPOSED and undeveloped. The 2021-era god-descent power fantasy and gods-and-desires themes are superseded early-iteration material (see Design history).

Stagnation vs Rampant Evolution (Vampirism vs Dracosis)

  • “Vampirism should be the opposite of dracosis” (M05173, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10).
  • Dracosis (Drykksar): “about evolving into a superior being. It’s about the future. About changing for the better. Changing to be strong.” (M05176, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10) — Rampant Evolution.
  • Vampirism (see the Vampires bestiary page): “about immortality”, “Preserving the present”, “Preserving the past” (M05177–M05180, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10) — Stagnation.
  • “That’s not quite opposites but it is a cool meta narrative of progressivism vs conservatism” (M05181, FreshToast, 2024-02-10); “Really, they are actually opposing ideas that just seem different” (M05182, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10); “Stagnation vs Rampant Evolution is compelling imo” (M05186, FreshToast, 2024-02-10).
  • Summary chart: “Vampiresim Vs Dracosis / Immortality Vs mortality / Change Vs preservation” (M05187, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10).
  • “I think vampires would be naturally conservative. Every old vampire should just be a weird boomer who cringes at current day cultural norms” (M05185, FreshToast, 2024-02-10).
  • “They are both external factors messing with the natural order of thing” (M05199, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10).
  • Vehicles for the theme: “We can discuss this through the royal family. With their outdated way of thinking” (M05183–M05184, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10); Raphael literally embodies both afflictions (M05192, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-10).

Humanity

  • “Humanity is too selfish to allow for another being to exist. (Humanity be like: How dare they be different? They must be just like us). Eventually, the hero’s lineage would have become like-dragons themselves.” (M02681, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22)
  • Explored short-term through the monster-grafting cult: humans attempting to escape their physical weakness at the cost of their humanity (M02717, FreshToast, 2023-02-24; see Cultboy).

Sun symbolism

  • “The sun is the giver of light. Like god said: ‘Let there be light.’. its cool.” (M02039, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14)
  • “Sun -> light, look nice -> see + plants grow -> food”; “Brain like sun”; “Sun feel good (sometimes)”; “Other times sun burns the wretched paleflesh” (M02040–M02043, FreshToast, 2023-02-14).
  • The sun is present on the Damaris family heraldry — the sun as giver and beacon to their people, which gets ‘eclipsed’ by a nasty happening (powerful visual metaphor) (001 §Damaris Family; see Clara Damaris).

Chaos / Order / Random / Luck PROPOSED

  • “It is key to figure out treatment of randomness in the game.” / “Chaos, order, random, luck theme??” (M01423–M01424, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-11). Raised as an open theme idea, not yet developed (001 §Chaos / Order / Random / Luck Theme).
  • Thematically adjacent in-world fact: thaumaturgy researchers have found no consistent link behind magic affinities, leading them to think affinity assignment is largely random (001 §Thaumaturgy — the mechanical treatment of randomness is a systems/magic matter).

Tone

  • Keep it light, keep it humorous, don’t take it too seriously — “but inject a sense of hopelessness somewhere” (M02714, FreshToast, 2023-02-24; 001 §Setting Goals).

Open questions

  • PROPOSED Whether “chaos, order, random, luck” becomes a real unifying theme was never discussed further (M01424, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-11; ledger marks the row partial).

Design history

  • 2021-02-13 — EVOLVED power fantasy concept (earliest recorded idea in the whole raw log): “You are a God descended from the from atop the mountains to cleanse the earth from its evils. From the sky you come thundering down. Your followers stand in awe of you and ever so appreciative. A power fantasy” — paired, in the same message, with the counter-note of “when serious person with many serious responsibilities shows a moment of softness and sincere empathy” (M00003, CrunchyConniptions). Early-iteration framing, superseded by the character-driven themes below.
  • 2021-03-05 — EVOLVED gods and desires theme: “The gods that fulfill the utmost desir of humankind”; people relate to fulfillment of desires and wish to have their own fulfilled; burdens lifted; “Harbinger and personification of Man’s deepest desires”; “The gods themselves” (M00015–M00019, CrunchyConniptions). Early iteration; the desire motif survives most directly in the Daemon of Want, a spirit later defined as formed from the rampant desires of the people (M02653, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • 2023-02-11 — PROPOSED chaos/order/random/luck floated as a possible theme alongside the question of how to treat randomness (M01423–M01424, spirit_noodle). Never developed.
  • 2023-02-14 — sun symbolism established and attached to the Damaris family heraldry (M02039–M02043, CrunchyConniptions & FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-22 — humanity theme stated: humanity too selfish to allow another being to exist; the hero’s lineage becoming like-dragons (M02681, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2024-02-10 — current headline theme agreed: Vampirism vs Dracosis as Stagnation vs Rampant Evolution / progressivism vs conservatism, to be discussed through the royal family, with Raphael embodying both (“This is good right?” — “I think so yeah”, M05190–M05191, CrunchyConniptions & FreshToast) (M05173–M05199).
