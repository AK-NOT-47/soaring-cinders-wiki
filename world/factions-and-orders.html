Factions & Orders

Caelian OdysseyNotes / DesignCulture
Also known as: Aqua Magia, Ashen Order, Reckoners, Crimson Covenant, Host of Ashes
First conceived undated

Magical, militant, and monster-hunting organizations from the submitted culture notes.

Summary - This page groups three faction notes: Aqua Magia, the Ashen Order, and the Reckoners. The Reckoners are the most developed: monster-hunting, magic-hunting orders built around self-augmentation, monster-blood anointing, and the Crimson Covenant. The Ashen Order splintered out of this tradition, while Aqua Magia is a Fioran state faction of aqueous sorcerers.

Aqua Magia

Aqua Magia is a Fioran state faction of sorcerers who specialise in aqueous sorcery.

Ashen Order

The Ashen Order is an anti-Dracosis knightly order. It began as the Host of Ashes, a Reckoner faction, before becoming a separate order.

The faction was once tied to anti-magic religious templar/witcher symbolism and retains some of that older symbolism, but it no longer takes part in drinking monstrous blood. The submitted note describes it as a former school that splintered from anti-magic religion and as a faction of anti-Dracosis people.

Founding

The Ashen Order was founded by a knight who joined the Host of Ashes and then usurped control.

Attire

The Ashen Order has abandoned all symbolism of the Reckoners and the Crimson Covenant.

Hierarchy

  • Squire: knight in training, assigned a mentor.
  • Knight: average member.
  • Officer: named but not defined.
  • Hand: the left and right hand of the supreme leader.
  • Supreme leader: unnamed in the submitted note.

Artifact

Ashen Wake is associated with the order in the submitted notes.

Reckoners

The Reckoners are magic and monster hunters, described by the note as a blend of witcher, templar, and Grey Warden ideas with schools. They promote self-augmentation similar to witchers and templars.

All Reckoners are anointed through a ritual using monster blood. Traditionally this blood comes from a dragon. The first order was founded by the grandson of Ajax. Members call one another kindred, and the order carries heavy blood symbolism.

Reckoners swear an oath to the Crimson Covenant, an ancient blood covenant. Each covenant has its own twist on the pledge, compared in the note to Green Lantern-style oaths.

Ranks

Four ranks are typical to a Reckoner faction, though not all factions recognise every rank.

  • Initiate: Reckoner in training, assigned a mentor.
  • Reckoner: average member; has sworn to the Crimson Covenant and survived the reckoning.
  • Sage: advisor and leader, similar to Kodlak. Hosts are often governed by a small council of sages. The sages of each school convene for sweeping decisions.
  • Martyr: ceremonial posthumous rank for those who give their lives to the cause.

Hosts

The Reckoners consist of several independent factions called Hosts. The following are the most renowned on Khorath:

  • Host of Blades: emphasises combat techniques to attack and overwhelm the unprepared and the monstrous. It is the oldest surviving host.
  • Host of Crows: bounty hunters and assassins who specialise in dealing with other humans, especially sorcerers. Originally from and based in Cahras.
  • Host of Eyes: emphasises stealth and ambush tactics against monsters that are difficult to face head-on.
  • Host of Sorrows: mostly concerned with horrors. They want to be scarier than the things they hunt, are known for welcoming non-humans, and were founded by Jett, a demi-human.
  • Host of Stars: researchers who seek to understand the creatures they hunt. They are situated in Fiore and mostly composed of sorcerers.

Open questions

  • The Crimson Covenant tenets are listed as a four-part structure in the source note, but the tenets themselves are blank.
  • Several host-specific details, attire entries, home bases, and oaths are placeholders in the source notes.
  • The Ashen Order’s supreme leader is unnamed.
