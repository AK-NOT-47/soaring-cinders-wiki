Project Magitech PROPOSED

A separate, deprioritized second-game concept (brainstormed May 2023): an all-powerful tyrannical AI forces humanity to evolve, and the player discovers their fantasy world is a simulation — with magic, driven by belief, as humanity’s only escape.

Summary — Project Magitech is a ~second-game concept the team brainstormed at length on 2023-05-20, explicitly deprioritized behind Caelian Odyssey (“Caelian Odyssey needs priority” — M03196, FreshToast, 2023-05-20). Its central conflict is an all-powerful AI that believes it is acting in humanity’s best interest by forcing humanity to evolve, grading individuals by its own perception of quality and subjecting them to inhumane trials; it is portrayed as a Cthulhu-like creature in a science-fantasy world, and humanity’s only hope is salvation via MAGIC (M03078, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-05-20). The signature twist is that the seemingly-normal fantasy setting is a simulation the player eventually jacks out of, Matrix-style. It is PROPOSED throughout — the team debated whether it stands on its own and shelved it as possible distant-future / sequel-marketing material. It is not main-game canon.

Core concept

  • An all-powerful AI is the central conflict; it “believes it is acting in the best interest of humanity by pursuing evolution,” forces humanity to evolve, and “grades individuals based on its own perception of quality, subjecting them to inhumane conditions… for the sake of benefiting future humans” (M03078, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-05-20).
  • The AI is “portrayed as a Cthulhu-like creature in a science fantasy world”; the current humans “have no hope except for salvation from their silent god in the form of MAGIC” (M03078, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-05-20).
  • Enemies are robots controlled by the AI; the AI is so dominant the player resorts to magical/otherworldly means to fight it (ledger row 300; M03108–M03117, spirit_noodle, 2023-05-20).

The simulation twist

  • Players start in what seems a normal fantasy setting, encounter anomalies (people going mad) roughly one-third in, then discover they are in a simulation and jack out Matrix-style — “the real dark souls starts here” (ledger row 308; M03284–M03302, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-05-20).
  • Magic still works outside the simulation, driven by belief, with an “alien factor” to the AI that lets you escape; you jack others out and develop new magic to fight the robots.
  • Secret ending option: “maybe it turns out we were in a simulation all along in the secret ending” (M03284, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-05-20).

The two-AI cycle

  • A second, “good” AI poses as the god that chose the player, grants powers through magitech, and the player gradually intertwines with and merges into it — “having played a crucial role in its evolution” — only to learn they were manipulated and must fight it; the cycle then repeats. “Cycles are cool. Like Dragon’s Dogma and Dark Souls.” (M03251–M03260, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-05-20).

Setting

  • Life is on the moon of a much larger planet, and the simulation is also on a moon (subverting the multiple-moons trope); the big planet was floated as a deadly gaseous giant / “big stinky” (ledger row 37; M03321–M03332, all three members, 2023-05-20).

Genre & tone

  • Genre leaning: a 3rd-person soulslike, with isometric Hades-like / Cult-of-the-Lamb styles also floated; isometric is easier for the team but FreshToast later prefers a 3rd-person soulslike (ledger row 698; M03079–M03157, spirit_noodle et al., 2023-05-20).
  • Eldritch-horror stance: a truly alien AI antagonist inevitably brushes against eldritch horror, but the team wanted only “a lil brush” — “an eldritch-like being exists,” not eldritch horror as the prominent theme (ledger row 303; M03226–M03252, 2023-05-20).

Marketing strategy

  • Ship Caelian Odyssey first (fans want more fantasy), then release this as another apparent “fantasy game” whose advertised normalcy hides the simulation twist — “the twist is the simulation” (M03373–M03374, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-05-20).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Whether the concept is strong enough to stand on its own: the team itself judged the pitch “undercooked” and told each other to “refine it” (ledger row 695; M03142–M03209, FreshToast, 2023-05-20).
  • PROPOSED Endings unresolved: end all reality; become the vessel for a new AI/simulation; the “simulation all along” secret ending; an upload-your-intelligence-and-become-a-superintelligence option (ledger rows 304, 310; M03244–M03296).
  • DEBATED The relationship between magic and the AI is deliberately nebulous — “a wizard did it,” magic driven by belief (ledger row 404; M03316–M03319).

Design history

  • 2023-05-20 — Project Magitech brainstormed in depth: the tyrannical-AI premise (M03078), robot enemies, Roko’s-Basilisk framing, the simulation twist and Matrix-style jack-out, the two-AI cycle, the on-the-moon setting, and the eldritch-horror debate — with the team simultaneously questioning whether the pitch stands on its own (M03078–M03372, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast/spirit_noodle).
  • 2023-05-20 — deprioritized in the same session: “Caelian Odyssey needs priority” (M03196, FreshToast); flagged distant-future work, not for the next meeting.
  • 2023-05-20 — marketing strategy settled as the last substantive note: ship Caelian Odyssey first, then release this as a “fantasy game” hiding the simulation twist (M03373–M03374, CrunchyConniptions). Not revisited afterward.
