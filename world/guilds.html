Guild System

DEBATEDCaelian OdysseyNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-11-16 (M05040, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-11-16 (M05050, CrunchyConniptions)

Guild System DEBATED

A proposed set of adventurer guilds for exploring, looting, and killing, with a rank ladder — and an isekai-style “gang of bullies” that treats the underdog protagonist roughly.

Summary — The team floated ordinary adventurer guilds “for exploring, looting and killing or whatnot” that the player could level up through (M05040, FreshToast, 2023-11-16). A ranking ladder was proposed — F, E, D, C, B, A, S (or metals/precious gems) — and, leaning into isekai convention, the guild would be “a little mean” (M05044), “treat her like the underdog” (M05045) “like every other isekai” (M05048), with “that gang of bullies” waiting when the underdog protagonist walks in (M05050, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-11-16). The distinct “reckoners” guild was explicitly not the one the party joins (M05040, FreshToast). Details remain unsettled — DEBATED.

Description

  • Guilds “for exploring, looting and killing or whatnot,” which the player “could maybe level up” (M05040, FreshToast, 2023-11-16).
  • The party would not join the “reckoners” guild specifically (M05037, M05040, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-11-16) — “so we have normal guilds in the game, for surzezzz?” confirms ordinary guilds exist alongside.

Rules / structure

  • Proposed rank ladder: F, E, D, C, B, A, S — “or metals/precious gems” (000 §Guild System).
  • Guilds could level up as an institution as well (000 §Guild System).

Social texture

  • “Maybe the guild is a little mean” (M05044), “Treat her like the underdog” (M05045), “Like every other isekai” (M05048) (CrunchyConniptions, 2023-11-16).
  • “You go to a guild, and there is that gang of bullies” (M05050, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-11-16), countered by FreshToast noting “plenty isekai have people treat new mercenaries nicely” (M05049, FreshToast, 2023-11-16). Ties into the low social standing of Mercenaries (Social Class).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Whether the rank system is letters (F→S) or metals/precious gems (001 §Guild System).
  • DEBATED Whether the guild treats the protagonist as a bullied underdog or new mercenaries are welcomed (M05049–M05050, 2023-11-16).

Design history

  • 2023-11-16 — guilds proposed for exploring/looting/killing with a level-up ladder, distinct from the “reckoners”; rank system (F→S or metals/gems) and the isekai “gang of bullies” underdog framing discussed (M05037–M05050, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions). Only recorded discussion; left unsettled.
