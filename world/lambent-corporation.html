Lambent Corporation SCRAPPED

The villainous antagonist organization of the early (2021) co-op souls-like iteration, themed around the crystalline being Lucite — abandoned when the project relaunched as a JRPG.

Summary — Lambent Corporation was the antagonist faction of the team’s first project era, the co-op souls-like whose protagonist was Leora (see Development Timeline). It is documented almost entirely through the logo-design work FreshToast did for it: a deliberately villainous, angular emblem meant to echo the “angular, faceted nature of Lucite” (a crystal-like being/thing) and be legible as a glowing boss-colored icon (M00559, FreshToast, 2021-08-02). The whole souls-like framing — Leora, the Lambent Corporation, and Lucite as its antagonists — was superseded when the project relaunched as a turn-based JRPG in February 2023 and never carried into Skyward Cinders / Caelian Odyssey.

Description

Lambent Corporation was framed as an antagonist organization whose bosses each carried an “assigned colour,” with the corporate emblem appearing “as a glowing icon in the light of a boss’ assigned colour” (M00559, FreshToast, 2021-08-02). The corporation is thematically fused with Lucite, the crystal-like being/thing whose faceted, jagged geometry the logo is built to evoke.

Logo & visual identity

The only substantive record of the corporation is FreshToast’s logo concept (M00559–M00563, FreshToast, 2021-08-02):

  • Simple enough to be understood “at a distance or a glance,” and deliberately not colour-dependent so it could be reused in many places (M00559).
  • “Somewhat villainous,” using angular shapes to emphasise “the angular, faceted nature of Lucite (since it’s a crystal-like being/thing)” (M00559).
  • “A little uneven and jagged, to try and lowkey get across that it can’t be controlled,” because “sharp means danger in most people’s minds” (M00560–M00561).
  • Versions produced: an orange-glow version and white versions; the proposed “official” primary advertising logo is “White with prismatic edge and glow” (M00562–M00563).

The full art discussion lives on Art & Animation Direction; this page records the corporation as a world/faction entity.

Open questions

  • No plot, membership, goals, or structure for the Lambent Corporation beyond “antagonist org themed on Lucite” are recorded in the notes — the corporation exists in the archive only as a logo brief.

Design history

  • 2021-08-02 — Lambent Corporation established as the souls-like era’s antagonist faction, documented via FreshToast’s logo concept keyed to Lucite’s crystalline nature (M00559–M00563, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02 onward — SCRAPPED the project relaunched as a JRPG; the Leora / Lambent Corporation / Lucite souls-like framing was dropped and not carried into Skyward Cinders or Caelian Odyssey (see Development Timeline Era 1 → Era 3). No later message revisits the corporation.
