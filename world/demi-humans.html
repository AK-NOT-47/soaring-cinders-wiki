Demi-Humans

Caelian OdysseyNotes / DesignCulture
First conceived 2023-06-04

The setting’s part-human peoples of mixed lineage, established as ordinary in scale — demi-humans “aren’t superhumanly tall” — with the Catfolk-human Thorne as the illustrative example.

Summary — Demi-humans are the world’s mixed-lineage part-human peoples. The one firm rule the notes set is that they are not superhumanly tall: “demi-humans aren’t superhumanly tall so more likely he’s near your height lol” (2023-06-04). Mixed lineages exist, but the only concrete illustration in the notes is a Catfolk/human combination sourced from the placeholder character Thorne “Tabby” Tumblemane, so that mixture is an illustration rather than settled species canon.

Description

  • Demi-humans are of mixed human/non-human lineage and are of ordinary human scale — explicitly “aren’t superhumanly tall” (2023-06-04).
  • The illustrative mixed lineage is Catfolk + human: Thorne is described as “this combination of Catfolk and human lineage” possessing “the body strength of a human with the agility and reflexes of a Catfolk,” with pointed ears and golden eyes (000 §Thorne; raw Thorne profile). — because this is drawn from a placeholder character, treat Catfolk/human as illustration, not confirmed species rule (001 §Demi-Humans).

Story connections

  • A demi-human party/companion candidate was floated to be paired with a creature “that’s supposed to be dangerous but is surprisingly loyal and docile,” alongside the existing Dianthus (2023-10-17).
  • One of the Caelian Odyssey cast is tagged in the design notes as a “Lycosian Male (Demi-Human)”.

Open questions

  • Whether Catfolk/human is a general demi-human rule or specific to Thorne; the notes give only the one placeholder-sourced example (001 §Demi-Humans).
  • What other lineages exist beyond Catfolk is not enumerated in the notes.

Design history

  • 2023-06-04 — demi-humans established as not superhumanly tall, of roughly human height, in discussion of the Catfolk-adjacent Thorne concept.
  • 2023-10-17 — a demi-human companion + “dangerous but loyal/docile” creature pairing floated alongside Dianthus. Latest note.
