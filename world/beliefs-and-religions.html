Summary - This page gathers several submitted belief and religion notes: Lunoiran influence/reach, luck philosophy, the unconfirmed theory of Mosaic Reality, personification, Argentism, Fieros, and the Spiral Path. The submitted notes are treated as the newest available version, while Obsidian links inside them have been converted to plain text.

Influence / Reach

Influence, also called Reach, is a Lunoiran idea based on Arabic wasta. It is a form of social currency: one’s reputation, connections, and ability to get things done through networks.

Luck

Luck is a philosophy inspired by Anglo-Saxon wyrd and karma, focused on cultivating good fortune.

Weal and woe

The note frames luck through Weal & Woe. Woe was originally thought of as simply bad, but the idea evolved to more closely represent Fate and Chaos.

Names

  • Pyra: flame; cultivating a small fire.

Mosaic Reality

The theory of Mosaic Reality is explicitly intended to remain unconfirmed. It serves as a possible TTRPG campaign plot point or a connection to other settings.

Personification

Cultures across the world attach great significance to names, especially names of important places and objects. Some believe sentimental items bear fragments of their owner’s soul.

Humans often project human traits, emotions, and intentions onto non-human entities. Naming an object humanises it further, making it easier to sympathise with as if it were alive. Emotion’s effect on the Flow gives names a semblance of magical power, intensifying with the strength and range of emotions tied to the name.

As an object gathers meaning and sentimentality from humans, its likelihood of becoming a relic increases. Once such a relic awakens, this emotion is personified as magic. A blade associated with countless acts of hatred might acquire unmatched sharpness, while a broken family heirloom could occasionally manifest a mirage of its original shape.

Machina are so reminiscent of living beings that people often treat them as sentient. Particularly beloved or feared machina risk having their cores twisted by a Flow confluence, causing dangerous malfunction or destruction. In very rare cases, a machina may instead achieve true sentience and act on a newly acquired will of its own.

Argentism

Argentism treats various metals, especially silver, as symbols of purity because of their alchemical properties.

Silver superstition

A common Argentist practice is to carry silver for good luck. This can be jewellery, a weapon, or simply a handful of half-glint coins.

The practice has spread throughout Khorath because of silver’s alchemical properties and its ability to ward off Accursed creatures. Travelling far without silver is widely considered to invite misfortune.

Only fine silver causes significant injury to the Accursed, but half-glint coins contain enough silver to inflict pain and light burns. Creatures such as vampires who try to pass as human may refuse to handle coins barehanded.

Fieros

Fieros is recorded as a Theran religion. The submitted note gives no further detail.

The Spiral Path

The Spiral Path is the least popular religion and has strong cult associations. The Graft Cult follows it. It is dismissive of other religions, strongly invested in Dracosis, and widely recognised as a cult.

Open questions

  • Influence/Reach has a definition but no examples of how it operates in Lunoiran society.
  • Fieros is named only as a Theran religion.
  • The Spiral Path’s doctrine and ritual structure are not defined beyond its cult reputation and Dracosis focus.
