History & Ages EVOLVED

The world’s historical backbone as currently conceived: a deep past in which dragons took over the dinosaurs, and a legendary age defined by the hero Ajax’s pact and his dragon-slaying — with the earlier “Age of Light” cosmological history preserved below as a superseded iteration.

Summary — In the current (Skyward Cinders) conception, in-world history centres on the age of Ajax: the only warrior truly brave enough to fight a dragon, who traded half his lifespan to the Daemon of Want for the power to steal strength (M02653, FreshToast, 2023-02-21), slew his dragon, and in doing so damaged the top of a mountain — causing the great river to form from several smaller ones (M03824, FreshToast, 2023-10-13). Deeper still, current dragons are descendants of dinosaurs, who never actually died off but were taken over by dragons (001 §Dracosis Core Concept; ledger row M02691, FreshToast, 2023-02-22). Two whole earlier framings of the world’s history — the CRISPR future and the Age of Light — were superseded and are recorded in Design history.

The deep past: dragons and dinosaurs

  • Current dragons are descendants of dinosaurs, who never actually died off but were instead taken over by dragons (001 §Dracosis Core Concept; M02691, FreshToast, 2023-02-22).
  • “Maybe that’s how dragons survive. they are killed and their power taken into another being. then they are merged with the being. and over and over again. like evolution. this is what the dragons did to the previous beings—dinosaurs?” (001 §Vaenngrym; see Vaenngrym and Drykksar).

The age of Ajax (legendary history)

  • Ajax became desperate for strength to defend his people — “the only warrior truly brave enough to fight a fucking dragon” — and traded half his lifespan to the Daemon of Want for the ability to steal the strength of others (M02653, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • The legend says Ajax split a mountain to make the twinned rivers (M03820, FreshToast, 2023-10-13); “the narrative is exaggerative he just damaged the top of a mountain during his fight with the dragon which causes a river to form from several smaller ones. Ajax was high of absorbing a dragon’s strength at the peak of his power” (M03824, FreshToast, 2023-10-13).
  • A lake the party goes under “was flooded into the state it’s in today by the great hero the second game’s all about” (M03873, FreshToast, 2023-10-13).
  • Ajax left behind seven relics as informal trials — his “legacy” — anticipating that a dangerous descendant would one day need to be stopped (001 §Ajax’s Legacy System; details on the Ajax page).
  • His power has diluted with each heir; Lysander and the bastard Damaris carry weakened versions (001 §Diluted Power in Descendants).
  • Vaenngrym, the dragon of the present story, is the son of Ajax’s dragon (001 §Vaenngrym).

Present-day historical texture

  • The political structure is no longer a kingdom: one county ruled by a countess or count, with three baronies (001 §Political Structure — geography pages cover the regions).
  • People have identified that monsters are growing just a little too fast (M02715, FreshToast, 2023-02-24) — rampant evolution is a live historical process, not a finished one (see Cosmology & Metaphysical Rules).

Open questions

  • None DEBATED in the history sections themselves; the undecided matters attached to this history (e.g. whether Locke gets infected with Dracosis) live on Drykksar and the character pages.

Design history

  • 2021-03-09 — SCRAPPED CRISPR future setting: an early, entirely different premise — “exploring the idea of a future with CRISPR” (M00027, spirit_noodle); super humans through CRISPR with super regeneration, intelligence and lifespan (M00021–M00024, CrunchyConniptions); “What happens if you take the current world and quadruple peoples life span, intelligence?” (M00028); “The next epoch of the human race. The age after the age of information and digitization. Technological advancement is halted and now biological advancement is the goal” (M00029–M00032, spirit_noodle); ethical dilemmas and conflicting factions with diametrically opposed philosophies (M00033). Both authors claimed the idea (“My idea!” — M00020, CrunchyConniptions; “lies. my idea” — M00025–M00026, spirit_noodle). Discussed but abandoned (001 §CRISPR Future Setting).
  • 2021-06-26 — EVOLVED Age of Light narrative (early iteration): “Undying are told to end the age of light to save their own kind. Heros are told to prolong the age light to continue the prosperity of their own kind. both push their own narrative to opposite ends. both can fulfill their kind’s wants or betray them. leading 4 final outcomes… which are essentially 2 outcomes actually. let there be light or no light” (M00242, CrunchyConniptions). Undying could be enemies to heroes “or be sun-bros” (M00242). The Undying species: “an ancient eternal species that was kept down by the heroes’ species. They became shriveled jerky due to the eons of suffering” (M00243, CrunchyConniptions); the undying counterparts of heroes aren’t good at borrowing other heroes’ powers, while the original undying is very good at that (M00244, CrunchyConniptions). This whole cosmological history belongs to the earliest concept phase and was superseded by the Ajax-centred history below.
  • 2023-02-21/22 — current historical backbone established: Ajax’s pact with the Daemon of Want (M02653, FreshToast); dragons as takers-over of dinosaurs in the deep past (M02691, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-13 — Ajax’s physical feats settled in their final, myth-vs-fact form: legend of splitting a mountain (M03820) vs the fact of damaging its top and creating the river (M03824); the flooded lake attributed to “the great hero the second game’s all about” (M03873, all FreshToast).
