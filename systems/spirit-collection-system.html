Spirit Collection System

EVOLVEDEarly ConceptNotes / Design
Also known as: Character Collection, Spirit Gacha, Character Acquisition System
First conceived 2021-05-26 (M00226, CrunchyConniptions) · last in chat 2021-05-26 (M00229, CrunchyConniptions)

Spirit Collection System EVOLVED

An early gacha-style character-collection layer: you collect the cast “like Pokémon, but they are spirits,” pop a capsule, and become the released spirit.

Summary — Players collect characters as spirits: pop a capsule, a spirit is released, and the player becomes that spirit (008 §11). Some spirits are heroic, some villainous, some “something else.” Acquisition runs on a points system that raises the probability of getting a specific character, with a retrievable-points twist that lets players either quell or embrace the randomness. Like the Two-Path System (Undying vs Heroes) and invasion anti-meta, it is part of the early co-op-online vision, flagged “Early Concept” in 003/004, and is not an active first-game system after the single-player reframing (008 §11 EVOLVED).

Core mechanics

  • People collect the characters like Pokémon, but they are spirits (008 §11).
  • Pop a capsule and the spirit is released.
  • The player becomes the spirit — you play as the collected spirit, not alongside it.
  • Some spirits are heroic, some villainous, some “something else.”

(Narrative note: the world’s “spirits” were later named Daemons; the collection framing here predates and is separate from that lore.)

Character acquisition system

A points system that improves the probability of getting a certain character (M00228, M00229, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-05-26; 008 §11; shared with the anti-meta points system):

  • A points system improves the probability of getting a certain character.
  • Once used, the point can be retrieved.
  • If a player embraces randomness, they could put points into something more beneficial instead — equipment, items, skills, etc.
  • Guiding lines: “Incentivize to embrace the randomness. Everyone using the same builds is boring” and “Give people the ability to quell the randomness at a cost. People want to be in control” (008 §11).

Open questions

  • EVOLVED Whether any spirit/character-collection layer survives into the single-player first game — the mechanic is inseparable from the gacha/co-op vision (008 §11; Parts 9-24). See Game Type & Structure (Overview).

Design history

  • 2021-05-26 — Spirit Collection introduced: collect characters like Pokémon but as spirits; pop a capsule, the spirit is released, and the player becomes the spirit; heroic/villainous/“something else” spirits (M00226, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-05-26; 008 §11).
  • 2021-05-26 — Character-acquisition points system defined: points improve a target character’s probability, are retrievable once used, and can instead be spent on equipment/items/skills if the player embraces randomness; “give people the ability to quell the randomness at a cost” (M00228, M00229, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-05-26; 008 §11).
  • Parts 9-24 — supersedes as active design. First game reframed single-player; the spirit-collection/character-gacha is tagged “Early Concept” (003/004) and kept as a record (008 §11 EVOLVED).
