Navigation Compass (Compass-over-Maps) CANON

How the player finds their way through the world and dungeons: a compass-first navigation model that deliberately avoids maps and minimaps.

Summary — Soaring Cinders navigates by compass, not map. The proto ships CANON · implemented compass-style node navigation with no minimap: each dungeon node presents next-node choices labelled with compass-style prompts — “Descend,” “Press into the dark,” “Take the faster, darker way” — rather than a spatial map (008 §3; proto src/core/campaign/types.ts DungeonNode.compass, src/web/screens.ts; GAME_DESIGN.md §14 "Compass > maps. No minimaps."). This grew out of a 2021 design rule that “maps are taboo” — a Skyrim-inspired preference for a compass that “guides you to the fun” over a map you stare at (M00092, M00108, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-05-13). The rule was later softened: the real taboo is a world too hard to traverse without a map, not maps themselves (M00115–M00116, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-05-13).

Description (current model)

  • Compass over maps: navigation emphasizes a compass; any actual map is “very simple and not that necessary” (M00108, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-05-13; 008 §3).
  • No minimap in the proto: the vertical slice ships node navigation with no minimap; each DungeonNode carries compass prompt labels for its onward choices (008 §3, §13; proto types.ts, screens.ts).
  • Node graph, never a spatial map: the playable dungeon “The Descent into Ajax’s Tomb” is a compass-navigated node graph — start → fight → branch → riverside camp → fight → boss — never a spatial map (008 §13; proto src/core/campaign/content.ts). See Dungeon-Run Loop for the full run structure.

Rules / Design intent

  • Minimal Maps is one of the summarized core design pillars — “compass-based navigation preferred” (008 §Summary of Core Design Pillars; see Game Type & Structure (Overview)).
  • Maps are taboo (debated): the standing design rule, quoted verbatim from the notes — “Maps are boring. Maps are tedious. Maps are effective and productive. Maps are not fun. Maps are for boring nerds” (M00092, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-05-13; 008 §3, §19).
  • Purpose of a map, if used: only to “guide people to the fun, not because MAPS are fun” — they guide you to the fun (M00095–M00096, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-05-13).
  • The real taboo: a world so hard to traverse yourself that you’re forced onto the minimap — flagged against Final Fantasy and Witcher as what to avoid (M00115–M00117, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-05-13; 008 §3).

Related systems

  • The river doubles as an in-world wayfinding aid — “the river also acts as a visual compass” (M04021, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-13). See River Flask and Dungeon-Run Loop.
  • The compass node graph is the traversal layer of the dungeon run; nodes may attach a fight, reward, ambush, or camp.

Open questions

  • DEBATED “Maps are taboo” is explicitly tagged debated: FreshToast disagreed about maps outright but accepted “pleasant alternatives,” liking Skyrim/Ghost-style maps that show where found things are (M00093, M00105–M00106, FreshToast, 2021-05-13). The compromise landed on compass-first with a de-emphasized simple map.
  • DEBATED Elevation cues — FreshToast wished the compass had “a better way to indicate elevation” (M00111, FreshToast, 2021-05-13); unresolved.

Design history

  • 2021-05-13 — the “maps are taboo” design rule is stated: maps are effective but not fun, “for boring nerds” (M00092, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-05-13). FreshToast pushes back, preferring Skyrim/Ghost-of-Tsushima maps that hint at cool stuff (M00093, M00105–M00106, FreshToast).
  • 2021-05-13 — the position lands on compass-over-maps: “The use of the compass, I love! Emphasising the map even less… the actual map is very simple and not that necessary” (M00108, CrunchyConniptions); “The compass is a neat minimap, definitely better in some ways” (M00109, FreshToast).
  • 2021-05-13 — Crunchy re-scopes the taboo: his real dislike is minimaps, and the real taboo is a world too hard to traverse without one (M00110, M00115–M00116, CrunchyConniptions) — refines the blanket anti-map stance.
  • 2023-10-13 — the world’s river is designated a “visual compass,” reinforcing in-world wayfinding over UI maps (M04021, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-13).
  • 2026-06-16 — implemented-code pass: the proto ships compass-style node navigation with no minimap, with per-node compass prompts, honouring the compass-over-maps pillar (008 §3, §13; proto campaign/types.ts, web/screens.ts). (Current presentation above.)
