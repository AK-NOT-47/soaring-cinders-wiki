Shields & Wounded State

Cross-IterationProto-Implemented
Also known as: Shield System, Wounded Mechanic, one-shot protection
First conceived 2023-02-19

Two survivability mechanics: a one-shot shield that saves you from a killing blow, and the Wounded flag that makes low-HP combatants more fragile.

Summary — Shields are a one-shot “extra life”: every character starts a fight with exactly 1 shield, and a blow that would be lethal instead drops them to 1 HP and shatters the shield; shields reset every encounter (008 §2 Shield System; proto src/core/combat/state.ts). Wounded = under half HP (strictly < 50% of current max); a Wounded defender takes +25% damage from every hit (008 §5 Wounded Mechanic; proto resolution.ts, WOUNDED_TAKEN = 1.25). Both serve the “end fast” battle philosophy — shields let players take risks, Wounded lets either side pick off weakened combatants. The shield began as a 2-shield system (2023-02-19) and was revised to 1 because “death on 2nd shield break is unintuitive” (2023-02-19).

Shield System — implemented (proto)

One-shot protection (“extra life”) (008 §2; proto state.ts)

  • Every character starts a fight with exactly 1 shield. When a blow would be lethal and the shield is intact, the character is instead dropped to 1 HP and the shield is consumed (“shield shatters — saved from a killing blow”). Shields reset to intact every encounter (makeCharacter/createCombat set shield: true; the lethal-blow check in applyEffect).
  • It stacks with the Protection buff, a separate once-per-fight effect that halves the next incoming hit before the shield ever triggers (status.ts protection).
  • Player-side mechanic; maybe a few bosses or Lysander’s party can break shields early — e.g. a 2nd-phase Lysander opening that immediately removes a shield (2023-02-19/20).
  • Purpose: gives leeway for big-damage attacks, makes death less imminent, lets players take risks / pick fights when estus is out (2023-02-20).

Wounded Mechanic — implemented (proto)

(008 §5 Wounded Mechanic; proto resolution.ts isWounded, WOUNDED_TAKEN = 1.25)

  • “Wounded” = strictly < 50% of current max HP. A Wounded defender takes +25% damage from every hit.
  • Applies to both sides: the player picks off weakened enemies; enemies threaten weakened party members. Reinforces the “end fast” philosophy (2023-10-30).

Related systems

  • Both mechanics push the “end fast” combat philosophy alongside Escalation Mechanic and finisher gating (008 §2 Combat Duration). See Action Points (AP) Economy.
  • Wounded is the surviving piece of the Healing & Health overhaul — the degrading-max-HP ceiling and Wounded were adopted together (Part 18).
  • Shields replaced the scrapped corpse/Remnant system as the frontline-defense answer (2023-02-20).

Open questions

  • Thane’s 2 shields not coded. The notes single out Thane as special with 2 shields (2023-02-19); the proto gives all characters exactly 1 with no per-character override (008 §2, design intent vs implemented`).
  • Weight-of-death concern. CrunchyConniptions repeatedly flagged that a “second chance” mechanic reduces the impact of death and slows momentum, suggesting it might work best as a boss-only mechanic (2023-02-20). FreshToast’s counter: it won’t make the game much easier “since we just account for it in the numbers” (2023-02-20).
  • Per-skill wounded interactions and a well-rested bonus are mentioned in the notes but the proto implements only the flat +25%-damage-taken amplifier (008 §5, design intent vs implemented`).

Design history

  • 2023-02-19 — original 2-shield concept: a character begins each fight with 2 shields and cannot recover them except through extraordinary means; a hit dropping HP to 0 loses a shield; losing both = defeated. Some characters might get passive boosts on shield loss; Thane probably gets a 3rd shield; really scary bosses (2nd-phase Lysander) can break shields early.
  • 2023-02-19 — revised to 1 shield: “I’m realising now that death on 2nd shield break is unintuitive” → “Instead have 1 shield which puts you to 1 instead of 0 once” — supersedes the 2-shield version; the proto matches this.
  • 2023-02-20 — shields settled as player-side only and confirmed to reset every fight. CrunchyConniptions proposed doubling down to fully replace the shadow/corpse system, mostly giving frontliners shields.
  • 2023-10-30 — the Wounded mechanic (final solution) was decided: under-half-HP characters become Wounded and more vulnerable; same for enemies; a well-rested counterpart floated; reinforces “end fast”.
  • Later (proto era) — Wounded implemented as a flat +25% damage taken below < 50% of current max HP; shield implemented as the 1-shield drop-to-1 version resetting each encounter (008 §2/§5, ). (Current presentation above.)
