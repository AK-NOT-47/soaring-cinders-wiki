Escalation Mechanic CANON

A per-fight counter that makes battles deadlier the longer they run, pushing them toward a rapid close.

Summary — Escalation starts at 0 and rises +1 per new round, +1 per critical hit (either side), +1 per character defeat (008 §2 Escalation Mechanic; proto src/core/combat/state.ts). As it climbs, enemy attackers gain an accuracy/crit edge; when it first reaches 20, every living hero gets a one-time +2 AP surge (see Action Points (AP) Economy). It is one of the twin “end fast” pressures alongside the Wounded mechanic. First conceived 2023-02-17 as a mechanic that “causes fights to become deadlier as they go on” (M02383, FreshToast, 2023-02-17).

Rules — implemented (proto)

CANON · implemented (008 §2 Escalation Mechanic; proto src/core/combat/state.ts, resolution.ts)

  • Counter: starts at 0; +1 per new round, +1 per critical hit (either side), +1 per character defeat (startNextRound, the crit/death branches in applyEffect).
  • Effect on lethality (enemy-only): only enemy attackers get an escalation edge — enemyEdge = floor(escalation / 4), adding enemyEdge × 2 to both accuracy and critical chance (≈ +2% acc and +2% crit per 4 points, enemy-side only) (resolveAttack). The player side gets no penalty from escalation.
  • Single break point at 20: the first time escalation reaches 20, every living hero gains a one-time +2 AP surge (and maxAp +2 for the fight) (grantApBonus). There is no escalation-10 break point and no recurring “extra AP each turn” in code.
  • Scaling skills (designed, not wired): some characters (Locke, Sable, most powerful enemies) are designed to have skills that increase or scale with escalation; no such skills are wired into the proto yet (008 §2).

Related systems

  • AP: the escalation-20 surge is the proto’s only escalation→AP interaction. See Action Points (AP) Economy.
  • Remnants (design intent, not coded): consuming OR letting a Remnant fade was meant to raise escalation, and character defeats both raise escalation AND leave a Remnant (M02507, M02618, FreshToast, 2023-02-19/20). The proto ships no Remnants — on defeat a character simply dies and the line compacts forward (008 §2 Remnant System; see Combat Position System).
  • Twin “end fast” pressure with the Wounded mechanic (008 §2 Combat Duration).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Escalation curve. The notes specify a graded curve — +2% acc/point up to 10; then +1% acc & crit/point up to 20 — with two break points (10 → +1 AP each; 20 → ongoing extra AP each turn) (M02620–M02624, FreshToast, 2023-02-20). The proto compresses this to a single floor(escalation/4) enemy-only acc+crit edge plus a one-time +2-AP surge at 20. The graded curve and the escalation-10 break point are unimplemented design intent (008 §2, DEBATED design intent vs implemented).
  • DEBATED Renaming “Escalation” was raised (“a bit of a mouthful and could be renamed”) but no replacement was chosen (Part 11). The proto keeps “Escalation” (008 §2).

Design history

  • 2023-02-17 — original concept: “Escalation is a mechanic which causes fights to become deadlier as they go on, bringing them to a more rapid close.” Begins at 0; increases per round, per certain skills, and by 1 on crits or a character dying; increases accuracy/crit/chance-effects by 2% per level; some characters have escalation-scaling skills (M02383, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-19/20 — tied to the Remnant (“Shadow”) system: character defeats raise escalation AND leave a shadow; consuming or letting a shadow fade also raises escalation (M02507, M02618, FreshToast) — the Remnant coupling is unimplemented.
  • 2023-02-20 — the graded two-break-point curve was written out: for each point up to 10, +2% accuracy; for each point up to 20, +1% accuracy and crit; first break point → every character instantly +1 AP; second break point → all characters get an extra AP each turn (M02619–M02624, FreshToast).
  • Later (proto era) — the graded curve was replaced by a single enemy-only floor(escalation/4) acc+crit edge, and the two break points collapsed to a single +2 AP surge at 20; +1 per round / +1 per crit / +1 per defeat survive (008 §2, DEBATED design intent vs implemented). (This is the current presentation above; supersedes the graded curve.)
