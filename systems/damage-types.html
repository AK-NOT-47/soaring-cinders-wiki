Damage Types & Mitigation CANON

Two damage channels — physical and magical — each countered by its own mitigation stat, plus the player-side toolkit for reducing incoming harm.

Summary — Combat damage is physical or magical. Physical damage is mitigated by Damage Resistance (a derived stat under Toughness) and physical armour; magical damage by Damage Resistance and magical armour (008 §5 Damage Types). In the proto these resolve as flat per-character numbers: def mitigates physical, res mitigates magical, each point ≈ 0.5% reduction (008 §5 Damage Formula; proto src/core/combat/resolution.ts). Player-side mitigation splits into four tools — Healing, Redirection, Evasion, and Disruption (008 §5; M05438, FreshToast, 2024-08-20). A DEBATED Divinity-inspired armor/resistance layer was discussed but not settled.

Damage types & the implemented formula

Damage channels (008 §5 Damage Types):

  • Physical Damage — mitigated by Damage Resistance (a derived stat under Toughness) and physical armor.
  • Magical Damage — mitigated by Damage Resistance and magical armor.

CANON · implemented Formula (no notes-era formula exists; these are the proto’s computed values — resolution.ts):

  • Accuracy: roll d100 vs clamp(attacker.acc + status accDelta + enemyEdge×2, 5, 99). Blind = −40 accuracy. A miss deals 0.
  • Crit: roll d100 vs clamp(attacker.crit + enemyEdge×2, 0, 95); a crit multiplies damage by ×1.6 and adds +1 Escalation Mechanic.
  • Magnitude: power × (1 + offense/100) × outMult, then × (1 − mitigation/200) (each mitigation point ≈ 0.5% reduction), then × inMult, then ×1.25 if the defender is Wounded, then ×1.6 if crit, then variance ±12%. offense = the attacker’s str (phys) or mag (mag); mitigation = the defender’s def (phys) or res (mag). Minimum 1 damage on any hit.
  • Protection buff halves the final damage of the next hit, then is consumed.
  • Healing: power × (1 + caster.mag/200) × variance(90–112%), minimum 1.

Player-side mitigation tools

CANON Four tools (008 §5 Damage Mitigation Tools; M05438, M05456–M05466, FreshToast/spirit_noodle, 2024-08-20):

  • Healing — scarce and capped; “like white health in Darkest Dungeon” but actually a degrading-max-HP ceiling (see below).
  • Redirection — a tougher character takes damage aimed at another (e.g. Thane’s taunt).
  • Evasion — reducing the likelihood of a character being hit (avoid rate in FE3H, dodge in any RPG).
  • Disruption — impairing the enemy’s ability to attack successfully or optimally: forcing a different action OR reducing accuracy.

Evasion vs Disruption (008 §5): Evasion = the character becoming harder to hit; Disruption = forcing the enemy to take a different action OR reducing their accuracy (“equivalent of avoid rate in FE3H”).

Healing & Health (degrading max-HP)

CANON Four healing sources, ascending: consumables (weakest, possibly removed), healing skills, the healing Jet, and the river heal (strongest, single-use) (008 §5 Healing & Health, Part 18). See the Jet and River Flask (items batch).

CANON · implemented Degrading Maximum HP: the healable HP ceiling starts full and ratchets down as you take damage, until you rest — healing is a stop-gap, not full recovery (008 §5; proto src/core/campaign/run.ts degradeCeilings, CEILING_DEGRADE_PER_FIGHT = 3, floor 60% of base). See Wounded for the under-half-HP amplifier.

Related systems

  • Shields & Wounded State — the Wounded flag adds ×1.25 to incoming damage; shields negate a lethal hit.
  • Escalation Mechanic — feeds the enemy accuracy/crit enemyEdge.
  • The 8×9 status bank modifies outMult/inMult and accuracy (Strength/Defense Up/Down, Blind, etc.) (008 §6).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Armor / Resistance layer (Divinity-inspired), Part 11. A Divinity-style physical/magical armor layer was discussed: armor gives a bonus to percentage resistances and takes damage simultaneously with health. Worked example: 10% base bleed resist + 20 phys armor → 30% total; after 10 physical damage → 10 phys armor left, 20% bleed resist (008 §5; M02889–M02898, FreshToast, 2023-03-05). Variant naming floated as Physical/Mental rather than Physical/Magical. Not settled, not implemented. See Armor System for the dedicated page on this debated layer and its equipment-model ties.

Design history

  • 2023-03-05 — original armor/resistance discussion: Divinity-style physical/magical armor giving a bonus to percentage resistances and taking damage alongside health; the bleed-resist worked example; possible Physical/Mental naming (M02889–M02898, FreshToast). (Ledger R16.) — remains DEBATED, not adopted.
  • 2023-10-30 — healing reframed: the “white health like Darkest Dungeon” framing was superseded by the degrading-max-HP ceiling; healing bound as a stop-gap (M04814–M04829, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast). (Ledger R26.)
  • 2024-08-20 — the four player-side mitigation tools (Healing, Redirection, Evasion, Disruption) and the Evasion-vs-Disruption clarification were laid out (M05438, M05456–M05466, FreshToast/spirit_noodle). (Ledger R29.)
  • Proto era — the two-channel physical/magical model was implemented as flat def/res mitigation in the damage formula (008 §5 Damage Formula, CANON · implemented). (Current presentation above.)
