Recurring Shopkeeper System

PROPOSEDCaelian OdysseyNotes / Design
Also known as: recurring merchant, plucky merchant, creepy merchant
First conceived 2023-02-15 (M02075, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-10-20 (M04448, FreshToast)

Recurring Shopkeeper System PROPOSED

One single, recurring shopkeeper who “appears everywhere because of game reasons,” always has something new to say, and never blocks your access to the menu — a silly (possibly creepy, possibly scamming) lil guy doing his best.

Summary — A PROPOSED system built around one recurring shopkeeper who shows up throughout the game and always has fresh dialogue, grown out of a “make a game where dialogue never repeats” discussion. He is quest-linked (has some element of quest progression), unvoiced, and not a barrier to the menu — his dialogue happens on the side, Slay-the-Spire style, so it never blocks the shop. The team’s guiding note is “keep it weird”, with an open DEBATED idea that he could be a creepy, subtly-scamming merchant. This is a design intent, not a decided or implemented system.

Core concept PROPOSED

  • One single, recurring shopkeeper who “appears everywhere because of game reasons” and “always has something new to say” — “a silly lil guy doing his best” (M04428, M04429, CrunchyConniptions; M04431, FreshToast, 2023-10-20).
  • Quest-linked: FreshToast had “planned for a shopkeeper who you meet a lot and has some element of quest progression” (M04430, FreshToast, 2023-10-20).

Delivery — Slay-the-Spire style, non-blocking

  • Unvoiced and not a barrier to the menu: “I think if we keep it unvoiced and not a barrier to the menu we good” (M04436, FreshToast, 2023-10-20).
  • Dialogue happens on the side, like Slay the Spire: “could work like slay the spire” / “dialogue on the side” (M04438, M04439, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-20). FreshToast: “Yeah I’d want it exactly like that” (M04441, FreshToast, 2023-10-20). The shop menu is always accessible; the character talk is decorative flavor layered beside it.

“Keep it weird”

The team explicitly embraced the improbability of one merchant being everywhere rather than explaining it away — “keep it weird… its fine… we know its a game… I am always happy to see the merchants” (M04432–M04435, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-20).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Creepy / scamming merchant: “will our shopkeeper also be creepy — like he somehow scamming you, he is getting the good deal not you… neh neh neh, that type” (M04442–M04447, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-20). FreshToast: “We could get one of those I like creepy merchants” (M04448, FreshToast, 2023-10-20). Whether he is played straight-silly or creepy-scamming is unsettled.
  • PROPOSED The exact nature of his “element of quest progression” is unspecified (M04430, FreshToast, 2023-10-20).

Design history

  • 2023-02-15 — first seed: a “recurring, plucky merchant character” (M02075, FreshToast, 2023-02-15; 000 Part 8 §Recurring Merchant, 002 §Recurring Merchant). At this point it is a character sketch, not yet a system.
  • 2023-10-20 — developed into a system during the shopkeeper-dialogue discussion: one shopkeeper who is everywhere with always-new dialogue (M04428–M04431), quest-linked (M04430), delivered unvoiced and Slay-the-Spire-side-style, non-blocking (M04436–M04441), with a “keep it weird” philosophy (M04432–M04435) and an open creepy/scamming variant (M04442–M04448, CrunchyConniptions & FreshToast, 2023-10-20) — current version; supersedes the bare “plucky merchant” sketch by giving it delivery rules and a quest hook.
