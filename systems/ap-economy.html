Action Points (AP) Economy

CANONCross-IterationMixed
Also known as: AP, Advantage Point System, Action Points, Zeal
First conceived 2023-02-02 (M00858, CrunchyConniptions) · last in chat 2023-02-20 (M02627)

Action Points (AP) Economy CANON

The per-turn action budget that gates how much a character can do in a round — the currency of every skill.

Summary — In the shipped proto, AP is a flat per-round budget that REFILLS each round — not a pool that starts at zero and is earned over the fight. Each hero has maxAp = 3; enemies have 2 (the boss has 4). Skills cost 1–3 AP; finishers cost 4+ AP and are gated to round 4+ or Escalation Mechanic ≥ 20 (008 §2 Action Points System; proto src/core/combat/content.ts, state.ts). Unspent AP does not carry over. The original notes-era design was the opposite — a per-character pool starting at zero that certain actions generate and powerful actions spend (M00858, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-02); the proto rejected that generate-and-spend model in favour of the flat refill.

Rules — implemented (proto)

CANON · implemented (008 §2 Action Points System; proto src/core/combat/state.ts)

  • Budget: heroes maxAp = 3; enemies 2; boss 4. At the start of every round, living characters’ AP is refilled to maxAp (startNextRound sets ap: c.maxAp).
  • Costs: skills cost 1–3 AP; finishers cost 4+ AP and are hard-gated to round 4+ OR escalation ≥ 20 (canPayAndCast).
  • No carry-over: unspent AP is reset by the per-round refill. A character’s turn ends automatically once their AP reaches 0 (reduceUseSkill calls endActorTurn when ap === 0).
  • Escalation surge (once): when Escalation Mechanic first reaches 20, every living hero gains a one-time +2 AP, and their maxAp is permanently raised +2 for the rest of the fight (grantApBonus, AP_BONUS_AT_ESCALATION = 20, AP_BONUS_AMOUNT = 2).
  • No AP economy verbs: there are no AP-generating skills, no held/auto-trigger skills, and no AP steal/remove effects in the proto (008 §2).

Related systems

  • The whole system exists to serve the “end fast” philosophy: skill strength was meant to scale with AP cost, and “if most fights end around turn 4, AP 4+ skills should be finishers” (M01868, M01869, FreshToast, 2023-02-13). See Escalation Mechanic.
  • Nearly all character skills consume AP; each weapon subtype has only 3–4 “neutral” skills (M01364, M01365, FreshToast, 2023-02-10).
  • Locke has a flame-based skill that grants AP to an ally (008 §Affinity Magic; M03070, spirit_noodle, 2023-03-18) — the “giving/taking/sharing” theme.

Open questions

  • DEBATED Generate-and-spend vs flat refill. The notes describe AP as a per-character pool that starts at zero and is gained over the fight (“Assume gain AP every turn”; AP steal rare and enemy-only; escalation break points feed AP). The proto rejects this in favour of the flat 3-per-round refill with one escalation-20 surge, reconciled in the GameBible: “3 AP per turn… Always full refund. Every turn. No carry over” (008 §2, DEBATED design intent vs implemented). Both are recorded: the pool is the notes design intent, the refill is what the game implements.
  • DEBATED Renaming AP to “Zeal” was the leading proposal (over Spirit / Grit / Tenacity), to tie it to a stamina/breath ludonarrative theme, but was not finalized (Part 11). It collides with the existing “Zeal” Acuity Jet shard (boosts initiative, Part 14), a clash that must be resolved before adoption. The proto keeps the plain term “AP” (008 §2).

Design history

  • 2023-02-02 — original concept (“Advantage Point System”): starts at zero per battle, accrues over the battle, unique per character (unlike Voice of Cards); some actions generate AP, stronger actions spend AP (M00858, CrunchyConniptions). (Ledger R05.)
  • 2023-02-10 — “maybe nearly all character skills use AP” (M01365, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-13 — AP scaling settled toward finishers: skill strength should increase (near-exponentially) per AP cost assuming AP-per-turn gain; 4+ AP skills = finishers because most fights end around turn 4 (M01868, M01869, FreshToast) — this survives into the implemented finisher gate.
  • 2023-02-13 — AP steal/remove effects are very rare and only available to enemies (M01872, FreshToast) — notes intent; not coded in the proto.
  • 2023-02-20 — AP listed alongside escalation/shields as a core system; briefly debated whether escalation was redundant with AP (“If anything I’d drop escalation… since it has the same goal as AP”; “AP’s pretty core rn”) (M02572, M02610, M02611, M02613, M02627, FreshToast).
  • Later (proto era) — the generate-and-spend pool was replaced by a flat 3-per-round refill (heroes) with a single escalation-20 +2 AP surge; unspent AP does not carry over (008 §2, DEBATED design intent vs implemented). (This is the current presentation above; supersedes the zero-start pool.)
