Accessibility & Combat-Signal Taxonomy

Skyward CindersNotes / Design
Also known as: Floating Status Text, Combat Signal Taxonomy, Offensive/Defensive Signals
First conceived 2023-02-10

The coarse offensive/defensive grouping that combat signals fold into, plus an optional auto-fading floating-text aid for readability and accessibility.

Summary — Enemy/ability signals group into a coarse taxonomy — offensive (attack / debuff), defensive (defend / buff), or both (2023-02-10; 008 §7). Alongside this, an accessibility aid was proposed: optional floating text explaining things like status effects that is small and fades on its own after a short time without requiring interaction, toggleable in settings (2023-02-10). This coarse taxonomy complements the detailed Enemy Intent icon set — it is the grouping the signals fold into, not a replacement for the icons.

Combat-signal taxonomy

Enemy/ability signals group into three coarse buckets (2023-02-10; 008 §7):

  • Offensive — attack / debuff.
  • Defensive — defend / buff.
  • Or both.

This is the coarser layer above the fine-grained Enemy Intent Indicator taxonomy (the Core Indicators and the 23-icon set). The two are complementary and should not be duplicated: the icon set says exactly what an enemy is about to do; this taxonomy is the offensive/defensive category it lands in.

Interacting with telegraphed intent

  • There are abilities that do extra damage to enemies that are going to attack — i.e. abilities that interact with an enemy’s telegraphed intent (2023-02-10; 008 §7).
  • No purely self-defensive abilities — except something like Thane’s taunt (2023-02-10; 008 §7).

Accessibility — optional floating status text

An accessibility aid, distinct from the combat telegraphs (2023-02-10; 008 §7):

  • Optional reminders that can be turned off in settings.
  • Small floating text explaining things like status effects.
  • Fades on its own after a short time without requiring interaction — no click/dismiss needed.
  • Purpose: accessibility / readability.

(Compare the proto’s implemented status bank (008 §6 Status Effects System) — the floating text would explain those effects to the player; the auto-fade text itself is not coded, it is design intent.)

Related systems

  • Enemy Intent Indicator System — the fine-grained intent icons this taxonomy groups.
  • The player-side rank-targeting / readability UI is a parallel readability system (008 §7 Rank-Targeting).
  • General accessibility caution in the notes: “just be wary to not lose too much accessibility” (2023-03-17).

Open questions

  • The floating-text aid and the offensive/defensive taxonomy are design intents, not decided/implemented systems; exact wording, timing of the fade, and how the taxonomy maps onto the final icon set are unspecified (008 §7).

Design history

  • 2023-02-10 — In one thread FreshToast proposes: vague enemy signals grouped as offensive (attack/debuff) / defensive (defend/buff) / both; abilities that do extra damage to enemies about to attack; no purely self-defensive abilities except Thane’s taunt; and an optional, settings-toggleable floating status text that fades on its own for accessibility (2023-02-10; 008 §7).
