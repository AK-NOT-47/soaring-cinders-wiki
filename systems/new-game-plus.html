Also known as: NG+

New Game+ System PROPOSED

A Fire-Emblem-Three-Houses-inspired second-playthrough layer where a special resource lets you buy skills early and gamble on random gear rolls.

Summary — In New Game+, players earn and spend a special resource to buy skills early and acquire random rolls on gear at varying rarities, inspired by Fire Emblem: Three Houses (M05684, FreshToast, 2024-09-04; 008 §15). Discovering a piece of gear unlocks it in a gacha menu for repeat random rolls; skills get cheaper the closer you are to their natural unlock; and obsolete gear can be broken down into the resource. The whole design assumes a classic levels-and-rarities gear model in the late game and is not implemented in the proto (008 §preamble lists NG+ among systems “not yet coded”).

Core concept

Inspired by Fire Emblem: Three Houses (M05684, 2024-09-04; 008 §15):

  • Earn and spend a special resource in NG+.
  • Buy skills early.
  • Acquire random rolls on gear at varying rarities.

Gear system (NG+)

  • Once you discover a piece of gear at any rarity, unlock it for the gacha menu, where you can purchase a random roll of it at available rarities (008 §15).
  • Rarities for purchase unlock as you progress through the game.
  • Gear found in the previous playthrough is available for purchase.
  • Lowest rarity is especially cheap — to encourage experimentation, or early acquisition of usually-unavailable build options.
  • Unique pieces of gear are available but at a higher price and with fixed statistics.
  • Gear purchased scales to level, and all gear can have its level (but not rarity) increased via the new resource.

Skills purchase (NG+)

  • Skills are expensive, but the closer you get to a skill’s natural unlock, the easier it is to acquire (008 §15).
  • Decision-unlock skills remain unavailable for purchase — but you can acquire the item used to get alternative-path skills, meaning all 14 path skills can be unlocked in NG+.

Resource acquisition (NG+)

  • Earned naturally alongside other rewards, but most efficiently acquired from quest completion and filling out the bestiary (008 §15).
  • You can now break down gear into this resource in addition to what is naturally found — so obsolete gear serves an additional purpose and can always be converted into something useful.

Assumptions

The whole NG+ design leans on how gear scales into the late game (M05685, M05686, FreshToast, 2024-09-04; 008 §15):

  • “This kinda assumes a lot about how gear scales into late game.”
  • “But I assume it all has classic levels & rarities” — i.e. levels inflate the numbers a bit and higher rarities give more/better bonuses.

This ties NG+ to the still-unimplemented Tier + Quality + Rune equipment model (see Progression & Difficulty and 008 §9 Equipment Progression), which is likewise design-target-only.

Open questions

  • PROPOSED Whether gear actually uses “classic levels & rarities” late-game — the entire NG+ economy depends on an assumption FreshToast flagged as unconfirmed (M05685-M05686, 2024-09-04; 008 §15 Assumptions).

Design history

  • 2024-09-04 — New Game+ proposed in full: a Three-Houses-inspired special resource to buy skills early and roll random gear; the gacha-unlock, cheap-lowest-rarity, unique-fixed-stat, level-scaling, skill-cost-near-unlock, all-14-path-skills, and gear-breakdown rules (M05684, FreshToast, 2024-09-04; 008 §15).
  • 2024-09-04 — Follow-up caveat: the design “assumes a lot about how gear scales into late game,” presuming classic levels & rarities (M05685, M05686, FreshToast, 2024-09-04; 008 §15 Assumptions).
  • 2026-06-16 — Implemented-code pass confirms NG+ is not yet coded — it remains notes-era design intent (008 §preamble).
