Reserve Skills System (Locke’s Borrow) DEBATED

Locke’s signature party mechanic: she borrows a designated skill from a benched ally, once per battle — leadership and versatility over personal power.

Summary — The Reserve Skills System lets Locke borrow a skill from a character in reserve to use, once per battle; each character has a designated reserve skill that Locke can call on (M01391, FreshToast, 2023-02-10; 008 §5). It exists to express Locke’s design identity: her strength is leadership and versatility rather than personal power, making her dependent on and defined by her party (M01392, FreshToast, 2023-02-10). The mechanic is designed but not implemented — it is documented in the GameBible and GAME_DESIGN.md §5.8 but sits on the proto’s Still-deferred list, absent from the combat reducer and UI (008 §5, DEBATED design intent vs implemented).

Description — the borrow mechanic (notes-design)

  • What it does: Locke borrows one skill from a character in reserve (a benched party member), usable once per battle (M01391, FreshToast, 2023-02-10; 000 Part 5 §Reserve Skills System).
  • Designated reserve skill: each character has a single designated reserve skill that Locke may use — not their whole kit (M01391; 000 Part 5).
  • Design intent: reflects that “Locke’s strength is in her leadership and versatility, rather than personal strength and thus dependent on her party” (M01392, FreshToast, 2023-02-10). It complements Locke’s support identity — she “warps around her party with a variety of skills and useful support effects” (M01881, FreshToast, 2023-02-13).

Rules / status

DEBATED design intent vs implemented — not yet wired in. The borrow-one-ally’s-skill mechanic is designed (GAME_DESIGN.md §5.8) but explicitly listed as Still-deferred; it is not in the combat reducer or UI (008 §5; proto GAME_DESIGN.md §17 "Still deferred"). Locke’s implemented kit is instead a fixed spear set — Pierce, Sunder, Phalanx, and the finisher Emberlance (008 §5; proto src/core/combat/content.ts). The exact implementation of reserve skills was flagged unresolved in the notes themselves (000 Part 5).

Related systems

  • Ties into the broader borrowing-power motif: in the two-path lore, “the original Undying is very good at borrowing powers” — a thread that later informs Locke’s leadership-through-borrowing design (M00244, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-06-26; 008 §10).
  • Layered on the position system and reserve/bench roster — a benched character is a “character in reserve.” See also the DEBATED team skills built around party pairings (008 §Team Skills).
  • Locke’s kit and role: see Locke and character skills.

Open questions

  • DEBATED Exact implementation is unresolved — the notes explicitly list “exact implementation of reserve skills” as an open item (000 Part 5); the proto ships Locke’s four spear skills only (008 §5).
  • DEBATED Whether the once-per-battle borrow ever ships, given it’s on the Still-deferred list (008 §5; GAME_DESIGN.md §17).

Design history

  • 2023-02-10 — original concept: FreshToast proposes “RESERVE SKILLS” — “locke gets to borrow a skill from a character in reserve to use, once per battle. Each character has a designated reserve skill, which Locke can use,” justified by Locke’s leadership/versatility identity over personal strength (M01390–M01392, FreshToast, 2023-02-10). (Origin.)
  • 2023-02-13 — reinforced by Locke’s support-warper framing: she “has some movement and modular abilities but does not need to even slot them” and “warps around her party with a variety of skills and useful support effects” (M01880–M01881, FreshToast, 2023-02-13).
  • 2026-06-16 — implemented-code pass: the borrow mechanic is documented but placed on the Still-deferred list; Locke’s implemented kit is the Pierce / Sunder / Phalanx / Emberlance spear set instead (008 §5; proto content.ts, GAME_DESIGN.md §17). (Current status: designed, not implemented.)
