PROPOSEDSkyward CindersNotes / Design

Party Consultation — Outcome Resolution PROPOSED

The dialogue mechanic where the player has Locke consult exactly one party member at a prompt — and the rules for how that choice pays off.

Summary — At certain prompts the player has Locke consult another party member, and the choice resolves against a hidden “correct answer” (M01893, FreshToast, 2023-02-14; 008 §20). Outcomes are usually neutral; a smart or lucky pick yields a good outcome; it rarely backfires humorously, always in good fun. Crucially, there is a correct answer, and one or more characters knows it — so consulting the right party member is rewarded. This page records only the outcome-resolution rules; the roster and flavour of the mechanic live in the narrative/dialogue docs.

Rules / effects (outcome resolution)

From the 2023-02-14 exchange (008 §20 PROPOSED):

  • Usually neutral — “most of the time it’s a neutral outcome” (M01894, FreshToast, 2023-02-14).
  • Smart or lucky → good outcome — “pick something smart or lucky and you get a good outcome” (M01894).
  • Rarely backfires, humorously — “rarely it backfires humorously when somebody messes up,” and “it’s always in good fun” (M01895, FreshToast; M01896, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14).
  • There is a correct answer — “but there is a correct answer” (M01897, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14).
  • One or more characters knows it — “one or more of the characters knows it” (M01898, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14). Consulting the character who knows is the path to the good outcome.

The team judged it “neat and easy to do honestly” (M01899, FreshToast, 2023-02-14) — i.e. low-cost, high-flavour.

Related systems

  • The consult prompt is a dialogue mechanic; the recurring Shopkeeper and the “dialogue never repeats” thread sit in the same conversational-systems space (008 §20).
  • Because the player picks exactly one party member, the mechanic leans on party composition — see Game Type & Structure (Overview) and the party roster in Locke’s circle.

Open questions

  • PROPOSED The mechanic is a design intent, not a decided/implemented system; exact prompt frequency, how “correct answer” knowledge is surfaced to the player, and the roster of consultable characters are not specified in 008 (008 §20).

Design history

  • 2023-02-14 — Consultation outcome model defined in a single exchange: neutral by default, good on a smart/lucky pick, rare humorous backfire, and a hidden correct answer that one or more characters knows; deemed “neat and easy to do” (M01893-M01899, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14; 008 §20).
