Armor System

Skyward CindersNotes / Design
Also known as: Physical/Magical Armor
First conceived 2023-02-11

Divinity-style Physical/Magical armour that provides percentage resistances and depletes alongside health — discussed but never settled or implemented.

Summary — Armour is modelled after Divinity: Original Sin’s two-track armour, but repurposed to grant percentage resistances rather than being a separate health pool. Armour takes damage simultaneously with health, and as it depletes the resistances it provides shrink. The two tracks are Physical and Magical (with “Physical and Mental” floated as an alternative framing). All of the Tier / Quality / Rune rules apply to armour as they do to weapons.

Status: this Divinity-style armour layer was discussed on 2023-03-05 but never settled or implemented — the same idea appears (with the identical bleed-resist worked example) on Damage Types & Mitigation, which is the canonical combat-mitigation page. The shipped proto instead mitigates damage with flat Physical/Magical stats (def/res), not a depleting percentage-resistance armour pool. This page records the concept and its equipment-model ties; see Damage Types & Mitigation for the implemented model.

Rules / Effects

  • Two tracks — Physical and Magical armour, drawn from Divinity, but here they provide a bonus to percentage resistances and take damage simultaneously to health (2023-03-05; 006 §Physical/Magical Armor). Alternative framing floated: Physical and Mental instead of Physical and Magical.
  • Depletes with health — armour takes damage simultaneously to health, not as a separate shield pool ahead of it (2023-03-05).
  • All equipment systems apply to armour — Tier (material base stats), Quality (Bronze/Silver/Gold bandwidth), and Rune slots all apply to armour as well as weapons (2023-02-11; 006 §All systems apply to armour as well).

Resistance mechanics example

Worked example from the notes showing how armour and a base resistance combine, and how the resist erodes as armour is chipped away (2023-03-05; 006 §Resistance Mechanics Examples):

  • 10% base bleed resist
  • 20 physical armour → 30% total bleed resist
  • 10 physical damage dealt → 10 physical armour left → 20% bleed resistance
  • (with 20% bleed resist as the fixed floor)

This worked example is transcribed from the notes’ shorthand; the exact formula relating armour points to resistance % is illustrative, not fully specified.
