Affinity System CANON

Every person is born attuned to one of three elemental affinities — fire, cold, or electricity — which shapes the magic they can channel.

Summary — Magic affinities are the working magic system for the main game (007 §Affinity System). Each character is attuned to one of three affinities — Fiery (Pyrokinetic), Cold (Cryokinetic), or Electric (Electrokinetic) — and characters begin the game already “awakened,” with their affinity and the barest knowledge of magic (M04315–M04316, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). Which affinity a person receives appears to be largely random; the academic study of affinities is called Thaumaturgy, and its practitioners have found no reliable predictor for who gets what (M03051, FreshToast, 2023-03-15).

The three affinities

Affinity Kinesis Personality association (theory, dismissed)
Fiery Pyrokinetic passionate & loyal
Cold Cryokinetic thoughtful & relaxed
Electric Electrokinetic creative & curious

The kinetic terms Pyrokinetic / Cryokinetic / Electrokinetic were coined alongside the affinity list (M03053, FreshToast, 2023-03-15). The personality pairings are a theory held by some, not established fact — see Origin below.

Note — Ruby’s listed Electric affinity is complicated by her identity as a magical item/catalyst who “cannot channel magic herself” (007 §Character Traits); the implemented proto instead ships her as a spell-casting Mender. Recorded as-listed here; the conflict lives on her character page.

Origin of affinity

Affinity is largely random (M03051, FreshToast, 2023-03-15). Several explanations have been proposed and dismissed:

  • Temperament — the passionate/thoughtful/creative pairings above; “often dismissed as vague and unjustified among academics in Thaumaturgy.”
  • Circumstances of one’s birth — time, place, etc.
  • Heritage.

“Yet, no consistent link has been found which leads researchers to think it is largely random” (M03051, FreshToast, 2023-03-15).

Thaumaturgy (academic study)

Thaumaturgy is the scholarly discipline that studies affinities (M03051, FreshToast, 2023-03-15). Its academics are the ones who dismiss the temperament theory and weigh the birth-circumstance and heritage theories, ultimately leaning toward affinity being largely random. See the broader magic-culture context in Sorcery (Luminarium System).

Testing an affinity — the Aeromantia plant

CANON Affinity is tested with an Aeromantia plant: a dandelion-like plant that reacts to your affinity when blown on (M03055–M03056, FreshToast, 2023-03-15). It was conceived as the setting’s equivalent of the Hunter × Hunter water-divination test — but framed around wind rather than water (M03054, FreshToast, 2023-03-15).

Baseline (main game)

CANON Characters start with their affinity and the barest knowledge of magic; each has already “awakened” (M04314–M04316, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Skill-design philosophy

The affinities carry an overarching theme of giving, taking and sharing (M03070, spirit_noodle, 2023-03-18):

  • Fire skills — an offensive or supportive set of skills; burning; AoEs (area-of-effect).
  • Lightning/Electric skills — about chaining attacks; “lightning is the link between two things.”
  • Overarching theme — giving, taking and sharing.

Locke’s signature flame skill that grants AP to an ally is cited as an example of this “giving” theme (M03070, spirit_noodle, 2023-03-18; see Locke Evertide).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Alternative framework — Genesis / Stasis / Nexus. A parallel triad tied to the giving/taking/sharing theme was proposed: Genesis (Pushing / Giving), Stasis (Pulling / Taking), Nexus (Sharing / Equalising) (M03732, spirit_noodle, 2023-10-06). The notes never clearly pick one as final. Pyro/Cryo/Electro is presented as the working system; Genesis/Stasis/Nexus is recorded as an alternative conception (007 §Genesis/Stasis/Nexus). This mirrors the same dual-framing flagged in 001.
  • DEBATED Ruby’s affinity vs. her catalyst identity — whether Ruby “has” Electric affinity or merely channels for others is unresolved between the affinity list, her lore identity, and the proto (007 §Character Traits; see Ruby (Skyward Cinders)).

Design history

  • 2023-03-15 — original concept: three known affinities (Fiery / Cold / Electric) with a per-character roster, the Pyro/Cryo/Electro kinetic naming, the temperament/birth/heritage theories and the “largely random” conclusion, the Thaumaturgy discipline, and the Aeromantia-plant test — all posted together (M03051–M03056, FreshToast). This is essentially the current system.
  • 2023-03-18 — skill-design philosophy layered on: giving/taking/sharing, fire = offense/support/AoE, lightning = chaining/links, and Locke’s AP-granting flame skill as the “giving” exemplar (M03070, spirit_noodle).
  • 2023-10-06 — the alternative Genesis / Stasis / Nexus triad proposed by spirit_noodle as a conceptual parallel to Fiery/Cold/Electric; never resolved against the working system (M03732) — supersedes nothing; recorded as an open alternative.
  • 2023-10-17 — baseline confirmed: characters begin already awakened, holding their affinity and the barest knowledge of magic (M04314–M04316, FreshToast).
