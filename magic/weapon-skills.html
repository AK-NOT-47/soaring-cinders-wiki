Weapon Skills (Designed Roster) EVOLVED

The per-weapon skill families the team designed on 2023-02-16 — Sword, Axe, Staff, Longbow, and Spear each get a basic attack plus a signature skill. The runnable proto ships a different, synthesized skill list, so these designs are documented intent rather than implemented content.

Summary — On 2023-02-16 the team laid out weapon-typed skills: Melee gets an axe, the Generalist a sword, the Mage a staff, and Ranged a bow (M02156, FreshToast, 2023-02-16). Each weapon has a basic attack and a signature skill built around rank (front/back) and positional reach. Most of these named designs (Slash’s overkill carry, Pierce’s resistance-halving, Wild Swing, Snipe, Modular AoE, Leaping Strike, Balanced Slash) are not implemented as written — the proto uses a separately synthesized roster (007 §Implemented Skill Roster; DEBATED design intent vs implemented). They remain valid future content.

Sword skills (Generalist)

  • Slash — single-target front attack; deals half overkill damage to the next enemy behind the target (M02157, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Pierce — single-target, front rank, low damage but halves the enemy’s damage resistance (M02157, 2023-02-16). Note: the proto’s “Pierce” is a flat power-7 jab with no resistance-halving — the designed effect is unimplemented (007 §Implemented Skill Roster).

Axe skills (Melee)

  • Slash — single-target melee attack; deals half overkill damage to the next enemy behind (M02158, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Wild Swing — a cleave attack that hits the two front enemies (M02158, 2023-02-16). “Cleave” is the keyword for a skill that hits all/multiple targets rather than one (007 §Keywords).

Staff skills (Mage)

  • Magic Attack — magic attack against any target (M02159, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Modular AoE — does damage to both front or both back depending on the caster’s rank (M02159, 2023-02-16) — an example of the modular skill system.

Longbow skills (Ranged)

  • Ranged Attack — ranged attack against any target (M02160, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Snipe — ranged attack, back rank only, high damage (M02160, 2023-02-16).

Spear skills (Thane)

Both are modular skills framed “(Front – Back)”:

  • Leaping Strike — (Front → Back) single-target attack (M02171, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).
  • Balanced Slash — (Front → Back) single-target attack with increased crit chance based on the target’s resistance (50% of Resistance) (M02172, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).

Thane’s spear kit is the canonical worked example of modular position-based skills (007 §Modular Skill System). See Character Skills (Per-Character Rosters) for Thane’s fuller kit (Pierce Attack, Taunt).

Keywords

  • Cleave — makes a skill hit all/multiple targets rather than one (007 §Keywords).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Design intent vs implemented: none of these named families ship in the proto as written; the proto’s Pierce/Sunder/Phalanx/Emberlance (Locke), Cleave/Bulwark/Rending Blow/Earthsplitter (Thane), etc. are synthesized names for the vertical slice (007 §Implemented Skill Roster). Implemented skills carry rank constraints only (fromRanks/targetRanks) with no per-rank effect deltas, so the designed Front/Back effect-switching remains intent (007 §Implemented Skill Roster).

Design history

  • 2023-02-16 — full weapon-skill roster laid out in one sitting: weapon-to-class mapping (M02156), Sword (M02157), Axe (M02158), Staff (M02159), Longbow (M02160) by FreshToast; Thane’s Spear skills Leaping Strike and Balanced Slash by CrunchyConniptions (M02171–M02172) — all same day.
  • 2026-06-16 (doc-era reconciliation) — checked against the runnable proto; the designed families were found not implemented as written, superseded in-engine by a synthesized roster, and re-tagged DEBATED design intent vs implemented (007 §Implemented Skill Roster). The designed families are retained as future content.
