Judgment Cut PROPOSED

A melee attack that can strike any target for solid damage at the cost of AP — an unassigned skill the team wanted in the game but never pinned to a character.

Summary — Judgment Cut is a melee skill that “targets anyone” and deals solid damage while spending AP (M02053–M02054, FreshToast, 2023-02-14). It began as a one-word reference to the Devil May Cry / Vergil move (M01292, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-09) and was later fleshed out into a concrete skill concept. Which character receives it was explicitly left open — “Some character, I don’t care who, gets a judgment cut attack” (M02053).

Description

  • Melee attack that can target anyone on the field, regardless of rank (M02054, FreshToast, 2023-02-14).
  • Deals solid damage but uses AP (M02054).
  • Nature of “targets anyone” makes it a rank-agnostic reach skill — unusual for a melee ability, which normally reaches only the front (see Weapon Skills (Designed Roster)).

Rules / Effects

  • AP-gated: consumes AP like other powerful actions; not a free basic attack (M02054, 2023-02-14; see Action Points (AP) Economy).
  • Damage: described only as “solid” — no numbers were assigned in the notes (M02054).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Which character gets it? Left deliberately undecided — “I don’t care who” (M02053, FreshToast, 2023-02-14). Still listed under Unresolved Questions in 007 (“Which character gets judgment cut attack”) (007 §Unresolved Questions).
  • The proto ships no skill named Judgment Cut; the implemented finishers are per-character (Locke’s Emberlance, Thane’s Earthsplitter, etc. — see Weapon Skills (Designed Roster) and 007 §Implemented Skill Roster). Judgment Cut remains an unimplemented concept.

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — first appears as a bare reference: “Judgement cut” (M01292, spirit_noodle) — a nod to the DMC/Vergil move, no mechanics attached yet.
  • 2023-02-14 — fleshed out into a skill concept: a melee attack targeting anyone, solid damage, costs AP, character-agnostic (M02053–M02054, FreshToast) — supersedes the bare reference; adds all current mechanics.
