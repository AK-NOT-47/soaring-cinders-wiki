The Flow

PROPOSEDCaelian OdysseyNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-10-13 (M03779, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-10-17 (M04227, FreshToast)

The Flow PROPOSED

The magical current a sorcerer channels — the raw substance of Sorcery in the Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium setting, and the source of that world’s river-and-current aesthetic.

Summary — Flow is the magical energy a sorcerer draws on and channels to cast (001 §Sorcery). A sorcerer’s aptitude with Flow has two facets: Capacity (how much magic you can produce — “capable of big magic”) and Channeling efficiency (how quickly you tire and how fast you cast) (001 §Sorcery; 007 §Sorcery Flow facet). Flow is scoped to the separate iteration Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium rather than main-game canon (001 §Luminarium). It also names the general aesthetic of the setting — the river-and-current motif the team leaned into (M03779, FreshToast, 2023-10-13).

The two facets

PROPOSED A sorcerer’s relationship to Flow breaks into two measures (001 §Sorcery; 007 §Sorcery Flow facet):

  • Capacity — how much magic one can channel; high capacity means one is “capable of big magic.”
  • Channeling efficiency — how tired you get and how fast you cast. Low efficiency means you fatigue quickly and cast slowly.

These are independent: a sorcerer can be enormous in Capacity yet poor in efficiency. Pandora is the canonical example — she “has immense Flow capacity (capable of big magic) but struggles to channel Flow efficiently (gets tired fast and casts slow),” and compensates with cunning and her inventions (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). Her uncontrolled capacity is precisely what makes her a danger to herself and others and drives her to the Luminarium academy (M04347–M04351, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Aesthetic and setting role

“Flow” doubles as the name for the general aesthetic of the world. When the team discussed making rivers — especially the big one — culturally important across Khorath (river-related holidays and sayings), FreshToast noted “This works pretty well with Flow and the general aesthetic of the setting” (M03779, FreshToast, 2023-10-13). The current/river motif of Flow interlocks with the river culture documented in the world’s locations and the River Flask healing item.

Related systems

  • Sorcery (Luminarium System) — the wider magic-culture of the Luminarium (Glyphs, facets, Caelian standards); Flow is the energy those systems channel. Each of the 4 Boyes represents “a general facet of Sorcery and magic” (M04226–M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Affinity System — the main-game elemental magic system; distinct from Luminarium Sorcery/Flow, though the two iterations share the same broader world.
  • Pandora — the character built to embody the Capacity-vs-efficiency split.

Open questions

  • PROPOSED The Flow’s exact mechanics, and whether “Capacity / Channeling efficiency” become in-game stats, are unspecified in the notes — only the two-facet concept and the Pandora example are given (001 §Sorcery; M04227).

Design history

  • 2023-10-13 — “Flow” first named, as the aesthetic/throughline of the setting, in the context of river culture: “This works pretty well with Flow and the general aesthetic of the setting” (M03779, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-17 — Flow given mechanical shape via Pandora: the Capacity / Channeling efficiency split, with Pandora as the high-capacity / low-efficiency exemplar (M04227, FreshToast). Later formalized in 001 as the two facets of Sorcery.
