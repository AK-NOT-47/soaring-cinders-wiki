Character Skills (Per-Character Rosters)

Skyward CindersNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-02-11

The exploration and combat skills sketched per party member — mostly proposed in the 2023-02-11 party-management dump, with per-character exploration skills flagged as “heavily debated” and never finalized.

Summary — Each party member was given draft exploration skills (for dungeon-crawling: discovery, risk mitigation, movement) and, in some cases, combat traits. Most stem from spirit_noodle’s 2023-02-11 party-management dump, with follow-on additions by CrunchyConniptions the same day. Whether exploration skills survive is **** — advocated passionately as Genshin-style party management, but flagged for scope creep (007 §Exploration Skills). Combat skills here are the notes’ design intent; the proto ships a different combat roster (see 007 §Implemented Skill Roster and Weapon Skills (Designed Roster)).

Named weapon skills (Slash, Pierce, Wild Swing, Snipe, Modular AoE, Leaping Strike, Balanced Slash) live on Weapon Skills (Designed Roster); the modular-skill mechanic on Skill System (Slots, Modular Skills, Costs); Judgment Cut is an unassigned skill.

Locke

  • Reserve borrow (combat): Locke can borrow a skill from a character in reserve, once per battle; each character has a designated reserve skill she can use (007 §Locke). Ties to her leadership/versatility role (2023-02-13).
  • Flame AP-grant skill: a flame-based skill that gives AP to another character — an expression of the affinity theme “giving, taking and sharing.” See Action Points (AP) Economy.
  • ** Weakness-finding:** “Maybe Locke’s thing is identifying weaknesses” — she finds enemy weak points (e.g. golem joints) and does extra damage depending on position and ability used. Liked, not finalized.
  • Warps around the party with support-effect skills; has movement/modular abilities but does not need to slot them (2023-02-13).

Thane

  • Exploration skill: provides a +5 defense buff (2023-02-11).
  • Combat — Pierce Attack: ignores a portion of enemy damage resistance (007 §Thane; cf. the Sword Pierce on Weapon Skills (Designed Roster)).
  • Combat — Taunt: makes enemies more prone to attack Thane (007 §Thane).
  • Spear modular skills: Leaping Strike and Balanced Slash — see Weapon Skills (Designed Roster) (2023-02-16).

Isaac

  • Exploration — see illusory walls: Isaac has an exploration skill that lets him see / spot illusory walls (2023-02-11). A later reframing gives him “sees secrets better but the torch reach is shortened heavily” (2023-02-11).
  • Combat role: magic specialist (Staff / Resolve), struggles to move in combat (more specialized) (007 §Isaac). Grew up a child magic prodigy who discovered the ability to manipulate reality with his mind (007 §Isaac).

Leonard

(Attribution corrected — the two magic-vision skills come from different authors on different messages.)

  • Exploration — light up a larger area: Leonard should be able to light up a larger area with his magic spells (2023-02-11).
  • Exploration — see in the dark: doesn’t need a torch — he can see in the dark with magic, useful against light-sensitive enemies that would otherwise spot your torch first (2023-02-11).
  • Combat — decoy: can create a decoy rather than turn invisible; the decoy is occasionally used for comedic effect in tandem with his social anxiety (007 §Leonard).
  • Has the best mobility at high AP cost; more modular skills (2023-02-13; 007 §Leonard).

Clara

  • Exploration — long-range initiate: Clara has a skill that initiates attacks from a longer distance (2023-02-11); reframed as “can attack from a distance, but then you are completely unable to detect traps” (2023-02-11).
  • Combat — delayed high-crit shot: a ranged attack with a 1-turn delay, high damage, that almost always crits; also has a retreat shot (007 §Clara).
  • Initiative: her initiative increases in the back position (007 §Clara).

Sable

  • Exploration — traps & lures: Sable can place traps or lure enemies, and can spot other traps herself (2023-02-11).
  • Combat: most mobile party member and the most AP-efficient; more modular skills; some directly scale with Escalation Mechanic (2023-02-13; 007 §Sable).

Ruby

  • Exploration — decoy copy: Ruby can send a copy of herself out so enemies attack the copy instead of the party (2023-02-11).
  • Exploration — see rare metals : “Maybe Ruby has an exploration skill that allows her to see rare metals” (2023-02-11) — fits the alchemy theme, not confirmed.
  • Combat identity is contested: notes give her Strength (a magic catalyst that cannot channel magic herself); the proto implements her as a spell-casting Mender (007 §Ruby; ).

Blood Magic Character (unnamed)

  • Someone gets an ability akin to blood magic: they can sacrifice a bit of their blood/HP to refill the jar (the healing Jet / resource). Very risky, a very big health drain (2023-02-11).
  • But the character can also retrieve blood — “it’s tricky,” designed to incentivize actually using him rather than treating him as a consistent sacrificial pawn.
  • ** Alternative:** FreshToast preferred the character sacrifice health to heal another directly rather than interact with the Jet/estus mechanic (2023-02-11). Character never assigned — placeholder.

Open questions

  • Do exploration skills survive at all? Passionately advocated (2023-02-11) but flagged for scope creep / overcomplication / turning into a platformer; alternative is character-agnostic puzzle dungeon design (007 §Exploration Skills). “Whether to keep exploration skills” is still open (007 §Unresolved Questions).
  • Which character gets Judgment Cut — unassigned.
  • Ruby’s Strength-catalyst identity vs the proto’s spell-casting Mender (007 §Ruby).

Design history

  • 2023-02-11 — party-management dump proposes the exploration skills per character: Thane +5 defense, Isaac illusory walls, Clara long-range initiate, Sable traps/lures, Leonard light-up-larger-area, Ruby decoy copy; plus the blood-magic HP-sacrifice character.
  • 2023-02-11 (same day) — CrunchyConniptions expands and re-frames: Isaac sees secrets but shorter torch, Clara attacks from distance but can’t detect traps; adds Leonard sees in the dark with magic (no torch) and the light-sensitive-enemy use case, plus proposed Ruby sees rare metals. FreshToast pushes back that exploration skills may not be needed and prefers direct HP-sacrifice healing.
  • 2023-02-13 — mobility/modular profiles firmed up per character (Thane/Isaac specialized, Sable/Leonard mobile, Locke warps).
  • 2023-02-14 — the unassigned Judgment Cut melee skill added to the pool.
