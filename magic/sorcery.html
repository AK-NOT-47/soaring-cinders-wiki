Sorcery (Luminarium System)

Caelian OdysseyNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-10-13

The academy magic of Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium — sorcerers channel Flow through a set of ~20 Glyphs, combining them through training into complex, often personal magic.

Summary — Sorcery is the magic system of the separate iteration Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium (001 §Luminarium), distinct from the main game’s Affinity System. A sorcerer channels Flow through Glyphs — a determined set of around 20 primitive magical symbols — and combines them into new, complex magic (2023-10-13, 2023-10-17). What combinations a sorcerer can achieve is “generally unique to a sorcerer or faction” and “the result of significant training,” so combined magic “can be roughly anything” (2023-10-17). Each of the Caelian Odyssey cast — the 4 Boyes and Cyrus — is designed to represent “a general facet of Sorcery and magic” (2023-10-17).

The Glyph system

Magic is built from Glyphs (2023-10-17; 001 §Glyphs System):

  • The team settled on around 20 glyphs (“It’s shaping up to be around 20”), though “exact definitions not yet clear” — what each glyph covers had not been fully pinned down (2023-10-13).
  • Combining glyphs into new, complex magic is “generally unique to a sorcerer or faction” and “the result of significant training,” meaning combined magic “can be roughly anything” (2023-10-17).
  • Named/known glyphs:
  • The River Glyph — originally the Water Glyph, renamed (2023-10-13). Alias: Water Glyph.
  • A sealing/binding glyph — “There is a glyph which does that,” raised when discussing a magic-sealing ability for Titan (2023-10-18).

Named glyph fusions from the cast: Cyrus later picks up Earth to produce glass sorcery and Motion to produce explosive sorcery, both fusions with his Flame affinity (2023-10-17; 002 §Cyrus).

Flow and the facets of Sorcery

Sorcerers channel Flow, whose two facets are Capacity (capable of big magic) and Channeling efficiency (how tired you get and cast speed) (001 §Sorcery; 007 §Sorcery Flow facet). Each of the 4 Boyes embodies a general facet of Sorcery (2023-10-17):

  • Alistair — specialisation: solves all problems with ice carefully shaped into weapons and tools (‘Frost’ affinity).
  • Colette — the power of names and foreign uses/interpretations of sorcery.
  • Pandora — the practical uses of Sorcery (enchantment, alchemy) and magical anomalies; the Flow Capacity-vs-efficiency exemplar.
  • Titan — ‘Body’ affinity, used to heal and punch; fights unarmed, supplemented with magic.
  • Cyrus — a well-rounded sorcerer “by Caelian standards,” the reference point for balanced Sorcery.

Baseline and awakening

Sorcerers start with their affinity and the barest knowledge of magic; each has already “awakened” (2023-10-17). This is the same awakening baseline used in the Affinity System.

Loadout rule — sorceries fixed once slotted

For the Luminarium iteration, sorceries are fixed once slotted: “While one can change weapons based on enemy weaknesses, sorceries remain fixed once slotted” — a deliberate constraint to keep character-building meaningful and to reject rapid Dark-Souls-style gear-swapping (006 §Loadout rule; 008 §Loadout rule; note 7294).

Related systems

  • The Flow — the magical current Sorcery channels.
  • Affinity System — the main-game elemental system; Sorcery is the Luminarium-iteration counterpart.
  • Thaumaturgy (covered under Affinity System) — the academic study of affinities in the main-game framing.

Open questions

  • Exact glyph count (“around 20”) and the meaning of each glyph are undefined in the notes.
  • The “sorceries fixed once slotted” loadout rule is noted but not finalized (006 §Loadout rule; note 7294).

Design history

  • 2023-10-13 — glyph system taking shape: “around 20” glyphs, definitions not yet clear; the Water Glyph renamed to the River Glyph.
  • 2023-10-17 — the combining rule set: glyph fusions are “unique to a sorcerer or faction… the result of significant training… can be roughly anything”; the 4 Boyes + Cyrus framed as facets of Sorcery, and the awakening baseline stated.
  • 2023-10-18 — a sealing/binding glyph confirmed to exist while discussing Titan’s magic-sealing ability.
