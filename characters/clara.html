Clara Damaris

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
Also known as: Marie, Leyla, Malenia
First conceived 2023-02-08 · last updated 2023-02-15

Clara Damaris CANON

Noble archer of the fallen Damaris family — designed low-luck, family-hates-her foil to Sable — who fights to restore her house’s honor and un-eclipse its sun.

Summary — Clara Damaris, 24, is a main-party back-liner from the noble Damaris family, a bloodline connected to Ajax and distantly related to Lysander and Bastard Damaris (002 §Clara). She is strong-willed with “big Edelgard energy” (2023-02-14) and “some tsundere qualities” (2023-02-09), and was deliberately built as a foil to Sable: Clara/Marie gets low luck and a family that hates her, Sable gets high luck and a family that loves her (2023-02-09). Her arc drives her from sheltered noble idealism to confronting the corruption inside her own house — vengeance vs. justice, with the player able to tip the outcome (2023-02-14).

Name / Identity

  • Family name: Damaris (002 §Clara). See the Damaris disambiguation for the family and Bastard Damaris.
  • Age: 24 — from the Part 5 ages list, where she still appears as “Marie 24” (2023-02-09).
  • The name evolved Malenia → Leyla/Marie → Clara (see Design history). “Vivienne” was floated as a more sophisticated alternative after Clara was settled, then redirected as possibly her mother’s name (2023-02-14).
  • Affinity: not specified. The doc’s “likely Fiery, given family connection to Ajax’s bloodline” is inference — PROPOSED (002 §Clara); she does not appear in the known-affinities list (2023-03-15).

Personality

  • Strong-willed and determined; carries herself with noble bearing despite her family’s fall (002 §Clara).
  • “Strong willed reaction, in line with her character, big Edelgard energy” (2023-02-14).
  • Tsundere, maybe — “Marie tsundere maybe? / Has some tsundere qualities?” (2023-02-09).
  • Does not curse until her final confrontation (2023-02-15) — and, in the earlier Thane’s-foil scene, temporarily drops her noble pretense to tell him “to fuck off and get over himself” (2023-02-10).
  • Dialogue consultation trait: “largely useful, with some gaping holes in her knowledge” (2023-02-14).
  • Compassionate but can be naive about harsh realities outside nobility; struggles with guilt over the family’s dark legacy (002 §Clara).
  • Listed as “Peak Waifu” in a scrapped party-order fork, but FreshToast countered: “I don’t think Clara will honestly turn out to be big waifu material” (2023-02-14).

Background & Motivations

  • Designed foil to Sable: “Have Marie with low luck and a family that hates her and Sable with high luck and a family that loves her” (2023-02-09); “Marie & Sable” is one of the core relationship pairings (2023-02-09).
  • Family Damaris is connected to Ajax’s bloodline — distant relation to Lysander and Bastard Damaris (002 §Clara).
  • Family uses silver cutlery — “literally born with a silver spoon in her mouth” (2023-02-12).
  • The sun is on the Damaris heraldry, leaning into connotations of the Sun as giver and beacon to their people; it gets ‘eclipsed’ by the nasty happenings when the family’s corruption is revealed — a deliberate visual metaphor for the fall from grace (2023-02-14). Maybe Clara’s dad “cares a little too much about the sun” (2023-02-14).
  • Motivations (002 §Clara): restore family honor through her own heroic deeds; distance herself from corrupt family members; prove nobility should serve the people; uncover what her family has been hiding.
  • Goals (002 §Clara): help Locke’s quest to keep the chaos emeralds from misuse; confront Bastard Damaris; rebuild the Damaris name as a symbol of hope.
  • Flaws (002 §Clara): sheltered upbringing, imposter syndrome, over-idealism about redemption, defensive when family honor is questioned.

Abilities & Traits

  • Party position: 2nd line (Back) (2023-02-10).
  • Has a retreat shot (2023-02-13).
  • Can move somewhat in combat but prefers not to — mobility tier shared with Ruby, between the static Thane/Isaac and the mobile Leonard/Sable (2023-02-13).
  • Skilled archer and tactician; trained in noble combat arts but lacks street-fighting experience (002 §Clara).
  • Exploration-skill brainstorm (heavily debated system): “Clara can have a skill that initiates attacks from a longer distance” (2023-02-11).

Relationships

  • Knows Thane’s Foil (the Paladin) from her past — possibly childhood friend or former betrothed (2023-02-10). She “relates to the foil’s morality and background but does not commiserate and comfortably opposes him” (2023-02-10).
  • Directly opposes Bastard Damaris, her corrupted family member (002 §Clara).
  • Forms a sisterly bond with Locke over shared burden of expectations (002 §Clara).
  • Core pairing with Sable (2023-02-09).

Role & Arc

  • Discovers the extent of her family’s corruption and involvement with the Drykksar parasite; must choose between family loyalty and doing what’s right; confronts Bastard Damaris alongside the party (002 §Clara).
  • Final confrontation with whoever did “the nasty shit in her family”: they attempt to monologue, and Clara interrupts to threaten them Princess Bride style — “I am heir Damaris. You have tainted our family’s reputation… Today you die.” She may be consumed by vengeance, or, if the player guides her to justice, says something less menacing and more hopeful (2023-02-14).
  • Growth: learns nobility is defined by actions, not bloodline; becomes a true beacon of hope — “the sun un-eclipsed” (2023-02-14; 002 §Clara).
  • Emotional beats (002 §Clara): horror at the family’s connection to the dragon experiments; removes her family crest, then puts it back on with new determination; chooses party over blood against Bastard Damaris.
  • Her presence legitimizes the party’s quest in the eyes of nobility; her arc parallels Locke’s from the opposite social standing (002 §Clara).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Name polish: “we settled on Clara but if we wanted something more sophisticated sounding, there’s Vivienne” — subsequently redirected as a candidate for her mother’s name, so Clara stands (2023-02-14). The doc still notes “Name change being considered” (002 §Clara).
  • DEBATED Affinity unassigned — she is absent from the known-affinities roster (2023-03-15); “likely Fiery” is doc inference only PROPOSED.
  • DEBATED Late-roster absence: the 2024 team-skill pairing list names only Locke, Ruby/Thane, Percy/Damian, Astrid/Sable — Clara is not in it (2024-04-11). No message removes her from the party; whether the roster shrank or the list was partial is unresolved.

Design history

  • 2023-02-08 — original concept as Malenia: tall graceful noblewoman from a fallen noble family of Lethria, kind, justice-driven, groomed for leadership, master archer, seeks to restore her family. Not street-smart but emotionally and academically intelligent; loyal and proud of her family, then realizes the family is not what she thought; initiative increases in the back position; high-damage delayed ranged skill that almost always crits; “Says ‘justice’ a little too much”. — the Elden Ring-borrowed name and the plush-red-skin visual draft were superseded.
  • 2023-02-08 — rename candidates: Leyla and Marie; identity crisis on discovering her family is bad, family were conspiring, vengeance vs. justice, the betrayer is still out there.
  • 2023-02-09 — as Marie: tsundere qualities; age 24; “Marie’s family traitor” slated as her personal antagonist; the low-luck/hated-family foil vs. Sable is coined; Marie & Sable named a core pairing.
  • 2023-02-10 — renamed Clara — first appearance of the name in the record: “Clara relates to the foil’s morality and background…”; knows Thane’s foil from her past; placed 2nd line with Ruby and Leon. — supersedes Marie.
  • 2023-02-13 — combat identity: moves somewhat but prefers not to; has a retreat shot.
  • 2023-02-14 — SCRAPPED male-“isekai protagonist” fork tagged her “Clara - Peak Waifu”; rejected because the current party order is mechanically justified, with FreshToast doubting the waifu label anyway. Same day: name confirmed settled (“we settled on Clara”), Vivienne floated then redirected to her mother; Princess Bride final-confrontation scene + Edelgard energy; sun-on-Damaris-heraldry eclipse metaphor.
  • 2023-02-15 — “Clara does not curse until her final confrontation” — the last dated Clara-specific note; matches the current presentation above.
