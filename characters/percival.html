Percival

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
Also known as: Percy, Isaac
First conceived 2021-08-01 (M00445, FreshToast) · last in chat 2024-04-11 (M05394)

Percival CANON

The main-game party mage — the renamed Isaac — an Electric-affinity caster with a Hermione-esque rival and a made arm called Conflux.

Summary — Percival, who goes by “Percy”, is the Skyward/Soaring Cinders main-game party mage: in Part 10 the name was proposed to replace “Isaac” as the party member (“I don’t really like Isaac much for a party member” — M02787, FreshToast, 2023-02-25), making Percy the renamed main-game mage (002 §Percival). His affinity is Electric (M03051, FreshToast, 2023-03-15). His rival is Rhea Falafill, and Pandora helps him make Conflux, his arm (M04343, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). He appears in the latest (2024) team-skill roster, paired with Damian (M05392, FreshToast, 2024-04-11). Beyond this, the notes give Percy very little direct detail — his arc is workshopped through his rival.

Name / Identity

  • CANON Full name Percival, short form Percy — “The Percy/Percival dichotomy appeals to me” (M02788, FreshToast, 2023-02-25); “percy is cool” (M02789, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-25).
  • CANON The name replaces Isaac as the party member, freeing “Isaac” for the rival/other character (M02784–M02787, FreshToast, 2023-02-25); reconfirmed months later: “Isaac is Percy and falafal is a foodstuff” (M03526, FreshToast, 2023-08-12). This rename is the documented root of the cross-doc Isaac/Percival confusion (002 §Percival).
  • The name itself dates to the 2021 name bank (M00445–M00446, FreshToast, 2021-08-01; M00511, spirit_noodle), where surname candidates included Lumenson, Brightsworth, Brightlord, and Astrum (M00495–M00499, spirit_noodle, 2021-08-01) — none confirmed.
  • CANON Affinity: Electric — “Electric - Percival, Ruby, Lysander” in the known-affinities roster alongside the main-game cast (M03051/M03052, FreshToast, 2023-03-15).

Abilities & Traits

  • Magic-focused party mage, inheriting the staff-wielder slot from the Isaac profile (002 §Isaac, §Percival) — see Isaac for the proto-implemented kit, which is coded under the old name.
  • Conflux (his arm): Pandora is “the person who helps Percival make Conflux (his arm)” (M04343, FreshToast, 2023-10-17) — a made/prosthetic arm; the liked “Sha’worr-ma is a contradiction personified…” paragraph was earmarked for reuse on the person who helped Percy make it (ledger R19, M03527+, FreshToast, 2023-08-12).
  • Team-skill pairing: in the 2024 position-based team-skill scheme he is paired with Damian (“- Percy/Damian”, M05392; “When she is beside Percy or Damian…”, M05394, FreshToast, 2024-04-11).
  • Dog-breed party comparison: "Percy - " was left blank (M03029, FreshToast, 2023-03-13).

Relationships

  • CANON Has a Hermione-esque rival, Rhea Falafill — “smart, straightforward but hardworking rather than talented” (M02721–M02722, FreshToast, 2023-02-25); named at M02835 (CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-27).
  • CANON The rival “could later side with Percy/Isaac against Eris” (M02723, FreshToast, 2023-02-25).
  • Pandora helps him make Conflux (M04343, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Sparse notes: beyond the Electric affinity, the Isaac→Percy rename, the Rhea rivalry, and Conflux, the notes give Percy very little detail; his arc is workshopped through Rhea. Treat anything more specific as undecided (002 §Percival).
  • PROPOSED Possible cross-iteration overlap: the Rhea-rival material was also written up in the Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium academy notes, so Percy/Rhea may overlap across iterations. This is a soft cross-reference, not a ruling that Percy belongs to the Luminarium — the affinity roster places Percival in the main-game cast (002 §Percival, citing M03051 and the ~4963-4965 character block).
  • Whether he inherits the Isaac Eris-confrontation arc wholesale under the new name is implied by the rename but never restated post-rename.

Design history

  • 2021-08-01 — “Percy” / “Percival” enter the name bank (M00445–M00446, FreshToast; M00511, spirit_noodle); surname riffs Percival Lumenson / Brightsworth / Brightlord / Astrum (M00495–M00499, spirit_noodle). No character attached yet.
  • 2023-02-25 — rename: the party mage Isaac becomes Percy — “What if Isaac was called Percy?”, “We could use Isaac for his rival character instead”, “I don’t really like Isaac much for a party member”, “The Percy/Percival dichotomy appeals to me” (M02784–M02788, FreshToast); approved “percy is cool” (M02789, spirit_noodle). — supersedes the name Isaac for the mage; see Isaac.
  • 2023-02-27 — the rival gets a name: “Rhea Falafill (Isaac’s rival)” (M02835, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-03-13 — party-as-dogs list leaves "Percy - " blank (M03029, FreshToast).
  • 2023-03-15 — Electric affinity assigned in the known-affinities roster (M03051/M03052, FreshToast).
  • 2023-08-12 — rename reconfirmed, “Isaac is Percy and falafal is a foodstuff” (M03526, FreshToast); the Sha’worr-ma “contradiction personified” paragraph is earmarked for the person who helped Percy make Conflux (Percy’s arm) — first mention of the arm (ledger R19: M03527, M03530, M03535–M03538, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-17 — the Conflux-maker is identified: Pandora “helps Percival make Conflux (his arm)” (M04343, FreshToast).
  • 2024-04-11 — Percy appears in the latest team-skill roster, paired with Damian (M05392, M05394, FreshToast) — current state.
