Percival

Skyward CindersNotes / DesignPrimary character
Also known as: Percy, Isaac
First conceived 2021-08-01

The main-game party mage — the renamed Isaac — an Electric-affinity caster with a Hermione-esque rival and a made arm called Conflux.

Summary — Percival, who goes by “Percy”, is the Skyward/Soaring Cinders main-game party mage: in Part 10 the name was proposed to replace “Isaac” as the party member (2023-02-25), making Percy the renamed main-game mage (002 §Percival). His affinity is Electric (2023-03-15). His rival is Rhea Falafill, and Pandora helps him make Conflux, his arm (2023-10-17). He appears in the latest (2024) team-skill roster, paired with Damian (2024-04-11). Beyond this, the notes give Percy very little direct detail — his arc is workshopped through his rival.

Name / Identity

  • Full name Percival, short form Percy — “The Percy/Percival dichotomy appeals to me” (2023-02-25); “percy is cool” (2023-02-25).
  • The name replaces Isaac as the party member, freeing “Isaac” for the rival/other character (2023-02-25); reconfirmed months later: “Isaac is Percy and falafal is a foodstuff” (2023-08-12). This rename is the documented root of the cross-doc Isaac/Percival confusion (002 §Percival).
  • The name itself dates to the 2021 name bank (2021-08-01), where surname candidates included Lumenson, Brightsworth, Brightlord, and Astrum (2021-08-01) — none confirmed.
  • Affinity: Electric — “Electric - Percival, Ruby, Lysander” in the known-affinities roster alongside the main-game cast (2023-03-15).

Abilities & Traits

  • Magic-focused party mage, inheriting the staff-wielder slot from the Isaac profile (002 §Isaac, §Percival) — see Isaac for the proto-implemented kit, which is coded under the old name.
  • Conflux (his arm): Pandora is “the person who helps Percival make Conflux (his arm)” (2023-10-17) — a made/prosthetic arm; the liked “Sha’worr-ma is a contradiction personified…” paragraph was earmarked for reuse on the person who helped Percy make it (2023-08-12).
  • Team-skill pairing: in the 2024 position-based team-skill scheme he is paired with Damian (2024-04-11).
  • Dog-breed party comparison: "Percy - " was left blank (2023-03-13).

Relationships

  • Has a Hermione-esque rival, Rhea Falafill — “smart, straightforward but hardworking rather than talented” (2023-02-25); the rival was named on 2023-02-27.
  • The rival “could later side with Percy/Isaac against Eris” (2023-02-25).
  • Pandora helps him make Conflux (2023-10-17).

Open questions

  • Sparse notes: beyond the Electric affinity, the Isaac→Percy rename, the Rhea rivalry, and Conflux, the notes give Percy very little detail; his arc is workshopped through Rhea. Treat anything more specific as undecided (002 §Percival).
  • Possible cross-iteration overlap: the Rhea-rival material was also written up in the Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium academy notes, so Percy/Rhea may overlap across iterations. This is a soft cross-reference, not a ruling that Percy belongs to the Luminarium — the affinity roster places Percival in the main-game cast.
  • Whether he inherits the Isaac Eris-confrontation arc wholesale under the new name is implied by the rename but never restated post-rename.

Design history

  • 2021-08-01 — “Percy” / “Percival” enter the name bank; surname riffs Percival Lumenson / Brightsworth / Brightlord / Astrum. No character attached yet.
  • 2023-02-25 — rename: the party mage Isaac becomes Percy — “What if Isaac was called Percy?”, “We could use Isaac for his rival character instead”, “I don’t really like Isaac much for a party member”, “The Percy/Percival dichotomy appeals to me”; approved “percy is cool”. — supersedes the name Isaac for the mage; see Isaac.
  • 2023-02-27 — the rival gets a name: “Rhea Falafill (Isaac’s rival)”.
  • 2023-03-13 — party-as-dogs list leaves "Percy - " blank.
  • 2023-03-15 — Electric affinity assigned in the known-affinities roster.
  • 2023-08-12 — rename reconfirmed, “Isaac is Percy and falafal is a foodstuff”; the Sha’worr-ma “contradiction personified” paragraph is earmarked for the person who helped Percy make Conflux (Percy’s arm) — first mention of the arm.
  • 2023-10-17 — the Conflux-maker is identified: Pandora “helps Percival make Conflux (his arm)”.
  • 2024-04-11 — Percy appears in the latest team-skill roster, paired with Damian — current state.
