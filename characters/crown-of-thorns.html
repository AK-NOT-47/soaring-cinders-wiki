Crown of Thorns (Concept)

An early character concept born from wordplay: a Game of Thrones-inspired character who has a crown — because “thrones sounds and looks like thorns.”

Summary — A character “based off of game of thrones” — “because its about a chair for the kingdom” — who “has a crown” (2023-11-07). The concept was sparked by simple wordplay: “thrones sounds and looks like thorns” (2023-11-07), and it landed: “This has unironically given me an idea” (2023-11-07). No further details were developed.

Concept

  • Name/image: Crown of thorns (2023-11-07).
  • Basis: Game of Thrones, “about a chair for the kingdom”; the character has a crown (2023-11-07).
  • Origin: the thrones/thorns pun (2023-11-07).
  • FreshToast’s unspecified follow-on idea (2023-11-07) was never elaborated in the chat.

Design history

  • 2023-11-07 — sole appearance: the full five-message pitch and FreshToast’s “unironically given me an idea” response.
