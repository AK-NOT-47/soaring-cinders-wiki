Aria Scala PROPOSED

Disowned noble turned magic-knight maid — the lead maid of the Maid Inn, the super-nice NPC you talk to for rest, who blesses weapons with a moe moe kyun.

Summary — Aria Scala is the lead maid (M01266, FreshToast, 2023-02-09) at the maid inn — “a place for disadvantaged women to find work” (M01262, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). She works the bar and is the NPC the party talks to for rest (M01267, FreshToast, 2023-02-09), is “super nice” (M01268, FreshToast, 2023-02-09), and performs a maid blessing on weapons “with the whole moe moe kyun thing” (M01269–M01270, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-02-09) — the food blessing is actually magic (M01271, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). Born noble, disowned for her aptitude for magic, she became a knight who enhances her sword skills with magic; her employer has been abducted and she is trying to find him (M01261, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).

Appearance

Tall and stunning; long, curly, golden hair; piercing blue eyes; curvaceous figure. Usually in maid attire — black and white dress, white apron, frilly white cap — yet carries herself with the air of a noble, always standing tall and proud (M01261, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).

Personality

  • A mixture of loyalty and pride: extremely dedicated to her duties, ensuring her employers are well taken care of (M01261, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).
  • Quite pompous — speaks in a haughty tone and expects the respect and attention a noble would receive (M01261).
  • Strong sense of honor; willing to do whatever it takes to protect those she cares about, even at personal risk (M01261).
  • “Aria is right and proper Maid” and cares a lot about the maid agency (M01264–M01265, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).

Background & Motivations

  • Born into a noble family; disowned when she showed an aptitude for magic, which was seen as a disgrace (M01261, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).
  • Taken in by a maid agency and trained as a maid; never forgot her noble roots and childhood lessons about honor and duty (M01261).
  • Discovered a talent for using magic to enhance her sword skills and became a knight, using her powers to protect those in need (M01261).
  • Motivated by loyalty and duty to her employers, and by the desire to prove herself as a knight and protector (M01261).
  • Her employer was abducted and she is trying to find him (M01261).

Role & Arc

  • Lead maid of the maid inn; works the bar; the rest-point NPC (M01266–M01267, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Performs the maid blessing on weapons (moe moe kyun); the blessing is genuinely magical (M01269–M01271, 2023-02-09).

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — full profile posted (appearance, personality, motives, background, abducted employer) (M01261, CrunchyConniptions); the maid inn framed as a place for disadvantaged women to find work / maid-cafe parody (M01262–M01263, FreshToast); role details added the same session: right-and-proper maid, agency loyalty, lead maid, bar/rest NPC, super nice, magical maid blessing (M01264–M01271, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast).
