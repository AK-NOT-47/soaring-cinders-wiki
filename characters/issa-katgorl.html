Issa Katgorl SCRAPPED

The Finesse/Luck slot of the homunculus-era party — a name and a stat, never developed further.

Summary — Issa Katgorl was the fourth member of the early four-stat party plan: Thane (Toughness), Gaius but now girl (Resolve), Ruby (Strength), Issa Katgorl (Finesse) (M01009, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-05). She received no profile beyond her combat role and does not appear in later party lineups; 002 files her under Archived Early Concepts.

Role & Function

  • Frontline with emphasis on finesse: “After that Issa Katgorl and somebody would emphasis finesse” (M01008, FreshToast, 2023-02-05).

Abilities & Traits

  • Associated with the Finesse stat (M01009, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-05).
  • “Katgorl would be our luck character” (M01011, FreshToast, 2023-02-05).

Design history

  • 2023-02-05 — created in the party stat-assignment session as the Finesse/Luck member (M01008–M01011, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-16 — absent from the next party lineup (“Thane-Melee-Spear, Locke-Melee-Sword, Sable-Ranged-Bow, Issac-Magic-Staff”, M02164, CrunchyConniptions); never mentioned again and archived in 002. — scrapped
