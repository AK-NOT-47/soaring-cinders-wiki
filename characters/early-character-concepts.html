Early character concepts

Early ConceptNotes / DesignWorking nameTertiary character
First conceived 2021-03-13

The 2021 brainstorm pile: one-line playable-character archetypes and narrative sketches from before any specific game existed. Archived; a few seeds later sprouted elsewhere.

Summary — Between March and June 2021 the group threw out a series of character archetypes and concept sketches, recorded in 002’s Archived Early Concepts section with the note that they “may or may not be integrated into final game designs” and should not be considered active characters (002 §Archived Early Concepts). None was developed past a few lines here, but several were into later entities, noted per section below.

Necromancer

Playable character archetype: makes cute and silly zombies (2021-05-25). The necromancer seed later grew into the fleshed-out dungeon-master character Lyra the Necromancer (2023-02-09), and the “necromancer revives enemies, so kill the weakest first” combat example (2021-05-26) fed the kill-order/rank-targeting design discussed in the systems docs.

Space/Time Manipulator

Playable archetype who manipulates space and time with immense limitations — “turns the game into something more like chess than a hack n slash” (2021-05-25). Space-time manipulation itself resurfaced in the magic notes (007 §Space-Time Manipulation).

Archetype roster: Berserker, Archer, Lancer, Rider, Caster, Swordmaster

Fate-style class names listed as traditional character types for reference (2021-05-25). No individual development.

Neon Power Character

A character with neon-like power from Infamous Second Son (2021-05-25). (2023-10-18)

Crystal Powers Character

A character with crystal powers (2021-05-25). Nothing further.

The Dancer

Name mentioned, no additional details (2021-05-25).

Gojo Character

A Gojo (Jujutsu Kaisen) character that bends space (2021-05-25).

Gilgamesh Character

Sits on a throne and shoots swords (2021-05-25). The image evolved into the Telekinetic Sword Boss in the encounters doc (005 §Telekinetic Sword Boss).

Villainous Berserker Spirit

Playable character with a loss-of-control mechanic: the player loses control sometimes and may even attack other players; could be instigated by certain actions (killing a certain enemy or using a certain combo); the player regains control by playing a certain melody (2021-05-26). Referenced again in the systems doc (008).

Vigilante Justice Concept

Narrative concept, a “Light vs L thing (Death Note)”: two individuals — one follows the system, the other delivers justice equal to the crime (arm for an arm, eye for an eye, life for a life). “Light is a righteous likeable person that wishes to undo injustice… a guy doomed to a path of despair and a sad end”; people understand and relate to him, but “however righteous and great this light is. In the end, he is still wrong. System must stay” (2021-03-13). The vigilante/pursuer idea later resurfaced in the Damian storyline, where a female vigilante/private investigator pursues him (2023-02-23).

Black and White Character

Meta-commentary concept: “They want boring, give them a character that is black and white” — a character who makes others black and white with his ability, leaning on what people want so “maybe they will grow bored of it and strive for something interesting” (2021-06-22).

Albedo + Gojo + Space-Time Character

From the Undying vs Heroes concept: 90% Albedo personality, 90% Gojo ability, with emphasis on space-time manipulation (2021-06-26). 002 notes this appears to be from an earlier/different game concept than the main Skyward Cinders.

Related (owned elsewhere — not detailed here)

  • The Undying vs Heroes concept also produced named characters: Leora (2021-08-16), and the name-mentions Aurelia and Orianna (2021-08-01) (002 §Undying vs Heroes). The early “mentee” Cyrus into the Caelian Odyssey antagonist — see Cyrus (disambiguation) (002 §Cyrus, pointer).
  • Zarek Reinhard — placeholder-named early version of Thane; his profile lives in Thane’s design history (002 §Zarek Reinhard).
  • Malenia/Leyla/Marie — name candidates for the character that became Clara (002 §Malenia/Leyla/Marie).
