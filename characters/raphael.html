Raphael

Skyward CindersNotes / DesignPrimary character
First conceived 2023-03-05

The true tragic monster of the story — a dual vampirism-and-dracosis afflicted fallen angel who masks as human, held together only by undiluted, focused hatred.

Summary — Raphael is “inhuman, our true (tragic) monster” (2024-02-10): the most frighteningly inhuman character in the story, driven and characterized by raw, evil hatred, “just not able to be reasoned or empathised with” (2024-02-10). He embodies both Vampirism and Dracosis — “Raphael would literally embody them both and it would drive him mad” (2024-02-10) — but instead “he rises above them to impress his own twisted will”: already too far gone to lose it any further (2024-02-10). Crucially, his inhumanity is enhanced by, but not the result of, his curses — it is the result of his circumstances and trauma (2024-02-10). Per the doc, he is the single most-workshopped character in the notes (002 §Raphael).

Appearance

  • Tall, with purple-orange-yellow gradient (“sunset”) irises like Vaenngrym and Lysander (2023-03-05).
  • Outwardly “calm, almost too calm” despite the vampirism, dracosis, inherited malefic pact and social/familial trauma (2024-02-10); distant, unemotive, contained — “implying it’s all he can manage to keep himself from losing it at any second” (2024-02-10).
  • Masks as human, “a wolf among sheep” (2024-02-10); stays mostly human-appearing until he socially unmasks his hatred, “turning physically and mentally monstrous. Revealing the scales and the fangs and the flames” (2024-02-10).

Affliction & Nature

  • Dual affliction: Vampirism + Dracosis (2024-02-10), framed against the thematic opposition Vampirism = immortality/preservation vs Dracosis = evolution/change (2024-02-10).
  • “Functional but broken in a way that makes him less human” — “a trifecta of curse, abuse and disease has blended together to make a whole new kind of monster” (2024-02-10); compared to Gollum / a split person (2024-02-10).
  • Combat against psychosis: “It is through this undiluted, focused fury that he can combat the psychosis one would suffer normally as a result of the draconic/vampiric mix” (2024-02-10). His deceptively well-kept manner is “not a result of discipline but a deranged state of interminable focus” (2024-02-10) — “The evil, post-ethical equivalent to post-nut clarity” / “Mentally mid-nut at all times” (2024-02-10).

Name significance

  • “Raphael is an angelic biblical name. We could lean into themes of purity for him” — “Like he was pure and kind but has since been corrupted. It has gone so far physically & mentally that it’s wrapped around into a new form of purity” (2024-02-10). A fallen angel — “like Satan kinda who’s the ultimate biblical evil but used to be god’s favourite lil boy” (2024-02-10).
  • Two variants, not finalized: corruption wrapping back around to purity, OR the simpler option — “make him ironically obsessed with purity despite impure intentions + vampire + dracosis” (2024-02-10).

Background & Motivations

  • Probably caused the fall of the royal family — “Maybe he caused the whole issue. He leaked the info to the public about the blood sacrifices?” / “Yeah he caused the fall most likely. Probably killed a few” (2024-02-10); “Like Raphael killed his family” (2024-02-10).
  • Past!Raphael resisted the vampirism, “trying to be good and kind in an unethical family setting and having that all backfire on him so hard, mostly as a result of his circumstances” (2024-02-10).
  • His inhumanity is “the result of traumatically twisting a kind, intelligent form of ambition into single-minded loathing” — enhanced by, not caused by, his curses (2024-02-10).

Sources of hatred

  • “He acts purely out of loosely directed hate. He hates his family, vampirism, his ugly scales, humanity all of it” (2024-02-10).
  • Resents Astrid “for daring to dig it all up and possibly restore the reputation of the people he hates the most” (2024-02-10). In one path Astrid may be “a little regretful regarding the fate of Raphael” (2023-10-24).

Role & Arc

  • The intended contrast: “Gideon, Lysander and other human characters do cruel, awful things by the end but mostly as a result of human flaws. Raphael is inhuman, our true (tragic) monster” (2024-02-10).
  • Design goal: “Through the good ol’ Tragic Backstory ™ people may begin to sympathise with him. Then we’ll pick at that, until they realise that he’s just not able to be reasoned or empathised with” (2024-02-10). “He needs to be relatable. The audience should sympathise. The audience should be furious on his behalf and almost want justify some of his actions” (2024-02-10), yet the hate “should go beyond reason to avoid any semblance of genuine humanity” (2024-02-10). “I think he should be totally, tragically fucked up” (2024-02-10).
  • The aim: “Present the philosophy conflict by the human conflict of Raphael” (2024-02-10), with drastic contrast between Past!Raphael and the “current day, evil-to-the-bone-marrow, super-dragon-vampire, evil-hate-fueled and irredeemable Raphael” (2024-02-10).

Open questions

  • Corruption-wraps-to-purity vs ironically-obsessed-with-purity (2024-02-10).

Design history

  • 2023-03-05 — first appearance as a named character: “Lysander and Raphael are tall with purple-orange-yellow gradient coloured irises like Vaenngrym” — establishing him among Ajax’s sunset-eyed line.
  • 2023-10-24 — story hook: in one path Astrid is “a little regretful regarding the fate of Raphael”.
  • 2024-02-10 — the extensive development session: dual Vampirism+Dracosis embodiment; rises above the madness through focused fury; angelic-name/purity theme and fallen-angel framing; trifecta of curse, abuse and disease; wolf-among-sheep masking with scales/fangs/flames reveal; caused the royal family’s fall and killed family members; resents Astrid; true tragic monster contrasted with the story’s human evildoers; Past!Raphael vs current Raphael contrast — current presentation; supersedes nothing, extends the 2023 sketch.
