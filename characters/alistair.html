Alistair CANON

One of the 4 Boyes: a Sylmiran Frost-affinity student at the Luminarium who represents specialisation — solving every problem with carefully shaped ice.

Summary — Alistair is a Sylmiran male in his early 20s, one of the 4 Boyes of Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium. He has the ‘Frost’ affinity and “represents specialisation, is highly focused on solving all problems using ice he carefully shapes into a variety of weapons and tools” (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). His archetype is still uncertain, and his inner life exists as a large brainstormed pool of conflicts plus a proposed mentor storyline.

Identity

  • Sylmiran male; early 20s; ethnically typical for western Khorath (Mediterranean) (M04227, M04242–M04243, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Affinity: Frost (M04227).
  • Archetype uncertain (M04227) — the 4 Boyes are each based on a nonmagical historical or fictional archetype (M04226, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Role & Abilities

  • One of the 4 Boyes; meant to represent a general facet of Sorcery and magic (M04226, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Represents specialisation: highly focused on solving all problems using ice he carefully shapes into a variety of weapons and tools (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Starts with his affinity and the barest knowledge of magic; has “awakened” (M04315–M04316, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Mentor concept PROPOSED

“A once-passionate mentor, now wearied by the constant misuse of magic for violence, encounters a protagonist who rekindles their faith in both humanity and the mystic arts. Inspired by this newfound hope, the mentor imparts rare and powerful magical knowledge to the pupil. The mentor is just average, but used to be passionate and that’s how the mentor became great. The pupil reignites that fire.” — “maybe Alistar?” (M04400–M04401, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-17).

Conflicts (brainstormed) PROPOSED

Eighteen-item conflict brainstorm list — design seeds, not yet finalized (M04402, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-17):

  1. Imposter Syndrome — feels he doesn’t truly belong in the academy
  2. Desire for Approval — wants the acknowledgment of a particular teacher or elder
  3. Overwhelm — struggles with managing his time and commitments
  4. Loneliness — despite friends, feels emotionally isolated
  5. Lost Friendship — grieves a broken friendship and wishes to mend it
  6. Performance Anxiety — freezes up during practical exams or duels
  7. Family Disapproval — his family doesn’t approve of his magical pursuits
  8. Homesickness — misses his home and struggles with academy life
  9. Identity Crisis — questions who he is and his purpose
  10. Fear of Failure — afraid of letting others down
  11. Overbearing Expectations — feels crushed by others’ expectations
  12. Cultural Clash — struggles with integrating into the academy’s culture
  13. Unfulfilled Dreams — has aspirations that seem unreachable
  14. Rebellion — wants to break away from traditions
  15. Trust Issues — finds it hard to trust others after a betrayal
  16. Conflict of Interest — torn between personal desires and duties
  17. Desire for Autonomy — wants to make decisions without external influences
  18. Hidden Vulnerability — conceals a weakness or fear he’s ashamed of

Open questions

  • Archetype uncertain (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Which (if any) of the 18 brainstormed conflicts, the family-legacy idea, and the mentor concept get finalized (M04308, M04400–M04402, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-17).

Design history

  • 2023-10-17 — initial concept in the 4 Boyes roster write-up: Sylmiran male, Frost affinity, archetype uncertain, represents specialisation via carefully shaped ice (M04227, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-17 — PROPOSED family legacy: “Perhaps he has a family legacy to uphold and is constantly pressured to be the best, but he genuinely wants to learn for the sake of knowledge and not prestige” — FreshToast: “Dig it” (M04308–M04309, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-17 — SCRAPPED lineage-unaware twist: “Alistair is unaware of his lineage until his magical abilities start manifesting, echoing the journeys of Harry Potter or Eragon” — FreshToast: “Idk about this part… Since he’s got a super focused skill set”; clarified that all the Boyes start with their affinity, the barest knowledge of magic, and have each awakened (M04310–M04316, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-17 — mentor concept drafted and tentatively attached: “maybe Alistar?” (M04400–M04401, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-10-17 — 18-item conflict brainstorm list posted (M04402, CrunchyConniptions).
