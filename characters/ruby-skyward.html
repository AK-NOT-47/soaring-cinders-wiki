Ruby (Skyward Cinders) CANON

A 2-year-old created being — a homunculus named for her eyes — who joins the party as its scatter-brained, pure-hearted back-line healer while searching for what it means to be “real.”

Summary — Ruby is a homunculus connected to Gideon and Eris’s research into life-force manipulation; Gideon borrowed another person’s soul to create her (M02178–M02179, FreshToast, 2023-02-16). Canonically 2 years old per the ages list (M01217–M01218, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09), she may appear older. In the browser proto she is implemented as the party’s only healer — the back-line “Mender” (002 §Main Party header, CANON — IMPLEMENTED). This page covers the Skyward Cinders party member; for the earlier homunculus-era character see Ruby (homunculus) (disambiguation: Ruby (disambiguation)).

Identity

  • Named Ruby “for her eyes”; the name was chosen because a ruby is an alchemical item/tool — “great she is called ruby cuz she is a magical item,” fitting the zero-to-hero alchemy theme (M00988–M00998, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-05).
  • Age: 2 — a created being; the canonical ages list gives “Ruby: 2” (M01217–M01218, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).
  • Affinity: Electric (002 §Ruby; affinity grouping “Electric - Percival, Ruby, Lysander”).
  • Dog-breed comparison: Labrador Retriever (M03029, spirit_noodle, 2023-03-13).
  • “A lot of me is in Ruby & Sable so I like them too” (M04966, FreshToast, 2023-11-15).

Personality

  • Scatter-brained, in a constant state of analytical information overload — like a puppy constantly distracted by new things (M02413–M02416, FreshToast, 2023-02-18).
  • Dialogue consultation trait: ignorant about common-sense situations, mostly hilariously useless, but will sometimes see the raw truth of the world due to fresh eyes (002 §Ruby) — the opposite of Thane’s consultation trait (002 §Thane).
  • Curious and inquisitive; innocent and pure-hearted despite dark origins; sees beauty and wonder in mundane things; emotionally intelligent despite lack of worldly knowledge; literal-minded but occasionally profound (002 §Ruby).
  • Flaws (002 §Ruby): naively trusting; easily distracted; lacks common sense socially; accidentally insensitive; struggles with moral situations beyond black/white; may have built-in limitations or programming she must overcome.
  • Ruby cries 2 times (noted specifically) (M02191, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).

Background & Motivations

  • Connected to Gideon and Eris’s research into life-force manipulation; a created being who escaped or was abandoned by her creators (002 §Ruby).
  • Homunculi require sacrificing a portion of your flesh and soul; Gideon borrowed another’s soul for Ruby but performed the ritual correctly (own soul) for Citrine; the player later finds out a little about the soul used to make Ruby (M02178–M02179, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Lieutenant Milf had hands in Ruby’s creation (and in Gaius’s expulsion) (M01221/M01225, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Ruby has an absent father (M01208–M01210, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-08).
  • Motivations (002 §Ruby): understand what she is and why she exists; experience everything the world has to offer; protect those who show her kindness; prove she has value beyond her origins; uncover the truth about Gideon’s and Eris’s experiments.
  • Goals (002 §Ruby): learn the world firsthand; confront Gideon about her creation and purpose; define her own identity; find acceptance and belonging with the party.

Abilities & Traits

  • Party position: 2nd line (Back) (002 §Ruby); proto confirms back-line placement.
  • Can move somewhat in combat but prefers not to; reliable through flexibility (M01874–M01879, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-13).
  • Electric affinity — possibly unstable or growing powers; support and utility specialist; unpredictable abilities reflecting her scattered nature; may have hidden potential related to her origins; adaptable — learns new applications of her powers quickly (002 §Ruby).
  • DEBATED Healer vs. melee: the early-concept homunculus profile calls Ruby a “Frontline melee character (with Thane)” who “lacks ability to channel magic herself but can act as catalyst for others” — while the proto implements her as a back-line dedicated healer/support mage (role “Mender”). Both keep her in a support function; the implemented version is the runnable truth for combat, the catalyst/melee framing is unimplemented lore (002 §Ruby DEBATED).
  • Weapon note: “Ruby arbitrarily has a hammer now because you asked” (M04056, FreshToast, 2023-10-13; ledger marks this row partial — the hammer provenance is not logged in 000).

CANON — IMPLEMENTED Proto combat values

(proto: src/core/combat/content.ts, src/core/combat/types.ts; 002 §Ruby)

  • id ruby, role “Mender”, glyph ✨, maxHp 26, maxAp 3, speed 7 (fastest hero), str 4 / mag 14 / def 6 / res 12 / acc 92 / crit 6. Start position 4 (Back line); her heals cast from ranks 3–4.
  • She is the party’s only healer:
  • Mend (2 AP) — focused single-target heal, 13.
  • Bless (1 AP) — applies Strength Up to an ally.
  • Purify (2 AP) — strips 2 afflictions + heals 5 (cleanse).
  • Benediction (4 AP, finisher) — a wave of healing across the whole party, 11 each.

Relationships

  • Gaius & Ruby pairing noted (M01223–M01224, FreshToast, 2023-02-09); the Gaius/Isaac–Ruby dynamic is inspired by Reigen and Mob (Mob Psycho 100) (M01189, FreshToast, 2023-02-08; “Isaac and Ruby have a dynamic inspired by Reigen and Mob?” — M02646, FreshToast, 2023-02-21). The academy is core to Gaius and somewhat related to Ruby, giving Locke her reason to visit it (M01227, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Citrine — her rival: “just a shinier psychopath version of her” (M00959, FreshToast, 2023-02-05), with inverted colours — yellow eyes and red hair vs Ruby (M01289, FreshToast, 2023-02-09); loyal to and cared for by her creator, like Jinx from Arcane (M01208–M01210, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-08).
  • The party becomes her “family” and teachers about the world; Locke becomes protective of her innocence (002 §Ruby).
  • PROPOSED Bonds with Percival over shared curiosity — not in the Discord notes; the source only pairs Ruby and Percival via shared Electric affinity (002 §Ruby).
  • Fills out Lyra’s dungeon survey with adorably earnest responses (002 §Ruby).
  • Team-skill pairing: Ruby/Thane — position-based team skills, e.g. when Astrid is behind Thane or Ruby she can do a thing (M05392–M05394, FreshToast, 2024-04-11).

Role & Arc

(002 §Ruby)

  • Early: comic relief through innocent questions and misunderstandings; the party gradually realizes she’s not quite “normal”; provides fresh perspective and unexpected wisdom.
  • Mid: a story about ends justifying means, connected to the Gideon and Eris themes; discovers her true nature/origins; must confront what she was made to be vs who she wants to be.
  • Late: confronts Gideon about her creation; chooses her own path and identity; rejects being defined by her creator’s intentions; proves her humanity through love and sacrifice.
  • Citrine sisterhood arc: Citrine comes to see Ruby as a wayward sister but cannot reconcile ironclad loyalty to her creator with Ruby’s intentions; the sisters fight; Ruby kills Citrine but spares the creator in her name (M02077, FreshToast, 2023-02-15). Ruby is a death/regret-theme avenue “once she kills Citrine” (M04659, FreshToast, 2023-10-24).
  • Emotional beats: the heartbreaking “What am I?” scene; the party affirming they care about who she is, not what she is; her “naive” perspective providing crucial insight (002 §Ruby).
  • Plot impact: her existence raises questions about the ethics of creation; contrast to Eris’s resurrection attempts — Ruby is about creating life; her fresh eyes see truth about the Ajax legend others miss (002 §Ruby).
  • Thematic link: ends-justifying-means connects the Ruby and Lysander stories — Ajax made a deal with the devil to protect people and paid a price; Lysander doubles down on demon power (M02228, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Healer (proto “Mender”) vs frontline melee catalyst (early homunculus profile) — the proto is the runnable truth, but the catalyst framing was never formally scrapped (002 §Ruby).
  • Hammer as her weapon: raw-anchored (M04056) but ledger-flagged partial — hammer appears only in a generic weapon list in 000.
  • “May have built-in limitations or programming she must overcome” (002 §Ruby) — unresolved.
