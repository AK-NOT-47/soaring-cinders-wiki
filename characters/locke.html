Locke Evertide

Skyward CindersMixedPrimary character
Also known as: Pyrrha
First conceived 2023-02-02

Main protagonist of Skyward Cinders: a 22-year-old village nobody with a Fiery affinity who earns everything she has, wields the spear Sunderer, and ends the story as the true heir of Ajax — not Lysander.

Summary — Locke (2023-02-15) is the leader of the player party. She starts as an inexperienced “small fish in a big pond” in awe of the hero Lysander, and her arc is the shift “from hero pleasing to people saving” (002 §Locke). Mechanically her strength is leadership and versatility rather than personal strength — she is dependent on her party, and her leadership is expressed through formation/team-skill mechanics (2024-04-11). She is one of the four heroes fully implemented in the browser proto (role “Spear / Heir”) (002 §Main Party header, ).

Identity

  • Full name suggested: Locke Evertide (2023-02-15; 002 §Locke).
  • Age: 22 (2023-02-09).
  • Female — “her leadership,” “her party” (002 §Locke).
  • Affinity: Fiery (002 §Locke; affinity groupings list “Fiery - Locke, Thane, Sable, Eris, Ajax”).
  • Dog-breed comparison: Akita (2023-03-13).
  • FreshToast: “In game 2 it’s probably Locke [as favourite]” — she’s “probably best girl for Game 2” (2023-11-15; 002 §Locke Notes).

Personality

  • Leader with a positive relationship with her party — deliberate contrast to Lieutenant Milf (002 §Locke).
  • Underdog character — “she’s nobody”; small fish in big pond (2023-11-16).
  • Flaws (002 §Locke): inexperienced and naive about the wider world; initially too eager to please/follow Lysander; self-doubt and imposter syndrome; stubborn once committed; tends to shoulder burdens alone.
  • Is a little creeped out by the golems for some reason (2023-02-16).

Background & Motivations

  • From a village that should be endearing to the player; her home village is nice to her (002 §Locke).
  • Even Thane begins more experienced than her; almost every skill or strength she ends up with is earned during the course of the game (2023-11-16).
  • Motivations (002 §Locke): initially wants to support and impress Lysander; prove herself worthy despite humble origins; protect people from those who would abuse power; eventually shift from hero-pleasing to people-saving; become a true hero on her own terms, not in Lysander’s shadow.
  • Goals (002 §Locke): gather the chaos emeralds (initially to help Lysander, later to keep them from him); grow strong enough to protect those who cannot protect themselves; stop Lysander and prevent misuse of Ajax’s power; return home as a genuine hero, not just a follower.
  • Her great-grandfather was a sailor; the crazy land Pirate he may have mentored informs Locke of her family history and key info such as a chaos emerald’s location (2023-02-26).

Abilities & Traits

  • Party position: Leader (002 §Locke).
  • Weapon: Spear (Pierce family) — wields the Sunderer, “the first of Ajax’s seven thingies,” acquired in the trial Locke passes at what turns out to be the secret entrance to Ajax’s Tomb (2023-09-10). The Sunderer is an explicit symbol of her status as heir to Ajax and of her independence from Lysander — the sword he gave her broke; the spear is power she sought herself, which he then covets (2024-08-24).
  • Reserve Skills System: can borrow a designated reserve skill from a character in reserve, once per battle (2023-02-10). Designed but NOT wired into combat — the proto implements her four normal skills only (002 §Locke).
  • Flame-based skill that gives AP to another character — part of the “Giving, taking and sharing” skill-design theme (2023-03-18); design intent, not implemented (002 §Locke).
  • Positioning: somewhat flexible — she moves dynamically to use skills, like the two mage characters (2023-02-20); “Locke has some movement and modular abilities but does not need to even slot them” (2023-02-13).
  • Overworld “special actions” should be things Locke does, taught/granted by allies: Thane teaches her to sneak, Sable teaches her to throw her voice, Leonard gives her a dash on cooldown (2023-02-11).
  • Carries a lantern in the prologue’s dark cave (002 §Locke); her portable light’s closest visual reference is a tiny hovering machina with freeform-shimmer dragonfly wings (2023-07-17).

Proto combat values

(proto: src/core/combat/content.ts, src/core/combat/types.ts; 002 §Locke)

  • id locke, role “Spear / Heir”, maxHp 30, maxAp 3, speed 6, str 12 / mag 6 / def 8 / res 6 / acc 92 / crit 12. Start position 2 (Mid line).
  • Pierce (1 AP) — quick spear thrust, hits enemy ranks 1–2, 7 phys.
  • Sunder (2 AP) — “The Sunderer bites deep”; 8 phys + Defense Down (the in-fiction Sunderer is wired in as this skill’s flavor).
  • Phalanx (2 AP) — braced full-weight lunge at the front foe, 12 phys.
  • Emberlance (4 AP, finisher) — “Ajax’s fire runs down the spear”; 17 phys, all enemy ranks; round 4+ or Escalation Mechanic 20.

Relationships

  • Locke & Thane pairing noted (2023-02-09); she has a moment with Thane where she struggles to travel far from home — like Sam’s “farthest from home” moment in LotR — and later comforts somebody else the same way to show growth (2023-10-13).
  • Locke & Himbo pairing noted (2023-02-09).
  • Lysander — the key relationship of the game: “The key is writing Lysander and how Locke bounces off Lysander”; models cited are Dutch & Arthur (RDR2) and All Might & Deku (MHA) (2024-05-18). She is in awe of him as her hero and nervous about how to address him (“sir”/“hero” rejected as cringe); the age gap is only about 4–5 years (2024-08-24).
  • Leadership is the “glue”: told through formation mechanics — she has many formations with others, maybe not optimal but brings the team together (2024-04-11).

Role & Arc

  • Chaos-emerald motivation chain (002 §Locke): runs into Lysander → wants to get stronger and support him → overhears Lysander and Eris discussing gathering the emeralds → decides to gather them herself to prove worthy → gathers them for herself → finally keeps them away from Lysander. She doesn’t become evil halfway; her reason is selfless — a people’s hero.
  • Prologue (002 §Locke): begins in medias res, Locke and Lysander already adventuring together (Lysander’s party minus Eris); at the end it rewinds to a proper intro. She reads a clue well and finds a chaos emerald Lysander misses. Whether she starts with a ranged weapon was an open question (2023-02-19) — the proto resolves her to the spear. In the 2024 prologue script draft, Lysander hands her a simple sword; she later falls, finds a large rusty spear, and discovers her talent for identifying weaknesses (2024-08-24). Lysander barely glances at her spear and calls the dead-end dungeon a waste of time — while Locke walked out with a strong relic, “the real prize… the spear + the faith of the party members she saved” (2024-08-24).
  • Dragon’s Blood choice (002 §Locke): on killing Vaenngrym, Locke may drink the dragon’s blood. No mechanical bearing, but it informs her opinion on Lysander’s pursuit of power and her understanding of Ajax; the Daemon of Want’s dialogue changes based on the choice, perhaps a mechanical change in that fight too.
  • Reason to visit the academy: it is core to Gaius and somewhat related to Ruby (2023-02-09).
  • Returning to the Luminarium as Locke will feel like seeing Anor Londo in DS3 (2023-10-13).
  • At the end of the story she more closely mirrors Ajax (the old dead hero) than the Evil Hero does (2023-02-09).
  • Shared-flame easter egg: Cyrus gets a powerful named attack in game 1 and Locke later earns the same or similar skill in game 2, since both wield flame (2023-10-31).
  • Final battle (002 §Locke): Lysander is killed by Locke in suitably dramatic fashion.

Key quotes

“Your light dims, Lysander…{she then looks up fiercely} Let it fade away.” (002 §Locke)

Open questions

  • Locke picking between her first melee, ranged, or magic weapon to introduce weapon types (2023-02-12) — vs. the proto’s fixed spear (007 marks this ).
  • Overworld exploration skills taught by allies (2023-02-11) — tagged Heavily Debated in 007/008.
  • Reserve Skills borrow mechanic — designed, still deferred from the proto (002 §Locke; GAME_DESIGN.md §17).
  • Mechanical change to the Daemon of Want fight based on the dragon’s-blood choice (002 §Locke).

Design history

  • 2023-02-02 — original concept: the protagonist/MC was intended to be the support/healer (“Main Character does support (like heal)”). Superseded — the support role was later reassigned to Ruby (002 §Locke [Design history]).
  • 2023-02-09 — named “Locke” in the party ages list, age 22; pairings Locke & Thane, Locke & Himbo noted.
  • 2023-02-10 — Reserve Skills System: Locke’s strength is leadership/versatility, she borrows a reserve skill once per battle.
  • 2023-02-14 — final pass on her name: “I like Locke… but there are other cool names. Like Pyrrha”; Crunchy: “Locke sounds like a protagonist”; Locke kept, “She needs a cool protagonist surname then”.
  • 2023-02-15 — surname candidate lands: Locke Evertide (with Leon Erhardt).
  • 2023-02-16 — initial party lineup lists Locke - Melee - Sword. Superseded by the spear.
  • 2023-02-19 — prologue in medias res sketched; open question “Locke starts with a ranged weapon?”. Resolved by the proto’s fixed spear.
  • 2023-09-10 — the Sunderer spear defined: first of Ajax’s seven relics, acquired in the trial Locke passes.
  • 2023-11-16 — underdog framing locked in: “she’s nobody,” every strength earned in-game, even Thane starts more experienced.
  • 2024-04-11 — leadership expressed mechanically through formations/team skills.
  • 2024-05-18 — Lysander–Locke relationship declared the key writing problem; Dutch/Arthur and All Might/Deku as models.
  • 2024-08-24 — Sunderer confirmed as explicit heir-to-Ajax symbol; prologue script draft (simple sword from Lysander → self-found spear); age gap with Lysander set at ~4–5 years.
  • 2024-08-28 — commercialisation-of-idols gag: Locke (or any band member) approached to endorse products after Lysander’s radio soda sell-out bit.
  • 2026-06-16 — proto roster reconciliation: Locke implemented in the vertical slice as “Spear / Heir” with the Pierce/Sunder/Phalanx/Emberlance kit; borrow mechanic still deferred (002 §Main Party header, ).
