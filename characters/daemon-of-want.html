Daemon of Want

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-02-21 (M02653, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-03-05 (M02902)

Daemon of Want CANON

The secret superboss: a spirit born from an entire kingdom’s rampant desire for a savior from the dragon, exceptionally powerful, cunning, and hiding for its own survival.

Summary — The Daemon of Want is a spirit formed from the rampant desires of the people for a saviour from the dragon and other woes (M02653, FreshToast, 2023-02-21). Because desire is vague and widespread, it is exceptionally powerful and cunning — not exactly evil so much as self-serving — and it understands that making its presence known would inevitably lead to its downfall, so it remains in hiding (M02656, FreshToast, 2023-02-21). It is the secret superboss the player can run into (M02658, FreshToast, 2023-02-21), and Sir Valerius is hunting it (M02902, FreshToast, 2023-03-05).

Nature & Daemon Lore

  • Daemons manifest when significant, shared emotion is experienced in a given area; they attach their identity, abilities and means of growth to the idea, concept or entity at the centre of that shared emotion (M02654, FreshToast, 2023-02-21). The more powerful the emotion and the more people, the stronger the daemon — which then feeds off the concept around which it formed (M02655, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Daemons can only be killed if bound, and binding a daemon to your will becomes more difficult the stronger and older it is (M02666, FreshToast, 2023-02-21). Thus the Daemon of Want is practically immortal so long as desire remains to feed it (M02667, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Daemons do die naturally if starved, but such daemons are typically quite weak — nothing like the Daemon of Want, which resulted from an entire kingdom’s shared woes (M02683, FreshToast, 2023-02-22). Multiple Daemons of Want can exist at a time (M02687, FreshToast, 2023-02-22).

Abilities & Traits

  • Its ability — the means to steal the strength of others — reflects its origin (M02657, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • The stolen power can only take and never give back; Eris is mistaken in her belief that the stolen life force could be used to resurrect the dead — a “true resurrection” is assumed impossible by any means, magical or otherwise (M02659–M02660, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Meta-design note: “the power is literally gaining EXP, to reflect Ajax/Lysander being parodies of a classic JRPG protagonist” (M02671, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).

Relationships

  • Ajax — Ajax’s strength-stealing power was acquired in a deal with the Daemon of Want: he traded half of his lifespan for the ability to steal the strength of others. The deal was struck when Ajax became desperate for strength to defend his people, “as the only warrior truly brave enough to fight a fucking dragon” (M02653, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Lysander and the bastard Damaris — inherited diluted versions of the daemonic power, which has waned with each heir; to recover it they must kill and drain the life of others (M02668, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Sir Valerius — is hunting the Daemon of Want (M02902, FreshToast, 2023-03-05).

Role & Arc

  • Secret superboss the player can run into (M02658, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Its dialogue changes based on whether Locke drinks the dragon’s blood after killing Vaenngrym (M02663, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).

Open questions

  • DEBATED “Perhaps a mechanical change in the fight as well?” based on the dragon’s-blood choice (M02664, FreshToast, 2023-02-21). CrunchyConniptions objected that having no mechanical change is unsatisfying — confusing and serving nothing compared to a mechanical change (M02678, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22). Unresolved.

Design history

  • 2023-02-19 — the spirits of the setting are named “daemons”: “Daemon is a good inbetween” — practically devils but more spirit than hell-creature (M02466–M02468, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-21 — full concept established in one session: origin as a spirit of the people’s desire for a saviour, Ajax’s half-a-lifespan deal, exceptional power and cunning from desire’s vagueness, hiding, secret-superboss role, dragon’s-blood dialogue variation, near-immortality while desire persists (M02653–M02667, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-22 — worldbuilding objection: if daemons persist after being born, doesn’t the world fill up with immortal daemons? Do you get a second Daemon of Want when the same shared woe occurs elsewhere? (M02676, CrunchyConniptions). Answered: daemons starve and die naturally if unfed, and yes, multiple Daemons of Want can coexist (M02683, M02687, FreshToast). Same day: objection that no mechanical fight change is unsatisfying (M02678, CrunchyConniptions) — still open.
  • 2023-03-05 — Sir Valerius established as hunting the Daemon of Want (M02902, FreshToast).
