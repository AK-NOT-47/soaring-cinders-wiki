Gaius Wornwall

EVOLVEDEarly ConceptNotes / Design
Also known as: Gaius Devmont, Gaius Ora'mo-ree, Gaius Qiundeldore, Gaius Penecillin, Gaius Doppleheimer, Thane Gaius
First conceived 2023-02-05 (M00934, CrunchyConniptions) · last in chat 2023-02-21 (M02646)

Gaius Wornwall EVOLVED

Chuunibyou animator-mage of the homunculus-era “loser heroes” party — a fired academic drowning in denial, later gender-swapped to female and eventually superseded.

Summary — Gaius is the Resolve-stat party member of the early (pre-Skyward) JRPG concept, a lab-coated object-animator whose profile was explicitly flagged for revision: from “highly intelligent prodigy” to a chuunibyou in denial who blames others for his failures (002 §Gaius Wornwall/Devmont). In the last direct planning of this roster he was “Gaius (but now girl)” (M01009, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-05). His signature dynamic — Reigen-and-Mob with Ruby — was later floated for Isaac and Ruby instead (M02646, FreshToast, 2023-02-21), and the whole entry now sits in 002’s Archived Early Concepts.

Appearance

  • Tall, lanky young person with wild black hair and piercing green eyes (002 §Gaius Wornwall/Devmont).
  • Often seen wearing a white lab coat, giving an eccentric appearance; playful grin, mischievous twinkle in the eye (002 §Gaius Wornwall/Devmont).

Personality

Current (post-revision) direction, per the “Personality Changes Needed” notes (002 §Gaius Wornwall/Devmont; M00934–M00949, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-05):

  • Changed from “highly intelligent” to chuunibyou.
  • In denial — “X is drowning in denial”.
  • NOT loyal; instead blames others for his failures.

Superseded original personality (kept for reference, marked “needs revision”): quick-witted with a sarcastic sense of humor, not afraid to make fun of those around him, can be aloof and distant, highly intelligent with a strong scientific mind, prone to overthinking and procrastination (002 §Gaius Wornwall/Devmont).

Background & Motivations

  • Original draft (flagged “needs revision”): child prodigy in magic; fascinated with magical abilities; discovered the ability to manipulate reality with his mind; struggles with the morality of his powers; goal to create a universal divination tool so everyone can recognize the majesty of the universe; motivated by a desire for recognition and to connect with people (002 §Gaius Wornwall/Devmont; M00934–M00949, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-05).
  • Changes demanded: he requires conflict — “write history of fuck up with blowup that leads to being canceled” (002 §Gaius Wornwall/Devmont).
  • That blowup was later pinned on Lieutenant Milf: she tampered with his experiment (the one time he was right, it explodes, making him doubt himself) because she wanted him out / he knew too much — she has hands in both Ruby’s creation and Gaius’ expulsion (M01221, M01225, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Age: 21 (M01217–M01218, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).

Abilities & Traits

  • Associated with the Resolve stat in the four-stat party plan: Thane (Toughness), Gaius-now-girl (Resolve), Ruby (Strength), Issa Katgorl (Finesse) (M01009, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-05).
  • Can animate objects — canonically demonstrated on a brick with wings (002 §Gaius Wornwall/Devmont).

Relationships

  • Gaius and Ruby are like Reigen and Mob (M01189, FreshToast, 2023-02-08); “Gaius & Ruby” also appears in the pairing structure notes (M01223–M01231, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Relationship with Himbo not established (002 §Gaius Wornwall/Devmont; M01223–M01231, 2023-02-09).

Role & Arc

  • BRICKWINGS story arc (M01176–M01188, FreshToast, 2023-02-08): the party needs to throw a brick but can’t throw it far enough; Gaius animates it and it grows wings; the brick flies through the window but escapes — it is now free. Later the party finds the brick again and Ruby berates Gaius for not taking care of it. The brick joins the party, is wholesome and beloved for a couple of scenes, then dies comically — a literal throwaway joke. Ruby relates to the brick.
  • Longer arc: Gaius slowly shifts from delusional obsession to working for actual practical change in the academy; Locke’s reason to visit the academy is that it’s core to Gaius and somewhat related to Ruby (M01226–M01227, M01233, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Surname never settled. Options discussed: Wornwall, Ora’mo-ree, Qiundeldore, Devmont, Penecillin, Doppleheimer, “Thane Gaius” (002 §Gaius Wornwall/Devmont; M00934–M00949, 2023-02-05). A separate brainstorm added Monroe (“Gaius Monroe”) among others (M01235–M01248, FreshToast, 2023-02-09) — none adopted.
  • DEBATED The “now girl” change (M01009) was never given a full re-profile; the archived text still reads male.

Design history

  • 2023-02-05 — original profile: tall lanky young man, lab coat, magic child prodigy, reality-manipulation, universal-divination-tool goal; surname options brainstormed in the same session (M00934–M00949, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-05 — revision notes attached almost immediately: chuunibyou instead of intelligent, in denial, blames others, needs a cancel-worthy blowup in his history (002 §Gaius Wornwall/Devmont).
  • 2023-02-05 — stat lineup lists “Gaius (but now girl), resolve” — changed to female (M01009, CrunchyConniptions). — supersedes the male presentation above
  • 2023-02-08 — BRICKWINGS arc written; Reigen/Mob dynamic with Ruby established (M01176–M01189, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-09 — age set to 21 (M01217–M01218); Lieutenant Milf made the author of his expulsion (M01221, M01225); academy arc: delusional obsession → practical change (M01226–M01227); second surname brainstorm (M01235–M01248).
  • 2023-02-21 — the Reigen/Mob dynamic is floated for Isaac and Ruby instead (“Isaac and Ruby have a dynamic inspired by Reigen and Mob?”, M02646, FreshToast); Gaius does not appear in the 2023-02-16 party lineup (Thane/Locke/Sable/Issac, M02164) and the profile was archived in 002. — character superseded
