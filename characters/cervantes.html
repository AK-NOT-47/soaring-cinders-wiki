Cervantes PROPOSED

A would-be social reformer turned outlaw bandit who greets the news of his allies’ deaths with dread mixed with excitement — “She’ll never take him alive.”

Summary — Cervantes “had dreams of rebellion and social reform but ended up in a life [of] banditry as an outlaw backed by cutthroat killers. Met with news of his former allies cut down, dread mixes with excitement. She’ll never take him alive.” (M01361, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). His armament was settled in the record’s finest design exchange: “Cervantes has gun? / but not gun / but gun / gun” (M01366–M01369, FreshToast, 2023-02-10) — preserved in doc 002 as “Has gun? But not gun. But gun. Gun.” (002 §Cervantes: Abilities & Traits).

Background & Motivations

  • Dreamed of rebellion and social reform; ended up in a life of banditry as an outlaw backed by cutthroat killers (M01361, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Met with news of his former allies cut down; dread mixes with excitement (M01361).
  • “She’ll never take him alive” (M01361) — the “she” is not identified in the notes.

Abilities & Traits

  • Has gun? But not gun. But gun. Gun. (M01366–M01369, FreshToast, 2023-02-10) — i.e., a gun-like weapon whose exact nature was left deliberately unresolved.

Open questions

  • PROPOSED Who is hunting Cervantes (“she”), and how his outlaw plot intersects the party, is undeveloped.

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — concept posted with an accompanying character playlist: reformer-turned-outlaw, allies cut down, “She’ll never take him alive” (M01361, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-10 — the gun exchange: “Cervantes has gun? / but not gun / but gun / gun” (M01366–M01369, FreshToast).
