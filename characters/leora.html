Leora

SCRAPPEDEarly ConceptNotes / Design
Also known as: Leora the outcast
First conceived 2021-08-01 (M00317, FreshToast) · last in chat 2021-08-31 (M00829, FreshToast)

Leora SCRAPPED

An early-concept protagonist — “the outcast” — with a sun association and short-range teleportation, who ultimately survived not as a character but as the game’s silhouette/emblem.

Summary — Leora was one of the earliest (2021) protagonist candidates for the project. She was framed as “the outcast,” a main protagonist character with a sun association and the ability to teleport short distances with some regularity, tied to sword-throwing/teleportation combat (M00317, FreshToast, 2021-08-01; M00668, FreshToast, 2021-08-16). The name did not carry into the later Skyward Cinders / Caelian Odyssey casts; its most durable legacy is as the game’s emblem/silhouette and a lingering “sun association” the team later tried to protect when naming other characters.

Appearance & Design legacy

  • Became the basis for the game’s silhouette / Leora emblem, with multiple design attempts explored (M00809, FreshToast, 2021-08-26). (Emblem/silhouette design work belongs to Art & Animation Direction.)
  • Floated to have magical skates, Shadow the Hedgehog style (M00829, FreshToast, 2021-08-31).

Abilities & Traits

  • Sun association (002 §Undying vs Heroes / Leora; echoed in the Cyrus surname debate, M00609, FreshToast, 2021-08-15).
  • Short-distance teleportation with some regularity: “Teleportation could just be a thing Leora can do with some regularity, albeit really short distance” (M00668, FreshToast, 2021-08-16), associated with sword-throwing/teleport mechanics.
  • Had magical capabilities generally (002 §Undying vs Heroes / Leora).

Role & Arc

  • Named as “Leora, the outcast” in an early character-name brainstorm (M00317, FreshToast, 2021-08-01).
  • Cast as a main protagonist character in the early “Albedo + Gojo + space-time” / Undying vs Heroes concept (002 §Undying vs Heroes / Leora). This is flagged in the source as “from an earlier/different game concept than the main Skyward Cinders.”

Relationships

  • Her sun association was invoked against the proposed surname “Cyrus Sunworth” — FreshToast’s “sole issue” was that “Sunworth” “takes away somewhat from Leora’s sun association” (M00609, FreshToast, 2021-08-15). See Cyrus Sunworth.

Open questions

  • DEBATED Whether “Leora” was ever intended to persist as a character or only as the emblem/silhouette name is not resolved in the notes; after 2021 she appears only as the emblem and as a naming-clash reference point.

Design history

  • 2021-08-01 — name proposed in a brainstorm list: “Leora, the outcast” (M00317, FreshToast).
  • 2021-08-15 — Leora’s sun association treated as established, used to argue against the “Sunworth” surname for the mentee Cyrus (M00609, FreshToast).
  • 2021-08-16 — abilities sketched: short-distance teleportation “with some regularity,” tied to sword-throw/teleport combat (M00668, FreshToast).
  • 2021-08-26 — repurposed into the game’s silhouette / “Leora emblem,” with several design attempts (M00809, FreshToast) — the character concept effectively gives way to the emblem here.
  • 2021-08-31 — last mention: “What if Leora had magical skates Shadow the Hedgehog style?” (M00829, FreshToast). The name does not recur in the later Skyward Cinders / Caelian Odyssey casts. Superseded / scrapped as a character; emblem legacy documented in Art & Animation Direction.
