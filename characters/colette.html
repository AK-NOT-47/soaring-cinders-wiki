Colette CANON

One of the 4 Boyes: the trilingual Lunoiran “heart” of the group — culture ambassador, voice of reason, empath, and the wanderlust-driven one.

Summary — Colette is a Lunoiran female in her early 20s, in a support role as the “heart” of the group. She represents “the power of names and foreign uses or interpretations of sorcery” and speaks Lunoiran, Imperial Lycosian and Fioran (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). She is big on cultural cross-pollination, acts as the group’s moral compass, and dreams of exploring the world beyond the academy (M04365–M04367, M04373–M04375, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Identity

  • Lunoiran female; early 20s (M04227, M04243, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • A bit darker complexion than the others (compared to the western-Khorath Mediterranean standard) (M04242, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Affinity undecided; archetype uncertain (M04227).

Role & Function

  • One of the 4 Boyes; support role — the “heart” of the group (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Meant to represent a general facet of Sorcery and magic (M04226, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Magical focus

  • Represents the power of names and foreign uses / interpretations of sorcery (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Trilingual: speaks Lunoiran, Imperial Lycosian and Fioran (M04227).
  • Big on cultural cross-pollination (M04367, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Personality

  • Culture Ambassador — acts as a bridge between the academy’s different magical cultures; sees the bigger picture, a good person looking out for the greater good (M04370–M04371, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Fiction / adventure / cool-history nerd who quotes things she’s read: “I dig the idea of her quoting things she’s read” (M04371–M04372, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Voice of Reason — often acts as the moral compass of the group (M04373, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-17; confirmed M04375).
  • Empath — can sense/feel the emotions of others, guiding her actions — “Yes and yes but not magically” (M04374–M04375, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Wanderlust

  • Dreams of exploring the world beyond the academy, fuelling her inventive spirit — “OH COLETTE. She’s number 9” (M04363–M04366, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • (Alt framing in the notes: “or is she scared of the world. Wanting to protect the world from her uncontrolled power?”) (M04356 list item 9 text, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-17; 002 §Colette: Wanderlust).

Dark secret

  • DEBATED Dark Secret = “maybe? idk” — may have a hidden past/secret she’s running from (potentially crucial to the plot); unresolved (M04370 item 3, M04371, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Conflicts (brainstormed) PROPOSED

Ten-item conflict brainstorm list — design seeds, not yet finalized (M04382–M04391, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-17):

  1. Need for Validation — seeks constant validation to affirm her self-worth
  2. Financial Struggles — feels pressure due to lack of funds
  3. Desire to Fit In — adopts personas to feel included
  4. Hidden Talent — has a non-magical talent she’s too shy to show
  5. Perceived Weakness — feels she’s not strong enough compared to peers
  6. Mentor Letdown — disappointed by someone she looked up to
  7. Social Anxiety — struggles with large gatherings or expressing herself in groups
  8. Desire for Recognition — wants her achievements to be recognized
  9. Seeking Role Models — looks for strong role models but often feels let down
  10. Loss — grieves the loss of a close friend or family member

Rejected ideas SCRAPPED

  • Prodigious learner / goody-two-shoes — rejected (“1. no”) (M04370–M04371, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Toph-like indomitable will — rejected (“4. no”) (M04370–M04371).

Design history

  • 2023-10-17 — initial concept in the 4 Boyes roster write-up: Lunoiran female, archetype uncertain, affinity undecided, “Support role? The ‘heart’ of the group”, power of names + foreign sorcery, trilingual (M04227, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-17 — Wanderlust (trait #9 from the Pandora vetting list) assigned to her: “OH COLETTE. She’s number 9. Big on that cultural cross polination” (M04363–M04367, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-17 — trait vetting: prodigious learner no; Culture Ambassador yes (“although that’s two things”); Dark Secret “maybe? idk”; Toph-like will no; Bookworm reshaped into a fiction/adventure/cool-history nerd who quotes things she’s read (M04369–M04372, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-17 — Voice of Reason and non-magical Empath confirmed (M04373–M04375, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-17 — 10-item conflict brainstorm posted (M04382–M04391, CrunchyConniptions).
