Damaris CANON (disambiguation)

“Damaris” is a family name in Skyward Cinders — it can refer to the noble family, its heir, its bastard, or a duplicate character entry in the docs.

Damaris may refer to:

The Damaris family

The noble family descended from Ajax. “Damaris is her family name, heraldry is what you call a family’s crest and such” (M02029, FreshToast, 2023-02-14). The sun is present on the Damaris heraldry/family crest as a giver/beacon to their people; it gets “eclipsed” by the nasty happenings as a visual metaphor for the family’s fall from grace, and un-eclipses when Clara becomes a true beacon of hope (M02017–M02020, FreshToast, 2023-02-14). Ajax’s descendants carry a diluted version of his take/steal power, which wanes with each heir (M02668, FreshToast, 2023-02-21), and are reputed to have sunset irises (M02904–M02906, FreshToast, 2023-03-05).

Clara Damaris

Party member and heir to the family (alias: Marie). In her final confrontation she declares: “I am heir Damaris. You have tainted our family’s reputation… Today you die.” (M02016, FreshToast, 2023-02-14; see Clara Damaris).

Bastard Damaris

Illegitimate member of the family, youngest of Ajax’s bloodline, first to exhibit truly prominent draconic features, and herald of the parasite Drykksar — a mid-game antagonist (M02668–M02694, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-21/22; see Bastard Damaris).

“Damaris” (standalone character entry in 002)

002 contains a separate entry, “Damaris (character - not same as Damaris family),” marked STATUS: UNCLEAR - LIKELY REFERS TO BASTARD DAMARIS (002 §Damaris (character)). Its only content is the pre-fight scene:

Damaris: people bad. I kill. Locke: give people chance, maybe good. no kill Astrid: fuck u. I kill. (M02682, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22)

The doc’s analysis: this scene is the confrontation with the Bastard Damaris — the dialogue matches his philosophy (humanity is flawed, must be eliminated) — and its recommendation is to remove the duplicate entry, since the dialogue is already integrated into the Bastard Damaris profile (002 §Damaris (character)). This wiki follows that reading: the scene lives on Bastard Damaris.
