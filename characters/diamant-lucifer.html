Diamant Lucifer PROPOSED

A retired assassin with an unwavering sense of justice, drawn back into violence by a past that won’t let him go.

Summary — Diamant Lucifer is a retired assassin in his late twenties who hung up his weapons after a long career, hoping to live out his life in peace — but he is pursued by a group of assassins for mysterious reasons and finds himself drawn back into the world of violence and danger (M01287–M01288, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09). Cold and calculating yet bound by a strong moral code, he never accepted contracts on innocents and keeps his promises no matter how dangerous the task (M01287).

Appearance

Late twenties; a towering 6’2" with a broad, muscular build; rough, weathered face testifying to a hard life; thick beard, hair tied back in a tight bun. Walks with a limp from an old injury sustained during his assassin days, but still holds himself with an air of confidence — always alert, never letting his guard down (M01287, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).

Personality

  • A man of few words; lets actions speak louder (M01287, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).
  • Cold, calculating, fiercely independent (M01287).
  • Unwavering sense of justice; will not hesitate to eliminate those who threaten it (M01287).
  • Strong moral code: will not accept contracts on innocent people; a man of his word who keeps his promises no matter how dangerous the task (M01287).

Background & Motivations

  • Born in the rough-and-tumble countryside; showed remarkable aptitude for hunting and marksmanship from a young age, catching the attention of a notorious assassin guild that recruited him at 18 (M01288, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).
  • Became one of the most feared assassins in the guild, taking contracts all over the world; grew to question the morality of the profession and retired (M01288).
  • Now lives in exile from the very group that took him in; never truly free from his past, constantly on guard against those who would eliminate him (M01288).
  • Pursued by a group of assassins for mysterious reasons (M01288).
  • Despite his desire for peace, driven by a strong sense of duty — will not hesitate to protect those he cares about, even at the cost of his own life (M01288).

Design history
