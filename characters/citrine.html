Citrine PROPOSED

Ruby’s rival-sister: the homunculus Gideon made correctly, ironclad-loyal to their creator, a “shinier psychopath version” of Ruby who dies by her hand.

Summary — Citrine is the second homunculus created by Gideon Azurith — this time with the ritual performed correctly, using his own soul (M02178–M02179, FreshToast, 2023-02-16). She is Ruby’s rival and dark mirror, visually inverted from her (yellow eyes, red hair — M01289, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). She sees herself as her master’s tool and, though she comes to love Ruby as a wayward sister, she cannot break her loyalty; the sisters fight, and Ruby kills her but spares their creator in Citrine’s name (M02077, FreshToast, 2023-02-15). Filed under Archived Early Concepts in 002, though her death was still cited as a regret-theme avenue as late as October 2023.

Appearance

  • Inverted colors from Ruby: yellow eyes and red hair (M01289, FreshToast, 2023-02-09) — versus Ruby’s red eyes and golden hair.

Personality

  • Initial concept: “Rival who is just a shinier psychopath version?” of Ruby (M00959, FreshToast, 2023-02-05); rival is like Jinx from Arcane (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • Sees herself as her master’s tool, a second-class being; when she sees a chance — potential for more — Citrine rejects it (M02181–M02183, CrunchyConniptions/spirit_noodle/FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Comes to respect and see Ruby as a wayward sister, but just as it seems she will be convinced, she cannot reconcile her ironclad loyalty to their creator with Ruby’s intentions (M02077, FreshToast, 2023-02-15).

Background & Motivations

  • Created correctly by Gideon using the proper ritual — his own flesh and soul — unlike Ruby, who was made with a borrowed soul (M02178–M02179, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Sent by her creator to gather criminals and outlaws; he harvests their souls to be converted into golems, and the golems serve to progress society along with Citrine (M02173–M02174, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).

Relationships

  • Ruby’s rival and, in effect, her sister — “though unwilling, the sisters must fight. One to protect and aide her allies turned family, the other to protect her own family” (M02077, FreshToast, 2023-02-15).
  • Loyal to and cared for by their creator Gideon Azurith — the inverse of Ruby’s absent-father dynamic (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).

Role & Arc

  • Antagonist who becomes sympathetic; dies by Ruby’s hand (002 §Citrine).
  • Ruby kills Citrine but spares the creator in Citrine’s name (M02077, FreshToast, 2023-02-15).
  • Her death powers one of the story’s death/regret avenues: “Ruby once she kills Citrine” (M04658–M04660, FreshToast, 2023-10-24).

Open questions

  • DEBATED 002 files Citrine under Archived Early Concepts (“do not consider these active characters”), yet the kills-Citrine beat was still being used in theme planning on 2023-10-24 (M04660) — whether her arc carries into the current Skyward Cinders iteration alongside Ruby is unresolved.

Design history

  • 2023-02-05 — rival concept raised during Ruby’s profile session: “just a shinier psychopath version” (M00959, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-09 — named Citrine, visual design set as Ruby’s inversion: yellow eyes, red hair (M01289, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-15 — full sisterhood arc written: wayward-sister respect, irreconcilable loyalty, the sisters’ duel, Ruby sparing the creator in her name (M02077, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-16 — deepened: made correctly with the proper ritual (M02178–M02179); soul-harvest errand for Gideon (M02173–M02174); “master’s tool / second-class being / rejects the chance for more” characterization (M02181–M02183).
  • 2023-10-24 — her death reaffirmed as a regret-theme avenue for Ruby (M04658–M04660, FreshToast); profile subsequently archived in 002. — current archived state
