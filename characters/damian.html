Damian DEBATED

Cold-affinity party member from Fiore, pursued by a vigilante/private investigator we meet first — underdeveloped, but present in the latest 2024 roster.

Summary — Damian is a Cold-affinity party member (M03051, FreshToast, 2023-03-15) from Fiore, “the country across the mountains” (M03826, FreshToast, 2023-10-13). His most concrete plot hook: he “is pursued by some vigilante/private investigator but we actually meet her first who then leads us to Damian” (M02701, FreshToast, 2023-02-23), a pursuit that “results in a romance plot” (M02770, FreshToast, 2023-02-25) — a premise originally sketched for Leon. Doc 002 flags him UNDERDEVELOPED — NEEDS DECISION, yet he appears in the latest team-skill roster, paired with Percy (M05392, FreshToast, 2024-04-11).

Name / Identity

  • Affinity: Cold — “Cold - Damian, Astrid, Soren, Cultboy” (M03051/M03052, FreshToast, 2023-03-15).
  • From Fiore, the country across the mountains (M03826, FreshToast, 2023-10-13).
  • The name “Damian” first appears in the 2021 name bank (M00514, spirit_noodle, 2021-08-01) — whether that entry is this character is unstated.

Background & Relationships

  • Pursued by a vigilante/private investigator; the party meets her first and she leads them to Damian (M02701, FreshToast, 2023-02-23). The hunter is “the proper, by-the-book archetype” and the pursuit “results in a romance plot” (M02769–M02770, FreshToast, 2023-02-25). This archetype claim is why the Hermione-esque rival for Isaac/Percy had to be a boy — reusing the dynamic “strikes too many parallels with Damian” (M02766–M02767, M02771, FreshToast, 2023-02-25).
  • DEBATED The pursued-romance premise was first written for Leon (“Leonard hunted by a cop, maybe finally turns into a romance plot?” — M01396, FreshToast, 2023-02-10); no message resolves which character owns it. See Leon’s page.

Abilities & Traits

  • Mobility intent: “let Damian/Cyril and Sable schmoove a bit” — a middle mobility tier next to the static Thane/Isaac (M02579, FreshToast, 2023-02-20).
  • Team-skill pairing (2024 scheme): paired with Percy; e.g. Astrid gets a positional bonus “when she is beside Percy or Damian” (M05392, M05394, FreshToast, 2024-04-11).
  • Dog-breed party comparison: Old English Sheepdog (M03029, FreshToast, 2023-03-13).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Doc 002 status: UNDERDEVELOPED — NEEDS DECISION, with options to (1) develop fully as the Cold-affinity party member from Fiore, (2) merge with Soren or Cultboy (both also Cold), (3) convert to an NPC from the Fiore region, or (4) remove if redundant (002 §Damian). Weight toward keeping: his presence in the 2024 roster (M05392) postdates that assessment.
  • DEBATED Ownership of the hunted-romance plot vs. Leon (M01396 vs. M02701/M02769–M02770).
