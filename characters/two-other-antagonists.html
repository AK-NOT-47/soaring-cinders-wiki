Two Other Antagonists & Himbo Antagonist EVOLVED

Two placeholder antagonist concepts from the very first antagonist roster (2023-02-09): a Team Rocket-style comedic-relief duo, and a “Himbo antagonist?” — both effectively absorbed by later characters.

Summary — In the first antagonist brainstorm, the roster listed “2 others, less important. Serve as comedic relief, team rocket style” (M01219, FreshToast, 2023-02-09) and, in the follow-up list, “Himbo antagonist?” (M01220, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). Neither was developed under these names. PROPOSED — inference The comedic-relief duo maps onto the pair of goofy, loyal-to-a-fault sibling pawns in Lysander’s party defined a week later (M02335–M02341, 2023-02-16), eventually named Flynt & Steele (M03394, FreshToast, 2023-06-04); the himbo archetype was explicitly filled by Flynt — “we at least have Flynt who’s probably a straight up himbo” (M03392, FreshToast, 2023-06-04).
