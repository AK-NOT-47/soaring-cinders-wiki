Two Other Antagonists & Himbo Antagonist

Skyward CindersNotes / DesignWorking nameTertiary character
Also known as: Team Rocket duo, Himbo Antagonist, Himbo
First conceived 2023-02-09

Two placeholder antagonist concepts from the very first antagonist roster (2023-02-09): a Team Rocket-style comedic-relief duo, and a “Himbo antagonist?” — both effectively absorbed by later characters.

Summary — In the first antagonist brainstorm, the roster listed “2 others, less important. Serve as comedic relief, team rocket style” (2023-02-09) and, in the follow-up list, “Himbo antagonist?” (2023-02-09). Neither was developed under these names. The comedic-relief duo maps onto the pair of goofy, loyal-to-a-fault sibling pawns in Lysander’s party defined a week later (2023-02-16), eventually named Flynt & Steele (2023-06-04); the himbo archetype was explicitly filled by Flynt — “we at least have Flynt who’s probably a straight up himbo” (2023-06-04).

Two Other Antagonists

  • Less important; serve as comedic relief, Team Rocket style (2023-02-09).
  • STATUS: PLACEHOLDER - NEEDS DEVELOPMENT OR REMOVAL (002 §Two Other Antagonists). Options recorded in the doc: (1) develop as recurring comic-relief villains with actual personalities and arcs; (2) remove if not essential; (3) replace with a single comic-relief antagonist. Recommendation: either fully develop with names, personalities, and motivations, or remove — half-developed comic relief can fall flat (002 §Two Other Antagonists).
  • The dated sequence suggests this slot was filled by Lysander’s two goofy sibling pawns, Flynt & Steele (2023-02-16, 2023-06-04).

Himbo Antagonist

  • Listed as “Himbo antagonist?” in the second antagonist list, alongside Thane’s death spirit, Sable’s 7 outlaws, Ruby’s rival and creator, and Marie’s family traitor (2023-02-09).
  • Relationship with Gaius not established — noted on the draft relationship diagram (2023-02-09).
  • “Locke & Himbo” pairing noted (2023-02-09) — the doc reads this pairing as suggesting a romantic interest with Locke (002 §Himbo Antagonist).
  • “Himbo Plot Ideas” was created as a list and left empty (2023-02-09); mentioned but no plot ideas filled in yet (002 §Himbo Antagonist).
  • STATUS: CONCEPT ONLY - NEEDS DEVELOPMENT OR REMOVAL (002 §Himbo Antagonist). Unclear if protagonist or antagonist despite section placement. Options recorded in the doc: (1) develop as antagonist-turned-ally with himbo personality (likeable but not bright); (2) move to protagonist/ally section if not truly antagonistic; (3) merge with Rhea/Magic Batman character if redundant; (4) remove if the concept doesn’t fit (002 §Himbo Antagonist).

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — both concepts appear in the first antagonist rosters: “2 others, less important. Serve as comedic relief, team rocket style” and “Himbo antagonist?”; draft relationship diagram notes Himbo & Gaius relationship not established; “Locke & Himbo” pairing; empty “Himbo Plot Ideas” list.
  • 2023-02-09 — same day, in the party design thread: “Resolve, replaces himbo” — an early sign the himbo slot was being repurposed (the Rhea Scala “Magic Batman” character’s Resolve stat “replaces Himbo” per 002 §Rhea Scala).
  • 2023-02-16 — the remaining 2 of Lysander’s party defined as a pair of siblings: largely unimportant, loyal to a fault, goofy, probably not all that bad — pawns. This fills the “two others, comedic relief” slot.
  • 2023-02-19 — joke beat: “Ajax could be our himbo” / “himbo legendary hero”.
  • 2023-06-04 — “We need a Himbo in the game somewhere” → “we at least have Flynt who’s probably a straight up himbo”; Flynt & Steele named — the himbo archetype lands on Flynt; matches the current presentation above.
