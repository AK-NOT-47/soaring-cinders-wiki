The Academy’s Head PROPOSED

The unnamed head of the Luminarium: an ancient, wise figure who has seen the rise and fall of many powerful sorcerers — “a good old dude dishing out wisdom.”

Summary — The Academy’s Head is “an ancient, wise character who’s seen the rise and fall of many powerful sorcerers. Holds secrets that can be central to the plot” (M04302, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-17). The character has no name yet; the concept was welcomed but not developed: “Oh I hadn’t given them much thought… I like a good old dude dishing out wisdom” (M04304–M04305, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Personality & Role

  • Ancient, wise character; has seen the rise and fall of many powerful sorcerers (M04302, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-17).
  • Holds secrets that can be central to the plot (M04302).
  • “the important part is old and wise” (M04303, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-17).
  • “I like a good old dude dishing out wisdom” (M04305, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Context: beyond the Big 6 there will be several professors/mentors featuring on the side (M04282–M04283, FreshToast, 2023-10-17) — the Head is the top of that supporting cast.

Open questions

  • No name, appearance, or plot specifics yet — only the “old and wise” core.

Design history

  • 2023-10-17 — pitched by CrunchyConniptions among academy-setting staples (tournaments, forbidden lore, magical creatures); FreshToast hadn’t given them much thought but likes a good old dude dishing out wisdom (M04302–M04305).
