The recurring, plucky merchant — the character face of the game’s Shopkeeper system.

Summary — A “recurring, plucky merchant” (M02075, FreshToast, 2023-02-15) who appears throughout the game as its shopkeeper. The character side is only this personality sketch; all of the mechanics — behavior, inventory, and how the shop works — live in the Shopkeeper system (002 §Recurring Merchant, cross-ref to 008 Game Mechanics).
