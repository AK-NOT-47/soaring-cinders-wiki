Sable CANON

A 19-year-old lucky, loud, lesbian bounty hunter who fights for money and thrills — always thinking about money, girls, or the guitar riff from Free Bird.

Summary — Sable is the party’s most mobile, most AP-efficient fighter (M01875, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-13): an energetic, impulsive mercenary from a working-class background who joins Locke’s party as a business opportunity and grows to genuinely care. She has high luck and a family that loves her — the deliberate inverse of Marie/Clara’s low luck and hateful family (M01222, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-09). She is not yet implemented in the browser proto — her whole combat kit is design-intent only (002 §Main Party header; 002 §Sable).

Identity

  • Age: 19 — corrected from “early 20s”; the ages list gives 19 (M01217, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).
  • Affinity: Fiery (002 §Sable).
  • Luck: High luck; has a family that loves her (M01222, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-09).
  • Appearance: she’ll have a wolf-cut / messy-bob hairstyle; early art WIP had trouble with the pose and making her look younger (M01444–M01447, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • Dog-breed comparison: Border Collie (M03029, spirit_noodle, 2023-03-13).
  • “A lot of me is in Ruby & Sable so I like them too” (M04966, FreshToast, 2023-11-15).

Personality

  • Always thinking about: money, girls, or the guitar riff from Free Bird at any given moment (M02413–M02414, FreshToast, 2023-02-18).
  • Gay (M02028, FreshToast, 2023-02-14) — but written feminine, not leaning into the tomboy-lesbian trope: “Being a lesbian doesn’t guarantee her becoming a tomboy,” “All kinds of girls be fuckin other girls”; not particularly tomboy or delicate either (M02028–M02034, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14).
  • “Cute Loud Gremlin” — her archetype tag from the scrapped meet-order list (M01905–M01916, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14).
  • Energetic and impulsive — acts first, thinks later; shameless flirt but genuinely romantic at heart; loves the thrill of adventure and combat; materialistic but not greedy — enjoys nice things and sharing them; fiercely protective of friends despite her casual attitude; uses humor and bravado to mask deeper insecurities (002 §Sable).
  • Flaws (002 §Sable): impulsive and reckless in pursuit of thrills; materialistic streak can make her seem mercenary; avoids serious emotional conversations with jokes; struggles to admit when she’s scared or hurt; too competitive, turning everything into a contest.

Background & Motivations

  • Bounty hunter/mercenary who works for money and thrills; comes from a working-class background — learned to fight for survival (002 §Sable).
  • CANON Tracks a specific list of 7 outlaws (mostly henchmen for other villains), hunting them for bounty to feed herself and her family. One of the hunters pursuing Leon is on this list (002 §Sable; 000 Part 5/8).
  • Motivations (002 §Sable): make enough money to live comfortably and support herself; prove she’s the best fighter/hunter in the business; find genuine connection and maybe settle down with the right woman; experience life to the fullest — no regrets.
  • Goals (002 §Sable): complete high-value bounties on her list; protect party members (even when they’re also on bounty lists); eventually choose between money and loyalty to friends; win the heart of someone special.

Abilities & Traits

All of Sable’s combat kit is design intent — she is not a built hero in the proto (002 §Sable).

  • Party position: 1st line (Front) (002 §Sable).
  • Most mobile and most efficient at using AP in the party (M01875, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-13); has an easy time moving with skills and more modular skills (M01874/M01880, FreshToast, 2023-02-13); “let Damian/Cyril and Sable schmoove a bit” (M02579, FreshToast, 2023-02-20).
  • PROPOSED — not implemented Has skills that directly increase or scale based on Escalation Mechanic. The escalation counter itself IS implemented in the proto, but no escalation-scaling player skill exists yet (002 §Sable).
  • Agile melee fighter — possibly dual-wielding or quick weapons; excels at chaining attacks and building momentum; can reposition easily and exploit openings; high-risk, high-reward playstyle (002 §Sable).
  • PROPOSED Naturally lucky — could make the luck scale/stat grow faster for her, tying her luck into a player-facing number (M04543, FreshToast, 2023-10-22).
  • Exploration: teaches Locke how to throw her voice (M01683, FreshToast, 2023-02-11) — part of the DEBATED exploration-skills scheme.
  • Attack animation (M05689–M05693, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2024-09-06): cat-style butt wiggle pre-pounce (from a Reddit cat video), “a low-stanced sort of sway,” as the anticipation idle animation while selecting a skill and targets — “and the music syncs with her dance.”

Relationships

  • Becomes close with Rhea (magic batman) through shared materialism and mutual attraction (002 §Sable); the sourced pairing lineage is Marie & Sable (M01231, FreshToast, 2023-02-09) plus “Sable and Thief become close through their shared materialism” (M01259, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • One of the hunters pursuing Leon is on Sable’s list — creates moral conflict (002 §Sable).
  • Initially sees the party as a business opportunity, grows to genuinely care (002 §Sable).
  • PROPOSED Bonds with Locke over both being “self-made” vs nobility — not in the Discord notes (002 §Sable).
  • Friendly rivalry with other frontline fighters (002 §Sable).
  • Team-skill pairing: Astrid/Sable (M05392, FreshToast, 2024-04-11).

Role & Arc

(002 §Sable)

  • Early: joins for money/bounties; treats the adventure as a job with benefits; comic relief through shameless flirting and materialistic comments.
  • Mid: discovers Leon has a bounty — must choose between money and friendship; meets/develops a relationship with Rhea through shared love of treasure; has a moment where choosing party over profit costs her significantly.
  • Late: fully commits to the party’s cause over personal gain; romantic subplot reaches resolution; proves loyalty in dramatic fashion; “learns that real treasure is friends made along the way (but also actual treasure is nice).”
  • Emotional beats: chooses to protect Leon instead of claiming the bounty; admits she’s scared of losing people; romantic confession scene; final battle fought for those she loves, not money.
  • Plot impact: her bounty-hunter connections provide an information network; bounty-vs-loyalty tension drives her development; represents found family vs transactional relationships; the romance subplot adds warmth and representation.

Not reconciled — she is unimplemented in the proto, so neither is runnable truth yet.
