Thorne "Tabby" Tumblemane

Skyward CindersNotes / DesignWorking nameSecondary character
Also known as: Tabby
First conceived 2023-06-04

A colossal Catfolk-human gentle giant filler NPC — 7’2" with the afro, ~300 lbs, a lover not a fighter who just wants a quieter life.

Summary — Thorne “Tabby” Tumblemane is a filler NPC concept: a colossal figure of 6’8" — 7’2" counting his vibrant auburn afro, “reminiscent of a lion’s mane” — weighing a robust ~300 lbs, of combined Catfolk and human lineage with the body strength of a human and the agility and reflexes of a Catfolk, plus pointed ears and captivating golden eyes (2023-06-04). His gentle, friendly personality is a stark contrast to his towering presence — a “gentle giant” (2023-06-04). “Big cat man, lover not a fighter” (2023-06-04). The name will probably change and he waits in reserve: “In my brain he shall reside until we have need of him” (2023-06-04).

Name / Identity

  • The name: “Tabby’s a bit too on the (cat) nose” — “This is a cute idea though”; “I’ll probably rename him tho, sorry” (2023-06-04). Will probably be renamed.
  • Lineage: mixed Catfolk and human — “cat man confirmed” via coin flip between wolf and cat: “wolf… no wait… cat… definetly cat” / “The coin flip always works” (2023-06-04).
  • Represent-two-groups rationale: asked whether he should specifically be a cat man, Crunchy wanted him “just mixed” — “He should represent two groups” (2023-06-04); hence the human + demi-human (Catfolk) combination.

Appearance

  • 6’8" plus a six-inch vibrant auburn afro like a lion’s mane — ~7’2" total; ~300 lbs (2023-06-04).
  • Pointed ears; captivating golden eyes.
  • Too big for a carriage, so he prefers to travel by foot; favorite worn-out shoes (the only pair that fits); uncomplicated wardrobe, since attire for his physique is hard to find.
  • Scale pushback: “demi-humans aren’t superhumanly tall so more likely he’s near your height lol” (2023-06-04).

Personality

  • Gentle and friendly, in stark contrast to his towering presence (2023-06-04).
  • Prefers to stay out of the limelight — not from shyness but to avoid the excessive attention his size attracts.
  • “Big cat man, lover not a fighter”; “Might bottom if pressed” / “Maybe he is a bottom and proud” / “soft top” (2023-06-04).

Conflict & Flaw

  • Grapples with the societal expectations of his intimidating physique; constantly at odds with local authorities and adventuring groups who want to use his strength when he’d prefer a quieter life (2023-06-04).
  • Biggest flaw: lack of ambition and direction — content to take life as it comes, overlooking opportunities; his trusting nature lands him in local politics and conflicts he’d rather avoid.

Role & Arc

  • A journey of acceptance and self-discovery: learning to navigate a world that sees him as more than what he wishes to be, and finding his place within it (2023-06-04).
  • Intended slot: filler NPC — “We need filler NPCs anyway” (2023-06-04).

Design notes

  • There’s a recognized “need for a Himbo in the game somewhere” (2023-06-04) — though this archetype was corrected from “himbo” toward “gentle giant” (2023-06-04), with Flynt noted as the party’s “straight up himbo” (2023-06-04).
  • “It’s a classic character type and a pretty contemporary one as well!” — “People love their gremlins, girlfailures and disastrous gays these days” (2023-06-04).
  • Real-world inspiration: a Reddit post about a 6’8", ~300 lb mixed Black-and-Mongolian man (2023-06-04) — “the most genetically threatening man on this earth” (2023-06-04).

Design history

  • 2023-06-04 — full write-up posted: colossal Catfolk-human “himbo,” worn-out shoes, limelight-avoidant; conflict and flaw sections, sparked by the Reddit post.
  • 2023-06-04 — archetype corrected: not a himbo but a “gentle giant”; mix the two; Flynt already covers straight-up himbo — supersedes the “himbo” framing above.
  • 2023-06-04 — identity settled for now: rename planned (“Tabby” too on the cat nose); should “represent two groups,” so mixed lineage; wolf-vs-cat coin flip lands on cat (“cat man confirmed”); “Big cat man, lover not a fighter”; shelved until needed.
