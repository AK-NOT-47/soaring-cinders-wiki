Thane's Foil (Paladin)

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / DesignWorking name
Also known as: The Paladin, Prideful Paladin, The Lion
First conceived 2023-02-10 (M01376, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-02-16 (M02347)

Thane’s Foil (Paladin) CANON

A zealous, life-affirming paladin — Thane’s perfect foil (death vs life), who hunts the Death spirit and lives in a world of black and white.

Summary — Thane’s Foil is a paladin with life associations who “lives in a world of black and white” (M01376, FreshToast, 2023-02-10). He is not strictly in the wrong from his own worldview — but he’s a bit of a dick about his righteousness, overreacts (M01384, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-10), and acts as judge, jury, and executioner. Independent of Lysander’s hero party (M02335–M02341, 2023-02-16), he hunts Thane and the Death spirit and provides a big, intentionally hard boss fight that Thane mechanically counters — to make Thane look cool (002 §Thane’s Foil). The character was never given a real name (placeholder_name: true).

Identity & Appearance

  • Title: Paladin or Knight; possible names to be determined — 002 suggests Valerius connections or a “Righteous”-themed name (002 §Thane’s Foil).
  • Affinity: likely Fiery or Electric (life-affirming elements) (002 §Thane’s Foil).
  • Connection: noble or religious order (002 §Thane’s Foil).
  • Animal motif: as Thane is the wolf, the Paladin is the Lion (M01416–M01417, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-11).
  • “Paladin must have winged bucket helm” (M01831, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-12).

Personality

  • Life associations — Thane is death, the Foil is life (M01376, FreshToast, 2023-02-10).
  • Paladin dedicated to the preservation of life and justice; lives in a world of black and white, no moral grey areas (M01376, FreshToast, 2023-02-10).
  • Not strictly in the wrong from his worldview, but a bit of a dick about his righteousness; “the paladin is overreacting” (M01384, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-10).
  • Acts as judge, jury and executioner based on his perspective of justice; genuinely believes he’s doing righteous work (002 §Thane’s Foil).
  • Cannot comprehend Thane’s acceptance of death as natural; sees the Death spirit as an abomination that must be destroyed; zealous and uncompromising; honorable in his own rigid way (002 §Thane’s Foil).

Background & Motivations

  • Member of nobility or a holy order dedicated to protecting life; possibly connected to Clara’s past (childhood friend, former betrothed, or family ally); lost someone to death, driving his zealous mission; sees himself as a crusader against death itself (002 §Thane’s Foil).

Motivations: eradicate death spirits and anything that “embraces” death; bring Thane to justice for perceived crimes (associating with the Death spirit); uphold a rigid code of justice without mercy; prove that absolute righteousness can triumph over moral ambiguity; save people from themselves — even if they don’t want saving (002 §Thane’s Foil).

Goals: hunt down and destroy Death (the spirit); capture or kill Thane for consorting with the death spirit; enforce his order’s laws absolutely; eliminate all “unnatural” beings (possibly including Ruby) (002 §Thane’s Foil).

Flaws: cannot see nuance or moral grey areas; righteousness blinds him to the harm he causes; overreacts to situations that don’t fit his worldview; refuses to listen or compromise; his pursuit of “justice” becomes persecution; pride in his righteous mission; cannot admit when he might be wrong (002 §Thane’s Foil).

Relationships & Dynamics

  • With Thane: perfect foil — death vs life, grey morality vs black-and-white, acceptance vs defiance. Eventually hunts down Thane and/or the Death spirit; Thane is the perfect counter for the boss fight mechanically; thematically both hold extreme positions around the grey Thane lives in (002 §Thane’s Foil). Thane’s summarized arc includes “dealing with spirit hunters including his foil” (M02347, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • With Clara: knows Clara from her noble past; possibly a childhood friend or former betrothed; she knows the good person he used to be before zealotry consumed him (002 §Thane’s Foil).
  • With the party: sees them as criminals or heretics for protecting Thane; may target other party members (Ruby as “unnatural,” Leon as fugitive) (002 §Thane’s Foil).
  • Not in Lysander’s hero party — the Prideful Paladin that Thane fights can be independent (M02335–M02341, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).

Abilities & Traits

  • Powerful paladin with holy/life magic; commands 2 henchmen in battle (fills 2 slots with them); skilled warrior and tactician (002 §Thane’s Foil).
  • Big cool tough fight — a significant boss battle; mechanically countered by Thane, but the difficulty is intentionally high to make Thane look cool (002 §Thane’s Foil).
  • Life-based abilities that contrast Thane’s death theme; possibly resurrection or healing powers; buff/support abilities for henchmen; a strong sense of honor prevents him from using underhanded tactics (002 §Thane’s Foil).

Role & Arc

Role in story: mid-game antagonist representing absolutism and zealotry; thematic opposite to Thane’s arc; tests whether Thane has truly found peace with death (002 §Thane’s Foil).

Confrontation: hunts the party, specifically targeting Thane and the Death spirit; Clara tries to reason with him based on their shared past; he cannot be reasoned with — too consumed by zealotry; epic boss fight where Thane shines as the perfect counter; the party must beat him but NOT kill him (002 §Thane’s Foil).

Resolution: defeated but survives the encounter; may have a moment of doubt after defeat; possibly returns later, changed by the experience; Clara’s history with him adds emotional weight; serves as a warning about taking beliefs too far (002 §Thane’s Foil).

Emotional beats: Clara’s plea to remember who he used to be; Thane’s calm acceptance contrasting with the Foil’s fury; a moment where his absolute certainty wavers; post-defeat realization that he’s become what he fought against (002 §Thane’s Foil).

Plot impact: escalates danger and urgency for the party; provides a major boss fight showcasing Thane; explores justice vs vengeance through a different lens; contrasts healthy acceptance (Thane) vs unhealthy rejection (Foil) of death; his connection to Clara adds personal stakes; shows the dangers of absolute thinking and zealotry; thematic parallel to the other antagonists who go too far for “righteous” goals (002 §Thane’s Foil).

Notes

  • Should be formidable and threatening but not purely evil; the tragedy is that he genuinely believes he’s righteous (002 §Thane’s Foil).
  • Nobility or actual knight status to be determined; could be connected to the Sir Valerius character (002 §Thane’s Foil).
