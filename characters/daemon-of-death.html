Daemon of Death

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
Also known as: DD, Death Daemon, Thane's Daemon, Death (Spirit)
First conceived 2023-02-09 (M01220, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-02-25 (M02759)

Daemon of Death CANON

Thane’s daemon — Death itself as a surprisingly timid, non-binary spirit who would rather comfort the dying than cause more death.

Summary — The Daemon of Death is Thane’s daemon: moderately powerful but not violent, preferring to comfort the dying rather than try to cause more death, and surprisingly timid (M02665, FreshToast, 2023-02-21). They are non-binary (“DD, the Death Daemon is enbie”, M02745, FreshToast, 2023-02-25), and the only romance in the party’s relationship structure is between Thane and Death (M01224, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Personality

  • Moderately powerful but not violent (M02665, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Prefers to comfort the dying than try to cause more death (M02665, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Surprisingly timid (M02665, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).

Identity

  • Non-binary (“enbie”) (M02745, FreshToast, 2023-02-25).
  • Working nickname “DD” — FreshToast wanted to workshop a cute nickname for them but nothing better emerged: “either I think of something better or they’re just death” (M02746–M02753, FreshToast/spirit_noodle, 2023-02-25). The nickname exists for the sake of their dynamic with Thane, who “seems the type to use irreverent nicknames” (M02757–M02759, FreshToast, 2023-02-25).

Relationships

  • Thane — the Daemon of Death is Thane’s daemon (M02665, FreshToast, 2023-02-21), and the only romance in the draft relationship diagram is between Thane and Death (M01223–M01224, spirit_noodle/FreshToast, 2023-02-09). Thane’s whole arc is themed on death and healthy mourning (002 §Thane).

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — earliest anchor: “Thane’s death spirit” (M01220, FreshToast); the draft relationship diagram fixes the party’s only romance as Thane × Death (M01223–M01224, spirit_noodle/FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-21 — reframed as a daemon under the newly named daemon system (spirits renamed “daemons” on 2023-02-19, M02466–M02468): moderately powerful, non-violent, comforts the dying, surprisingly timid (M02665, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-25 — DD established as enbie; nickname workshop (“DD” kept as working name) motivated by Thane’s irreverent-nickname habit (M02745–M02759, FreshToast/spirit_noodle).
