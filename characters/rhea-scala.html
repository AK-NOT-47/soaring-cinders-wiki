Rhea Scala

EVOLVEDSkyward CindersNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-02-09 (M01249, CrunchyConniptions) · last in chat 2023-02-09 (M01260)

Rhea Scala EVOLVED

A “magic batman with social anxiety” — the party’s magic thief and primary comedic relief, revised the same day from an original female solo-thief protagonist concept.

Summary — In its final form, the thief character is a male magic thief rather than a standard one (M01253–M01254, FreshToast, 2023-02-09): “female gaze simp bait” (M01255, FreshToast, 2023-02-09) with major social anxiety (M01256, FreshToast, 2023-02-09), who “suddenly vanishes” (M01257, FreshToast, 2023-02-09) — summed up as “Magic batman with social anxiety” (M01258, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09). He serves as primary comedic relief (M01260, FreshToast, 2023-02-09) and becomes close with Sable through their shared materialism (M01259, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). Mechanically, the character introduces the Resolve stat and “replaces Himbo” in the roster (M01252, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Abilities & Traits

  • Magic thief, not a standard thief (M01253, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Signature move: suddenly vanishing — the “magic batman” bit (M01257–M01258, 2023-02-09).
  • Carries the Resolve stat; the character slot replaces the earlier Himbo concept (M01252, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Relationships

  • Close with Sable through shared materialism (M01259, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Role & Arc

  • Primary comedic relief (M01260, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Major social anxiety played for characterization and comedy (M01256, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Open questions

  • PROPOSED Is this character the same as Leon (originally Leonard)? The final magic-thief traits — social anxiety, vanishing/misdirection, comedic relief — reappear the next day on Leonard, whose decoy skill is “used for comedic effect, in tandem with his social anxiety” (M01833, FreshToast, 2023-02-12), and Leonard first appears immediately after this revision (M01394, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-10). No message explicitly equates the two; doc 002 keeps “Rhea Scala (Magic Batman)” as its own entry and still names “Rhea” as Sable’s materialism friend (002 §Sable). Inference only.
  • DEBATED The male version’s actual name is never stated; “Rhea Scala” is the original female concept’s name.

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — original concept SCRAPPED: female thief protagonist “Rhea Scala” (M01249–M01251, CrunchyConniptions). Young woman, 5’7", lean and athletic; curly brown hair in a short cut above the shoulders; shallow blue eyes reflecting a quiet, resolute nature; dresses in all black with leather gloves and a mask covering the lower half of her face on a job; always sharp and put together. Anti-social solo worker with trust issues who yearns to be part of a crew; pretends to be the group’s leader but deep down lacks the confidence to actually lead; always ready to escape; obsessed with treasure hunting and “always scheming for the ultimate heist.” Grew up on the streets, honed her talent for thievery, and became one of the most sought-after thieves in the city — yet always felt something was missing (M01249, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09; 002 §Original Concept - Female Thief).
  • 2023-02-09 — revised the same day into the final version (M01252–M01260, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions): Resolve stat, replaces Himbo; magic thief rather than standard; switch to male; female-gaze simp bait; major social anxiety; suddenly vanishes; “Magic batman with social anxiety”; close with Sable through shared materialism; primary comedic relief — supersedes the female concept above.
