Rhea Scala

Skyward CindersNotes / DesignSecondary character
First conceived 2023-02-09

A “magic batman with social anxiety” — the party’s magic thief and primary comedic relief, revised the same day from an original female solo-thief protagonist concept.

Summary — In its final form, the thief character is a male magic thief rather than a standard one (2023-02-09): “female gaze simp bait” (2023-02-09) with major social anxiety (2023-02-09), who “suddenly vanishes” (2023-02-09) — summed up as “Magic batman with social anxiety” (2023-02-09). He serves as primary comedic relief (2023-02-09) and becomes close with Sable through their shared materialism (2023-02-09). Mechanically, the character introduces the Resolve stat and “replaces Himbo” in the roster (2023-02-09).

Abilities & Traits

  • Magic thief, not a standard thief (2023-02-09).
  • Signature move: suddenly vanishing — the “magic batman” bit (2023-02-09).
  • Carries the Resolve stat; the character slot replaces the earlier Himbo concept (2023-02-09).

Relationships

  • Close with Sable through shared materialism (2023-02-09).

Role & Arc

  • Primary comedic relief (2023-02-09).
  • Major social anxiety played for characterization and comedy (2023-02-09).

Open questions

  • Is this character the same as Leon (originally Leonard)? The final magic-thief traits — social anxiety, vanishing/misdirection, comedic relief — reappear the next day on Leonard, whose decoy skill is “used for comedic effect, in tandem with his social anxiety” (2023-02-12), and Leonard first appears immediately after this revision (2023-02-10). No message explicitly equates the two; doc 002 keeps “Rhea Scala (Magic Batman)” as its own entry and still names “Rhea” as Sable’s materialism friend (002 §Sable). Inference only.
  • The male version’s actual name is never stated; “Rhea Scala” is the original female concept’s name.

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — original concept : female thief protagonist “Rhea Scala”. Young woman, 5’7", lean and athletic; curly brown hair in a short cut above the shoulders; shallow blue eyes reflecting a quiet, resolute nature; dresses in all black with leather gloves and a mask covering the lower half of her face on a job; always sharp and put together. Anti-social solo worker with trust issues who yearns to be part of a crew; pretends to be the group’s leader but deep down lacks the confidence to actually lead; always ready to escape; obsessed with treasure hunting and “always scheming for the ultimate heist.” Grew up on the streets, honed her talent for thievery, and became one of the most sought-after thieves in the city — yet always felt something was missing (2023-02-09; 002 §Original Concept - Female Thief).
  • 2023-02-09 — revised the same day into the final version: Resolve stat, replaces Himbo; magic thief rather than standard; switch to male; female-gaze simp bait; major social anxiety; suddenly vanishes; “Magic batman with social anxiety”; close with Sable through shared materialism; primary comedic relief — supersedes the female concept above.
