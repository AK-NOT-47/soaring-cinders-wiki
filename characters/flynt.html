Flynt CANON

One of Lysander’s two pawns — an orphaned, adoptive sibling of Steele, probably “straight up a himbo,” who wields the massive sword Warpath and dies tragically midway through the game.

Summary — Flynt is one of the pair of orphaned, adoptive siblings who work personally for Lysander: largely loyal-to-a-fault, goofy pawns, mildly antagonistic at best (M02335–M02341, 2023-02-16; M03394, M03397, FreshToast, 2023-06-04). He is probably “straight up a himbo” (M03392, FreshToast, 2023-06-04) and CANON dies tragically midway through the game, after which his weapon passes — broken — to his sibling Steele (M03395, M04661, FreshToast).

Abilities & Traits

  • CANON Wields Warpath — a massive sword “like Guts’ Dragonslayer” (Berserk reference) (M04661, FreshToast, 2023-10-24).

Relationships

  • Orphaned, adoptive sibling of Steele; both work personally for Lysander (M03394, FreshToast, 2023-06-04).
  • Flynt/Locke and Thane are probably the avenues for the death/regret theme (M04658, FreshToast, 2023-10-24).

Role & Arc

  • CANON Dies tragically midway through the game (M03395, FreshToast, 2023-06-04). A tentative tie exists to the spear/late-game conflict beats — his death “could also be the same fight” as a spear-related fight (M05555, FreshToast, 2024-08-24; see 004).
  • After his death, Steele picks up Warpath; the weapon is broken from then on and becomes a relic that can temporarily retake its complete form — carrying the influence of the dead visibly into the latter parts of the story (M04661, FreshToast, 2023-10-24).

Design history

  • 2023-02-16 — conceived unnamed: the remaining 2 of Lysander’s party are a pair of siblings, largely unimportant, loyal to a fault, goofy, probably not all that bad — pawns (M02335–M02341, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-06-04 — named Flynt; “we at least have Flynt who’s probably a straight up himbo”; orphaned adoptive siblings working personally for Lysander; dies tragically midway through the game (M03392–M03397, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-24 — Warpath defined (Guts’ Dragonslayer-like sword); Steele picks it up broken after Flynt dies; Flynt/Locke named as a death/regret avenue (M04658, M04661, FreshToast).
  • 2024-08-24 — tentative placement: his death could be the same fight as the spear beat (M05555, FreshToast) — matches the current presentation above.
