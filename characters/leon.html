Leon Erhardt

DEBATEDSkyward CindersNotes / Design
Also known as: Leonard
First conceived 2023-02-10 (M01394, CrunchyConniptions) · last in chat 2023-02-25 (M02769)

Leon Erhardt DEBATED

Fugitive back-line rogue on the run from the law — quick-witted, paranoid, and hunted by a cop who becomes his romance arc — though the hunted-romance premise was later re-attributed to Damian.

Summary — Leon Erhardt, 22, is a main-party back-liner defined by being hunted (M01394, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-10): a fugitive for a crime he may or may not have committed, guarded and roguish, hiding genuine kindness under bravado (002 §Leon). His signature plot is the cat-and-mouse with a cop/detective hunter that “maybe finally turns into a romance plot? Hardcore character development for both” (M01396, FreshToast, 2023-02-10). His name evolved Leonard → Leon (“leonard is a nerd. Leon is a lion.” — M02066, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-15). Status is DEBATED because the pursued-by-an-investigator romance was later attached to Damian instead (M02701, FreshToast, 2023-02-23), and Leon is absent from the latest (2024) team-pairing roster — see Open questions.

Name / Identity

  • Full name: Leon Erhardt (M02079, FreshToast, 2023-02-15).
  • Age: 22 (002 §Leon).
  • Originally Leonard; changed because Crunchy kept stumbling on it — “leonard is a nerd. Leon is a lion.” (M02066, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-15); “Leonard also means lion” so the change “keeps the meaningful part of the name” (M02067, M02065, FreshToast, 2023-02-15).
  • Affinity: not specified; the doc’s “suggest Electric or Cold” is inference — PROPOSED (002 §Leon). He does not appear in the known-affinities list (M03051, FreshToast, 2023-03-15).

Personality

  • Initially guarded and paranoid due to being hunted; quick-witted with a sharp tongue — humor as defense mechanism (002 §Leon).
  • Brave but reckless; loyal to a fault once trust is earned; “roguish charm” over genuine kindness; struggles to be vulnerable or ask for help (002 §Leon).
  • Has social anxiety, played against his decoy skill for comedy (M01833, FreshToast, 2023-02-12).
  • Tagged “Leonard - Shy but Capable Friend” in the scrapped male-protagonist party-order fork (M01905, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14) SCRAPPED.

Background & Motivations

  • Being hunted by law enforcement for a crime he may or may not have committed; backstory suggests he was framed or took the fall for someone else; has been on the run living by his wits (M01394, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-10; 002 §Leon).
  • One of the hunters is on Sable’s list — creates conflict when Sable wants the bounty (M01395, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-10).
  • Motivations (002 §Leon): clear his name, protect those he cares about from the crossfire, prove he’s more than a criminal, find a place to stop running.
  • Flaws (002 §Leon): paranoid and slow to trust; lies “for others’ protection”; reckless when cornered; sees himself as a burden; unprocessed trauma from being hunted.

Abilities & Traits

  • Party position: 2nd line (Back) (M01399, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-10).
  • Best mobility in the party, at high AP cost (M01876, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-13); he and Sable “have an easy time moving with their skills and have more modular skills” (M01874, FreshToast, 2023-02-13).
  • Decoy skill: “Leonard can create a decoy, rather than turn invisible. The decoy is occasionally used for comedic effect, in tandem with his social anxiety” (M01833, FreshToast, 2023-02-12).
  • Exploration brainstorms (heavily debated system): “Leonard should be able to light up a larger area with his magic spells” (M01422, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-11); might let you notice traps earlier — “I’m not really sold though” (M01517, FreshToast, 2023-02-11); could give Locke a dash on a cooldown (M01683, FreshToast, 2023-02-11).
  • Street-smart; skilled in stealth and misdirection; throwing weapons / ranged combat; underground contacts from his time on the run (002 §Leon).

Relationships

  • Hunted by a detective/cop who becomes his romantic interest: “Leonard hunted by a cop, maybe finally turns into a romance plot? Hardcore character development for both” (M01396, FreshToast, 2023-02-10); “Mostly comedic, eventually romantic plot” (M01397, FreshToast, 2023-02-10). The detective begins questioning whether Leon is actually guilty (002 §Leon).
  • Bounty tension with Sable (M01395, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-10).

Role & Arc

(002 §Leon, expanding the M01394–M01397 premise)

  • Early: joins the party as a temporary alliance; keeps everyone at arm’s length; comic relief through paranoia.
  • Mid: the hunt intensifies; Sable discovers the bounty; forced to open up when the party protects him anyway; investigates who actually committed the crime.
  • Romance subplot: cat-and-mouse → mutual respect → attraction; detective finds inconsistencies in his case; they uncover the real culprit together.
  • Resolution: name cleared; decides whether to rejoin society or keep adventuring; learns to trust and be vulnerable.
  • Emotional beats: the party chooses him over bounty money; he confesses what he’s running from; confrontation with his framer; the detective chooses him over duty.
  • Plot impact: fugitive status creates external pressure; underground contacts feed the party information; parallels Locke’s need to prove herself worthy.

Open questions

  • DEBATED Who owns the hunted-romance plot? Later messages attach the identical premise to Damian: “Damian is pursued by some vigilante/private investigator but we actually meet her first who then leads us to Damian” (M02701, FreshToast, 2023-02-23), and the by-the-book hunter archetype “better serves the girl hunting Damian down… Which then results in a romance plot” (M02769–M02770, FreshToast, 2023-02-25). No message explicitly retires Leon’s version, so it is unresolved whether the plot migrated to Damian or two pursuit plots briefly coexisted. Doc 002 keeps the detective romance on Leon; recency favors Damian.
  • DEBATED Affinity unassigned (absent from M03051, FreshToast, 2023-03-15).
  • DEBATED Late-roster absence: the 2024 team-skill pairing list (Locke, Ruby/Thane, Percy/Damian, Astrid/Sable) does not include Leon (M05392, FreshToast, 2024-04-11); no removal was ever stated.

Design history

  • 2023-02-10 — original concept: “Leonard is being hunted” (M01394, CrunchyConniptions); a hunter is on Sable’s list (M01395); hunted by a cop, turning into a romance plot with hardcore development for both, mostly comedic then romantic (M01396–M01397, FreshToast); slotted 2nd line with Ruby and Clara (M01399, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-11/12 — kit sketches: lights up a larger torch area (M01422, spirit_noodle); trap-noticing floated but “not really sold” (M01517, FreshToast); decoy instead of invisibility, comedic with his social anxiety (M01833, FreshToast, 2023-02-12).
  • 2023-02-13 — mobility identity: easy mover with modular skills (M01874, FreshToast); best mobility at high AP cost (M01876, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-14 — SCRAPPED male-protagonist fork tags him “Shy but Capable Friend”; fork rejected as the current party order is mechanically justified (M01905, M01914–M01915, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast). Crunchy asks to rename because “lee oh nard” is hard to keep straight (M01907–M01913).
  • 2023-02-15 — rename Leonard → Leon approved: “leonard is a nerd. Leon is a lion” / “Leonard also means lion” (M02062–M02068, FreshToast/spirit_noodle/CrunchyConniptions) — supersedes Leonard; surname set in “Locke Evertide / Leon Erhardt” (M02079, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-23/25 — the pursued-by-investigator romance premise is voiced for Damian instead (M02701, 2023-02-23; M02769–M02770, 2023-02-25, FreshToast) — conflicts with the 2023-02-10 Leon version; unresolved, see Open questions.
