Sha'worr-ma the Second

Sha’worr-ma the Second PROPOSED

A stand-alone character concept — a chaos-philosopher who breaks every chain — whose celebrated description was earmarked for reuse on the person who helped Percy make Conflux. Identity provisional.

Summary — Sha’worr-ma the Second is a full character write-up posted alongside the second Rhea profile (M03523, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-08-12). The name landed — “Shaworr’ma though, now we’re talking. THAT’s a name” (M03527, FreshToast, 2023-08-12) — and her personality paragraph was explicitly earmarked: “I like this part and am gonna steal it for the person that helped Percy make Conflux” (M03535, FreshToast, 2023-08-12). So the name and description may attach to the Conflux-creator rather than remaining a separate character; treat the identity as not yet settled (002 §Sha’worr-ma, provisional-identity note). FreshToast’s verdict on the prose: “It’s very dramatic, if a bit waffle-y” (M03537, 2023-08-12).

Appearance

Sinewy elegance, both striking and unsettling; intense, almost hypnotic eyes that “burn with an unquenchable thirst for the unattainable”; alabaster complexion contrasted by ink-black tattoos of cryptic symbols on her forearms, representing her refined and unrestricted desires. Every movement exudes confidence, calculated grace, and a hint of chaos (M03523, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-08-12).

Personality

A contradiction personified — to some a philosopher, to others a lunatic. Like a flame, unpredictable, “illuminating paths of liberation while also consuming everything in her wake.” Both seeker and destroyer; the allure of anarchy is a game and a challenge, and she thrives amidst chaos, finding it the truest form of liberation (M03523, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-08-12). This is the paragraph FreshToast earmarked for the Conflux-helper reuse (M03535).

Goals & Motivation

  • To break every chain, visible or invisible, and to guide — or force — society to recognize and embrace its purest, unfiltered desires (M03523, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-08-12).
  • Believes only by liberating one’s deepest, most genuine desires can true liberation be attained; her extreme interpretation drives her to force this perspective on others, believing it an act of emancipation (M03523).

Conflict

The more she strives to free others, the more entangled she becomes in the very chains she seeks to break — a mission rooted in freedom that often results in tyranny, making her both liberator and oppressor. She fears that, in her quest for true freedom, she may have enslaved herself to her own ideology (M03523, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-08-12).

Background

Raised in a strict, regimented society that stifled individuality; a chance encounter with a forbidden text about “the pure essence of freedom” ignited a flame that grew into an inferno, leading her to rebel, question, and renounce her past, then journey through the world until her evolving philosophy set her against “the very fabric of society itself” (M03523, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-08-12).

Open questions

  • PROPOSED Does “Sha’worr-ma the Second” survive as her own character, or does the name/description fold into the Conflux-creator? Note that Pandora was later named as the person who helps Percival make Conflux (M04343, FreshToast, 2023-10-17) — the reuse target and this concept were never explicitly reconciled.
  • The name is a food pun (shawarma), sibling to Rhea Falafill’s falafel pun (M03526–M03530, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-08-12).
