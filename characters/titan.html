Titan

One of the 4 Boyes: a demi-human wuxia martial artist with the ‘Body’ affinity, who heals and punches. “Titan” is a placeholder — he needs a proper name.

Summary — Titan is a Lycosian male demi-human in his early 20s, the wuxia protagonist / eastern martial artist archetype among the 4 Boyes of Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium. He fights unarmed, supplemented with magic, using his ‘Body’ affinity “to heal and punch” (2023-10-17). He is the least developed of the Boyes — an explicitly flagged open design problem.

Identity

  • Lycosian male (Demi-Human); early 20s; ethnically typical for western Khorath (Mediterranean) (2023-10-17).
  • Placeholder name, needs a proper one. FreshToast: “Also I’m trying not to get used to calling him that” (2023-10-18).
  • Archetype: wuxia protagonist / eastern martial artist.

Abilities & Traits

  • ‘Body’ affinity — uses it to heal and punch (2023-10-17).
  • Fights unarmed, supplemented with magic.
  • Starts with his affinity and the barest knowledge of magic; has “awakened” (2023-10-17).

Character concept

  • Seems the type to respect magical creatures (2023-10-17).
  • Should get a magical pet (really likes animals) — “something that’s supposed to be dangerous but is surprisingly loyal and docile” (Dianthus already covers the pet idea for the group, but the demi-human getting one was liked) (2023-10-17).

Open questions

  • Needs a proper name.
  • Character direction entirely open: “I have no idea what to do with titan. I need direction” (2023-10-17).
  • Sealing/binding magic concept: “What if titan can seal off magic such that opponents and himself can’t cast magic temporarily — he punches them in the meantime” — FreshToast: “There is a glyph which does that. I’m not sure he is the pick though” (2023-10-18).

Design history

  • 2023-10-17 — initial concept in the 4 Boyes roster write-up: “[Titan] — Lycosian Male (Demi-Human) - Placeholder name, needs a proper one - Wuxia protagonist / eastern martial artist archetype - ‘Body’ affinity. Uses this to heal and punch - Fights unarmed, supplemented with magic”.
  • 2023-10-17 — magical-pet question: the demi-human could get something supposed to be dangerous but surprisingly loyal and docile; “He seems the type in my mind to respect magical creatures”.
  • 2023-10-17 — direction flagged as an open problem: “I have no idea what to do with titan. I need direction”.
  • 2023-10-18 — sealing/binding magic pitched; a glyph already does that and FreshToast isn’t sure Titan is the pick; also resisting getting used to the placeholder name.
