DEBATEDSkyward CindersNotes / DesignWorking name
First conceived 2023-03-03 (M02887, CrunchyConniptions) · last in chat 2023-03-03 (M02887)

Guardian of Relic DEBATED

A potential character who guards the relic Ajax left behind to contain his own power — one of two competing explanations for where that relic is when the finale needs it.

Summary — In the Lysander 2nd-phase finale plan, Ajax “prepared for this moment” and left behind a relic that had helped him contain his own power. That relic “is with Locke or another character that we have to create that is the guardian of the relic” (M02887, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-03-03). The Guardian only exists as one branch of this unresolved either/or — hence DEBATED, and the character was never named or designed.

Role & Arc

  • If created, the Guardian holds Ajax’s containing relic until the Lysander 2nd-phase fight, where the relic lets the curse’s power be defeated (M02887, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-03-03; see the Ajax silhouette scene).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Who carries the relic? Options in the same message (M02887, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-03-03):
    1. The relic is with Locke;
    2. A new guardian-of-the-relic character is created;
    3. Alternative: “it was on lysander the whole time and suppressed him without his knowledge but it fell from him or was somehow broken.” No later message resolves this.

Design history

  • 2023-03-03 — sole mention, inside the Lysander 2nd-phase finale outline: relic with Locke, OR a to-be-created guardian character, OR unknowingly on Lysander the whole time (M02887, CrunchyConniptions).
