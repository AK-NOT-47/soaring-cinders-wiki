Eris

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-02-10 (M01398, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-10-24 (M04668)

Eris CANON

The conceited manipulator in Lysander’s party — the “evil milf” from Isaac’s background, funding despicable research to resurrect a lost loved one, until Lysander turns her to dust.

Summary — Eris is the smart one manipulating Lysander: a conceited manipulator, very full of herself, member of Lysander’s party and the evil milf from Isaac’s background (M02201–M02203, M02225, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16). After losing someone she turned cold in how far she’s gone to bring them back — her entire motivation is resurrecting a person she loves, not vengeance (M02242, 2023-02-16; M04668, FreshToast, 2023-10-24) — but she is mistaken: stolen life force cannot resurrect the dead, and ‘true resurrection’ is assumed impossible by any means (M02659, FreshToast, 2023-02-21). She is the obvious twist villain — only for the twist to turn back to Lysander, who betrays and slays her (M02201, M02204, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).

Identity & Appearance

  • Affinity: Fiery (002 §Eris; raw affinity list groups Fiery: Locke, Thane, Sable, Eris, Ajax).
  • Name meaning: Eris is the Greek goddess of conflict and strife (M02324, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • “I would let her kill me” — 002 lists this under Eris as a design-goal quote (noting it was voiced as a design goal for Death); the raw anchor phrases it about Death’s design (M02349, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).

Personality

  • Smart, manipulating Lysander (M02225, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Conceited manipulator; very full of herself (002 §Eris).
  • Evil milf from Isaac’s background; member of Lysander’s party (M02202–M02203, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-02-16).

Background & Motivations

  • Has hands in funding despicable research — Gideon Azurith is doing research for Eris so she can use the power but sacrifice other people’s lifespan instead of her own (M02236, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).
  • Has a giant mansion (002 §Eris; the party heads to her mansion/villa to confront her — M02245–M02248, 2023-02-16).
  • After losing someone, turned cold in how far she’s gone to bring them back; eventually goes off the deep end after realizing she’s gone too far and they’d never love her the way she is now (M02242, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Mistaken in the belief that stolen life force could possibly be used to resurrect the dead; ‘true resurrection’ is assumed impossible by any means (M02659, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).

Motivations: resurrect her lost loved one by any means necessary; prove that death can be conquered through knowledge and power; manipulate Lysander to gain Ajax’s power or make her own pact; fund research into life-force manipulation (via Gideon); go as far as needed, regardless of moral cost (002 §Eris).

Goals: acquire Ajax’s power or a similar life-draining ability; complete research into “true resurrection”; use Lysander as a tool; gather resources and test subjects for experiments (002 §Eris).

Flaws: grief has driven her to monstrous acts; cannot accept that death is final; arrogant belief that she can succeed where all others failed; her manipulative nature makes her underestimate genuine bonds; goes off the deep end when she realizes her loved one wouldn’t love what she’s become; conceited and full of herself; cannot admit her quest is futile (002 §Eris).

Relationships & Dynamics

  • Ties to Thane — perhaps he’s the one who tells her death is truly final (M02242–M02244 context, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Isaac intends to confront her — from Ruby’s story end the party learns Eris funds the despicable research and heads to her giant mansion/villa; she tries to silence/hunt them, climaxing in a big fight; a Big W for Isaac (M02245–M02248, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-02-16). Isaac’s Hermione-esque rival could later side with Isaac against Eris (M02723, FreshToast, 2023-02-25).
  • Manipulating things to acquire Ajax’s power or make her own pact with the same spirit (M02201, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).

Role & Arc

  • Theme: healthy mourning vs going off the deep end (002 §Eris).
  • Appears in the prologue with Lysander and Locke: she and Lysander discuss gathering the chaos emeralds to defeat the dragon or something (M02419, FreshToast, 2023-02-19); at the trial cavern she and Lysander assume their relic intel was false (M03670, FreshToast, 2023-09-10). One prologue sketch starts with Lysander’s party minus Eris (M02443, FreshToast, 2023-02-19).
  • Could seem like the obvious twist villain, only to twist back to Lysander (M02204, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Eris finds out Lysander doesn’t care about the demon powers and tells him about soul/sacrifice; Lysander interrupts by grabbing her by the head — she’s immediately dust (M02282–M02283, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • The party tells Lysander about her in an effort to create division; he seems unphased, then for phase 2 “Lysander fucking magically eats his other party members” (M02249–M02255, 2023-02-16).
  • The dragon’s spoken threat foreshadows this: the same attack the dragon uses against the party, Lysander uses on Eris (M02271–M02276, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).

Symbolism & notes

  • Has to eat an apple dramatically, in reference to the apple of discord: Eris (Greek mythology) threw the apple of discord into the world, said it was for the “cutest, most uwu gril in the land,” and people fought over it, eventually causing a really nasty war. If Eris eats it herself, it symbolically implies an element of narcissism or conceitedness (M02317–M02322, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Name options considered (jokes): “Eris evilwizard,” “Eris cutebutwillbetrayyou,” “Eris Barkbarkawoo”; “pretty name” (M02331–M02333, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Who does Eris want to resurrect? Crunchy wanted her selfish and hungry for power — “Just a bad person” (M02677, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22) — while the later note keeps her motivation as resurrecting a person she loves, not vengeance (M04668, FreshToast, 2023-10-24).
  • DEBATED Whether she survives to a second, final confrontation: possibly survives the mansion fight and runs back to the hero; may be killed by Lysander after, or fights with him scarred and weakened (M02245–M02252, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • DEBATED Death staging: head-grab-to-dust (M02282–M02283) vs the alternate where Lysander says “It sings to me” and plunges his sword into her (M02270, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).
  • DEBATED “Lyra or Eris is a vampire?” (M01398, FreshToast, 2023-02-10).
