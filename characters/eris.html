Eris

Skyward CindersNotes / DesignPrimary character
First conceived 2023-02-10

Eris

The conceited manipulator in Lysander’s party — the “evil milf” from Isaac’s background, funding despicable research to resurrect a lost loved one, until Lysander turns her to dust.

Summary — Eris is the smart one manipulating Lysander: a conceited manipulator, very full of herself, member of Lysander’s party and the evil milf from Isaac’s background (2023-02-16). After losing someone she turned cold in how far she’s gone to bring them back — her entire motivation is resurrecting a person she loves, not vengeance (2023-02-16, 2023-10-24) — but she is mistaken: stolen life force cannot resurrect the dead, and ‘true resurrection’ is assumed impossible by any means (2023-02-21). She is the obvious twist villain — only for the twist to turn back to Lysander, who betrays and slays her (2023-02-16).

Identity & Appearance

  • Affinity: Fiery (002 §Eris; raw affinity list groups Fiery: Locke, Thane, Sable, Eris, Ajax).
  • Name meaning: Eris is the Greek goddess of conflict and strife (2023-02-16).
  • “I would let her kill me” — 002 lists this under Eris as a design-goal quote (noting it was voiced as a design goal for Death); the raw anchor phrases it about Death’s design (2023-02-16).

Personality

  • Smart, manipulating Lysander (2023-02-16).
  • Conceited manipulator; very full of herself (002 §Eris).
  • Evil milf from Isaac’s background; member of Lysander’s party (2023-02-16).

Background & Motivations

  • Has hands in funding despicable research — Gideon Azurith is doing research for Eris so she can use the power but sacrifice other people’s lifespan instead of her own (2023-02-16).
  • Has a giant mansion (2023-02-16; 002 §Eris).
  • After losing someone, turned cold in how far she’s gone to bring them back; eventually goes off the deep end after realizing she’s gone too far and they’d never love her the way she is now (2023-02-16).
  • Mistaken in the belief that stolen life force could possibly be used to resurrect the dead; ‘true resurrection’ is assumed impossible by any means (2023-02-21).

Motivations: resurrect her lost loved one by any means necessary; prove that death can be conquered through knowledge and power; manipulate Lysander to gain Ajax’s power or make her own pact; fund research into life-force manipulation (via Gideon); go as far as needed, regardless of moral cost (002 §Eris).

Goals: acquire Ajax’s power or a similar life-draining ability; complete research into “true resurrection”; use Lysander as a tool; gather resources and test subjects for experiments (002 §Eris).

Flaws: grief has driven her to monstrous acts; cannot accept that death is final; arrogant belief that she can succeed where all others failed; her manipulative nature makes her underestimate genuine bonds; goes off the deep end when she realizes her loved one wouldn’t love what she’s become; conceited and full of herself; cannot admit her quest is futile (002 §Eris).

Relationships & Dynamics

  • Ties to Thane — perhaps he’s the one who tells her death is truly final (2023-02-16).
  • Isaac intends to confront her — from Ruby’s story end the party learns Eris funds the despicable research and heads to her giant mansion/villa; she tries to silence/hunt them, climaxing in a big fight; a Big W for Isaac (2023-02-16). Isaac’s Hermione-esque rival could later side with Isaac against Eris (2023-02-25).
  • Manipulating things to acquire Ajax’s power or make her own pact with the same spirit (2023-02-16).

Role & Arc

  • Theme: healthy mourning vs going off the deep end (002 §Eris).
  • Appears in the prologue with Lysander and Locke: she and Lysander discuss gathering the chaos emeralds to defeat the dragon or something (2023-02-19); at the trial cavern she and Lysander assume their relic intel was false (2023-09-10). One prologue sketch starts with Lysander’s party minus Eris (2023-02-19).
  • Could seem like the obvious twist villain, only to twist back to Lysander (2023-02-16).
  • Eris finds out Lysander doesn’t care about the demon powers and tells him about soul/sacrifice; Lysander interrupts by grabbing her by the head — she’s immediately dust (2023-02-16).
  • The party tells Lysander about her in an effort to create division; he seems unphased, then for phase 2 “Lysander fucking magically eats his other party members” (2023-02-16).
  • The dragon’s spoken threat foreshadows this: the same attack the dragon uses against the party, Lysander uses on Eris (2023-02-16).

Symbolism & notes

  • Has to eat an apple dramatically, in reference to the apple of discord: Eris (Greek mythology) threw the apple of discord into the world, said it was for the “cutest, most uwu gril in the land,” and people fought over it, eventually causing a really nasty war. If Eris eats it herself, it symbolically implies an element of narcissism or conceitedness (2023-02-16).
  • Name options considered (jokes): “Eris evilwizard,” “Eris cutebutwillbetrayyou,” “Eris Barkbarkawoo”; “pretty name” (2023-02-16).

Open questions

  • Who does Eris want to resurrect? Crunchy wanted her selfish and hungry for power — “Just a bad person” (2023-02-22) — while the later note keeps her motivation as resurrecting a person she loves, not vengeance (2023-10-24).
  • Whether she survives to a second, final confrontation: possibly survives the mansion fight and runs back to the hero; may be killed by Lysander after, or fights with him scarred and weakened (2023-02-16).
  • Death staging: head-grab-to-dust vs the alternate where Lysander says “It sings to me” and plunges his sword into her (2023-02-16).
  • “Lyra or Eris is a vampire?” (2023-02-10).

Design history

  • 2023-02-10 — earliest raw mention: “Lyra or Eris is a vampire?” — still open.
  • 2023-02-16 — main villain design session: manipulating things to acquire Ajax’s power or make her own pact, betrayed and slain by Lysander; identified as the evil milf from Isaac’s background and a member of Lysander’s party; double-twist structure; “Eris smart, manipulating him,” though if Lysander is dumb he gets too strong for her to handle; Gideon researching for her; grief motivation — turned cold after losing someone, goes off the deep end; mansion confrontation and survival debate; final-boss staging with the dust splash image; alternate “It sings to me” staging; apple-of-discord symbolism, name meaning, joke surnames.
  • 2023-02-19 — prologue roles sketched: discusses gathering the chaos emeralds with Lysander; one sketch starts with Lysander’s party minus Eris.
  • 2023-02-21 — resurrection lore locked: she is mistaken; ‘true resurrection’ is impossible by any means; the power can only take.
  • 2023-02-22 — pushback on motive: Crunchy wanted her selfish and power-hungry, “Just a bad person” — unresolved against the grief motive.
  • 2023-02-25/28 — Isaac’s rival could side with him against Eris; villa level idea with Eris floated for a new character.
  • 2023-09-10 — prologue: she and Lysander assume the relic intel about the cavern was false.
  • 2023-10-24 — motive reaffirmed: “Eris isn’t seeking vengeance but her entire motivation is resurrecting a person she loves” — matches the current presentation above.
