Elara PROPOSED

A weary merchant of the Caelia marketplace who hooks the player into the self-aware Moonstone Mania collectathon.

Summary — Elara is a “weary merchant” of the Caelia marketplace who appears in an illustrative dialogue snippet written to demonstrate the tone of the Moonstone Mania quest (M05271, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13). The player approaches her about a moonstone-hoarding scheme; she reacts with alarm (“Moonstones?! Are you mad?”), warns them off, then reluctantly hands over a starting stash to kick off the quest. She functions as a specific, named face of the game’s recurring merchant / Shopkeeper role. As she originates in a “quick example” scene rather than finalized design, she is tagged PROPOSED.

Personality

  • Weary and dryly exasperated by the player’s “eccentric exploits” (M05271, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13).
  • Sardonic: “Moonstones?! Are you mad? Those things are rarer than a dragon with manners!” and “don’t come crying to me when you’re neck-deep in angry werewolves. Here’s your starting stash. Now get out there, you lunatic!” (M05271, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13).

Role & Arc

  • Quest-giver for Moonstone Mania: the player pitches a moonstone-collecting scheme (a riff on their old gem-hoarding exploits with sapphires, rubies and emeralds), and Elara reluctantly provides the starting stash that begins the quest (M05271, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13).
  • Located in the Caelia marketplace (M05271, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13). See Caelia.

Related pages

  • Elara is best understood as a named instance of the recurring merchant role; the shop mechanics themselves live in Recurring Shopkeeper System.
  • The quest she opens: Moonstone Mania (Meta Collectathon Venture).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Whether Elara is a finalized, canonical NPC or merely an example name used to pitch the quest’s tone — she appears only in the “quick example” scene (M05271, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13), and no later design work develops her.

Design history

  • 2024-02-13 — Elara introduced in a “quick example” scene demonstrating how the Moonstone Mania quest could be self-aware and funny about its own absurdity (M05271–M05272, CrunchyConniptions). She has no earlier or later appearances; the scene is her sole source.
