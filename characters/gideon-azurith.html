Gideon Azurith

Early ConceptNotes / DesignSecondary character
Also known as: Ruby's creator, the creator
First conceived 2023-02-05

Ruby’s creator: a born psychopath with an acquired messiah complex who literally converts people into tools — harvesting outlaws’ souls into golems to “progress society.”

Summary — Gideon Azurith is the alchemist who created the homunculi Ruby (with a borrowed soul — incorrectly) and Citrine (with his own — correctly) (2023-02-16). A highly utilitarian pragmatist, he sees society’s weaknesses as things to be corrected by any means: he sends Citrine to gather criminals and outlaws, harvests their souls into golems, and justifies it because the golems help people (2023-02-16). He is also doing research for Eris so she can use her power while sacrificing other people’s lifespans instead of her own (2023-02-16). Ruby ultimately spares him, in Citrine’s name (2023-02-15).

Name & identity

  • Gideon means greater warrior/destroyer — a biblical name that plays into his messiah complex (2023-02-16).
  • Azurith is a portmanteau of Azur + Lith: azur means blue, lith means stone — completing the primary color cycle with Ruby & Citrine (2023-02-16).

Appearance

  • Wears “the whore gloves” (wristless gloves) (2023-02-16).
  • In the background of his visual, there are clipped fairy wings (2023-02-16) — associated with the creator/golem-origin imagery.

Personality

  • Highly utilitarian pragmatist; sees weaknesses and flaws in society as something to be corrected by any means (2023-02-16).
  • Born a psychopath; acquired a messiah complex after witnessing the mistreatment of others and society’s inefficiencies (2023-02-16).

Background & Motivations

  • Submitted character note marks Gideon Azurith as an Antagonist (Submitted character notes section Gideon Azurith, undated; imported 2026-07-08).
  • Justifies his actions by sending golems to help people; the golems progress society along with Citrine (2023-02-16).
  • The golem economy: sends Citrine to gather criminals and outlaws, harvests their souls to be converted into golems — the literal conversion of people into tools (2023-02-16).
  • Homunculus creation requires sacrificing a portion of your own flesh and soul; Gideon borrowed the soul of another when creating Ruby, but performed the ritual correctly when creating Citrine (2023-02-16).
  • Doing research for Eris — so she can use the power but sacrifice other people’s lifespan instead of her own (2023-02-16).

Relationships

  • Creator and “absent father” of Ruby, who seeks his approval; he deemed her a failed prototype and gave her away (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • Creator of Citrine, who is loyal to and cared for by him (002 §Citrine).
  • Spared by Ruby, in Citrine’s name, after the sisters’ duel (2023-02-15).

Role & Arc

  • Ruby’s creator and antagonist; connected to Eris’s research (002 §Gideon Azurith).
  • Thematic placement in the villain roster: Gideon, Lysander and the other human characters do cruel/awful things mostly from human flaws — in contrast to Raphael, the story’s inhuman true tragic monster acting from raw evil hatred (2024-02-10).

Open questions

  • 002 files him under Archived Early Concepts, but he was still being used as a benchmark human villain in February 2024 — whether Gideon survives into the current iteration’s antagonist roster is unresolved.

Design history

  • 2023-02-05 — exists only as Ruby’s unnamed creator in her backstory: deemed her a failed prototype, gave her to lower-tier mages (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • 2023-02-15 — the sisters’ arc ends with Ruby sparing “her creator” in Citrine’s name.
  • 2023-02-16 — full character session: named Gideon Azurith with the warrior/messiah + blue-stone etymology; psychopath-with-messiah-complex characterization; golem soul-harvest economy; ritual details for Ruby vs Citrine; whore gloves and clipped fairy wings visuals; research-for-Eris plot link. — this is the fullest version and the page’s current presentation
  • 2024-02-10 — cited as an example of human-flaw villainy in contrast to Raphael’s inhuman evil; profile filed under Archived Early Concepts in 002.
