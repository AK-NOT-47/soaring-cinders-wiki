Isaac

DEBATEDSkyward CindersMixed
First conceived 2023-02-10 (M01399, CrunchyConniptions) · last in chat 2023-08-12 (M03526)

Isaac DEBATED

Chuuni scholar-mage of the main-game party and one of the four heroes actually implemented in the proto — but renamed Percy in the notes, with “Isaac” freed for the rival character; roster placement is undecided.

Summary — Isaac is the main-game party’s staff-wielding mage (002 §Isaac), a “Chuuni Wizard” (M01905, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14) whose stated goals are to “become the super awesomest ultimate mage ever” and to “find out why he was canceled” (M02344, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16). In the notes he was renamed Percy (“What if Isaac was called Percy?… We could use Isaac for his rival character instead” — M02784/M02786, FreshToast, 2023-02-25; reconfirmed “Isaac is Percy”, M03526, FreshToast, 2023-08-12) — see Percival. He remains a separate page because, against the rename, Isaac (not Percy) is one of the four heroes built in the proto’s vertical slice (Locke, Thane, Isaac, Ruby) (002 §Isaac), so which name the main-game mage carries is DEBATED.

Name / Identity

  • CANON Chuuni wizard / scholar-mage of the main-game party; weapon: Staff (magic) (002 §Isaac, citing Part 8).
  • EVOLVED Renamed Percy/Percival in Part 10 (M02784–M02788, FreshToast, 2023-02-25), freeing “Isaac” for his rival character; “Isaac is Percy and falafal is a foodstuff” (M03526, FreshToast, 2023-08-12). Current lore presentation of the renamed mage lives at Percival.

Abilities & Traits

  • Mobility: Thane and Isaac “struggle to move and are more specialised” (M01874, FreshToast, 2023-02-13); “characters like Thane and Isaac are probably really static and don’t like to move” (M02578, FreshToast, 2023-02-20).
  • Exploration brainstorm (heavily debated system): “Isaac has an exploration skill that allows him to see/spot illusory walls” (M01422, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-11); “Some dungeon you want to use Issac and others you want to use sable… Issac can see secrets better but the torch reach is shortened heavily” (M01448, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-11).
  • [CORRECTED to match code] The notes’ “1st line (Front)” label (M01399, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-10) is superseded: the proto implements Isaac as a back-line mage, start position 3 (Back) — a 24-HP glass cannon belongs in the back (002 §Isaac; proto: src/core/combat/content.ts).
  • CANON — IMPLEMENTED Proto combat values: id isaac, role “Sorcerer”, glyph 🔥, maxHp 24 (squishiest hero), maxAp 3, speed 5, str 4 / mag 16 (highest) / def 5 / res 10 / acc 90 / crit 10 (002 §Isaac; proto: src/core/combat/content.ts, src/core/combat/types.ts).
  • CANON — IMPLEMENTED Four skills, all isMagic (blocked by Silence), reaching ranks 1–3 (002 §Isaac; proto: src/core/combat/content.ts):
    • Spark (2 AP) — bolt of magic, 9 mag.
    • Frostbind (2 AP) — 7 mag + Speed Down.
    • Shock (2 AP) — 6 mag + Blind.
    • Inferno (4 AP, finisher) — fire across the whole enemy line, 10 mag to all.
  • [NOTE] His implemented element is mixed — fire, cold, and lightning; the code comment says “Fiery / electric damage”, consistent with the generalist chuuni-mage framing (002 §Isaac).

Relationships

  • CANON Pairs with Ruby in a Reigen/Mob dynamic — “Isaac and Ruby have a dynamic inspired by Reigen and Mob?” (M02646, FreshToast, 2023-02-21); the pairing was originally tagged to “Gaius & Ruby” (M01189, FreshToast, 2023-02-08).
  • Has a Hermione-esque rival — “smart, straightforward but hardworking rather than talented”, who “could side with Isaac against Eris” (M02721–M02723, FreshToast, 2023-02-25); named Rhea Falafill (M02835, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-27). After the rename this is Percy’s rival.

Background & Motivations

  • CANON “Become the super awesomest ultimate mage ever” and “find out why he was canceled” (M02344, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16).

Role & Arc

  • CANON Following from Ruby’s story, Isaac learns Eris has been funding despicable research; the party heads to Eris’s giant mansion where Isaac intends to confront her (she is the “evil milf from Isaac’s background”); “Big W for Isaac this way” (M02245–M02247, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-02-16; 002 §Isaac).
  • Along the way, the party uncovers the truth behind the hero’s power — what he was close to uncovering previously (002 §Isaac).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Roster placement (002 §Isaac): options are (1) develop fully as the main-game party mage with the Eris arc, (2) merge with another frontline character, (3) convert to an NPC informing on Eris, (4) remove if redundant. Concrete weight toward “keep”: Isaac is implemented in the proto and treated as a live playable pick (M01448, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-11), while the rename (M02784–M02788) points to Percy carrying the mage identity in lore. The 2024 team-pairing roster lists Percy, not Isaac (M05392, FreshToast, 2024-04-11).
  • DEBATED Whether the freed “Isaac” name actually ends up on the rival character (M02786, FreshToast, 2023-02-25) — the rival was subsequently named Rhea Falafill (M02835), so the freed name has no confirmed new owner.

Design history

  • 2023-02-10 — first roster appearance: Isaac listed 1st line with Thane and Sable (M01399, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-11 — exploration niche: spots illusory walls / sees secrets at the cost of torch reach (M01422, spirit_noodle; M01448, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-13/20 — mobility identity: static, struggles to move, specialised (M01874, FreshToast; M02578, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-14 — “Issac - Chuuni Wizard Friend” in the SCRAPPED male-protagonist fork (M01905, CrunchyConniptions); Thane’s surname changed to Farrow specifically “to differentiate him from Isaac” (M01923, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-16 — motivations set: canceled + awesomest mage (M02344, CrunchyConniptions); Eris-confrontation arc, “Big W for Isaac this way” (M02245–M02247, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-21 — Reigen/Mob dynamic with Ruby (M02646, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-25 — Hermione-esque rival brainstormed (M02721–M02723, FreshToast); rename proposed: Isaac → Percy, “I don’t really like Isaac much for a party member”, Isaac freed for the rival (M02784–M02788, FreshToast; “percy is cool”, M02789, spirit_noodle) — supersedes “Isaac” as the mage’s name; see Percival.
  • 2023-08-12 — rename reconfirmed: “Isaac is Percy and falafal is a foodstuff” (M03526, FreshToast); rival documented as Rhea Falafill, “Isaac’s Nemesis” (M03519, CrunchyConniptions). — current state: the lore mage is Percy; the proto still implements him as isaac.
