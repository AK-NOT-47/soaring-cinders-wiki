Death (Spirit)

Skyward CindersNotes / DesignPrimary character
Also known as: DD, Death Daemon, Daemon of Death
First conceived 2023-02-05

Death (Spirit)

The non-binary personification of death — a timid, comforting spirit who appears wherever people die to ease their passing, feared as a bad omen, and Thane’s romantic interest.

Summary — Death is a spirit (later: daemon) and Thane’s romantic interest — the game’s only romance (2023-02-09; 002 §Death: Relationships). They are non-binary (2023-02-16, 2023-02-25). Death spirits don’t bring death: “they appear where death occurs and ease folks’ passing,” but because they always appear where people die, many believe them to be a frightful bad omen (2023-02-10). Thane’s daemon of death “is moderately powerful but not violent and prefers to comfort the dying than try to cause more death. Surprisingly timid” (2023-02-21).

Appearance

  • Design goal: “I want death’s design to make people say ‘I would let her kill me’” (2023-02-16).
  • “They can be hot” — “We have plenty of gril waifus already. Let the enbie waifu flourish” (2023-02-16).

Personality

  • Shy — “actually attracted to edgy boy [Thane] because they like his jokes and the lack of fear or aversion, making them feel accepted” (2023-02-05).
  • Not violent; prefers to comfort the dying rather than cause more death; surprisingly timid (2023-02-21).

Nature & Role

  • Like many other spirits, death is “just a manifestation of the dreams and fears of people that grows over time (like chainsawman devils)” (2023-02-05).
  • Appears where death occurs and eases folks’ passing; widely mistaken for a frightful bad omen (2023-02-10).
  • The spirits were later renamed daemons (2023-02-19), making them “DD, the Death Daemon” (2023-02-25). As a Daemon, Death manifests the way other daemons do — shared emotion attaching to a concept (see Daemon of Want for the daemon mechanics) (2023-02-21).
  • Counted as non-binary in the cast’s gender tally — set apart from the “8 boys and 9 girls” of the major cast, alongside Vaenngrym, because neither has a conventional gender (2023-02-25).
  • Hunted, along with Thane, by the paladin Thane’s Foil: “Eventually hunts down thane or death spirit” (2023-02-10); “Thane is death, Foil is life” (2023-02-10).

Relationships

  • Thane — romantic interest. Thane’s arc runs through them: “running from (literal) death, finding out death’s kinda cute, giving death a smooch, holding death’s hand at a fireworks display, dealing with spirit hunters including his foil, protecc his new deathly enbie waifu, being free to cuddle death and be happy he’s alive so he can move on from his guilt and mourning” (2023-02-16). Origin of the pairing: Thane’s near-death numbness leads to him being pursued by death Final-Destination-style, until it turns out death is shy and attracted to him — “They bang” (2023-02-05).
  • A nickname for Death was workshopped because “Thane seems the type to use irreverent nicknames” (2023-02-25); “DD is what I have for now,” otherwise “they’re just death” (2023-02-25).

Open questions

  • No settled nickname for Thane’s dynamic — “no need to workshop a better name, unless it comes to you effortlessly through some sort of epiphany” (2023-02-25).

Design history

  • 2023-02-05 — original concept, built off Thane’s (“edgy boy”) near-death numbness: pursued by death Final Destination-style; death as a manifestation of people’s dreams and fears; turns out death is shy and attracted to him; “They bang”; “a cool metaphor and cute way of dealing with death without edgy boy actually dying”.
  • 2023-02-05 — Death-as-a-child alternative: “at first, in my mind, i kind of perceived death as a child, partly because of an inherent thought in my brain that thinks they must have poor judgement skills” — “but i dig this approach too” — discarded in favor of the shy/attracted adult approach.
  • 2023-02-10 — world rules: death spirits ease passing rather than bring death; mistaken for a bad omen; the Foil hunts Thane or the death spirit; “Thane is death, Foil is life”.
  • 2023-02-16 — identity and design goals: NB confirmed against the “but what if she was hot” push; “I would let her kill me” design target; “Let the enbie waifu flourish”; Thane’s full arc through Death sketched.
  • 2023-02-19/21 — spirits renamed daemons; Thane’s daemon of death characterized: moderately powerful, non-violent, comforting, surprisingly timid.
  • 2023-02-25 — “DD, the Death Daemon is enbie”; nickname workshop for the Thane dynamic ends unresolved — “DD is what I have for now” / “they’re just death”.
