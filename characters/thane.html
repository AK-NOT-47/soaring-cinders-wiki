CANONSkyward CindersMixed
First conceived 2021-08-01 (M00423, FreshToast) · last in chat 2024-04-11 (M05394)

Thane Farrow CANON

The party’s 34-year-old frontline vanguard: a wolf-motif ex-mentor haunted by his dead student, whose whole theme is death — up to and including a romance with Death itself.

Summary — Thane Farrow is the tank of Locke’s party: slowest, toughest, and most experienced. A former mentor whose mentee died, he carries survivor’s guilt, gallows humor, and a growing numbness to his own near-death experiences. Thematically he embodies “healthy mourning vs going off the deep end” and is the one who tells Eris that death is truly final (M02242–M02244, FreshToast, 2023-02-16). He is one of the four heroes implemented in the browser proto (role “Vanguard”) (002 §Main Party header, CANON — IMPLEMENTED).

Identity

  • Full name: Thane Farrow — surname proposed 2023-02-14 to differentiate him from Isaac (M01923, FreshToast) and approved 2023-02-15 (M02057, spirit_noodle). The rename rationale: character names should sound as distinct from one another as possible, cf. Elden Ring (M02058–M02060, FreshToast, 2023-02-15).
  • Age: 34 (ages list, M01217, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).
  • Affinity: Fiery (002 §Thane).
  • Animal motif: “Wolfman” / “the wolf” (M01293–M01294, FreshToast, 2023-02-09) — paired against his Foil the Paladin, “the Lion” (M01416–M01417, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-11). The motif ties to a sneak/exploration role: the wolf teaches Locke how to sneak and can grant a +5 defense exploration buff (002 §Thane).
  • Dog-breed comparison: Anatolian Shepherd (M03029, spirit_noodle, 2023-03-13).

Personality

  • Death associations throughout — his foil character is life/paladin; the two “live in grey” together (002 §Thane).
  • Has near-death experiences over the course of the game and becomes increasingly numb to them (M01832, FreshToast, 2023-02-12).
  • Dialogue consultation trait: generally useful, with a whole lot of melancholy and subtle gallows humor (the opposite of Ruby) (002 §Thane).
  • Flaws (002 §Thane): emotionally distant, gallows humor as defense mechanism; numb to danger and his own mortality; overly protective of Locke due to past failure; struggles to be vulnerable; may take unnecessary risks because he doesn’t fear death enough; trouble letting go and moving forward.
  • Character moment: another character sits explaining where they got their scar — Thane cannot remember where he got his scars from. Too many to remember. All the same to him (M01211–M01214, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-08).
  • “I imagine Thane carries a lot of his comrades and wants and dreams with him. A bit of them lives with him.” (M04646, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-24)

Background & Motivations

  • Was a mentor; his mentee dies — tragedy (M01828, FreshToast, 2023-02-12). Begins more experienced than Locke; haunted by his failure to protect his student; carries guilt and questions whether he’s worthy to teach or lead again (002 §Thane).
  • Motivations (002 §Thane): atone for failing his previous mentee; protect Locke and the party from the same fate; come to terms with death and loss in a healthy way; prove to himself he can still make a difference; eventually accept that death is natural, not something to defy or fear.
  • Goals (002 §Thane): ensure Locke doesn’t repeat his mistakes; help the party survive; confront his own trauma and survivor’s guilt; find peace with Death (literal spirit and concept); tell Eris that death is truly final — an important plot moment.

Abilities & Traits

  • Party position: 1st line (Front); combat-focused (002 §Thane).
  • Positioning: static — Thane (with Isaac) struggles to move in combat, more specialized; can move somewhat but prefers not to; reliable through toughness (M01874–M01881, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-13; M02576–M02579, FreshToast, 2023-02-20).
  • DEBATED Has a taunt ability — design intent from his earliest statblock (M02169, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16), NOT yet implemented; the proto’s Thane has no taunt, and the “no purely defensive non-taunt abilities” rule keeps taunt on the roadmap (002 §Thane).
  • Shield system: Thane gets 2 shields “because special boi” in the revised one-shield system (M02492–M02497, FreshToast, 2023-02-19).
  • Exploration: teaches Locke how to sneak (M01683, FreshToast, 2023-02-11) — part of the DEBATED exploration-skills scheme.

CANON — IMPLEMENTED Proto combat values

(proto: src/core/combat/content.ts, src/core/combat/types.ts; 002 §Thane)

  • id thane, role “Vanguard”, glyph 🛡️, maxHp 44 (party tank — highest HP), maxAp 3, speed 4 (slowest hero), str 14 / mag 2 / def 16 / res 8 / acc 90 / crit 8. Start position 1 (Front line).
  • Cleave (2 AP) — heavy swing at a near foe, 10 phys.
  • Bulwark (1 AP) — braces behind the shield; raises own Defense and Resistance (his defensive ability in code, in place of a taunt).
  • Rending Blow (2 AP) — 8 phys + Bleed.
  • Earthsplitter (4 AP, finisher) — shatters the ground beneath the whole enemy line, 9 phys to all.

Relationships

  • Locke & Thane pairing noted (M01228, FreshToast, 2023-02-09); he shares the moment where Locke struggles to travel far from home (M04135, FreshToast, 2023-10-13).
  • Romantic interest: Death (Spirit) — the only romance in the relationship structure is between Thane and Death (M01223–M01224, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). His daemon is the Daemon of Death: moderately powerful but not violent, prefers to comfort the dying, surprisingly timid (M02665, FreshToast, 2023-02-21). DD is enbie (non-binary), and FreshToast wants a cute nickname for them — the nickname is for their dynamic with Thane, who uses irreverent nicknames (M02745–M02759, FreshToast, 2023-02-25).
  • Ties to Eris — “perhaps he’s [the] one who tells her death is truly final”; her arc of unhealthy mourning contrasts his (M02242–M02244, FreshToast, 2023-02-16). Theme: healthy mourning vs going off the deep end (002 §Thane).
  • Thane’s Foil (the Prideful Paladin, “the Lion”) — his life-themed counterpart; they “live in grey” together. The Paladin does not have to be in the hero party — he can be independent (M02335–M02341, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Team-skill pairing: Ruby/Thane (M05392–M05394, FreshToast, 2024-04-11).

Role & Arc

  • Eventually hunted down by Thane’s Foil (the Paladin) or the death spirit (002 §Thane).
  • “Have Thane be perfect counter for boss fight but lean into difficulty, thus making Thane look cool af” (002 §Thane).
  • Death/regret theme avenues run through Flynt/Locke and Thane (M04658, FreshToast, 2023-10-24).
  • Portraits exist in first draft (M04734–M04737, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-28).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Who hunts Thane down — his Foil the Paladin, or the death spirit? (002 §Thane)
  • Unresolved joke/aside: “make Thane’s shoes filthy…maybe?” (M01401, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-10; logged in 009 §Unresolved Questions).

Design history

  • 2021-08-01 — the name “Thane” first appears in the notes, years before the rest of the party (M00423, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-05 — “I think Ruby and Thane should be our 2 frontline melee characters” (M01006, FreshToast); attribute concept Thane = Toughness (M01009, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-08 — the scar gag: too many scars to remember (M01211–M01214, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-09 — age 34 (M01217); the only romance in the party structure: Thane & Death (M01223–M01224); “Thane wolfman” motif floated (M01293–M01294, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-11 — animal motifs fixed: Thane the wolf, the Paladin the Lion (M01416–M01417, CrunchyConniptions); Thane teaches Locke to sneak (M01683, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-12 — backstory: was a mentor, mentee dies, tragedy (M01828); multiple near-death experiences, increasing numbness (M01832, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-14 → 02-15 — surname changed to Farrow to differentiate from Isaac; approved (M01923, FreshToast; M02057, spirit_noodle).
  • 2023-02-16 — initial lineup Thane - Melee - Spear (M02164, CrunchyConniptions); first statblock: Weapon Spear, skills Pierce (ignores a portion of enemy damage resistance) and Taunt, HP 30 (M02169); skills Leaping Strike and Balanced Slash drafted (M02171–M02172). Superseded — the proto gives the spear to Locke and Thane a sword-and-board Vanguard kit with no taunt.
  • 2023-02-19 — shield revision: 1 shield that drops you to 1 HP once; Thane gets 2 because special boi (M02492–M02497, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-20 — mobility intent: Thane and Isaac are static and don’t like to move (M02576–M02579, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-24 — carries his comrades’ wants and dreams with him (M04646, CrunchyConniptions); named as a death/regret-theme avenue (M04658, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-28 — first-draft Thane portraits shared (M04734–M04737, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2024-04-11 — team-skill pairing structure puts him with Ruby; position-based team skills (e.g. Astrid behind Thane or Ruby) (M05392–M05394, FreshToast).
  • 2026-06-16 — proto roster reconciliation: implemented as “Vanguard,” HP 44, Cleave/Bulwark/Rending Blow/Earthsplitter; taunt still unimplemented (002 §Main Party header, CANON — IMPLEMENTED).
