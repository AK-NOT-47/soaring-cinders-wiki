The 4 Boyes

Caelian OdysseyNotes / DesignPrimary character
Also known as: The Four Boyes, Big 6
First conceived 2023-10-13

The four-student core cast of Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium — Alistair, Pandora, Titan and Colette — plus the design philosophy that binds them and the “Big 6” that completes the roster.

Summary — The 4 Boyes are the ensemble protagonists of the Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium iteration, students at the Luminarium magic academy. The game deliberately has no singular main protagonist — “Cyrus, our antagonist, is the keystone for our theme. We do not have a singular main protagonist” (2023-10-17). Together with Cyrus and Tenebrae they form the “Big 6” — “It’s all we really need” (2023-10-17).

Design philosophy

(2023-10-17; 002 §The 4 Boyes + Cyrus)

  • Each of the 4 Boyes is based on a nonmagical historical or fictional archetype. Considered: the thief, samurai, knight, explorer or cowboy.
  • Cyrus is both a young, archetypal fantasy mage and representative of a well-rounded sorcerer by Caelian standards.
  • Their personal conflicts should be largely internal and — unlike Skyward Cinders — highly secondary to the main plot (with the exemption of Cyrus).
  • Each of the 4 Boyes is meant to represent a general facet of Sorcery and magic.
  • All are in their early 20s (2023-10-17).
  • Cyrus, Alistair, Pandora and Titan are all ethnically typical for western Khorath (“like, mediterranean”); Colette is a bit darker (2023-10-17).
  • They each start with their affinity and the barest knowledge of magic; they’ve each “awakened” (2023-10-17).

The Big 6

“The big 6 characters though are the 4 boyes, Cyrus and Tenebrae” (2023-10-17):

  1. Alistair — Sylmiran male, ‘Frost’ affinity; represents specialisation.
  2. Pandora — Lycosian female, Victorian/Western inventor; represents practical sorcery and magical anomalies.
  3. Titan — Lycosian male demi-human (placeholder name), wuxia martial artist; ‘Body’ affinity.
  4. Colette — Lunoiran female, the “heart” of the group; represents the power of names and foreign interpretations of sorcery.
  5. Cyrus — the main/final antagonist and thematic keystone.
  6. Tenebrae — the daemon of mania entangled with Cyrus.

Beyond the Big 6, “there will be several” professors/mentors, “and others learning with them, who feature on the side” (2023-10-17) — see Nyssa and the Academy’s Head.

Story scope

  • Theme/message of the game is the “letting go” idea discussed earlier (2023-10-17).
  • No antagonist faction/rival group to the Luminarium: “Ok I’ve given it some thought. No antagonist faction to the Luminarium. We’re gonna keep it about the Luminarium” (2023-10-17). All the bad stuff is chalked up to Cyrus and Tenebrae — “keep it in house so as to keep it simple” (2023-10-17).
  • River scene: the 4 Boyes at the riverside recharging their flask, a reflective moment on how far they’ve come — while one of the last scenes is not the boyes but the rival, who confesses his sins to the river yet tragically hardens his resolve to side with the daemon (2023-10-13).
  • Maybe each of the academy houses has a guide, and the one for the boyes’ house is cool and nice (2023-10-17).

Design history

  • 2023-10-13 — first “4 boyes” reference, in the reflective riverside/flask scene sketch for this iteration.
  • 2023-10-17 — design philosophy laid down: nonmagical archetypes, internal conflicts secondary to plot, each representing a facet of Sorcery; the full roster write-up “The 4 Boyes + Cyrus and current concepts regarding them” posted, with the note “(I’m gonna edit this over time)”.
  • 2023-10-17 — demographics fixed: western-Khorath Mediterranean for all but Colette (a bit darker); all early 20s.
  • 2023-10-17 — iteration named “Caelian Odyssy - The Luminarium”; team decides to focus on “those 5 specific characters” (the 4 Boyes + Cyrus) first, Cyrus being the most developed.
  • 2023-10-17 — theme anchored: “letting go”; Cyrus the keystone; no singular main protagonist; the Big 6 declared: the 4 Boyes, Cyrus and Tenebrae.
  • 2023-10-17 — antagonist-faction question raised by CrunchyConniptions and settled: none — keep it in house and about the Luminarium.
