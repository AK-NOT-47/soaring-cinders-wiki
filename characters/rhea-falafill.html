Rhea Falafill

Rhea Falafill DEBATED

Percy’s Hermione-esque rival: a fiercely competitive, knowledge-hungry elf girl who exists in two unreconciled write-ups — a rival who wants a better future for all, and a nemesis who discards ethics for results.

Summary — Rhea Falafill was conceived as a rival for Isaac (who later became Percy): “smart, straightforward but hardworking rather than talented,” “a little too proper, too by-the-book” (M02722, M02725, FreshToast, 2023-02-25). Two full profiles exist — the First Version (rival) of 2023-02-27 and the Second Version (nemesis) of 2023-08-12 — and which one gets finalized is an explicitly open question (002 §Rhea, reconciliation note; 000 Part 14). Both are recorded below; where they agree the trait is shared core, where they diverge the divergence is noted.

Iteration note: Percy is the main-game party mage; the Rhea-rival material was workshopped in the Caelian Odyssey academy notes, and whether Percy/Rhea overlaps into that iteration is PROPOSED, not settled (002 §CAELIAN ODYSSEY header, Note on Percival/Percy).

Name / Identity

  • CANON The name is a food pun on “falafel,” tied to the Isaac→Percy rename: “Isaac is Percy and falafal is a foodstuff” (M03526, FreshToast, 2023-08-12). Name jokes ran wild: “did you just call a character falafel” (M02836, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-27); “Fuckin falafal??” (M02840, FreshToast); “Also ethereal means she’s a ghost. Pointed ears means an elf ghost. Falafel the elf ghost” (M02852–M02855, FreshToast, 2023-02-27).
  • DEBATED Gender: the notes lean toward girl — “I think it should be a girl… dunno about a boy” (M02731, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-25); “I think girl is very befitting for this kind of archetype” (M02735, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-25); FreshToast: “Okeydokey” after “You think we should just play to type?” (M02737–M02739). Discussed but not hard-locked; a boy was briefly suggested to avoid parallels with Damian’s hunter (002 §Rhea: Name / Identity).

Appearance (shared core)

Long, dark hair to the waist; pointed ears (an elf); green eyes; short/petite and slender with graceful, ethereal movements. Wears an elegant gown in shades of blue and green with intricate embroidery/patterns “reminiscent of an older world” (M02835, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-27; M03519, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-08-12).

Personality (shared core)

  • Strong-willed, intelligent, fiercely independent; passionate, competitive, determined; a perfectionist (M02835; M03519).
  • A sore loser — losses become “gnawing obsessions that haunt her for days” (M02835; M03519).
  • Very pragmatic — and, per the Second Version, “often discarding ethics for results” (M03519, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-08-12).
  • Archetype refs: “smart, straightforward but hardworking rather than talented,” “a little too proper, too by-the-book” — like the short loud character from Kaguya, or Yunyun from Konosuba (“Yunyun’s a bit too nice but her practical differences to megumin for sure”) (M02722–M02728, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-25).

Goals & Motivation

  • Shared: driven by a thirst for knowledge, especially forbidden/ancient arts (M02835; M03519).
  • First Version (rival): wants to use the ancient secrets of magic to “create a better future for all”; motivated by protecting those she loves and fighting injustice and oppression; torn between her desire for knowledge and her sense of duty — the secrets could be dangerous in the wrong hands (M02835, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-27).
  • Second Version (nemesis): wants to “craft a world sculpted by her ideals,” where bindings of any kind are nonexistent; loss and grief molded her perspective; her quest is also personal — freeing herself from the “invisible chains” of her past and societal expectations; acutely aware the knowledge “teeters on the edge of chaos” yet irresistibly drawn to it “like a moth to a flame” (M03519, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-08-12).

Background (shared core)

Grew up in a strict household where magic was forbidden/taboo and knowledge tightly restricted; her curiosity put her at odds with her family. She discovered a hidden trove/library of ancient, crumbling texts, which ignited her hunger for magic (M02835; M03519).

Relationships CANON

  • Percy’s rival; “Later this person could side with Isaac against Eris” (M02723, FreshToast, 2023-02-25).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Which version of Percy’s rival will be finalized — First Version (rival) or Second Version (nemesis)? Explicitly listed as an open question (000 Part 14; 002 §Rhea reconciliation note).
  • DEBATED Gender: girl leaned toward, not hard-locked (M02730–M02739, 2023-02-25; 002 §Rhea: Name / Identity).
  • PROPOSED Whether Percy/Rhea overlaps into the Caelian Odyssey iteration at all (002 §CAELIAN ODYSSEY header note).

Design history

  • 2023-02-25 — concept born: “Brainstorming the meat for the middle of the plot, I wonder if Isaac needs a hermione-esque rival” — smart, hardworking rather than talented, too proper/by-the-book; could later side with Isaac against Eris; Kaguya/Yunyun archetype refs; gender debated, girl leaned toward (“play to type”) (M02721–M02739, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions/spirit_noodle).
  • 2023-02-27 — First Version full profile posted: “Rhea Falafill (Isaac’s rival)” — elf appearance, sore-loser perfectionist, better-future-for-all goals, knowledge-vs-duty conflict, forbidden-magic household background (M02835, CrunchyConniptions). The falafel name-pun pile-on follows, coining “Falafel the elf ghost” (M02836–M02855, spirit_noodle/FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-08-12 — Second Version posted: “Rhea Falafill (Isaac’s Nemesis)” — same shared core but darker: discards ethics for results, wants a world sculpted by her ideals free of all bindings, moth-to-flame conflict (M03519, CrunchyConniptions). FreshToast anchors the rename chain: “Isaac is Percy and falafal is a foodstuff” (M03526). Neither version has been chosen — both stand, status DEBATED.
