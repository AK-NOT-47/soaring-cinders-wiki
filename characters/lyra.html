Lyra

PROPOSEDSkyward CindersNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-02-09 (M01272, CrunchyConniptions) · last in chat 2023-11-15 (M04954)

Lyra PROPOSED

Chaotic necromancer “dungeon master” found at the end of her own dungeon, holder of one of the seven chaos emeralds, who asks you to rate her dungeon on fantasy Yelp.

Summary — Lyra is a necromancer who builds her own dungeons and is encountered at the end of one, post-boss (M01273–M01274, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). She holds one of the seven chaos emeralds (M01280, FreshToast, 2023-02-09), lures the party in with a scene showing off her treasure (M01279, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09), and asks you to rate her dungeon on fantasy Yelp — Ruby fills out the survey (M01276–M01278, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). Later notes place her comfortably “in a dungeon somewhere” (M02727, FreshToast, 2023-02-25) and float pink hair for her (M04954, FreshToast, 2023-11-15).

Appearance

  • Mysterious figure with striking, dark features: raven-black hair in loose waves down to her waist; piercing, deep-set green eyes; bewitching beauty enhanced by an imposing 6-foot stature and slender build; always dressed in a long, dark cloak that conceals her appearance and protects her from the elements of the dungeons she creates. “She got bodacious boobies.” (M01272, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09)
  • DEBATED Maybe has pink hair — “Who has pink hair?” / “I was thinking several side characters” / “Maybe Lyra too” (M04952–M04954, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-11-15) — conflicts with the raven-black hair of the original profile.

Personality

  • Chaotic and unpredictable; follows her own path (M01272, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).
  • Highly intelligent and cunning; manipulates those around her to her advantage; arrogant and confident; disregards social norms; deeply emotional underneath (M01272).
  • “Subtly horny” (M01275, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Asks you to rate her dungeon on fantasy Yelp; a survey (M01276–M01277, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Background & Motivations

  • Driven to create her own dungeon, free from the constraints of society’s rules; seeks to prove that the way of chaos and the path of the Necromancer is the true way of power (M01272, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).
  • Born into a family of powerful, well-respected Necromancers; grew to question their strict rules, felt stifled, and set out to forge her own path, embracing her inner chaos; has traveled the world gathering knowledge and power (M01272).
  • Her dungeons are a testament to her mastery of the dark arts (M01272).

Role & Arc

  • Dungeon master: found at the end of a dungeon, post-boss (M01273–M01274, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Has one of the seven chaos emeralds (M01280, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • A scene where she shows off the treasure to lure you in (M01279, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).
  • Ruby fills out her dungeon survey (M01278, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Open questions

  • DEBATED “Lyra or Eris is a vampire?” — raised and never resolved (M01398, FreshToast, 2023-02-10).
  • DEBATED Pink hair vs the profile’s raven-black hair (M04952–M04954, 2023-11-15).

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — full profile posted (appearance, chaotic personality, necromancer-family background) (M01272, CrunchyConniptions); role built out the same session: dungeon master, post-boss encounter, subtly horny, fantasy-Yelp survey, Ruby fills it out, treasure-lure scene, chaos emerald holder (M01273–M01280, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-10 — vampire question raised: “Lyra or Eris is a vampire?” (M01398, FreshToast) — unresolved.
  • 2023-02-25 — placement confirmed feasible: “I’m pretty sure I can fit Lyra in comfortably in a dungeon somewhere” (M02727, FreshToast).
  • 2023-11-15 — pink hair floated for several side characters, “Maybe Lyra too” (M04952–M04954, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast).
