Pandora CANON

One of the 4 Boyes: the Victorian/Western-inventor archetype of the Luminarium — immense magical capacity she can barely channel, compensated with cunning, cannons and automatons.

Summary — Pandora is a Lycosian female in her early 20s who occupies the farthest back position of the group. She represents “both the practical uses of Sorcery - such as enchantment and alchemy - and also magical anomalies” (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17). Her defining quirk: immense Flow capacity (capable of big magic) but inefficient channeling — she tires fast and casts slow, making up the deficiency with cunning and her creations. She was the first of the Boyes to click: “Pandora makes the most sense in my brain. The others are still forming” (M04342, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Identity

  • Lycosian female; early 20s; ethnically typical for western Khorath (Mediterranean) (M04227, M04242–M04243, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Victorian/Western Inventor archetype; affinity undecided (M04227).
  • Occupies the farthest back position (M04227).

Role & Function

  • One of the 4 Boyes; represents both practical uses of Sorcery (enchantment and alchemy) and magical anomalies (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Helps Percival make Conflux (his arm) (M04343, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Magical focus

  • Makes things like magic bombs, her cannon ‘Rainfall’, or the automaton ‘Dianthus’ (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Dianthus eventually attains sentience — “and they maybe kiss a lil UwU” (M04227).
  • Especially into ergonomic, supportive stuff like prosthetics and machina (M04346, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Names the stuff she makes after smells she likes (M04227).
  • Rainfall gives her a highly controlled magical outlet — “A safe way to use her capacity… (kinda like a prosthetic for a disability, I’m now realising)” (M04350–M04352, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Unique quirk CANON

  • Immense Flow capacity (capable of big magic) but struggles to channel Flow efficiently — gets tired fast and casts slow. Makes up for the overall deficiency with her cunning and creations (M04227, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • “She’s basically a danger to herself and everyone around her” (M04347, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Joining motive / Backstory

  • Easiest motive of the group for joining the Luminarium: her immense Flow capacity combined with a lack of training makes her a danger to herself and everyone around her (M04347, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • “Could be just blows up her house and has to leave before it gets worse” — she has a weakness in her lack of control and would want to seek a solution; passionate about sorcery and its implementations but hampered by casting risk and ease of fatigue (M04348–M04349, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Rainfall is (sorta) that solution: the safe, highly controlled outlet answering this danger (M04350–M04351, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).

Conflicts (brainstormed) PROPOSED

Fourteen-item conflict brainstorm list — design seeds, not yet finalized (M04393, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-17):

  1. Self-Imposed Isolation — pushes people away for fear of getting hurt
  2. Perceived Inferiority — feels her magic is less impressive than others’
  3. Reluctant Leadership — unsure if she’s cut out for leadership roles she’s thrust into
  4. Desire for Legacy — wants to ensure her inventions outlive her
  5. Creative Block — struggles with periods of lacking inspiration (maybe goes to the dungeon because it clears her head)
  6. Fear of Outshining — holds back to avoid overshadowing friends; doesn’t show off how powerful her inventions are
  7. Reluctance to Trust — finds it hard to open up or rely on others; once she does, she’s full in it
  8. Friendship Betrayal — grapples with a past betrayal and trust issues
  9. Guilt over Priorities — feels guilty for prioritizing work over relationships (family in particular; becomes a habit)
  10. Struggle with Self-care — neglects her well-being in pursuit of goals; often sick, malnourished or sleep-deprived, so she faints and falls asleep now and then
  11. Fear of Exposure — fears her secrets or vulnerabilities will be exposed
  12. Regretful Decision — made a choice she deeply regrets; that one love she could’ve had but didn’t have the courage for
  13. Financial Dependence — relies on someone for financial support and feels trapped (a mentor who sees her potential and invests in her and her inventions)
  14. Hidden Passion — hides a passion she fears others won’t understand
