Minor & Unnamed Characters

Skyward CindersNotes / DesignWorking nameTertiary character
Also known as: Animal Lover, The Hero, The Wanderer, Hikari the Shinobi, Brickwings, The Brick, BRICKWINGS
First conceived 2023-02-05

Grouped page for one-note and unnamed characters: the animal lover, the Hero-as-antagonist concept, the Wanderer, Hikari the shinobi, and Brickwings the animated brick.

Summary — Five small character concepts from the February 2023 brainstorm, each with only a line or two of source material. None has been developed beyond its original mention; statuses are per-section below.

Animal Lover (unnamed)

A character/character moment: an animal lover has their food stolen away from them by a seagull as they’re eating — and they’re not mad about it. In fact, they’re elated; they root for the bird, praising its survival skills and will to live. “you go, bird.” (2023-02-08)

The Hero (antagonist concept)

“We fight the hero” (2023-02-08) — one of the game’s gaming-deconstruction pillars, listed alongside “we fight a blacksmith who changes their armor based on your attack” and “we don’t level up” (2023-02-08). The idea of the classic JRPG hero as the thing you fight resonates with Ajax/Lysander later being described as “parodies of a classic JRPG protagonist” (2023-02-21), but no direct link was stated in the notes.

The Wanderer

“Female short” — “The Wanderer” (2023-02-05). Mentioned with no additional details provided (002 §The Wanderer).

Hikari the Shinobi

From the ninja village in the mountains — a village of ninjas with supernatural ninja powers where you fight the hokage (2023-02-14). “You meet hikari the shinobi with huge anime tiddies” (2023-02-14); paired with “the other cute shy one with normal tiddies”, countered by “No shy girls always have the bigger tiddies” (2023-02-14). The shy companion remains unnamed.

Brickwings (The Brick)

The BRICKWINGS story arc (2023-02-08), beat by beat (2023-02-08):

  • The party needs to throw a brick but can’t throw it far enough; Gaius animates the brick and it gets wings.
  • The brick flies through a window but escapes — “brick is now free”.
  • Later the party finds the brick again, and Gaius is berated by Ruby for not taking care of it.
  • The brick joins the party; it is wholesome and beloved; it gets a couple of scenes.
  • The brick dies comically — a “literal throwaway joke”.
  • “Ruby relates to the brick” — consistent with the Gaius & Ruby dynamic being “like Reigen and Mob” (2023-02-08).

Design history

  • 2023-02-05 — The Wanderer noted: “Female short / The Wanderer”.
  • 2023-02-08 — gaming-deconstruction pillar “We fight the hero”; the animal-lover seagull moment; the full BRICKWINGS arc improvised in a single burst.
  • 2023-02-14 — Hikari the shinobi and the unnamed shy companion introduced with the mountain ninja village.
