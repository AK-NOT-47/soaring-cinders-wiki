Cyril/Cyrus Erhardt PROPOSED

The “Magic Robin Hood for Knowledge” — a character who steals from the Grand Library to share what’s kept from the public.

Summary — Cyril/Cyrus Erhardt is a “Magic Robin Hood for knowledge” (M02444, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-19): knowledge is kept from the public in the Grand Library, and he wants to acquire and share it (M02447, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-19). He appears to be a different character from the Caelian Odyssey Cyrus — see Cyrus (disambiguation) and Cyrus Sunworth.

Iteration note: conceived on 2023-02-19 amid main-game (Skyward Cinders era) plot brainstorming (Locke/Lysander prologue, chaos emeralds); doc 002 files him under the Caelian Odyssey section only with the caveat “This appears to be a different character from the Cyrus in Caelian Odyssey” (002 §Cyril/Cyrus Erhardt, Notes).

Background & Motivations

  • Magic Robin Hood for Knowledge (M02444, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-19).
  • Knowledge is kept from the public in the Grand Library; “Cyrill is wants to acquire and share the knowledge” (M02447, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-19).

Name

  • First given as Cyril Erhardt (M02444, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-19); later the same day rendered Cyrus Erhardt (M02465, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-19). Surname Erhardt is settled; the given name shifts Cyril → Cyrus (ledger row, R13). Note the Erhardt surname also appears in main-game naming (“Leon Erhardt” was a hero-name candidate, M01943–M01944 era — see Leon Erhardt).

Design history

  • 2023-02-19 — concept: “Magic Robin Hood for knowledge - Cyril Erhardt” (M02444, CrunchyConniptions); expanded: knowledge kept from public in the Grand Library, he wants to acquire and share it (M02447, spirit_noodle).
  • 2023-02-19 — name rendered “Cyrus Erhardt” (M02465, spirit_noodle) — given name Cyril → Cyrus; never developed further.
