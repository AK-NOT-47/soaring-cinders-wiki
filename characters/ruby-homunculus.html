Ruby (homunculus)

EVOLVEDEarly ConceptNotes / Design
Also known as: Lalateya
First conceived 2023-02-05 (M00950, CrunchyConniptions) · last in chat 2023-10-24 (M04660)

Ruby (homunculus) EVOLVED

Lalateya “Ruby” — a failed-prototype homunculus built by alchemy, discarded by her creator and now discovering the world with fresh, scatter-brained eyes; the Strength-stat frontliner of the homunculus-era party.

Summary — Ruby (first drafted under the name Lalateya, M00952, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-05) is an artificial being created by Gideon Azurith using a borrowed soul instead of his own, which made her “defective”: her programmed purpose clashes with the innate morality that came with a real soul (M02178–M02179, FreshToast, 2023-02-16). Deemed a failed prototype, given away, and eventually misplaced, she journeys to uncover the cause of her failures while serving as the party’s unassuming-but-strong frontline melee (M01006, M01010, FreshToast, 2023-02-05). Her arc peaks in killing her rival-sister Citrine but sparing their creator in Citrine’s name (M02077, FreshToast, 2023-02-15). This is the homunculus-era version; see Ruby (disambiguation) for disambiguation from the later Skyward Cinders Ruby.

Appearance

  • Female; 6 feet tall, around 200 pounds; smooth, angular body — originally described as metal, changed to fleshy but artificial (002 §Lalateya/Ruby; M00950 “Female short” opens the profile, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-05).
  • Piercing red eyes with golden flowing hair; alternative look: red eyes with white hair and a refined black & gold outfit (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • Color scheme: white, black, red & yellow — “they’re the 4 steps of the magnum opus” (M00980, FreshToast, 2023-02-05); warm colors because protagonist (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • Form designed for combat; explicitly NOT incredible speed and agility (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • Lacks the ability to channel magic herself but can act as a catalyst for others’ magical abilities (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).

Personality

  • Model of efficiency with a deep sense of morality and justice; pragmatism-vs-morality is the emphasis (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • Highly curious and analytical, constantly gathering and processing information — scatter-brained, in a constant state of analytical information overload, “like puppy constantly distracted by new things” (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • Easily teased; her humor comes from interacting with an unknown world. Reference: Power from Chainsaw Man (new to being human) (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • “Like the doll from bloodborne looks up at hunter and humans” (M00962, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-05).
  • Dialogue-consultation trait: ignorant about common-sense situations, mostly hilariously useless, but will sometimes see the raw truth of the world due to fresh eyes (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).

Background & Motivations

  • Built as a homunculus through alchemy; created with a specific mission but repeatedly failed due to an undetermined error. Root cause: a clash between her moral values and the purpose programmed by her creator, who failed to acknowledge that life and morality go hand in hand (002 §Lalateya/Ruby; M00950–M01001, 2023-02-05).
  • Homunculi require sacrificing a portion of your own flesh and soul to create; Ruby’s creator borrowed the soul of another instead of using his own. The player eventually finds out a little about the soul used to make Ruby (M02178–M02179, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Deemed a failed prototype, given to lower-tier mages for experiments, eventually misplaced in an unknown city; embarked on a journey of self-discovery (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • Lieutenant Milf has hands in Ruby’s creation (and in Gaius’ expulsion) (M01221, M01225, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Motivations: uncover the cause of her past failures; understand the world and its inhabitants; find her place; forge a new path separate from her creator’s intentions — while still seeking approval from the aloof, disinterested creator she sees as a parent (“absent father”) (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • Age: 2 (M01217–M01218, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-09).

Abilities & Traits

  • Associated with the Strength stat; frontline melee character alongside Thane (M01006, M01009, 2023-02-05).
  • “Ruby being unassuming but strong would fit the trope” (M01010, FreshToast, 2023-02-05).
  • Can move somewhat in combat but prefers not to; reliable through flexibility; “Must Protect” in the party-order impressions (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).

Relationships

  • Creator: Gideon Azurith — absent father figure; she seeks his approval (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • Rival: Citrine — “Rival who is just a shinier psychopath version?” (M00959, FreshToast, 2023-02-05); loyal to and cared for by their creator; rival is like Jinx from Arcane (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • Gaius: Gaius and Ruby are like Reigen and Mob (M01189, FreshToast, 2023-02-08); “Gaius & Ruby” pairing noted (M01223–M01231, 2023-02-09). Later re-floated as an Isaac-and-Ruby dynamic (M02646, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Ruby relates to the brick in the BRICKWINGS story (M01176–M01188, FreshToast, 2023-02-08).

Role & Arc

  • Story about ends justifying means — connected to Gideon’s and Eris’s themes (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).
  • Sisterhood tragedy: Citrine comes to see Ruby as a wayward sister but cannot reconcile her ironclad loyalty to their creator with Ruby’s intentions; the sisters must fight; Ruby kills Citrine but spares the creator in Citrine’s name (M02077, FreshToast, 2023-02-15).
  • Ruby “once she kills Citrine” is listed as one of the character avenues for the death/regret theme (M04658–M04660, FreshToast, 2023-10-24).

Name reasoning

  • Ruby is great for a homunculus made by alchemy; a ruby is an item, maybe a tool in alchemy — “she is a tool” (M00993, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-05). Called Ruby because she is a magical item; the conversion of iron to gold and other alchemic concepts fit the zero-to-hero theme (002 §Lalateya/Ruby).

Design history

  • 2023-02-05 — profile drafted under the name Lalateya (M00952, CrunchyConniptions): 6-ft combat android, metal body, red eyes, golden hair, catalyst for others’ magic, failed-prototype backstory.
  • 2023-02-05 — changed from android/metal to fleshy-but-artificial homunculus made by alchemy; renamed Ruby for the alchemical-tool symbolism (“she is a tool”, M00993); magnum-opus color scheme (M00980); rival concept raised (M00959). — supersedes the android framing
  • 2023-02-05 — party role fixed: Strength stat, frontline melee with Thane, unassuming but strong (M01006–M01010, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-08 — BRICKWINGS arc: Ruby berates Gaius over the brick and relates to it; Reigen/Mob dynamic with Gaius (M01176–M01189, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-09 — age 2 (M01217–M01218); Citrine’s inverted-color design (M01289); Lieutenant Milf’s hand in Ruby’s creation (M01221, M01225).
  • 2023-02-15 — sisterhood ending written: sisters fight, Ruby kills Citrine but spares the creator in her name (M02077, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-16 — homunculus soul lore: creator borrowed another’s soul for Ruby, did the ritual correctly for Citrine; player learns a little about Ruby’s soul (M02178–M02179, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-24 — Ruby-after-killing-Citrine named as a regret-theme avenue (M04658–M04660, FreshToast). The character subsequently carried forward into the Skyward Cinders roster as Ruby (Skyward Cinders); this homunculus profile was archived in 002. — current archived state
