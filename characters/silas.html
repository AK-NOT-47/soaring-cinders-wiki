Silas PROPOSED

An underdeveloped character whose one established plot is a romance with the detective hunting him down.

Summary — Silas is a named character whose plot the group agreed “needs some work” (M02346, FreshToast, 2023-02-16; M02343, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16). His one workshopped hook: “Silas has his own romance with the detective hunting him down” — and the detective “can be gril” (M02363–M02364, FreshToast, 2023-02-16), to which Crunchy responded “thats hot / being hunted” (M02366–M02367, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16). The name “Silas” itself is established (M02080, FreshToast, 2023-02-15; 002 §Silas).

Role & Arc

  • Plot explicitly unfinished: “We need to work out Silas and Thane’s plots” (M02343, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-16); “Silas needs some work” (M02346, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Romance with the detective hunting him down; the detective can be a girl (M02363–M02364, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Working on a “Name for Silas” was logged as a task for Lettuce (spirit_noodle) alongside map, story and playtest work (M02401, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-17).

Open questions

  • DEBATED The hunted-by-an-investigator romance premise is nearly identical to the one attached to Leon (M01396, FreshToast, 2023-02-10) and later to Damian — which character finally owns it is unresolved (see Leon Erhardt §Open questions).
  • PROPOSED Everything beyond the name and the romance hook remains to be designed (“Needs work on plot” — 002 §Silas).

Design history

  • 2023-02-15 — name dropped in a naming session: “Silas” (M02080, FreshToast), alongside Locke Evertide / Leon Erhardt (M02079) and Yvette (M02081).
  • 2023-02-16 — plot flagged as needing work (M02343, CrunchyConniptions; M02346, FreshToast); romance-with-pursuing-detective hook added, detective can be a girl; “thats hot / being hunted” (M02363–M02367, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-17 — “Name for Silas” assigned as an open task (M02401, CrunchyConniptions).
