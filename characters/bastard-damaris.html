Bastard Damaris

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
Also known as: The bastard, Damaris (character)
First conceived 2023-02-21 (M02668, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-02-22 (M02694)

Bastard Damaris CANON

The illegitimate youngest of Ajax’s bloodline — first to exhibit truly prominent draconic features, herald of the parasite Drykksar, who wants to replace humanity with something superior.

Summary — The Bastard Damaris is an illegitimate member of the Damaris family, the youngest of Ajax’s bloodline and the first to exhibit truly prominent draconic features (M02693, FreshToast, 2023-02-22). Rejected for both his bastard status and his emerging features, he embraced the transformation instead of fearing it and became a herald or champion of sorts for the draconic parasite Drykksar (M02694, FreshToast, 2023-02-22). Intelligent, relatable, and obviously a bit mad, he denounces tribalism as selfish and wants to replace humanity with something superior — not completely unjustified, but he goes way too far (M02688–M02690, FreshToast, 2023-02-22).

Appearance

  • Youngest of the bloodline (Ajax’s lineage); first to exhibit truly prominent draconic features (M02693, FreshToast, 2023-02-22).
  • Instead of scales, has portions of skin that are cracked and metallic (M02692, FreshToast, 2023-02-22).
  • Has dragon scales growing on his skin as his physical flaw (early framing — M02680, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22; refined to cracked-and-metallic the same day).
  • Maybe speaks some of the dragon language (M02681, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22).
  • His appearance marks him as “other” — rejected by society due to both bastard status and draconic features; a visual representation of what Ajax’s bloodline has become (002 §Bastard Damaris).

Personality

  • Herald or champion of sorts for the draconic parasite (Drykksar) (M02694, FreshToast, 2023-02-22).
  • Intelligent and relatable (M02680, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22).
  • Denounces tribalism as selfish; wants to replace humanity with something superior; obviously a bit mad; not completely unjustified but goes way too far (M02688–M02690, FreshToast, 2023-02-22).
  • Articulate and philosophical about his beliefs; genuinely believes he’s saving the world, not destroying it; bitter about his rejection by family and society (002 §Bastard Damaris).

Background & Motivations

  • Born as an illegitimate child of the Damaris family; rejected due to both bastard status and emerging draconic features (002 §Bastard Damaris).
  • The Drykksar parasite affected him more strongly than previous generations; instead of fearing transformation, he embraced it (002 §Bastard Damaris).
  • Sees humanity’s flaws and cruelty as a terminal disease (002 §Bastard Damaris).

Motivations: replace “flawed” humanity with superior draconic evolution; prove his family wrong for rejecting him; accelerate the spread of Drykksar to create a new world order; become herald of what he sees as inevitable evolution; get revenge on the society that shunned him (002 §Bastard Damaris).

Goals: spread the Drykksar parasite to transform humanity; destroy the systems and institutions that maintain the current order; eliminate those who resist transformation (including Clara and other Damaris); become fully draconic himself (002 §Bastard Damaris).

Flaws: obviously mad despite logical-sounding arguments; cannot see that his “solution” is genocide; blinded by bitterness and desire for revenge; underestimates his opponents due to arrogance; fails to properly utilize his draining power before defeat; tragic in that his pain is real but his solution is monstrous (002 §Bastard Damaris).

Relationships & Dynamics

  • Direct antagonist to Clara — she represents the legitimate family that rejected him (002 §Bastard Damaris).
  • Parallels Lysander — both misuse Ajax’s legacy. Lysander has developed the daemonic power but not his draconic heritage, while the bastard is the youngest of the bloodline, first to exhibit truly prominent draconic features, but has little understanding of his ability to take (M02693, FreshToast, 2023-02-22).
  • Contrast to the party — they were also outcasts but chose connection over destruction (002 §Bastard Damaris).
  • Scene before the fight where Locke tries to reason with him; Astrid ultimately rejects his philosophy with “fuck u. I kill.” (M02682, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22).

Abilities & Traits

  • Affinity: Fiery (inherited) (002 §Bastard Damaris).
  • Has a diluted version of Ajax’s power, which waned with each heir; to recover, he has to kill and drain the life of others (M02668, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Has little understanding of his ability to take; understands the power but has such little strength to begin with that he fails to properly utilize it before he is killed (M02669, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • His power is perfect for the eradication of humanity (M02681, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22).
  • “Faster, stronger, and smarter than anything Locke has ever seen” (M02680, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22).
  • Draconic features grant enhanced physical abilities; can speak/understand the draconic language; as herald of Drykksar he can spread or control the parasite (002 §Bastard Damaris).

Role & Arc

Role in story: mid-game antagonist representing the corruption of Ajax’s legacy; philosophical opponent who challenges the party’s worldview; boss fight that tests Clara’s resolve (002 §Bastard Damaris).

Confrontation — scene just before the fight (M02682, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22):

Damaris: people bad. I kill. Locke: give people chance, maybe good. no kill Astrid: fuck u. I kill.

Killed by the party, but his ideology highlights real problems in the world; death prevents him from realizing his limited power (002 §Bastard Damaris).

Emotional beats: a moment where he almost convinces someone he’s right; Clara’s horror at seeing what her family bloodline produces; his death being somewhat tragic despite his monstrous goals (002 §Bastard Damaris).

Plot impact: represents the worst-case outcome of Ajax’s bloodline and Drykksar; forces Clara to confront her family’s dark legacy; raises questions about evolution, humanity, and what makes us “human”; drives mid-game conflict and stakes; his connection to Drykksar sets up potential sequel hooks; a philosophical antagonist who makes players think, not just fight (002 §Bastard Damaris).

Design history

  • 2023-02-21 — first appears in the power lore: “Lysander and the bastard Damaris have diluted versions of this power which has waned with each heir”; he understands the power but has too little strength to use it before he is killed (M02668–M02669, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-22 — Crunchy’s full backstory pitch (early version, partly superseded): a flaw society overtly criticizes — a real, physical flaw people belittle him for; “If he were provided love and kindness, he would be a hero like Ajax… he would surpass Ajax”; framed as “the child of a royal girl who had an affair with Lysander but told no one to not tarnish the hero’s reputation”; his mother died at birth, her dying wish that her father care for him; maybe dragon scales growing on his skin; “He could have been the very next step for humanity, but clearly, humanity is undeserving and should instead be eradicated”; faster, stronger, smarter than anything Locke has seen (M02680–M02681, CrunchyConniptions). The affair-with-Lysander parentage did not carry into the docs, which settle on “illegitimate child of the Damaris family” (002 §Bastard Damaris).
  • 2023-02-22 — the pre-fight scene written (“people bad. I kill.”) (M02682, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-22 — FreshToast refinements, the current version: draconic features related to the power in Ajax’s descendants (since he ate a dragon) — but the power has nothing to actually do with dragons, that’s a myth, a red herring (M02684–M02685); denounces tribalism as selfish, wants to replace humanity with something superior, a bit mad, goes way too far (M02688–M02690); cracked and metallic skin instead of scales (M02692); Lysander-vs-bastard contrast (M02693); herald or champion of the draconic parasite (M02694) — matches the current presentation above.
