Ajax

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-02-09 · last updated 2024-08-24

Ajax CANON

The old dead himbo legendary hero — the only warrior brave enough to fight a dragon, whose “dragon-blood” strength was really a pact with the Daemon of Want, and whose legend props up the villain while his true heir turns out to be Locke.

Summary — Ajax is the long-dead legendary hero whose legend “grants the evil hero their reputation but is in fact more closely mirrored by Locke at the end of the story” (2023-02-09). Ancestor and predecessor to Lysander (2023-02-16), he was “the goodest boy” — a himbo legendary hero (“It fits really really well”) (2023-02-19). Desperate for strength to defend his people as the only warrior truly brave enough to fight a dragon, he traded half his lifespan to the Daemon of Want for the ability to steal the strength of others (2023-02-21). Foreseeing his power’s misuse by his descendants, he left behind relics, a containing relic, and a tomb (2023-02-24, 2023-09-10). Affinity: Fiery (002 §Ajax).

Background & Motivations

  • Old dead hero; the basis for the legend which grants the evil hero (Lysander) their reputation, though in fact more closely mirrored by Locke at the end of the story (2023-02-09).
  • Ancestor/predecessor to Lysander, “who supposedly was infused with great strength by a beneficial, otherworldly being” (2023-02-16).
  • The deal: desperate for strength to defend his people — the only warrior truly brave enough to “fight a fucking dragon” — he traded half his lifespan to the Daemon of Want (a spirit formed from the people’s rampant desire for a saviour) for the ability to steal the strength of others (2023-02-21).
  • Cover story: he got his strength from drinking dragon’s blood after wounding one. In truth, that was the first instance of him using the power to drain life from the dragon during battle; he later killed the dragon with that stolen strength (2023-02-21).
  • Foresees his abilities being misused by his descendants and leaves behind “something like his sword that isn’t really a solution”; the relics may lead to his tomb (2023-02-24).
  • Damaged the top of a mountain during his fight with the dragon (while “high off absorbing a dragon’s strength at the peak of his power”), causing a river to form from several smaller ones — the legend of him splitting a mountain is narrative exaggeration (2023-10-13).

Appearance & Traits

  • Draconic features related to his power, “since he ate a dragon” — but the power has nothing to actually do with dragons; that’s myth, a red herring (2023-02-22; 002 §Ajax). His descendants are reputed to have sunset irises (2023-03-05).
  • Affinity: Fiery — listed alongside Locke, Thane, Sable and Eris (002 §Ajax).
  • “I want Ajax to be the goodest boy” / “Ajax could be our himbo” / “himbo legendary hero” (2023-02-19).

Relics, Tomb & Legacy

  • Prepared a relic that had helped him contain his own power, left behind for the moment a descendant becomes dangerous (2023-03-03).
  • The seven relic pieces are “some object owned by Ajax that was shattered, related to the spirit he made a pact with”; the relics are how the player learns about Ajax and his bloodline, and should be sentimental to him (2023-02-19).
  • The relics are informal trials — the Ajaxian trials, “those challenges left behind by Ajax” (2023-03-06, 2024-02-13). People misinterpret completing them as acquiring Ajax’s power, “but it is the act of acquiring that makes one powerful”; his legacy is in fact a final task — to challenge his descendant and prevent them from consuming thousands in pursuit of power (2023-03-06).
  • Has a tomb — Ajax’s Tomb: the dead end of the cavern trial where Locke earns Sunderer is actually its secret entrance (2023-09-10).
  • Family motto, which turns out to be literal because of the deal: “Half as long, twice as bright” (2023-02-16).
  • Sunderer becomes an explicit symbol of Locke’s status as heir to the role of Ajax (2024-08-24).

Appearance in story

  • His silhouette appears at the start of Lysander’s 2nd-phase fight, saying a few heroic words to inspire the player: “Weary warriors, a curse of sorrowed days past stands in front of you but with the relic of old at hand, the curses power can be defeated. Now, stand tall and fight.” (2023-03-03).

Relationships

  • Lysander — descendant; inherits a diluted version of the stolen-strength power (2023-02-16).
  • Vaenngrym — imagined as the son of Ajax’s dragon (2023-02-22).
  • Daemon of Want — the spirit he made his pact with (2023-02-21).
  • Locke — his true mirror and eventual heir (2023-02-09, 2024-08-24).

Open questions

  • “So Ajax is dragonborn?” — questioned, with the answer being that the draconic connection is myth/red herring (2023-02-22; 002 §Ajax Notes).

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — original concept: old dead hero Ajax as basis for the legend granting the evil hero their reputation, actually mirrored by Locke at the end.
  • 2023-02-16 — lineage: Lysander descended from Ajax, “supposedly infused with great strength by a beneficial, otherworldly being”; deal-with-the-devil framing and ends-justify-means theme; “perhaps Ajax’ strength came from devouring his enemies”; family motto “Half as long, twice as bright”.
  • 2023-02-19 — relics defined as a shattered object owned by Ajax, tied to his pact spirit; relics as the way we learn about him; “Was Ajax actually a decent guy, to contrast Lysander?” → goodest boy / himbo legendary hero.
  • 2023-02-21/22 — the deal formalized: Daemon of Want, half his lifespan for strength-stealing; dragon-blood cover story; draconic features in his descendants since he ate a dragon; power is literally “gaining EXP,” a parody of classic JRPG protagonists.
  • 2023-02-24 — foresees misuse; leaves behind something like his sword “that isn’t really a solution”; maybe the relics lead to his tomb.
  • 2023-03-03/06 — Lysander 2nd-phase silhouette scene with the “Weary warriors…” quote; containing relic; sunset irises reputed in his descendants; relics as informal trials, legacy as a final task to challenge his heir.
  • 2023-09-10 / 2023-10-13 — Sunderer trial’s dead end is the secret entrance to Ajax’s tomb; mountain-splitting demystified as damage during the dragon fight, source of the rivers.
  • 2024-02-13 / 2024-08-24 — “Ajaxian trials” named as already in-game; Sunderer confirmed as explicit symbol of Locke as heir to the role of Ajax.
