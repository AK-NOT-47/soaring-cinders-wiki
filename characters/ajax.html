Ajax

CANONSkyward CindersNotes / Design
First conceived 2023-02-09 (M01284, FreshToast) · last in chat 2024-08-24 (M05558)

Ajax CANON

The old dead himbo legendary hero — the only warrior brave enough to fight a dragon, whose “dragon-blood” strength was really a pact with the Daemon of Want, and whose legend props up the villain while his true heir turns out to be Locke.

Summary — Ajax is the long-dead legendary hero whose legend “grants the evil hero their reputation but is in fact more closely mirrored by Locke at the end of the story” (M01284, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). Ancestor and predecessor to Lysander (M02198, FreshToast, 2023-02-16), he was “the goodest boy” — a himbo legendary hero (“It fits really really well”) (M02434–M02437, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-19). Desperate for strength to defend his people as the only warrior truly brave enough to fight a dragon, he traded half his lifespan to the Daemon of Want for the ability to steal the strength of others (M02653, FreshToast, 2023-02-21). Foreseeing his power’s misuse by his descendants, he left behind relics, a containing relic, and a tomb (M02718, FreshToast, 2023-02-24; M03670, FreshToast, 2023-09-10). Affinity: Fiery (002 §Ajax).

Background & Motivations

  • Old dead hero; the basis for the legend which grants the evil hero (Lysander) their reputation, though in fact more closely mirrored by Locke at the end of the story (M01284, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • Ancestor/predecessor to Lysander, “who supposedly was infused with great strength by a beneficial, otherworldly being” (M02198, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • The deal: desperate for strength to defend his people — the only warrior truly brave enough to “fight a fucking dragon” — he traded half his lifespan to the Daemon of Want (a spirit formed from the people’s rampant desire for a saviour) for the ability to steal the strength of others (M02653, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Cover story: he got his strength from drinking dragon’s blood after wounding one. In truth, that was the first instance of him using the power to drain life from the dragon during battle; he later killed the dragon with that stolen strength (M02651, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Foresees his abilities being misused by his descendants and leaves behind “something like his sword that isn’t really a solution”; the relics may lead to his tomb (M02718, FreshToast, 2023-02-24).
  • Damaged the top of a mountain during his fight with the dragon (while “high off absorbing a dragon’s strength at the peak of his power”), causing a river to form from several smaller ones — the legend of him splitting a mountain is narrative exaggeration (M03824, FreshToast, 2023-10-13).

Appearance & Traits

  • Draconic features related to his power, “since he ate a dragon” — but the power has nothing to actually do with dragons; that’s myth, a red herring (M02684, FreshToast, 2023-02-22; 002 §Ajax). His descendants are reputed to have sunset irises (M02906, FreshToast, 2023-03-05).
  • Affinity: Fiery — listed alongside Locke, Thane, Sable and Eris (002 §Ajax).
  • “I want Ajax to be the goodest boy” / “Ajax could be our himbo” / “himbo legendary hero” (M02434–M02437, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-19).

Relics, Tomb & Legacy

  • Prepared a relic that had helped him contain his own power, left behind for the moment a descendant becomes dangerous (M02887, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-03-03; see Guardian of Relic).
  • The seven relic pieces are “some object owned by Ajax that was shattered, related to the spirit he made a pact with”; the relics are how the player learns about Ajax and his bloodline, and should be sentimental to him (M02420, M02432, FreshToast, 2023-02-19; see chaos emeralds).
  • The relics are informal trials — the Ajaxian trials, “those challenges left behind by Ajax” (M02907, FreshToast, 2023-03-06; M05288, FreshToast, 2024-02-13). People misinterpret completing them as acquiring Ajax’s power, “but it is the act of acquiring that makes one powerful”; his legacy is in fact a final task — to challenge his descendant and prevent them from consuming thousands in pursuit of power (M02908–M02909, FreshToast, 2023-03-06).
  • Has a tomb — Ajax’s Tomb: the dead end of the cavern trial where Locke earns Sunderer is actually its secret entrance (M03670, FreshToast, 2023-09-10).
  • Family motto, which turns out to be literal because of the deal: “Half as long, twice as bright” (M02303, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Sunderer becomes an explicit symbol of Locke’s status as heir to the role of Ajax (M05558, FreshToast, 2024-08-24).

Appearance in story

  • His silhouette appears at the start of Lysander’s 2nd-phase fight, saying a few heroic words to inspire the player: “Weary warriors, a curse of sorrowed days past stands in front of you but with the relic of old at hand, the curses power can be defeated. Now, stand tall and fight.” (M02887, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-03-03).

Relationships

  • Lysander — descendant; inherits a diluted version of the stolen-strength power (M02198, FreshToast, 2023-02-16).
  • Vaenngrym — imagined as the son of Ajax’s dragon (M02686, FreshToast, 2023-02-22).
  • Daemon of Want — the spirit he made his pact with (M02653, FreshToast, 2023-02-21).
  • Locke — his true mirror and eventual heir (M01284, FreshToast, 2023-02-09; M05558, FreshToast, 2024-08-24).

Open questions

  • “So Ajax is dragonborn?” — questioned, with the answer being that the draconic connection is myth/red herring (M02673, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-22; 002 §Ajax Notes).

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — original concept: old dead hero Ajax as basis for the legend granting the evil hero their reputation, actually mirrored by Locke at the end (M01284, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-16 — lineage: Lysander descended from Ajax, “supposedly infused with great strength by a beneficial, otherworldly being” (M02198, FreshToast); deal-with-the-devil framing and ends-justify-means theme (M02228, CrunchyConniptions); “perhaps Ajax’ strength came from devouring his enemies” (M02257, FreshToast); family motto “Half as long, twice as bright” (M02303, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-19 — relics defined as a shattered object owned by Ajax, tied to his pact spirit; relics as the way we learn about him; “Was Ajax actually a decent guy, to contrast Lysander?” → goodest boy / himbo legendary hero (M02420, M02432–M02437, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-21/22 — the deal formalized: Daemon of Want, half his lifespan for strength-stealing; dragon-blood cover story; draconic features in his descendants since he ate a dragon; power is literally “gaining EXP,” a parody of classic JRPG protagonists (M02651, M02653, M02671, M02684, FreshToast).
  • 2023-02-24 — foresees misuse; leaves behind something like his sword “that isn’t really a solution”; maybe the relics lead to his tomb (M02718, FreshToast).
  • 2023-03-03/06 — Lysander 2nd-phase silhouette scene with the “Weary warriors…” quote; containing relic (M02887, CrunchyConniptions); sunset irises reputed in his descendants (M02906); relics as informal trials, legacy as a final task to challenge his heir (M02907–M02909, FreshToast).
  • 2023-09-10 / 2023-10-13 — Sunderer trial’s dead end is the secret entrance to Ajax’s tomb (M03670, FreshToast); mountain-splitting demystified as damage during the dragon fight, source of the rivers (M03824, FreshToast).
  • 2024-02-13 / 2024-08-24 — “Ajaxian trials” named as already in-game (M05288, FreshToast); Sunderer confirmed as explicit symbol of Locke as heir to the role of Ajax (M05558, FreshToast).
