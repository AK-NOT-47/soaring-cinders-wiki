Fiore CANON

The country across the mountains, home of Damian — a nation as river-dependent as its neighbours, its river flowing opposite Lycosia’s.

Summary — Fiore is the country across the mountains from the other regions (M03826, FreshToast, 2023-10-13). It is highly dependent on its river, which runs throughout the country and travels in a different direction from Lycosia’s. Its language is Fioran, and its river’s name is meant to be twinned with Lycosia’s.

Geography

  • A country across the mountains from the other regions (M03826, FreshToast, 2023-10-13).
  • Has a big river running throughout it; the nation is highly dependent on their river on the other side of the mountains (003 §Countries and Regions).
  • Fiore’s river travels in a different direction from Lycosia’s river (M03834–M03836, FreshToast, 2023-10-13; 003 §Countries and Regions).

Culture

  • Language: Fioran is the spoken language (003 §Countries and Regions).
  • River culture: The river system is similarly reflected in Fiore’s geography and culture, part of the setting-wide reverence for rivers (003 §Countries and Regions).

Story connections

  • Origin of Damian: Fiore is where Damian is from (M03826, FreshToast, 2023-10-13).
  • Twinned river names: The Fioran and Lycosian rivers should have twinned/related names tied to the lore that Ajax “split a mountain” to form them from several smaller rivers (M03819–M03824, FreshToast, 2023-10-13; 003 §Rivers). See Lycosia and the regional overview.

Design history

  • 2023-10-13 — Fiore established as “the country across the mountains where Damian is from” (M03826, FreshToast), alongside the twinned-river lore (M03819–M03824, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-13 (later in the same session) — river detail confirmed: Fiore’s river runs throughout the country and flows in a direction different from Lycosia’s (M03834–M03836, FreshToast).
