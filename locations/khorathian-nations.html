Khorathian Nations

Caelian OdysseyNotes / DesignNation
Also known as: Cahras, Lunoir, N, Straid, Sylmir, Uldrav, Volanta, H Covenant
First conceived undated

This page collects the Khorathian nations and political blocs that are important to the setting but do not yet all need standalone articles. The latest notes supersede older conflicts: Lycosia is the imperial centre, Fiore and Lunoir form the provisional H Covenant, Straid is a Lycosian vassal, and Cahras remains deliberately separate from the rest of the continent.

Lycosian Empire

The Lycosian Empire spans western Khorath. Its core is Lycosia, the ruling state, and its broader reach includes several neighbours and dependencies:

  • Lycosia is the imperial seat and the central power of western Khorath.
  • N is a provisional name for a technologically innovative coastal people north of Lycosia. The note marks its inspiration as Greek/S.
  • Sylmir lies north-east of N and is the religious centre of Argentism.
  • Volanta is an archipelago off the western shore, associated with Spanish, Maori, and elven inspirations, pyroclastic sorcery, and black powder.
  • Straid is a floating isle and vassal city-state of Lycosia. It hovers south of the Lycosian coast and otherwise does not meaningfully participate in continental politics. Its inspiration is Greek/Celtic.

H Covenant

The H Covenant is a provisional alliance name in the notes. It joins Fiore and Lunoir against the Lycosian Empire:

  • Fiore is a sorcerous monarchy east of Lycosia, separated from it by a mountain range. Its inspiration is Italian/Egyptian.
  • Lunoir lies east of Fiore. It is described as a political nightmare-scape with vampire aristocrats, a “shadow of the moon” motif, and French/X inspirations.
  • The covenant has a treaty with the nomadic Uldrav in the southern desert wasteland.

Independent or ruined powers

  • Cahras is an independent republic in northernmost Khorath. It is mysterious, hermetic, and avoids other peoples. The notes describe the Cahresian cultural direction as Crow and a bastardised Japanese influence, with Japanese words altered toward a Nordic or dwarven sound.
  • Uldrav is a coastal desert and barren steppe region to the south. A burgeoning Uldravi civilisation fell to ruin under unknown circumstances, leaving the region largely ungoverned and unsettled. The Ebonscars are sections of Uldravi desert made of black ebon sand.

Open questions

  • H and N are still placeholder labels in the submitted notes.
  • Several national inspirations are intentionally rough design shorthand rather than in-world ethnographic labels.
