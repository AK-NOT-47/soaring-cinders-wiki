Ninja Village CANON

A village of ninjas hidden in the mountains — home to shinobi with supernatural powers and a hokage you fight, tucked away on Sneak Peak Mountain.

Summary — The Ninja Village is a village of ninjas in the mountains whose inhabitants have supernatural ninja powers (M01966–M01970, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-02-14). You fight the hokage here (M01969, FreshToast, 2023-02-14). Its characters include Hikari the shinobi and another shy character. It is explicitly not the Naruto sky-village.

Location

  • A village of ninjas in the mountains (M01966–M01968, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14).
  • Associated with Sneak Peak Mountain, the cloud-hidden mountain rumored to harbor bandits with near-supernatural stealth (003 §Ninja Village, §Sneak Peak Mountain).
  • Clarification: Not the Naruto village — when asked “is it there that you find the Naruto-village” (M01962, CrunchyConniptions), the answer distinguishes this as its own mountain village; the Naruto-style village “is in the sky” (M01964, FreshToast, 2023-02-14; 003 §Ninja Village).

Features

  • Supernatural ninja powers: The inhabitants have supernatural ninja powers (M01970, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14).
  • Fight the hokage: “You fight the hokage” here (M01969, FreshToast, 2023-02-14).

Characters

  • Hikari the shinobi — introduced as “hikari the shinobi with huge anime tiddies” (M01973, FreshToast, 2023-02-14). See the Character Guide.
  • Another shy character — “the other cute shy one with normal tiddies” (M01974, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14).
