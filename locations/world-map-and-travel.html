World Map, Travel & Settlement Geography

CANONCaelian OdysseyProto-Implemented
Also known as: Navigation Philosophy, Settlement Patterns, Map Layout
First conceived 2021-05-13 · last updated 2023-10-13

World Map, Travel & Settlement Geography CANON

How you move through the world and how it’s laid out — compass over maps, the river as the spine of civilization, danger rising toward the edges.

Summary — Traversal favors a compass over maps (Skyrim reference): the actual map is deliberately simple and “not that necessary.” The “REAL TABOO” is a world so hard to traverse yourself that you’re forced to stare at a minimap. Geographically, society clusters along the river and coast (safer, freshwater); the river doubles as a visual compass, and much commerce moves on it by canal. The map’s outer portions are less settled and more dangerous, with the hardest dungeons far from the river — possibly in a barren desert or underground.

Navigation philosophy

  • Compass over maps (Skyrim reference); the compass is “a neat minimap, definitely better in some ways” (2021-05-13; 003 §Navigation Philosophy).
  • The actual map is very simple and not that necessary (003 §Navigation Philosophy).
  • Design taboo: the “REAL TABOO” is when it’s too hard to traverse the world yourself, so you have to use a minimap/maps — a concern raised against Final Fantasy and Witcher examples (2021-05-13).
  • Goal: “Let there be chaos. let there be freedom. let there be adventure” (003 §Navigation Philosophy).
  • Implemented: CANON — proto in-dungeon navigation is compass-style labels with no minimap (e.g. “Press into the dark”, “Take the faster, darker way”) (proto: node compass field; src/web/screens.ts). See Dungeon Authoring & Design Canon.
  • Task noted: “Make world map” was logged as a to-do (2023-02-17).

Rivers as the world’s spine

  • Cultural importance: rivers are culturally important across multiple nations (003 §Rivers). See the countries/regions batch for Lycosia, Fiore, and others.
  • The river also acts as a visual compass (2023-10-13).
  • Twinned names: the Lycosian and Fioran rivers have related/twinned names, tied to their shared origin (2023-10-13). See Mountains — Key Mountain, Sneak Peak & Ashen Crags §Key Mountain for the river-formation lore.
  • River Healing: rivers provide special healing/restoration abilities via the river flask (items batch); refill points are the wells.
  • Conservation & reverence: CANON the river is a protected area held in deep respect; keeping it clean is a strong cultural value, and fouling it is socially prohibited (“they are kind of absurd about it”) — deliberately the opposite of real-world rivers like the Nile (2023-10-13).
  • Pray-toward-river practice: SCRAPPED a Mecca-direction analogue where everyone prays toward the river was rejected because the river is very long, making a single prayer direction impractical (003 §Rivers).

Settlement patterns / map layout CANON

  • Settlement distribution: settlements are coastal and river-centered; society is denser around the river and coast because it’s safer and gives freshwater (2023-10-13; 003 §Settlement Patterns).
  • Towns can exist close to the river but not right next to it — “the river is for everyone, not for the people that happen to settle on the river” (2023-10-13).
  • Much commerce travels on the river via canals (2023-10-13).
  • Map danger gradient: the outer portions of the map are less settled and more dangerous; the harder dungeons are far from the river, and the hardest may be in a barren desert or underground (2023-10-13). This connects to the Desert Dungeon and the deep-underground tomb.

  • 2023-02-17 — “make world map” logged as a to-do.
  • 2023-10-13 — geography fleshed out: river-and-coast-centered settlement, canals for commerce, the river as visual compass and protected cultural spine, the outer-map danger gradient with the hardest dungeons far from the river. Current presentation.
  • 2026-06-16 — proto confirms compass-style, minimap-free navigation in the implemented dungeon (003 header; proto: screens.ts).
