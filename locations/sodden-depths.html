The Sodden Depths

Caelian OdysseyNotes / DesignRegion
First conceived 2023-03-15

A location name reserved in the notes, with almost nothing attached to it yet.

Summary — “The Sodden Depths” is a named location whose nature, contents, and placement are undefined. Only “Location name established” appears in the notes (2023-03-15). The name is reserved and awaits definition.

What is (and isn’t) known

  • Only the name exists in the notes; no geography, contents, or story role were recorded (003 §The Sodden Depths).
  • The proto’s gamebible frames it as the drowned, water-bound region framing the Water Dungeon, but this is the gamebible’s inference, not established in our notes (003 §The Sodden Depths).
  • Status: name reserved, needs definition.

Open questions

  • What is the Sodden Depths — a dungeon, a region, a biome? Is it the same as, or the setting of, the Water Dungeon? Unresolved (003 §The Sodden Depths).
