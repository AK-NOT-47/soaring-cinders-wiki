Santier CANON

The distant continent — the only place the world-healing panacea fruit is known to grow, and the place Arthur Marais vanished on his final travail.

Summary — Santier is a distant continent and the sole known home of the panacea fruit, the miraculous curative from which Ambrosia is refined (M03661, FreshToast, 2023-09-07). Panacea trees are vanishingly rare and strange; those who eat the fruit claim they can hear the trees “sing.” Arthur Marais discovered and named the panacea here and vanished on his final trip; only the Ambrosia recipe returned to Khorath, without him.

Geography

  • A distant continent (003 §Santier).
  • Panacea trees are so rare and sparsely scattered that Arthur only ever handled seven mature panacea during his time on Santier (M03661, FreshToast, 2023-09-07).
  • The trees are always found in a clearing “bereft of all but the most resilient flora,” and the local wildlife will not feed on the fruit — warning signs of its darker side effects (M03661, FreshToast, 2023-09-07).
  • The third and largest tree Arthur found was isolated in a pool of clear water (M03661, FreshToast, 2023-09-07).

Features — the panacea and its strangeness

  • The “singing” trees: Those who have eaten panacea claim they can hear the trees sing while nearby; the sound intensifies the more one eats, until it becomes an intolerable furore. Arthur used this sound to locate the third tree (M03661, FreshToast, 2023-09-07).
  • The fruit: Panacea instantly restores vitality to perfect condition and even restored Arthur’s lost arm; it bears no seed, decays within days of picking, and its flavour cannot be described except by analogy to cherished memories (M03661, FreshToast, 2023-09-07).
  • Cross-reference: The panacea and Ambrosia — and panacea’s madness/revelation side effects — are primarily item/consumable lore. See the Item & Consumables guide (006 / raw M03661).

Story connections

  • Arthur’s disappearance: Santier is where Arthur Marais vanished on his final travail. Only the Ambrosia recipe returned to Khorath, created on that last trip, without Arthur, who never came back (M03661, FreshToast, 2023-09-07; 003 §Santier).
