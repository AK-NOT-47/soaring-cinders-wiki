Caelia (World)

Caelian OdysseyNotes / DesignRegion
Also known as: Alara, Iora, Corpus, Somnus
First conceived undated

Caelia is the world of Caelian Odyssey. This article describes the planet and its cosmological layers, distinct from the existing Caelia civic hub article.

Moons

Caelia has two named moons:

  • Alara is Caelia’s large moon.
  • Iora is Caelia’s smaller moon.

World spheres

Caelia is traditionally divided into two layers or spheres. These may be interpreted either as different states of being or as the same world perceived through different senses. Astrologers theorise that the spheres extend beyond the planet itself and into space.

The spheres are considered both places and states of being. Some traditions take their distinction as evidence that ordinary perception is incomplete or limited.

Corpus

Corpus is the material sphere: the physical world and the sphere most people experience most directly.

Somnus

Somnus is the conscious sphere, also understood as the spirit world. Spirits exist here, and pneuma exists in a non-corporeal state. Dreams and thoughts mingle within Somnus and can affect other realms. It is an abstract mirror of the world, and some horrors are believed to originate here.
