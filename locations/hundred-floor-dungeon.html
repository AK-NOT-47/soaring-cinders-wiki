The 100-Floor Dungeon (Early Concept) EVOLVED

The game’s very first shape: a single 100-floor co-op dungeon, later abandoned for a single-player JRPG with many distinct dungeons.

Summary — In the earliest concept (Part 1), the whole game was one 100-floor dungeon, character-based (like Genshin) and played co-op online — the “Coop Dark Souls” framing. Early floors were solo-able; later floors grew complex enough that co-op became central. This was superseded as the project moved toward a single-player JRPG with multiple distinct dungeons; it is recorded here for lineage. See Dungeon Authoring & Design Canon and Elemental & Themed Dungeon Types for the successor design.

The original concept

  • Core game concept: a 100-floor dungeon, character-based (Genshin-like), co-op online (M00128, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-05-25).
  • Difficulty scaling: early floors are so easy people can solo them; as you progress it becomes more complex; reliance on co-op becomes central (Ghost of Tsushima nightmare-raids reference) (003 §Difficulty Scaling).

Why it evolved

EVOLVED The single-100-floor structure was tied to the co-op “Coop Dark Souls” framing and was superseded once the game became a single-player JRPG with multiple distinct dungeons (003 §100 Floor Dungeon).

Note (editorial): DEBATED — editorial inference The claim that the 100-floor concept “may have evolved into the multiple-dungeon system” is an editorial inference, not stated in the notes. The notes record the 100-floor concept (Part 1) and the multiple/elemental dungeons (Part 3) separately without connecting them (003 §Difficulty Scaling).

Design history

  • 2021-05-25 — original concept: one 100-floor dungeon, character-based, co-op online, with difficulty scaling that pushes toward co-op on later floors (M00128, CrunchyConniptions).
  • later — EVOLVED superseded by a single-player JRPG with multiple distinct dungeons; see Dungeon Authoring & Design Canon (003 §100 Floor Dungeon).
