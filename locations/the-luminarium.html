The Luminarium CANON

The magic academy that gives the Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium iteration its name — a central hub where the whole story is kept “in house” and about the academy itself.

Summary — The Luminarium is a magic academy and hub area, the central setting of the separate iteration Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium (2023-10-17). It was proposed as a Dark Souls Firelink/Majula-style central safe point rather than a mechanical river-tether (2023-10-13). By design there is no antagonist faction to the Luminarium — the story is kept “in house” and about the academy (2023-10-17). It is not the same place as the main game’s Magic Academy (the former cathedral).

Type & function

  • Type: Magic academy / hub area; central to the plot (003 §The Luminarium).
  • As a hub: Proposed as a hub — “but what we CAN do is make a hub… I.e. the Luminarium” — a central safe point in the Dark Souls tradition (2023-10-13).
  • Design philosophy: “Keep it about the Luminarium” — no antagonist faction to this location; all the bad stuff is chalked up to Cyrus and Tenebrae, keeping it “in house” (2023-10-17; 003 §The Luminarium).

Features

  • Houses with guides: The academy has houses, each with a guide; the guide for “the boyes’ house” is described as cool and nice (2023-10-17).
  • Forbidden lore: Areas in the academy library are off-limits — secrets that are forbidden for reasons the plot can explore (proposal by CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-17; 003 §The Luminarium).
  • Magical tournaments: Held here as a classic way to show character growth, introduce rivals, and deepen friendships (CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-17; 003 §The Luminarium).
  • Magical creatures: Present as both threats and allies, some used in magical training (003 §The Luminarium).
  • The Academy’s Head: An ancient, wise character who has seen the rise and fall of many powerful sorcerers and holds secrets central to the plot (2023-10-17).

Story connections

  • Locke’s homecoming: Locke returns to the Luminarium later in the game, and the intent is that “returning to the Luminarium as Locke will be like seeing Anor Londo in Dark Souls 3” — an emotional homecoming via a familiar location (2023-10-13; 003 §The Luminarium).

Not to be confused with the Magic Academy

CANON The Luminarium is not the main game’s Magic Academy (the former cathedral). They belong to different iterations: the Magic Academy is the main game’s academy (core to Gaius’s story, formerly a cathedral), while the Luminarium is the academy setting of Caelian Odyssey: The Luminarium. They are not interchangeable (003 §Story Connections, Iteration Distinction note).

Design history

  • 2023-10-13 — the Luminarium proposed as a hub area — a Dark Souls Firelink/Majula-style central safe point — instead of a mechanical river-tether; the Anor-Londo-homecoming idea for Locke’s return floated the same day.
  • 2023-10-17 — the iteration named “Caelian Odyssey - The Luminarium”; features fleshed out — houses with guides, forbidden lore, magical tournaments, magical creatures, and the Academy’s Head.
  • 2023-10-17 — the antagonist question raised — “Is there a rival to the Luminarium?” — then decisively answered: no antagonist faction, keep it “in house” and about the Luminarium — supersedes the rival-faction proposal.
