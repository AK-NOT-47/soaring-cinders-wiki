The Grand Library CANON

The place where knowledge is kept from the public — the target of the “Magic Robin Hood for Knowledge.”

Summary — The Grand Library is where knowledge is deliberately withheld from the public. It is the central location of Cyril/Cyrus Erhardt’s story: he is a “Magic Robin Hood for Knowledge” who wants to acquire and share the knowledge the Library keeps locked away (M02444–M02465, CrunchyConniptions/spirit_noodle, 2023-02-19).

Features & story connection

  • Feature: knowledge is kept from the public here (003 §Grand Library).
  • Story connection: tied to Cyril/Cyrus Erhardt, the “Magic Robin Hood for Knowledge” (M02447, spirit_noodle, 2023-02-19).
  • Conflict: Cyril wants to acquire and share knowledge that is being kept from the public (003 §Grand Library).

Related theme: the “forbidden lore” motif — off-limits areas of an academy library — is one of the three agreed setting features and appears at The Luminarium as well (M04297–M04300, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-10-17). Whether the Grand Library and the Luminarium’s forbidden-lore stacks are the same place is not stated in the notes.

Design history

  • 2023-02-19 — the Grand Library is established as the store of withheld knowledge, and the setting for Cyril/Cyrus Erhardt’s “Magic Robin Hood for Knowledge” arc; his given name shifts Cyril → Cyrus and his surname settles as Erhardt (M02444–M02465, CrunchyConniptions/spirit_noodle).
