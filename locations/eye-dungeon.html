The Eye Dungeon CANON

A dungeon full of watching eyes — get seen, and the boss learns how to beat you.

Summary — The Eye Dungeon is a dungeon where eyes constantly follow you. You must destroy the eyes and avoid being seen; if the eyes see you, the end boss gathers information about your party and crafts countermeasures tailored to your specific composition. It is an information-gathering mechanic whose stakes are set at the boss fight (M04224, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-15).

Mechanic

  • Eyes constantly follow you throughout the dungeon (003 §Eye Dungeon).
  • Gameplay: you have to destroy the eyes and avoid being seen by them.
  • Consequence of being seen: the end boss gathers information about you and creates countermeasures against your specific party by analyzing that information — an adaptive boss (M04224; 005 Bestiary §adaptive boss).
  • Strategy: the more you avoid detection, the less the boss can tailor itself; the fewer eyes see you, the easier the fight.

Related systems

The adaptive-boss behavior is catalogued in the bestiary. The stealth/avoidance loop connects to the darkness and lantern stealth mechanics used across dungeons.

Design history

  • 2023-10-15 — Eye Dungeon conceived: eyes that follow you; being seen lets the end boss build party-specific countermeasures (M04224, CrunchyConniptions).
