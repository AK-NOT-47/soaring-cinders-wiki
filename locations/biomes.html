Environmental Biomes

Caelian OdysseyNotes / DesignRegion
Also known as: Level-Design Biomes, Environmental Locations
First conceived 2023-03-09

The overworld’s palette of scenic biomes, gathered as level-design references — most from a TikTok list, since real ones are usually too huge to use directly.

Summary — Under a Caribbean-inspired baseline climate, the world mixes many distinct biomes for variety. A level-design biome list was gathered (from a TikTok) on 2023-03-11: thermal pools, coastal biomes, redwood forest, sinkhole forests, salt flats, and the singular Garden of the Gods; most real-world versions “are too HUGE to use directly” (2023-03-11). A separate set of biomes exists specifically to host ambush encounters: foggy bogs, open fields, and dark areas (2023-03-09).

Overall climate

  • Tone: Caribbean-inspired climate — warm, tropical environments likely predominate (003 §Climate and Weather).
  • Variety: despite the Caribbean baseline, the world includes diverse biomes (salt flats, thermal pools, redwood forests, etc.).
  • Regional inspiration: environments may draw from Spain, Italy, and Greece for biome variety (2023-02-09).

Scenic / level-design biomes

Gathered as references (2023-03-11; 003 §Environmental Locations and Biomes):

  • Thermal Pools — a particularly interesting biome; caught the team’s eye the most among environmental options.
  • Coastal Biomes — particularly interesting; the Caribbean climate influences the overall setting.
  • Redwood Forest — tall, imposing trees creating a unique environment.
  • Sinkhole Forests — forest growing in and around sinkholes; caught the team’s eye.
  • Salt Flats — scenic, the desert equivalent of white flower fields; floated as a boss fight “on a sea of endless white.” some found it “bleh boring,” others saw potential; spirit_noodle “didn’t care about salt flats” but liked everything else.
  • White Flower Fields — used as the comparison point for other scenic biomes.
  • Open Fields — contain charging bulls; used for ambush gameplay (2023-03-09).
  • Countryside — rough-and-tumble terrain, may draw from Spain, Italy, and Greece.

Garden of the Gods

  • Type: a singular place rather than a regular, repeatable environment (2023-03-11).
  • Aesthetic: “wonderful”; spirit_noodle liked it most of all the options.
  • Location: hidden in a basin within the mountain range — “unless we hide it in a basin within the mountain range or something” (2023-03-11).

Ambush biomes

Designed specifically for ambush encounters (2023-03-09; 003 §Environmental Locations and Biomes):

  • Foggy Bogs — contain ninja frogs (“foggy bogs with ninja frogs sounds AWESOME”).
  • Open Fields — contain charging bulls.
  • Dark Areas — designed specifically for ambush encounters and stealth gameplay.

Spooky / atmospheric

  • Spooky Areas — atmospheric locations for horror/tension elements (003 §Spooky Areas).

Cross-references

  • Ashen Crags (volcanic region) and the key mountains are covered under Mountains — Key Mountain, Sneak Peak & Ashen Crags.
  • Biome variety feeds Dungeon Authoring & Design Canon’s “not just caves” principle (mansions, forests, mountain paths, ruins, temples).

Open questions

  • Whether salt flats are worth using — split between “bleh boring” and “scenic af / has potential” (2023-03-11).

Design history

  • 2023-02-09 — regional environment inspiration set: Spain, Italy, Greece.
  • 2023-03-09 — ambush biomes named: open fields with charging bulls, foggy bogs with ninja frogs, dark areas.
  • 2023-03-11 — level-design biome list gathered (thermal pools, coastal, redwood, sinkhole forests, salt flats, Garden of the Gods in a mountain basin); noted most real biomes are too huge to use directly. Current presentation.
