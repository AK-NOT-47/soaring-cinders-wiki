Mountains — Key Mountain, Sneak Peak & Ashen Crags

The setting’s major peaks: the river-source mountain that is the dragon’s lair, the cloud-hidden “Sneak Peak,” and the volcanic Ashen Crags.

Summary — Three mountain features are established. The Key Mountain is the dragon’s lair at the source of the twinned rivers, its top damaged by Ajax in his legendary dragon fight — damage that (narratively exaggerated as “splitting the mountain”) formed the rivers themselves. Sneak Peak is a cloud-hidden mountain wreathed in rumor, home to the Ninja Village and its stealthy bandits. The Ashen Crags are the volcanic region around the volcano, tied to the dragon’s domain. See also World Map, Travel & Settlement Geography for how rivers and mountains structure the map.

Key Mountain (Dragon’s Lair)

  • Follow the river to reach the top of the mountain, which is where the river starts and where the dragon is (2023-10-13; 003 §Key Mountain). The party seeks the summit “because the dragon is there.”
  • The dragon’s power stems from some feature of the mountain.
  • Ajax damaged the top during his fight with the dragon (003 §Key Mountain).
  • River-formation connection: Ajax’s damage to the mountaintop — narratively exaggerated as “splitting the mountain” — caused the twinned rivers to form from several smaller ones; the lake they feed was later flooded into its present state by the hero of the second game (“SO it’s A BIG DEAL”) (2023-10-13). Ajax was “high off absorbing a dragon’s strength at the peak of his power” when he did it. See World Map, Travel & Settlement Geography §Rivers.

Sneak Peak Mountain

  • A mountain foreshadowed and hidden by clouds, surrounded by rumor (2023-02-14; 003 §Sneak Peak Mountain).
  • Rumors include it harboring bandits with near-supernatural stealth; so much intrigue that many people search for concrete information about what happens there.
  • The reveal is the name itself — “Sneak Peak,” an intentional pun on “sneak peek.”
  • Location of the Ninja Village (the mountain ninja village, distinct from the Naruto sky-village) — see the settlements batch (2023-02-14).
  • Mountain paths here are used as terrifying dungeon locations — see Dungeon Authoring & Design Canon §Variety.

Ashen Crags (Volcanic Region)

  • The region around the volcano (2023-02-15; 003 §Geography and Natural Features).
  • Story connection: the volcanic region is tied to the dragon’s domain and the dragon confrontation; the dragon can “smash through rock, chase you down the volcano” (2023-02-11).
  • Cross-reference: the volcano’s heat is the origin of Dragon Glass (obsidian-like material formed from stone melted by the dragon’s breath then cooled; suggested names Hyrrglas / Bruniglas) — primarily item lore.
  • The Magic Academy (former cathedral) is sited near the Ashen Crags. The notes list the academy and the crags separately without linking them; the placement is a reconciliation, not stated in the notes (003 §Geography).

Other mountain features

  • Garden of the Gods is hidden in a basin within the mountain range — see Environmental Biomes.
  • Terrifying mountain paths are one of the planned non-cave dungeon types — see Dungeon Authoring & Design Canon.

Design history

  • 2023-02-11 — volcano established as the dragon’s domain: the dragon can chase you down the volcano.
  • 2023-02-14 — Sneak Peak Mountain conceived: cloud-hidden, rumor-wreathed, name is a pun, hosts the Ninja Village.
  • 2023-02-15 — the volcanic region is named the Ashen Crags.
  • 2023-10-13 — the Key Mountain is fixed as the river-source and dragon’s lair; Ajax’s mountaintop damage is retconned into the origin of the twinned rivers. Current presentation.
