Khorath

Caelian OdysseyNotes / DesignNation
First conceived undated

Khorath is the macro-setting for Caelian Odyssey and the most developed of the world’s six continents. It is the main continent currently framing the political map, with western Khorath dominated by the Lycosian Empire and its surrounding rivals, vassals, and independent peoples.

The name Khorath comes from the original Uldravi, before their fall. That makes the continent’s name a historical survival from a ruined civilisation rather than a neutral modern label.

Political shape

The current location notes frame Khorath through a westward concentration of named nations and political blocs:

  • Lycosia is the ruling state of the Lycosian Empire.
  • Fiore sits east of Lycosia, separated by a mountain range.
  • The provisional northern nation currently called N lies north of Lycosia and is technologically innovative.
  • Sylmir lies north-east of N and functions as a religious centre for Argentism.
  • Volanta is an archipelago off the western shore.
  • Straid is a floating isle south of the Lycosian coast and a vassal city-state of Lycosia.
  • Uldrav lies in the southern desert and steppe, where a former civilisation fell into ruin.
  • Cahras is an independent republic in northernmost Khorath.

Story focus

The game setting is currently centered on southern Lycosia and Straid. Khorath remains the wider continental stage around them, while Santier is a separate equatorial continent beyond the human settlement pattern described in the latest notes.

Open questions

  • Khorath is one of six continents, but the other continents are not fully named in the submitted location notes.
  • The current notes include some provisional labels, including H and N, that should be treated as placeholders until renamed.
