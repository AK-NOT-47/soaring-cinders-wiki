Gacha Fountain

DEBATEDCaelian OdysseyMixed
First conceived 2023-02-14 (M01978, CrunchyConniptions) · last in chat 2023-03-29 (M03075, FreshToast)

Gacha Fountain DEBATED

A wishing-fountain-style gambling feature where the player throws money in for a random gacha roll — mostly garbage, but with a rare legendary weapon behind a 1-in-500 pity system.

Summary — The gacha fountain is a fountain the player drops money into for a gacha-style reward (M01981, FreshToast, 2023-02-14). It mostly gives garbage or nothing, but the player is told a legendary weapon might be inside; there is a 0.2% (1/500) chance per attempt, and after 500 attempts the weapon is guaranteed via a pity system (M02002–M02007, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14). Its placement was left open in the notes between the capital and a harder dungeon; the proto later canonically sites it in Caelia’s marketplace hub. It is peppered with funny easter eggs.

Description

  • A gambling fountain: The player drops money into the fountain for a gacha-style reward (M01981, FreshToast, 2023-02-14; 003 §Gacha Fountain Mechanic).
  • Framing: The fountain “mostly gives garbage or nothing,” but the player is told there might be a legendary weapon in there (M02002–M02003, FreshToast, 2023-02-14; 003 §Gacha Fountain Rewards).
  • Design temptation: The reward is deliberately kept bad, because “gambling is fun” and players will keep going anyway — “I’d go back until I get that item” (000 Part 7 §Gacha Fountain Debate).

Rules / Effects

  • Legendary drop chance: 0.2% — “1/500 goes will getcha the item” (M02006, FreshToast, 2023-02-14; 003 §Gacha Fountain Details).
  • Pity system: Guaranteed after 500 attempts — “Maybe if they do it 500 times we can have it be guaranteed then” (M02007, FreshToast, 2023-02-14; M01989, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14 “it will have pity system”).
  • Act scaling: CANON — proto The reward should scale based on which act the player obtains it in (003 §Gacha Fountain Details; proto GAME_DESIGN.md §14).
  • Easter eggs: Dropping enough money triggers a “lil easter egg” (M01986, FreshToast, 2023-02-14); the group wanted “all kinds of funny stuff” and a guaranteed funny easter egg every ~10 attempts (M02012–M02013, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14). Named example: a “hot lady comes out and gives you a sword, names you king” (M01980, FreshToast, 2023-02-14).
  • Visual reference: PROPOSED The fountain “could be like the birds from Dark Souls” (M03075, FreshToast, 2023-03-29).

Location DEBATED

  • CANON — proto The proto sites the gacha fountain in Caelia — the marketplace hub — with the 0.2% legendary / 1-in-500 pity / act-scaling rules (proto GAME_DESIGN.md §14; 003 §Gacha Fountain Details).
  • DEBATED The Discord notes left the placement open between the capital (“dropping money in a gacha fountain in the capital,” M01981, FreshToast, 2023-02-14) and a harder dungeon alternative (M01978–M01979, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-14). It was never finalized in the brainstorm.
  • PROPOSED — proto-derived inference Equating the notes’ Capital with Caelia is a reconciliation made by the proto, not stated in the Discord notes (003 §Gacha Fountain Details).

Related systems

  • Part of Caelia, the setting’s marketplace hub, alongside the merchant Elara and the Moonstone Mania quest (003 §Marketplace).
  • Ties into the broader monetization/gacha discussion the group had for the game (see Monetization & Business).

Open questions

  • DEBATED Where the fountain lives — capital vs harder dungeon in the notes; resolved to Caelia only by the proto (003 §Gacha Fountain Details).
  • DEBATED “Exact gacha fountain mechanics and scaling” were flagged unresolved in the notes (000 Part 7 §Unresolved Questions).

Design history

  • 2023-02-14 — original concept: CrunchyConniptions proposes a fountain in the harder dungeon where you sacrifice a consumable for a “maybe gacha style” reward (M01978–M01979). FreshToast reframes it as “dropping money in a gacha fountain in the capital” (M01981). The core rules land the same day: mostly garbage, a told-about legendary, 1/500 chance, 500-attempt pity, easter eggs (M01986–M02013, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-14 — the harder-dungeon / consumable-sacrifice variant is superseded by the money-in-fountain version; the consumable-sacrifice alternative is SCRAPPED because a random-chance reward “doesn’t seem like a great reward” and is likely not profitable (003 §Gacha Fountain Details, Rejected Variant). Supersedes the harder-dungeon proposal.
  • 2023-03-29 — visual reference floated: the fountain “could be like the birds from Dark Souls” (M03075, FreshToast).
  • 2026-06-16 — proto reconciliation: GAME_DESIGN.md §14 canonically sites the gacha fountain in Caelia with the 0.2% / 1-in-500 / act-scaling rules, resolving the notes’ open capital-vs-dungeon placement in Caelia’s favor (003 §Gacha Fountain Details).
