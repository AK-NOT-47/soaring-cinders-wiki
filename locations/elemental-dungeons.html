Elemental & Themed Dungeon Types CANON

The roster of dungeon flavors — elemental (lava, water, poison, magic, forest, desert) and enemy-themed (goblin, vampire, dragon, skeleton).

Summary — Rather than one mega-dungeon (see the superseded 100-Floor Dungeon), the game uses many distinct dungeons, each with a flavor that incentivizes different skills and characters. Two organizing schemes appear in the notes: elemental dungeons (Lava, Water, Poison Swamp, Magic, Forest, Desert) and enemy-type areas (Goblin, Vampire, Dragon, Skeleton Tomb). This page is the combined roster; general dungeon rules live in Dungeon Authoring & Design Canon.

Elemental dungeons

The listed elemental dungeon types (M00853, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-02; 003 §Elemental Dungeons):

  • Lava Dungeon
  • Water Dungeon — the proto’s gamebible ties this to the drowned region it calls the Sodden Depths, though that link is the gamebible’s inference, not established in the notes.
  • Poison Swamp Dungeon
  • Magic Dungeon
  • Forest Dungeon
  • Desert Dungeon — note that the notes elsewhere suggest the hardest dungeon “may be in the middle of a barren desert or underground” (see World Map, Travel & Settlement Geography).

Only the type names are recorded; individual contents were not detailed in the notes.

Enemy-type areas

Each area tends to have a main enemy type (M05365–M05376, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2024-03-12; 003 §Enemy Type Areas):

  • Goblin Area — dominated by goblin enemies.
  • Vampires Area — dominated by vampire enemies.
  • Dragon Area — dragon or dragon-related enemies. See Vaenngrym (bestiary).
  • Skeleton Tomb — a desired dungeon type featuring skeletons; skeletons were deemed near-mandatory: “Dungeons and no skeletons? A travesty,” with room for “a funny skeleton” (M05365–M05376). This theme is realized in the prologue dungeon’s Bone Gallery and Ossuary Sentinel.

Related design

Dungeon variety extends beyond elements and enemies to whole settings — mansions, forests, ruins, temples — see Dungeon Authoring & Design Canon §Variety.

Design history

  • 2023-02-02 — elemental dungeon list first logged: Lava, Water, Poison Swamp, Magic, Forest, Desert (M00853, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2024-03-12 — enemy-type areas established: goblin, vampires, dragon, plus the mandatory skeleton tomb (M05365–M05376, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast).
