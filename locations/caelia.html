Caelia

DEBATEDCaelian OdysseyMixed
Also known as: Caelia's Breath, Strayed, Straid, Straid Isle, Strayed Isle, Capital
First conceived 2023-02-08 (M01170, FreshToast) · last in chat 2024-02-13 (M05266, FreshToast)

Caelia DEBATED

The bustling marketplace hub at the heart of Caelian Odyssey — a warm, Caribbean-flavoured trading centre whose exact nature (isle, city, or whole region) is still an open thread.

Summary — Caelia is the setting’s central marketplace hub: a bustling, Caribbean-climate town of unique food and active trade, home to the merchant Elara, the start of the Moonstone Mania quest, and the gacha fountain. Its name went through a naming brainstorm (Caelia, Caelia’s Breath, Straid/Strayed, Straid Isle) and the setting itself lends its name to the iteration Caelian Odyssey. What Caelia is — a marketplace hub, a coastal isle, or a whole region — is genuinely DEBATED, which is why this page’s status is DEBATED.

Classification — the open thread DEBATED

The notes and the proto’s gamebible disagree on what Caelia is (003 §Notable Features):

  • The proto’s gamebible / regions codex floats it as a coastal isle/region in its own right, under the “Straid Isle” variant name.
  • GAME_DESIGN.md (canon) and the proto’s locations codex class it as a marketplace hub, not a nation or region.

This is a lore/classification difference rather than a mechanic. Both readings are recorded; whether Caelia is an isle, a city, or a whole region is unresolved. See Open questions.

Features

  • Marketplace: A bustling marketplace and active trading hub (003 §Marketplace).
  • Food: Home to unique food items — Crunchy Lettuce (a crunchy, refreshing packet street food, an inside joke on CrunchyConniptions) and noodle dishes (M04934, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-11-07; 003 §Notable Features). Food lore is detailed in the Item guide.
  • Climate & geography: Caribbean-inspired (tropical/warm) climate; biome variety may draw from Spain, Italy and Greece (003 §Notable Features).
  • Reactivity: Caelia can undergo significant changes based on player actions — e.g. the “Cult of Mundane Moments” scenario (003 §Notable Features).

The gacha fountain

Caelia is the CANON — proto home of the gacha fountain, where the player drops money for a gacha-style roll (mostly garbage, with a rare legendary weapon on a 0.2% / 1-in-500 pity system, scaling by act). The proto’s canon resolves a long-open placement question in Caelia’s favor (GAME_DESIGN.md §14; 003 §Gacha Fountain Details). See the gacha fountain page for full rules.

PROPOSED — proto-derived inference, not in Discord notes — Equating the notes’ Capital (which “has a gacha fountain”) with Caelia is itself a reconciliation. The Discord notes say only “Capital: Has a gacha fountain” (M01981, FreshToast, 2023-02-14) and later “in capital (or harder dungeon alternative)”; they never identify the Capital as Caelia. The identity is derived from the proto, not stated in the notes (003 §Gacha Fountain Details).

Story connections

  • Moonstone Mania: The merchant Elara is found in the Caelia marketplace; the quest “Moonstone Mania” begins here (M05266–M05272, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13; 003 §Marketplace). See also the Marketplace point-of-interest page.

Open questions

  • DEBATED What Caelia is: marketplace hub (GAME_DESIGN.md canon) vs coastal isle/region (“Straid Isle” in the gamebible). Unresolved (003 §Notable Features).
  • PROPOSED Whether the notes’ Capital is the same place as Caelia (proto-derived inference, not in the notes) (003 §Gacha Fountain Details).

Design history

  • 2023-02-08 — naming brainstorm: Caelia, Caelia’s Breath, Strayed, Straid, Straid Isle, Strayed Isle all floated in a song/place-naming session (M01170–M01175, FreshToast). Caelia became the settled name and lent it to the Caelian Odyssey iteration.
  • 2023-02-14 — the notes’ Capital established as the home of the gacha fountain (“dropping money in a gacha fountain in the capital,” M01981, FreshToast) — later reconciled to Caelia by the proto.
  • 2023-11-07 — Caelian food established: Crunchy Lettuce and a noodle dish (M04934, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2024-02-13 — the Moonstone Mania quest and the merchant Elara placed in the Caelia marketplace (M05266–M05272, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2026-06-16 — proto reconciliation: GAME_DESIGN.md classes Caelia as a marketplace hub and canonically sites the gacha fountain here, while the gamebible’s “Straid Isle” reading keeps the isle/region interpretation alive — the classification remains DEBATED (003 §Notable Features, §Gacha Fountain Details).
