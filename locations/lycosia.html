Lycosia CANON

The southern country that is the seat of the empire — a river-defined great power whose big river runs through the south.

Summary — Lycosia is a country in the south and the seat of an empire, “good in terms of power” (2023-10-13). Its “big boy” river runs through the south and serves as a social and economic throughline for the nation. Its language is Imperial Lycosian, and its river’s name is meant to be twinned with Fiore’s.

Geography

  • A country in the south, with a big river in the south (003 §Countries and Regions).
  • Lycosia’s river flows in one direction, distinct from Fiore’s river, which flows in a different direction (2023-10-13; 003 §Countries and Regions).
  • There is one main big river plus other smaller, less-important rivers across the setting; Lycosia’s is one of the two named great rivers (2023-10-13).

Politics & culture

  • Political status: Seat of empire; good in terms of power (2023-10-13). In the same discussion the struggling rival country was framed as a republic or dictatorship (2023-10-13).
  • River culture: The river is a social and economic throughline for Lycosia; society is denser around the river and coast because it is safer and provides freshwater, and much commerce travels the river via canals (2023-10-13; 003 §Settlement Patterns).
  • Language: Imperial Lycosian is the spoken language (003 §Countries and Regions).

Story connections

  • Twinned river names: The Lycosian and Fioran rivers should have twinned/related names, tied to the lore that Ajax “split a mountain” to make them (2023-10-13; 003 §Rivers). See Fiore and the regional overview.

Design history

  • 2023-10-13 — Lycosia established as the seat of an empire, “good in terms of power” , during the river-politics session that also produced the twinned-river idea and rejected a single-nation river monopoly.
  • 2023-10-13 (later in the same session) — river detail expanded: the big southern river as a social/economic throughline, flowing in one direction opposite Fiore’s, with commerce moving by canal.
