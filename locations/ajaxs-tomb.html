Ajax’s Tomb & the Prologue Dungeon CANON

The game’s opening dungeon — a dark cave that turns out to hide the secret entrance to the tomb of the legendary hero Ajax.

Summary — The prologue dungeon is the first area of the game: a dark cave where Lysander leads an expedition (with Eris and Locke Evertide) hunting a chaos emerald. During an ambush, Locke is separated from the others, and while alone she acquires the Sunderer. What the party reaches at the end is not a dead end but the secret entrance to Ajax’s Tomb — found because Locke read a clue Lysander misread. The proto implements this whole lineage as one coded graph dungeon, “The Descent into Ajax’s Tomb” (proto: src/core/campaign/content.ts, id: 'ajax_tomb_descent').

Story role

  • Type: dark cave; the initial dungeon visited at the beginning of the game (003 §Tutorial Dungeon).
  • Lysander is here looking for a chaos emerald; the lantern-carrying Locke accompanies him. It introduces the Lysander–Locke dynamic and Lysander’s protective-but-dismissive power (M02442–M02450, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-02-19).
  • Beat sheet (M03669, FreshToast, 2023-09-10): Lysander center, flanked by Locke and Eris; Locke’s sword is lost/broken; combat is broken by a puzzle showcasing Locke’s competence; an ambush separates Locke; she finds/acquires the Sunderer; reunites for another battle; Eris commends her; stealth & light mechanics are introduced; a final climactic test ends the tutorial.
  • Screenplay (first draft, M05476–M05521, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2024-08-24): Lysander leaps in and gives Locke his backup sword (“a hero looks out for his companions”); the borrowed sword shatters against an ancient golem; Locke falls to a lower level, finds a rusty spear, discovers the golems’ joint weakness, and defeats two golems; the sword is symbolic of power Lysander gave her — foreshadowing his later betrayal.

The hidden connection

What they reach at the end is not a dead end but a secret entrance to Ajax’s Tomb. Lysander and Eris assume their intel about a relic in the cavern was false; Locke finds it because she read the clue well (“the joint, not the wall” — the clue Lysander misread) (003 §Tutorial Dungeon §Hidden Connection; M03670, FreshToast, 2023-09-10). The Sunderer is the first of Ajax’s seven relics, acquired in the trial Locke passes.

Secret: the tutorial dungeon later gains an additional secret section towards endgame, where the rumored chaos emerald is actually found (003 §Tutorial Dungeon §Secret).

Ajax’s Tomb proper

  • Access: the sealed sun-marked door, set with a sunset-colored gem, revealed beyond the boss (proto: content.ts, outro + node n5).
  • Significance: related to Ajax’s legacy and the chaos-emerald relics. Brought together, the relics don’t grant superpowers — they give a revelation, a way to learn about Ajax and his bloodline (003 §Seven Chaos Emeralds; M02428–M02438, FreshToast, 2023-02-19).
  • Ajaxian trials already exist in-game: challenges left behind by Ajax (M05288, FreshToast, 2024-02-13).

Implemented node path CANON — proto

The coded graph (proto: src/core/campaign/content.ts) dramatizes the beats above:

  • The Spare Sword (start) — where the fall left the party; Lysander’s borrowed backup blade.
  • The Crumbling Stair (fight) — goblins nest in the rubble (goblin_imp ×2 + slime).
  • Branch A — Glimmer in the Rubble (treasure) — the borrowed sword shatters on the slime; Locke pulls free the Sunderer (relic reward).
  • Branch B — Ambush in the Narrows (elite, ambush) — river brigands and the restless dead strike first, scattering formation; “+4 max HP each” (Trial of Nerve).
  • A Breath by the Underground River (camp) — a clean, cold underground river; fill the flask and rest (“Rivers are for everyone — even down here”). See Camps & the Wells (Bonfire Network).
  • The Bone Gallery (fight) — an ossuary of the honored dead (slime + skeleton_archer ×2); heal 40% reward.
  • The Ossuary Sentinel (boss, tomb_golem) — a colossus of bound bone and adamant stone guarding the threshold; beyond it, the sealed sun-marked door — the entrance to Ajax’s Tomb proper.

Design history

  • 2023-02-19 — prologue conceived: dark cave, Locke’s lantern, Lysander hunting a chaos emerald he fails to find (misread clue), in medias res opening; the dungeon later gets a secret endgame section (M02442–M02450, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-09-10 — the tutorial dungeon’s end is reframed as the secret entrance to Ajax’s Tomb, and the Sunderer becomes the first of Ajax’s seven relics (M03669, M03670, FreshToast). Supersedes the plain “dead end” reading.
  • 2024-08-24 — full first-draft screenplay: borrowed sword shatters against a golem, Locke falls and finds the rusty spear, foreshadowing Lysander’s betrayal (M05476–M05521, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast). Current presentation.
  • 2026-06-16 — proto encodes all of the above as one graph, “The Descent into Ajax’s Tomb” (003 §The Descent into Ajax’s Tomb; proto: content.ts).
