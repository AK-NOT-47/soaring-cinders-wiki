The town partially ruined by a dragon attack, which the party helps rebuild over the course of the game — a living progression system in place form.

Summary — This is a town partially ruined by a dragon attack “after it yoinkies the sploinky,” with repairs that progress over time as the game continues (M01823, FreshToast, 2023-02-12). It functions as a town-progression system: reconstruction happens gradually as the player advances. The town has no finalized proper name in the notes, so its working name is used here.

Description

  • A town partially ruined by a dragon attack, phrased in the chat as happening “after it yoinkies the sploinky” (M01823, FreshToast, 2023-02-12).
