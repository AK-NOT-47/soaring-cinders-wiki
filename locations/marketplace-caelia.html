Marketplace (Caelia) CANON

The bustling trading heart of Caelia — an active hub where the merchant Elara sends you off on the Moonstone Mania quest.

Summary — The Marketplace is the bustling trading centre within Caelia, staffed by the weary merchant Elara and serving as the starting point of the Moonstone Mania quest (M05266–M05272, FreshToast/CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13). It is the specific point of interest at the core of Caelia’s identity as a marketplace hub; broader lore about Caelia lives on the Caelia page.

Description

  • A bustling marketplace and active trading hub (003 §Marketplace (Caelia)).
  • Set in Caelia: “A bustling marketplace in Caelia” (M05271, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13).

NPCs

  • Elara — a weary merchant of the Caelia marketplace, who the player approaches to kick off the Moonstone quest (M05271, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13; 003 §Marketplace).

Story connections — Moonstone Mania

  • The Moonstone Mania quest begins here: the player pitches a moonstone-hoarding scheme to Elara (a riff on their old gem-hoarding exploits with sapphires, rubies and emeralds), and Elara reluctantly hands over a starting stash (M05271, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13).
  • The quest escalates until the reclusive Archmage Xarthus dubs the player the “Moonstone Messiah” (M05271–M05272, CrunchyConniptions, 2024-02-13). The correct Skyrim-style reward is actually a permanent buff to find more valuables in chests (M05266, FreshToast, 2024-02-13). Quest details are in the Quest guide.
