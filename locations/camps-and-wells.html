Camps & the Wells (Bonfire Network)

CANONCaelian OdysseyProto-Implemented
Also known as: Bonfire Network, Wells, Rest Points, Camp System
First conceived 2023-02-17 (M02381, FreshToast) · last in chat 2023-10-13 (M03959)

Camps & the Wells (Bonfire Network) CANON

The rest-and-resupply network — dungeon camps at the threshold and world-spanning wells that act like Dark Souls bonfires.

Summary — Rest points come in two forms. Inside a dungeon, the main camp occupies the beginning of the dungeon; resting there restores resources and (in the proto) the degrading max-HP ceiling, and originally “resets the dungeon dark souls style” (M02381, FreshToast, 2023-02-17). Across the overworld, wells are “our bonfires” — some man-made, some natural — where you refill the healing flask; any fresh-water source will do, but river water is especially good (M03953, M03959, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast, 2023-10-13). Both are tied to the River Healing resupply scheme.

Camp system (inside dungeons)

  • Location: while in a dungeon, the main camp occupies the beginning of the dungeon (003 §Camp System).
  • Purpose: resting and resource management within dungeon exploration.
  • Original rule: “resting here resets the dungeon dark souls style” (M02381, FreshToast, 2023-02-17).
  • Implemented: CANON — proto camp/rest nodes occupy the dungeon (node kind camp); resting restores the per-fight degrading max-HP ceiling (which degrades 3/fight, floored ~60% of base, and only rest restores it) (proto: src/core/campaign/run.ts). In the prologue dungeon, the camp is “A Breath by the Underground River,” realizing River Healing underground (“Rivers are for everyone — even down here”).

Wells / rest points (the bonfire network)

  • Description: wells exist around the world — “our bonfires” — some man-made, some naturally occurring (M03953, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-13; 003 §Wells / Rest Points).
  • Refill rule: any fresh-water source will do to refill the healing flask, but one from the river is especially good (M03959, FreshToast, 2023-10-13).

Design lineage: the whole idea derives from Dark Souls’ “bonfire and estus system” (M00140, CrunchyConniptions, 2021-05-25). The team deliberately rejected DS1-style bonfire variance (some bonfires giving more flasks than others), following the later Souls games that did away with it (M04109, FreshToast, 2023-10-13).

Cross-reference

Tied to the River Healing / river flask mechanic (items batch). The river-dependent resupply scheme was itself extensively debated and largely walked back as too limiting (see the Estus/healing debate in the item/mechanics docs). The wells being “any fresh-water source” is part of that walk-back — you are not chained to the river itself.

Open questions

  • DEBATED How strictly resupply depends on the river vs. any well/fresh-water source — the balance was still being negotiated (003 §Wells cross-reference; item/mechanics docs).

Design history

  • 2021-05-25 — origin: adopt Dark Souls’ “bonfire and estus system” (M00140, CrunchyConniptions).
  • 2023-02-17 — dungeon camp defined at the start of the dungeon; resting “resets the dungeon dark souls style” (M02381, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-13 — wells established as the world’s bonfire network (“The wells are our bonfires”), refillable from any fresh-water source though river water is best; bonfire variance rejected (M03953, M03959, M04109, CrunchyConniptions/FreshToast). Current presentation.
  • 2026-06-16 — proto confirms camp nodes and the degrading-HP-ceiling restore-on-rest model (003 §Camp; proto: run.ts).
