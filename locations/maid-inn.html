Maid Inn (MAIDEN) CANON

The rest-stop inn and maid-cafe parody where you talk to NPCs to rest — and where the maid’s “moe moe kyun” weapon blessing is secretly real magic.

Summary — The Maid Inn (“MAIDEN,” a wordplay on the name) is an inn and rest location styled as a maid-cafe parody (M01262–M01263, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). It is framed as a place for disadvantaged women to find work, and functionally it is the rest stop where you talk to an NPC to rest (M01262, M01267, FreshToast, 2023-02-09). It has a bar, and the resident maid blesses weapons with a “moe moe kyun” theme — a blessing that is actually functional magic.

Type & purpose

  • Type: Inn / rest location / maid-cafe parody (M01263, FreshToast, 2023-02-09; 003 §Maid Inn).
  • A place for disadvantaged women to find work (M01262, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • A rest stop: The resident maid “works the bar” and is “the NPC you talk to for rest” (M01267, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Features

  • Has a bar (M01267, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • The maid & the blessing: A super-nice maid (M01268, FreshToast) blesses weapons (M01269, CrunchyConniptions) “with the whole moe moe kyun thing” (M01270, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).
  • The food blessing is real magic: “The food… blessing is actually magic” — a functional gameplay element, not just flavor (M01271, FreshToast, 2023-02-09).

Name note

“MAIDEN” is a wordplay on maid + inn (M01285–M01286, FreshToast, 2023-02-09; 003 §Maid Inn).

Story connections

  • The cross-reference table floats a possible connection between Clara / Marie and the Maid Inn (003 §Character-Location Connections).
