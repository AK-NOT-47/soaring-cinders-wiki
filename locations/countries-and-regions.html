Countries and Regions CANON

The nation-states and major regions of the Caelian Odyssey world — a river-defined geopolitics of empires, underdog nations, and one distant panacea-bearing continent.

Summary — The setting’s politics revolve around rivers. Lycosia is the seat of an empire; Fiore lies across the mountains; the independent underdog nations Cahras and Uldrav sit between the great powers; Khorath is the broad region where much of the story plays out and where rivers are culturally sacrosanct; and the peaceful nation Lethria is homeland to a noble character. This page collects the countries and regions that are too small for their own file; the larger ones (Lycosia, Fiore, Santier, Caelia) have dedicated pages.

The single most-debated question in this brainstorm was who controls the river — a proposal that one country monopolize it (M03827, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-13) was ultimately rejected in favor of “a river just belongs to the country who owns the land” (M03855, FreshToast, 2023-10-13). See Open questions.

Khorath CANON

Khorath is a major region/country where significant story events occur (003 §Countries and Regions).

  • Culture: Rivers are culturally important across Khorath, expressed through river-related holidays and sayings rather than being tied to any one religion (M03777–M03779, FreshToast, 2023-10-13; 003 §Countries and Regions).
  • Demographics: Cyrus, Alistair, Pandora and Titan are all ethnically typical for western Khorath — a Mediterranean look; Colette is described as a bit darker (M04242–M04243, FreshToast, 2023-10-17).
  • Story connection: The recipe for Ambrosia was returned here — carried back from Santier without Arthur, who vanished on that final travail (003 §Countries and Regions; see Santier).

Cahras and Uldrav CANON

Two independent underdog nations — small political entities that stand apart from the great powers (M03849, FreshToast, 2023-10-13; 003 §Cahras and Uldrav). They emerged from the same river-politics discussion that established the empire of Lycosia and the struggling rival country framed as a republic or dictatorship (M03847, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-13).

Lethria CANON

  • Description: A small and peaceful nation (Part 4 World Bible; 003 §Cahras and Uldrav).
  • Temperament: Known for a strong sense of justice and compassion; values peace and often strives to bring about harmony (003 §Cahras and Uldrav).
  • Story connection: Homeland of the Lethria noble — the Malenia / Leyla / Marie character, a daughter of a noble family groomed for leadership around the theme “Vengeance vs Justice” (003 §Cahras and Uldrav; see the Character Guide).
  • Provenance note: Lethria is present in the brainstorm notes (Part 4) and carried into the proto’s region codex (gamebible/data/regions.js); it had been omitted from the guide’s nation list and was added on 2026-06-16 (003 §Cahras and Uldrav). It does not resolve to a dated raw Discord message under this spelling, so its first_conceived is taken from the Part 4 World Bible.

Related pages

  • Lycosia — seat of the empire, its river in the south.
  • Fiore — country across the mountains, home of Damian.
  • Santier — distant continent, sole source of the panacea fruit.
  • Caelia — the marketplace hub whose classification as isle / city / region is unresolved.

Open questions

  • DEBATED River control: whether one prosperous nation should monopolize the river (proposed M03827, M03853, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-13) or whether “a river just belongs to the country who owns the land” (M03855, FreshToast, 2023-10-13). The monopoly idea was explicitly floated with a jealous rival country “always looking for a way to get some” (M03828–M03829, CrunchyConniptions, 2023-10-13) but was rejected in the guide’s canon framing.

Design history

  • 2023-10-13 — the geopolitics were laid down in a single session: Fiore established as the country across the mountains where Damian is from (M03826, FreshToast); a proposed river monopoly with a jealous rival (M03827–M03829, CrunchyConniptions); Lycosia named the seat of an empire, “good in terms of power” (M03843, FreshToast); the struggling rival framed as a republic or dictatorship (M03847, CrunchyConniptions); and Cahras and Uldrav named as independent underdog nations (M03849, FreshToast).
  • 2023-10-13 — the river-monopoly proposal was resolved against: “A river just belongs to the country who owns the land” (M03855, FreshToast) — supersedes the monopoly idea, which survives only as an Open question.
  • 2026-06-16 — Lethria added to the nation list from the Part 4 World Bible and the proto’s region codex, having previously been omitted (003 §Cahras and Uldrav).
